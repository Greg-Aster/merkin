This is a working reference for an art-plush collaboration around the **Snuggaloid** — a small furry creature from the MEGAMEAL universe. In the fiction they were sold as companion pets but their origin is uncertain; in the real world we want to make them as one-off handmade art objects, fist-sized or smaller, permanently sealed, with strange internals you can feel through the fur. Each unit is unique, logged in a public site registry, and explicitly **not a toy and not for children**. The goal is to translate the existing lore into a real, collectible object without losing the unsettling tone that makes Snuggaloids interesting.

## Mythology

In-universe, Snuggaloids come in many shapes, sizes, and colors, and make adorable noises — but some are reported to watch people sleep, small groups have been seen in the dark with glowing eyes, and they gather objects from refuse bins. Food and small items go missing near them (especially from fridges), and they are routinely blamed for lost puzzle pieces. Some owners vanish. A handful of stranger reports describe glowing, color-shifting, mild radiation, floating when unobserved, or passing through containers without opening doors, leading to the persistent rumor that they are transdimensional or only partly present in normal space. None of this needs to be literal in the physical object; it just shapes the voice we use around it.

## Build

Each Snuggaloid should be fist-sized or smaller, with a bright, strange, cute exterior in varied shape and color, and sealed permanently — no zippers, no openings. The fill should feel *odd* in the hand: dense shredded memory foam, or a hardened/squishy gel-like core. Embedded inside are small objects that can be detected by touch through the body. Examples we've discussed:

- 3D-printed organs, fake anatomy, a finger
- A puzzle piece, charms, tiny relics, glitter
- A ob tampon, a condom, a fake blood packet
- A photo of the owner's house
- A note from a child factory worker insisting conditions are good
- Smaller Snuggaloids inside

## Registry & Custom Orders

Buyers can pick an existing unit or commission a custom one. Custom orders should feel like a MEGAMEAL form rather than a normal plush configurator — some options should be absurd or unsettling (*no blood*, *radioactive*, *sonar sensing / non-ocular*, *extra organs*).

Every completed unit becomes a registry entry on the site, tracking:

- Unit name and number
- Photos
- Physical notes (size, materials, colors, exterior traits)
- Internal notes (Hints of what's inside)
- A short in-universe lore note
- Status: *available*, *claimed*, *archived*, *missing*, *custom*, or *not in your area*

## Store Page Tone

The real facts stay plain: one-off handmade art plush, not for children, sealed, with unique internal objects detectable by touch, every unit unique. The in-universe flavor — *not in your area*, *bonding status unknown*, *owner records incomplete*, *orientation drift reported*, *do not store near unfinished puzzles* — sits decoratively around that factual core, never replacing it.
