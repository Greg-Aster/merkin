## What is a Snuggaloid?

A **Snuggaloid** (variant spelling: *Snuggloid*) is a fictional creature from the extended Science fiction cosmic horror fast food pardoy franchise - MEGAMEAL. It is a small furred entity originating on Planet Qarnivor. Marketed as a companion pet by the Qarnivor Companion Division, the species is now better known for its post-extinction emergence and the persistent rumor that it is transdimensional or only partly present in normal space.

Traits include bright coloful often multihued fur, number of and limbs eyes vaires, and features also vary - every snugglaoid is unique. They are routinely blamed for lost puzzle pieces, food and small items go missing in their vicinity. There is a mysterious element about them and many facts about them remain unknown. Mysterious stories circulate abouth them, glowing eyes, mild radiation, levitation when unobserved, passage through sealed containers, watching owners sleep, extreme avoidance by other household pets, strange smells,some owners disappear.

In the real world, Snuggaloids exist as one-off handmade art objects produced by MEGAMEAL Product Development. **Not a toy. Not for children.**

## Timeline

- **Pre-extinction Qarnivor** -- Qarnivor Companion Division launches the commercial Snuggloid program."
- **~5 years before nuclear exchange** -- The SNUGGLOIDS™ commercial airs on Qarnivor media networks.
- **The Spork Annihilation Events** -- Pro-Spoon and Anti-Spoon factions exchange nuclear ordnance. Surface organic life ends on planet Qarnivor.
- **Post-exchange interregnum** -- Hardened automated war factories continue manufacturing combat drones post life extinction. Pro-Spoon and Anti-Spoon machines fight on without surviving operators.
- **~1 year after last organic signal** -- A passing trade vessel detects anomalous energy signatures on Qarnivor's surface. First Snuggloid sensor traces logged.
- **The Emergence** -- Entities resembling the commercial product appear across the irradiated wasteland. Sizes range from rodent-scale to Kaizu. Bodies phase in and out of normal space; fur glows.
- **Machine response** -- Some war drones classify Snuggaloids as targets. Other drones fail to detect them at all and pass through their positions.
- **Visitor reports** -- A figure documented only as "(name redacted for legal reasons)" returns with recordings; Dr Linda Sue mounts an unauthorized expedition and is added to the missing persons registry.
- **Present** -- Qarnivor is observed at distance by Gluttony Cartel bio-prospectors and rogue xenolinguists. Off-world Snuggaloid sightings continue. A healthy collectible market of these companion creatures continue to grow despite manufacturing stoppage. Owner-disappearance cases continue to accumulate.

## Store, Registry & Development Status

- **Store** -- [Store listing](/store/snuggaloids/) shows current units. Currently listed as *not in your area*. Future plans will be able to select available units or customize your own, cusomizable options include radioactivy level, fur color/pattern, extra organs, extra blood/no blood "Vampire style", number of eye "ocular level", scent glands ect.
- **Registry** -- Every completed unit is logged at the [Snuggaloid Registry](/snuggaloids/) with name and number, photos, exterior notes (size, materials, colors), internal hints, and a short in-universe lore note. Status flags: *available*, *claimed*, *archived*, *missing*, *custom*, *not in your area*.
- **Development status** In development - several prototype made but havent one a good design yet.

## Design Notes

- **Form** -- size varies from hand sized to small plush. Exterior can be faux fur, felted or other. The Snuggaloid has a 3d printed interior skeleton, and odd items embeded. Ideas include tiny organs, puzzle piece blood capsules, notes, pictures lore, condom or ob tampon ect. The design is permanently sealed and the interior components can be felt though the outside, but cannot be accessed without destroying the Snugglaoid.
