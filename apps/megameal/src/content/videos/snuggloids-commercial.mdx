## Commercial File

Before the Snuggloids became a post-extinction biological and metaphysical problem, they were sold as comfort companions. This commercial belongs to that earlier, brighter, more legally confident phase of the disaster.

It plays best as a piece of product mythology: cheerful claims, impossible assurances, and the kind of mascot logic that only becomes ominous after the historical record catches up.
