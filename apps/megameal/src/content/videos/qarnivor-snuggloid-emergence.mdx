## Archival Note

Immediately following the events on planet Qarnivor after the Corporate Empire, there exists a single account of an encounter with the Snuggloids that is of some scholarly interest.

Despite the incomplete transcript and unreliable nature of the narrator, the account is believed to come from the enigmatic figure mentioned in Qarnivor historical records as the name redacted for legal reasons. Her documented visit shortly after the nuclear devastation provides one of the only eyewitness reports of the Snuggloid emergence.

Of particular note is the timeline between the emergence of the Snuggloids and the extinction of the Qarnivores. It was previously assumed there was no overlap between these two events. If this account is taken at face value and assumed correct, there may be some link between the two.

## The Witness Problem

The narrator's description of glowing fur and entities that phased in and out of visibility aligns with later scientific observations of the Snuggloids. That does not make the account reliable. It makes it difficult to dismiss.

There is also the Starbug reference, which appears in several other historical accounts across different extinction events. The problem with recurring impossible details is that they can be coincidence, contamination, or evidence. Historical departments prefer the first two options because they involve less paperwork.

## Why It Matters

If the witness account is correct, the Snuggloids were not simply discovered after the devastation. They may have emerged during the final collapse, while the Qarnivore extinction event was still unfolding.

That changes the shape of the event. It suggests the Snuggloids were not only survivors, scavengers, or cute little post-apocalyptic anomalies. They may have been observers. They may have been participants. They may have been something waiting for the correct amount of ruin.

The video does not resolve that question. It gives the account a place to breathe.
