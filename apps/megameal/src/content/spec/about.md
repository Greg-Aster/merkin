## About The Project

**MEGAMEAL is a mixed-media cosmic horror fast food parody of capitalism.**

This is a site we make because we want it to exist. It's not monetized. It doesn't run ads. It isn't optimized for clicks, engagement, or growth, and it isn't trying to be a brand. There's no plan to scale it, sell it, or turn it into anything other than what it is.

Megameal is intended to be somewhere with spirit. Somewhere you can sit inside for a while without being measured, tracked, or sold to.

We make it because we like art, and we believe this kind of thing has a place in the world.

---

If you want to know what any of it means, the answer is mostly in the work.
