## Chef's Note

True gastronomy is not merely about taste. It is about the full spectrum of sensory experience: sight, aroma, texture, memory, and the faint suspicion that dessert is looking back at you.

The inspiration for this chromatic confection came during a bioluminescence harvest on the oceanic moon of Triton-7. The organisms pulsed with internal light, creating an underwater tapestry of living color. I wanted that same vibrancy inside a cake.

And the candied sweetmeats? That is where we leave ordinary dessert behind. Imagine the comforting density of a traditional cake crumb, the warmth of ginger, and a savory whisper that should not work but does.

## Cake Ingredients

- 300 g grated carrot
- 200 g fresh ginger, finely grated
- 250 g cake flour
- 150 g almond flour or finely ground nut flour
- 3 eggs
- 150 ml neutral oil
- 100 g beet powder or finely pureed roasted beet
- 50 g spirulina powder, used lightly for color
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp cardamom
- 2 tsp baking powder
- Pinch of salt

## Frosting and Crumble

- 400 g cream cheese
- 200 g confectioners' sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 200 g candied ginger, chopped
- Optional: crisp bacon or candied mushroom crumble for the sweetmeat note
- Optional: edible shimmer powder

## Method

1. Divide the dry cake mixture into four bowls.
2. Tint one portion with carrot, one with beet, one lightly with spirulina, and one with ginger and spice.
3. Whisk eggs and oil, then divide across the bowls.
4. Fold each batter gently. Preserve the colors instead of beating them into beige compliance.
5. Bake the layers at 350 F for 20 to 25 minutes.
6. Cool completely.
7. Whip cream cheese, confectioners' sugar, and vanilla until smooth.
8. Stack the layers with frosting between each one.
9. Finish the top with candied ginger and the optional sweetmeat crumble.

## Service and Preservation

Slice with a sharp knife to reveal the colored strata. Serve cold or just below room temperature. Leftovers keep in an airtight container for up to four days, assuming the cake has not achieved mobility.
