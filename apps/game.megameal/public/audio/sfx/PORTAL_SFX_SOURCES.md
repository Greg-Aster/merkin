# Portal SFX Sources

The `portal-*.mp3` files are edited from downloaded CC0 sound packs:

- `portal-awaken.mp3`, `portal-reveal.mp3`
  - Source: "4 Space / Portal Sounds" by Ecrivain on OpenGameArt.
  - License: CC0.
  - URL: https://opengameart.org/content/4-space-portal-sounds
- `portal-activate.mp3`
  - Source: `teleport_02.ogg` from "50 CC0 Sci-Fi SFX" by rubberduck on OpenGameArt.
  - License: CC0.
  - URL: https://opengameart.org/content/50-cc0-sci-fi-sfx
- `portal-hover.mp3`
  - Source: "Interface Sounds" by Kenney on OpenGameArt.
  - License: CC0.
  - URL: https://opengameart.org/content/interface-sounds
- `portal-drag.mp3`, `portal-cycle.mp3`
  - Source: "50 CC0 Sci-Fi SFX" by rubberduck on OpenGameArt.
  - License: CC0.
  - URL: https://opengameart.org/content/50-cc0-sci-fi-sfx
- `portal-impact.mp3`
  - Source: `explosion_01.ogg` from "50 CC0 Sci-Fi SFX" by rubberduck on OpenGameArt.
  - License: CC0.
  - URL: https://opengameart.org/content/50-cc0-sci-fi-sfx

Downloaded source files were trimmed or transcoded for short site UI effects.
