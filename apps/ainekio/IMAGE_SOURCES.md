# Ainekio Image Sources

The Ainekio visual set uses public-domain or public-domain-released source images from Wikimedia Commons. Local files are cropped or resized derivatives of these sources.

- `Forbiddenplanetposter.jpg` - theatrical poster for *Forbidden Planet* (1956), featuring Robby the Robot. Wikimedia Commons marks the poster art public domain in the United States because copyright renewal was not found.
- `Robby_the_Robot.JPG` - photo of a Robby the Robot replica at the Carnegie Science Center Robot Hall of Fame. The photographer released the photo into the public domain.
- `Robot_Monster.jpg` - publicity poster for *Robot Monster* (1953). Wikimedia Commons marks it public domain in the United States because it was published without a copyright notice.
- `The_Invisible_Boy.jpg` - theatrical poster for *The Invisible Boy* (1957), featuring Robby the Robot. Wikimedia Commons marks the poster art public domain in the United States because copyright renewal was not found.
