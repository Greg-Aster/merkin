Ainekio's locomotion stack has evolved from eight open-loop servo channels playing fixed keyframe tables on V1 to a twelve-servo, closed-loop kinematic and **on-chip neural locomotion architecture** on V2. Today the system spans six integrated layers:

1. **Compact 12-DOF linkage kinematics** (`35 mm` carrier, `20 mm` crank, `28 mm` pickup, `38 mm` rod, `55 mm` distal leg) with onboard analytical inverse kinematics and 8-pass Gauss-Newton Cartesian workspace boundary projection (`0.8.150+`).
2. **Closed-loop `ESP32-P4` execution** using 4th-wire potentiometer feedback through a `CD74HC4067` multiplexer (with One-Euro velocity filtering) and `208 Hz` `LSM6DS3` `FusionAhrs` attitude feedback, achieving `2 mm` settle accuracy in `Stand` and closed-loop phase synchronization in `Walk` (`0.8.123+`).
3. **The 349-example SQLite Freestyle motion library (`489`-row `motion-targets-v1` dataset)**, pairing finite target programs, shaft-arrival verification, native-`stand` physical jam/fall recovery, and Big Brother escalation.
4. **Host neural gait streaming (`50 Hz`)**, spanning early `walk_nominal_v1` carpet trials (`2026-11-02`) and the `600M` / `800M`-step **Stanford Pupper V3 MJX host actor** validated across physical slick hardwood and carpet-seam bouts (`2026-11-09`).
5. **Full-CAD GPU Hardware-Twin Reinforcement Learning (`4,096` MuJoCo envs)**, modeling the 12-bit ADC + rail sag, `208 Hz` `LSM6DS3` + `FusionAhrs`, `PCA9685` `4.07 µs` quantization, multi-surface friction (`0.14–1.45`), and `21–40 mm` ledges (`tensor_sensors.py` + `tensor_body.py`).
6. **On-Chip `50 Hz` `ESP32-P4` MicroDuck Inference (`0.8.159-p4-microduck-v17`, `2026-11-11`)**, distilling the `547 KB` teacher policy via 2-round DAgger into a **`110,432-byte` (`107.8 KB`) `int8` student** (`720 → 128 → 64 → 32 → 12`) that runs natively on Core 1 inside the `ESP32-P4`'s **`128 KB` L2 cache** with zero Wi-Fi step jitter.

## 1. V2 linkage kinematics and workspace geometry

Unlike V1's direct two-servo legs, each V2 leg uses three actuators mounted high in the upper leg housing:
- **Shoulder roll (`h`)**: Rotates the leg plane laterally (`±h` about body `X`) to control stance width, lateral balance, and sideways crabbing.
- **Upper-carrier pitch (`alpha`)**: Directly rotates the `35 mm` upper carrier link.
- **Knee linkage crank (`theta`)**: Rotates a `20 mm` input crank that drives a `38 mm` connecting rod attached to a `28 mm` pickup radius on the `55 mm` distal lower leg (`beta`).

![Labeled front-left linkage mechanism diagram showing the coupled carrier, crank, coupler, and lower leg](/posts/gait-development/2026-09-15/mechanism.png)

### Evolution of the V2 linkage dimensions

| Iteration | Upper Carrier | Input Crank | Pickup Radius | Connecting Rod | Distal Lower Leg | Notes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| **Sep 15 FL Baseline** | `40 mm` | `21 mm` | `20 mm` | `40 mm` | `60 mm` | Initial OpenHarmony-derived planar sweep study |
| **Sep 19 Compact Probe** | `40 mm` | `22 mm` | `24 mm` | `36 mm` | `55 mm` | Single-leg `10 / 20 / 30 mm` advance study |
| **Sep 21 Variable Gait** | `40 mm` | `24 mm` | `24 mm` | `38 mm` | `55 mm` | Four-leg continuous `Stride %` and `Motion rate` solver |
| **Oct 6 – Nov 6 Production V2** | **`35 mm`** | **`20 mm`** | **`28 mm`** | **`38 mm`** | **`55 mm`** | Higher mechanical leverage (`~20%` improved lifting torque) for the `650 g` body |

### Planar forward and inverse kinematics

In the leg's sagittal plane, the lower-leg orientation `beta` is a nonlinear function of the crank input `theta` and carrier angle `alpha`:

```text
P = C + R(theta) (P0 - C)
v = P - O
d = length(v)
a = length(Q0 - O)
L = length(P0 - Q0)            # connecting rod length (38 mm)
c = (a² + d² - L²) / (2 a d)
psi = atan2(v.y, v.x) - acos(c)
beta = wrap(psi - atan2((Q0 - O).y, (Q0 - O).x))

D = O + R(alpha) (D0 - O)
F_XY = D + R(beta) (F0_XY - D0)
F_CAD = H + Rx(h) ((F_XY.x, F_XY.y, F0.z) - H)
```

Because equal positive and negative crank changes produce asymmetric changes in `beta`, the `ESP32-P4` solves the exact four-bar/five-bar closure at runtime and accounts for the rotating sole hull rather than assuming a constant gear ratio or fixed vertical foot offset.

## 2. Closed-loop `ESP32-P4` `Stand` and `Walk` execution

The `ESP32-P4` controller combines the analytic gait planner with real-time joint and inertial feedback:

- **4th-wire shaft arrival verification**: All twelve `MG90S` servos route potentiometer wiper voltages through the `CD74HC4067` multiplexer into the P4 ADC, calibrated with per-joint curves in Body Control. Rather than assuming a pose completed merely because a timer expired, the controller verifies physical shaft arrival before advancing target sequences.
- **Closed-loop `Stand` (`2 mm` settle)**: During `Stand`, local feedback and `LSM6DS3` (`208 Hz`) attitude leveling drive all four feet to their target reference and settle the body within **`2 mm`** of the geometric target.
- **Stack repair (`0.8.123`) & continuous `Walk`**: Firmware `0.8.123` resolved the FreeRTOS motion-task stack overflow that previously interrupted multi-joint feedback solving, enabling sustained closed-loop `Stand` and `Walk` execution.
- **Cartesian boundary projection (`0.8.150`)**: When an operator, Freestyle program, or neural policy requests a foot target outside the physical `35/20/28/38/55 mm` linkage envelope, firmware `0.8.150` projects the target onto the nearest valid point on the Cartesian workspace boundary so the leg tracks smoothly without linkage lockup or servo stall.

## 3. The 349-example SQLite Freestyle library and recovery

Alongside algorithmic gaits and the 23 compiled finite gestures (`Wave`, `Bow`, `Stretch`, `Worm`, `Shrug`, `Lay Down`, `Play Dead`, `Sit`, `Rest`, `Upright`, etc.), V2 features **Freestyle**: a generative motion system backed by an editable **SQLite library of 349 curated examples** in Body Control.

- **Finite target programs**: Given a natural-language prompt and current joint/IMU state, `Freestyle Qwen` (or escalated **Big Brother** inference) emits a complete, finite sequence of joint, foot, or body targets with explicit speeds, holds, and LCD facial expressions.
- **Verified shaft arrival**: Each step in a Freestyle program waits for measured potentiometer arrival (`CD74HC4067`) within tolerance before advancing to the next keyframe—proven across nine-target supported `Stretch` and lift/sway trials.
- **Event-driven jam and fall recovery**: Rather than looping blindly inside the model, Freestyle executes a program once and relies on real hardware events (`tipped`, `jammed`, `stalled`) from the `ESP32-P4` to trigger targeted recovery programs that unjam a limb or return the body to `Stand`.

## 4. Neural gait streaming (`walk_nominal_v1`) and physical carpet trials

To move beyond hand-tuned trajectories, Body Control includes a **50 Hz neural policy streamer** and demonstration/imitation training pipeline:

- **Demonstration-trained policy (`walk_nominal_v1`)**: Trained across `360,000` imitation updates across five locomotion commands (`forward`, `reverse`, `turn_left`, `turn_right`, and `stand_hold`). In five-command simulation benchmarks, `walk_nominal_v1` achieved `27.82 mm/s` forward directly and `22.45 mm/s` through the connected Body Control → `ESP32-P4` execution path with zero falls.
- **Physical carpet trials (`2026-11-02`)**: Streaming `walk_nominal_v1` at `50 Hz` to the `650 g` physical V2 robot on carpet verified end-to-end closed-loop neural command streaming, shaft-feedback capture, and attitude tracking under real surface friction and linkage compliance.
- **`v6-pending` GRU observation schema**: Captured carpet telemetry directly informed the recurrent **`v6-pending` GRU** architecture, which expands the observation vector with historical joint position errors, measured shaft velocities, `208 Hz` IMU angular rates, gravity projection, and active command phase so the policy can adapt to servo lag and carpet drag online.

## 5. Quadruped reinforcement learning & real-world neural walking (`2026-11-05` – `2026-11-06`)

To train policies that transfer cleanly from simulation to the `650 g` robot without hand-crafted demonstrations, four quadruped reinforcement-learning frameworks and a full-CAD physics pipeline were integrated in November 2026:

| RL / Simulation Milestone | Architecture & Role in Ainekio |
| --- | --- |
| **Walk in the Park (`DroQ`)** | Sample-efficient Dropout Q-Functions (`DroQ`) actor-critic pipeline adapted for direct quadruped real-world / fine-tuning data collection and low-sample policy updates |
| **Microduck RL** | Compact bipedal/quadruped locomotion reward shaping, phase-clock conditioning, and domain randomization reference adapted to small servo-driven linkages |
| **Stock Pupper V3 MuJoCo Baseline** | End-to-end validation of the JAX / Brax / MuJoCo MJX GPU training pipeline on the proven 12-DOF Stanford Pupper V3 quadruped model before swapping in Ainekio's closed-chain geometry |
| **Ainekio Full-CAD MJCF in JAX/Brax/MJX** | Direct export of the complete Blender V2 CAD assembly—including exact part masses totaling `650 g`, `35/20/28/38/55 mm` four-bar/five-bar closed-loop equality constraints, `MG90S` actuator dynamics, and sole collision meshes—into MuJoCo XML (`MJCF`) for massively parallel GPU reinforcement learning |

### Resolving MJX training failure modes: tiny hops $\rightarrow$ "scootybooty" $\rightarrow$ centered stride (`200M` steps)

Training a closed-chain four-bar quadruped over 200 million MJX steps required diagnosing and resolving two distinct locomotion failure modes before sim-to-real deployment:

1. **Rapid tiny hops (`35M`-step rollout, [watch in Gallery](/gallery/#rl-tiny-hops-35m))**: With narrow initial action scaling, the policy vibrated its feet in rapid miniature hops (`20–27 mm` fore-aft span) rather than sweeping through full four-bar strides.
2. **"Scootybooty" reverse dragging (`200M`-step Run #4, [watch in Gallery](/gallery/#rl-scootybooty-200m))**: Widening the action scale fixed forward stride length, but the policy parked its rear legs in an asymmetric split stance and dropped its rear torso to `0.044 m` during `-0.10 m/s` reverse commands—dragging/scooting its back end along the floor instead of stepping cleanly.
3. **Centered-stride & upright reverse (`200M`-step Run #5, [watch in Gallery](/gallery/#rl-centered-stride-200m))**: Penalizing off-center foot parking and shaping foot air-time credit increased `eval/episode_feet_air_time` **`3.5×`** (`157.29` $\rightarrow$ `557.11`), widened fore-aft foot swing **`2.2×–2.8×`** (`56.6–63.7 mm` centered around home `X_body`), eliminated chassis and knee collisions (`0.0`), and kept reverse walking upright at **`0.072 m`** (`rear_z_min = 0.060 m`, zero rear scoot).

## 6. On-chip `50 Hz` `ESP32-P4` MicroDuck inference, L2-cache DAgger distillation & hardwood/seam bouts (`2026-11-08` – `2026-11-11`)

Between November 8 and November 11, 2026, neural locomotion moved from host-streamed WebSocket frames directly onto the robot's microcontroller (`0.8.159-p4-microduck-v17`; full milestone notes in [Nov 11 On-Chip Neural Gait & Ainekio OS](/posts/2026-11-11-on-chip-neural-gait-and-ainekio-os/)):

![On-chip MicroDuck distillation and 50 Hz ESP32-P4 execution pipeline](/posts/2026-11-11-progress/on-chip-microduck-distillation.webp)

- **GPU Hardware Twins (`4,096` MuJoCo envs)**: `tensor_sensors.py` and `tensor_body.py` replicate the 12-bit `CD74HC4067` ADC scan (rail sag `0.96–1.02`, `1.5%` stale hold, One-Euro velocity filter), `208 Hz` `LSM6DS3` + `FusionAhrs` yaw-zeroed gravity projection, exact 4-bar coupler Jacobian + `PCA9685` `4.07 µs` PWM quantization, multi-surface floor friction (`0.14–1.45`: 45% slick wood, 35% wood/carpet seam, 20% carpet), and `21–40 mm` ledges—verified bit-for-bit against the C firmware via `native.c`.
- **2-Round DAgger Teacher-to-Student Distillation (`547 KB` → `107.8 KB`)**: Compressing the `720 → 512 → 256 → 128 → 12` teacher (`547,040 B`) via 2 rounds of Dataset Aggregation (`MAE = 0.0577`) and PPO fine-tuning produced **`microduck-v17-p4`** (`720 → 128 → 64 → 32 → 12`, **`110,432 B` / `107.8 KB`**, `-79.8%`), keeping the entire symmetric `int8` `"AMDK"` weight binary hot inside the **`ESP32-P4`'s `128 KB` L2 cache**.
- **Native Core 1 `50 Hz` Execution**: Pinned to **Core 1** (`output_task`, `20,000 µs` period) with 8-way unrolled `int8 × int16 → int32` MACs and 8-pass Gauss-Newton Cartesian boundary projection, `microduck-v17-p4` reaches **`+0.213 m/s` forward**, **`-0.179 m/s` reverse**, and **`±60.9°/s` yaw turn** with `0` falls across 8 evaluation scenarios (deleting the `831`-line legacy `gait_learning.c`).
- **Physical `600M` / `800M` Pupper V3 MJX Floor Trials (`2026-11-09`)**: In parallel, the host-streamed Stanford Pupper V3 MJX actor (`pupper_mjx_actor.py`) was trained to `600M` and `800M` steps and validated on physical `ainekio-p4-e6ebe5` across **6 slick hardwood bouts** and **7 carpet↔hardwood seam transitions** with zero falls.
