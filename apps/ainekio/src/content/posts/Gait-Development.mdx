Ainekio's locomotion stack ([`Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot`](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot)) has grown from eight open-loop servo channels playing fixed keyframe tables on V1 to a twelve-servo, closed-loop kinematic and **on-chip neural walking architecture** on V2:

1. **Compact 12-DOF four-bar kinematics** (`35 mm` carrier, `20 mm` crank, `28 mm` pickup, `38 mm` rod, `55 mm` distal leg) with analytical inverse kinematics and 8-pass Gauss-Newton Cartesian workspace boundary projection.
2. **Closed-loop `ESP32-P4` execution** reading 4th-wire potentiometer feedback through `CD74HC4067` channels `C4–C15` into `GPIO49` (`ADC2_CH0`) alongside `208 Hz` `LSM6DS3` attitude sensing, settling within `2 mm` in `Stand` and synchronizing gait phases in `Walk`.
3. **The 349-motion Freestyle library** and **489-row** training dataset (**73** curated library motions + **416** synthesized fault-recovery trajectories returning to `Stand`).
4. **Imitation and host neural gait streaming (`50 Hz`)**, spanning early carpet trials and `600M` / `800M`-step Stanford Pupper V3 MJX host policies validated across slick hardwood and carpet seams.
5. **Full-CAD GPU Hardware-Twin Reinforcement Learning (`4,096` MuJoCo environments)** modeling the exact `650 g` CAD masses, 12-bit ADC scan and rail sag, `208 Hz` IMU filter, `PCA9685` `4.07 µs` PWM quantization, multi-surface floor friction (`0.14–1.45`), and `21–40 mm` ledges.
6. **On-chip `50 Hz` MicroDuck gait on `ESP32-P4`**, distilling the `547 KB` teacher policy via 2-round DAgger into a **`110,432-byte` (`107.8 KB`) `int8` student** (`720 → 128 → 64 → 32 → 12`) that runs natively on Core 1 inside the `ESP32-P4`'s **`128 KB` L2 cache** with zero Wi-Fi step jitter.

## Linkage kinematics and workspace geometry

Unlike V1's direct two-servo planar legs, each V2 leg places three `MG90S` actuators high inside the upper servo bracket near the torso:

- **Shoulder roll (`h`)**: Rotates the leg plane laterally (`±h` about body `X`) to control stance width, lateral balance, and sideways crabbing.
- **Upper-carrier pitch (`alpha`)**: Directly rotates the `35 mm` upper carrier link.
- **Knee linkage crank (`theta`)**: Rotates a `20 mm` input crank that drives a `38 mm` connecting rod attached to a `28 mm` pickup radius on the `55 mm` distal lower leg (`beta`).

### Planar forward and inverse kinematics

In the leg's sagittal plane, the lower-leg orientation `beta` is a nonlinear function of the crank input `theta` and carrier angle `alpha`:

```text
P = C + R(theta) (P0 - C)
v = P - O
d = length(v)
a = length(Q0 - O)
L = length(P0 - Q0)            # connecting rod length (38 mm)
c = (a² + d² - L²) / (2 a d)
psi = atan2(v.y, v.x) - acos(c)
beta = wrap(psi - atan2((Q0 - O).y, (Q0 - O).x))

D = O + R(alpha) (D0 - O)
F_XY = D + R(beta) (F0_XY - D0)
F_CAD = H + Rx(h) ((F_XY.x, F_XY.y, F0.z) - H)
```

Because equal positive and negative crank changes produce asymmetric changes in `beta`, the `ESP32-P4` solves the exact four-bar closure at runtime and accounts for the curved 3D-printed rocking sole rather than assuming a constant gear ratio or point foot.

## Closed-loop Stand and Walk execution

The `ESP32-P4` controller pairs the analytic gait planner with real-time joint and inertial feedback:

- **4th-wire shaft arrival verification**: All twelve `MG90S` servos route potentiometer wiper voltages through `CD74HC4067` channels `C4–C15` into `GPIO49` (`ADC2_CH0`), smoothed by a One-Euro velocity filter and calibrated with per-joint curves in Body Control. Rather than assuming a pose completed because a timer expired, the controller verifies physical shaft arrival before advancing target sequences.
- **Closed-loop `Stand` (`2 mm` settle) & `Walk`**: During `Stand`, local joint feedback and `LSM6DS3` (`208 Hz`) attitude leveling drive all four feet to their target reference and settle the torso within **`2 mm`** of the geometric target, while continuous `Walk` synchronizes phase transitions against measured leg tracking.
- **Cartesian boundary projection**: When an operator, Freestyle program, or neural policy requests a foot target outside the physical `35/20/28/38/55 mm` linkage envelope, onboard 8-pass Gauss-Newton projection maps the target onto the nearest reachable boundary point so the leg tracks smoothly without linkage lockup or servo stall.

## Freestyle motion library and fault recovery

Alongside algorithmic gaits and 23 compiled finite gestures (`Wave`, `Bow`, `Stretch`, `Worm`, `Shrug`, `Lay Down`, `Play Dead`, `Sit`, `Rest`, `Upright`), V2 runs **Freestyle**: a generative motion system backed by an editable **349-motion library** in Body Control.

- **489-row posture & fault-recovery dataset**: The local `Qwen3.5-0.8B` LoRA specialist was trained on a **489-row** dataset combining **73** curated motions from the 349-motion library with **416** synthesized hardware fault-recovery trajectories that safely unload snagged feet and settle back into a stable `Stand`.
- **Verified shaft arrival**: Given a plain-English request and live joint/IMU telemetry, Freestyle synthesizes a finite spatial IK program (`body`, `feet`, `pose`, `move`, `oscillate`, `gait`) paired with **59** `ST7789` LCD expressions, waiting at each phase boundary for measured `CD74HC4067` potentiometer arrival within tolerance.
- **Event-driven jam & fall recovery**: If a foot catches on carpet pile mid-gesture, the `ESP32-P4` raises a non-terminal `servo_stall` event while keeping holding torque alive so the gateway can splice in a live backoff-and-`Stand` recovery trajectory.

## Imitation walking and physical carpet trials

Before training pure reinforcement-learning policies from scratch, Body Control validated `50 Hz` closed-loop neural command streaming through imitation learning:

- **Demonstration-trained policy**: Trained across `360,000` imitation updates spanning five locomotion commands (`forward`, `reverse`, `turn_left`, `turn_right`, and `stand_hold`), achieving `27.82 mm/s` forward in simulation and `22.45 mm/s` through the full gateway-to-`ESP32-P4` control loop with zero falls.
- **Physical carpet trials (`2026-11-02`)**: Streaming `50 Hz` targets to the `650 g` V2 robot on living-room carpet verified closed-loop 4th-wire shaft-feedback capture and `208 Hz` attitude tracking under real surface friction and four-bar linkage compliance.
- **Proprioceptive history design**: Real carpet logs showed where single-step observations lagged behind `MG90S` gearing backlash and pile drag, motivating multi-step proprioceptive history windows (`15 × 48 = 720` features) in subsequent RL trainers.

## Reinforcement learning and real-world neural walking

To train policies that transfer directly from simulation to the `650 g` physical robot without hand-authored trajectories, a full-CAD MuJoCo physics export and four quadruped RL references were brought onto the bench in November 2026:

| RL / Simulation Milestone | Role in Ainekio Locomotion |
| --- | --- |
| **Walk in the Park (`DroQ`)** | Sample-efficient Dropout Q-Functions actor-critic reference adapted for low-sample fine-tuning |
| **Microduck RL** | Phase-clock conditioning, reward shaping, and domain randomization adapted from `OpenDuck` to closed-chain hobby linkages |
| **Stanford Pupper V3 MuJoCo Baseline** | End-to-end verification of the JAX / Brax / MuJoCo MJX GPU training pipeline on a proven 12-DOF quadruped before swapping in Ainekio's geometry |
| **Ainekio Full-CAD MJCF** | Direct export of the 375-mesh Blender V2 assembly—exact `650 g` part masses, `35/20/28/38/55 mm` four-bar equality constraints, `MG90S` speed-torque ceiling, and curved 3D-printed rocking soles—into MuJoCo XML (`MJCF`) using Conjugate Gradient (`500 Hz` physics / `50 Hz` control) |

### Fixing MJX failure modes: tiny hops $\rightarrow$ "scootybooty" $\rightarrow$ centered stride (`200M` steps)

Training a closed-chain four-bar quadruped over 200 million MJX steps exposed two distinct sim-to-real failure modes before clean walking emerged:

1. **Rapid tiny hops (`35M`-step rollout, [watch in Gallery](/gallery/#rl-tiny-hops-35m))**: With narrow initial action scaling, the policy vibrated its feet in miniature hops (`20–27 mm` fore-aft span) instead of sweeping through full four-bar strides.
2. **"Scootybooty" reverse dragging (`200M`-step Run #4, [watch in Gallery](/gallery/#rl-scootybooty-200m))**: Widening the action scale fixed forward stride length, but the policy parked its rear legs in an asymmetric split stance and dropped its rear torso to `0.044 m` during `-0.10 m/s` reverse commands—scooting its back end across the floor instead of stepping.
3. **Centered stride & upright reverse (`200M`-step Run #5, [watch in Gallery](/gallery/#rl-centered-stride-200m))**: Penalizing off-center foot parking and shaping foot air-time credit increased `feet_air_time` **`3.5×`** (`157.29` $\rightarrow$ `557.11`), widened fore-aft foot swing **`2.2×–2.8×`** (`56.6–63.7 mm` centered around home `X_body`), eliminated chassis and knee collisions (`0.0`), and kept reverse walking upright at **`0.072 m`** (`rear_z_min = 0.060 m`, zero rear scoot).

## On-chip 50 Hz MicroDuck gait on ESP32-P4

Between November 8 and November 11, 2026, neural walking moved off the Wi-Fi link and directly onto the robot's microcontroller (milestone details in [Nov 11 On-Chip Neural Gait & Ainekio OS](/posts/2026-11-11-on-chip-neural-gait-and-ainekio-os/)):

![On-chip MicroDuck distillation and 50 Hz ESP32-P4 execution pipeline](/posts/2026-11-11-progress/on-chip-microduck-distillation.webp)

- **GPU Hardware Twins (`4,096` MuJoCo environments)**: Parallel GPU environments model the 12-bit `CD74HC4067` (`C4–C15` → `GPIO49`) ADC scan (rail sag `0.96–1.02`, `1.5%` stale hold, One-Euro velocity filter), `208 Hz` `LSM6DS3` yaw-zeroed gravity projection, exact four-bar coupler Jacobian + `PCA9685` `4.07 µs` PWM quantization, multi-surface floor friction (`0.14–1.45`: 45% slick wood, 35% wood/carpet seam, 20% carpet), and `21–40 mm` ledges—verified bit-for-bit against the C firmware.
- **2-Round DAgger Teacher-to-Student Distillation (`547 KB` → `107.8 KB`)**: Compressing the `720 → 512 → 256 → 128 → 12` teacher (`547,040 B`) through two rounds of Dataset Aggregation (`MAE = 0.0577`) and PPO fine-tuning produced an `int8` student (`720 → 128 → 64 → 32 → 12`, **`110,432 B` / `107.8 KB`**, `-79.8%`) that fits hot inside the **`ESP32-P4`'s `128 KB` L2 cache**.
- **Native Core 1 `50 Hz` Execution**: Pinned to **Core 1** (`20,000 µs` period) with 8-way unrolled `int8 × int16 → int32` multiply-accumulates and 8-pass Gauss-Newton Cartesian boundary projection, the on-chip policy reaches **`+0.213 m/s` forward**, **`-0.179 m/s` reverse**, and **`±60.9°/s` yaw turn** with `0` falls across 8 evaluation scenarios.
- **Physical `600M` / `800M` Pupper V3 MJX Floor Trials (`2026-11-09`)**: Alongside the on-chip MicroDuck policy, the host-streamed Pupper V3 MJX actor was trained to `600M` and `800M` steps and validated on the physical V2 robot across **6 slick hardwood bouts** and **7 carpet-to-hardwood seam transitions** with zero falls.
