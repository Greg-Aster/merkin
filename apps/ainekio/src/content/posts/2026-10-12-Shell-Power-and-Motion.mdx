# Printing the exterior

I have begun printing the exterior shell, made further weight modifications,
and switched to a **4 A power supply**. Alongside the physical build, I have
started integrating YOLOE and done extensive work on MetaHuman's node workflows
and trained Qwen 0.8B intent and task models.

The robot could [stand, walk and respond to commands on October 8](/posts/2026-10-08-standing-walking/),
without the carapace. Shell fit is next; Active Operator remains untested.

## Lift each foot before lowering the body

The local motion authoring now gives **Lay Down** and **Play Dead** individual
lift-and-place steps before lowering the body. The generator specifies an
**8 mm lift**, **0.8 seconds per initial foot placement**, then **2 seconds of
body lowering**. Both sampled clips retain a seven-second authored duration and
the original start and terminal references. Runtime speed settings can change
playback duration.

The sampled files show a useful reduction in peak joint speed:

| Offline quantity | Lay Down: previous → revised | Play Dead: previous → revised |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Authored duration | 7.0 → 7.0 s | 7.0 → 7.0 s |
| Peak joint speed | 216.36 → 137.59°/s | 308.62 → 156.16°/s |
| Lowest modeled sole height | −0.067 → approximately 0 mm | −0.240 → approximately 0 mm |
| Minimum modeled body clearance | 0.15 → 0.15 mm | 0.15 → 0.15 mm |
| Hardware qualified / collision checked | No / no | No / no |

The P4 source retraces the grounded clip in reverse before entering a new named
motion, with a short alignment segment. Hardware testing of this recovery is
still pending.
