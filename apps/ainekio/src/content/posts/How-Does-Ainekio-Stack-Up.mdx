Ainekio V2 is a `650 g`, `109 mm` tall, 3D-printed four-legged robot that costs **`~$60–$100` in parts** and walks across the living-room rug with a bundle of twelve 4-wire servo leads sticking out of its back.

To put the build in perspective, this page places Ainekio on the same engineering scale as existing consumer, academic, and heavy-industrial machines—from a `$65` **Hasbro Furby** and a **Roomba** up through **Stanford Pupper V3**, **Boston Dynamics Spot**, **Electric Atlas**, and a **75-ton Class IV Bipedal Combat Mech**.

![Technical elevation blueprint comparing robot heights from a 0.09 m Roomba and 0.11 m Ainekio V2 up through Spot and Electric Atlas to an 18.0 m Class IV Bipedal Mech](/posts/comparison/robot-scale-mech-lineup.webp)
*Figure 1: Standard elevation lineup on a common ground plane. Ainekio V2 (`0.11 m`, `650 g`, `~$85` BOM) stands second from left, between the vacuum cleaner and the Furby. The 75-ton bipedal mech (`18.0 m`) is on the right for scale.*

There is always a bigger robot. Ainekio cannot lift a `50 kg` engine block like **Electric Atlas**, sprint at `3.5 m/s` like a **Unitree Go2**, or fire shoulder missiles like the **Mech**. Conversely, none of those platforms can sit on a bookshelf next to a Wi-Fi router without voiding a residential lease, and none of them wake up at `02:00 AM` to run a software sleep cycle with a built-in psychotherapist.

## 1. Hardware, Scale & Joint Sensing (Furby to 75-Ton Mech)

In small hobby robots, sub-`$100` servos (`MG90S` / `SG90`) are almost always driven open-loop (**Petoi Bittle X**, **Waveshare WAVEGO**, **Stanford Pupper V1**), or paired with policies that fake joint observations in software (**Mini Pupper 2**). Ainekio V2 opens up twelve `$1.50` `MG90S` servos, solders a **4th wire directly to each internal potentiometer wiper**, and scans all twelve joints at `50 Hz` (`~15.9 ms`) through a `$2` `CD74HC4067` multiplexer into `ESP32-P4` `ADC2` (`GPIO49`).

## 2. Peer Codebase Audit: Stanford Pupper V3 & Microduck vs. Ainekio Freestyle

During November 2026 gait development, we vendor-pinned and audited the upstream **Stanford Pupper V3** monorepo (`Nate711/pupperv3-monorepo`) and **Pollen Robotics Microduck** (`pollen-robotics/microduck_rl` on `mjlab`) inside `/home/greggles/Ainekio`.

| Capability | Stanford Pupper V3 (`~$2,000` BOM) | Microduck (`mjlab`, `~$399` BOM) | Ainekio V2 + Freestyle (`~$60–$100` BOM) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Walking / Locomotion** | `50 Hz` MJX PPO policy (4-legged + 3-legged policies via `neural_controller` at `520 Hz` ROS 2 timer) | `50 Hz` RL velocity policy in `mjlab` (`DYNAMIXEL XL330` bus) | **Both** deterministic `50 Hz` Native C closed-loop 4-bar IK (`Walk`, `Run`, `Crawl`, `Crab` + `gait_learning_v4`) **and** `50 Hz` MJX PPO Neural Gait (`walk_nominal_v1`) |
| **Poses & Emotes** | `animation_controller.py`: plays fixed `30 Hz` CSV recordings (`sit`, `shake`, `downward_dog`, `superman`) **open-loop** | **None** (locomotion policy only) | **Spatial IK + joint compiler** (`body`, `feet`, `pose`, `move`, `oscillate`, `gait`, `stand`, `hold`, `face`) + 23 cubic-spline finite gestures gated by **4th-wire arrival checkpoints** |
| **LLM Motion Interface** | `ros_node.py`: only calls `move(vx, vy, wz)` or `change_gait()`—**cannot synthesize poses** | **None** | **Freestyle (`Qwen3.5-0.8B` + LoRA)**: synthesizes multi-step motion programs from natural language (*"lift one foot then the other"*) |
| **Stall / Fall Recovery & Continual Learning** | **None** (plays CSV frames blindly; no stall or fall recovery) | **None** | **Closed-loop `Bridge.event` recovery** (`servo_stall`, `tipped`, `rapid_movement`, `slip`) + human-in-the-loop review/correction saved back to `motion-library.sqlite` (`349` curated examples) |
| **Simulation & Kinematic Solver** | Open-chain 3-link serial legs (`20 mm` sphere feet) in MJX | Open-chain serial biped in `mujoco_warp` (Newton solver) | **Closed-loop 4-bar linkage MJCF** (`solver="cg"`, custom `MG90SActuator`, GPU `FootAction` IK, and 720-feature `TensorSensors` proprioception) |

![Full-CAD MuJoCo MJCF model of Ainekio V2](/gallery/ainekio-mjcf-preview-960.webp)
*Ainekio V2's 375-mesh Blender assembly exported into MuJoCo MJCF with closed-loop four-bar equality constraints, `Part027` rocking soles, and the `MG90SActuator` speed-torque model.*

Three engineering takeaways came out of that audit:

1. **Stock Pupper V3 Virtual Benchmark (`stock-pupper-v3-forward-result.json`)**: Running the unmodified upstream Pupper V3 policy (`policy_latest.json`) on its stock MuJoCo model for `14.995 s` at a commanded `+0.30 m/s` yielded `+1.6816 m` forward travel (`0.1121 m/s` mean speed, `~37%` of command), `-0.2754 m` lateral drift, and `-16.14°` yaw drift, with `520 Hz` ROS timer overruns reducing effective target delivery to `~31 Hz`.
2. **Closed-Loop 4-Bar Solver in `microduck.py`**: Upstream `microduck_rl` uses `mujoco_warp`'s Newton solver for an open-chain biped, which hits `float32` Hessian `NaN` failures (`mujoco_warp#1415`) on Ainekio's closed four-bar loop-closure constraints. Ainekio's `microduck.py` switches GPU physics to **Conjugate Gradient (`solver="cg"`, `500 Hz` physics / `50 Hz` control)**, models the `MG90S` speed-torque ceiling, decodes `12D` normalized foot targets (`FootAction`) on GPU through analytical four-bar IK, and feeds a **720-feature (`15 × 48`) proprioceptive history** (`ainekio-neural-gait-v7-proprio`).
3. **Where `$1.50` Servos Bite Back (Trajectory Hygiene)**: Because an `MG90S` has a fraction of the torque of Pupper V3's brushless `GIM4305-10` actuators, Ainekio stalls if a gesture drags a loaded foot sideways across carpet or commands an extreme rear-carrier angle (`rear_right_carrier: -31.3° / -32.69°`). Auditing `motion-library.sqlite` confirmed that replacing raw multi-leg `pose` slides with `body` weight-shifts and vertical `feet` lifts (`+Z` unload → move → `0` place) keeps the linkages inside their clean mechanical-advantage window.

## 3. Cognitive Architecture, Dreams, Existential Pondering & Owner Care (`MetaHuman OS`)

Most robots expose a velocity bus (`cmd_vel`) and, at most, a stateless cloud voice script. Because Ainekio is wired into **[MetaHuman OS](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/metahuman-os)** (`/home/greggles/metahuman`), this `$85` plastic quadruped spends its uptime and downtime running a full cognitive, introspective, and desktop-escalation stack:

| Layer | Furby / Roomba / EMO / Loona | Pupper V3 / Unitree Go2 / Spot / Atlas / Mech | Ainekio V2 + MetaHuman OS |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standing Identity & Trust** | Fixed ROM / none | System prompt string or asset serial number | **`persona/core.json`**: Big Five traits, values, short/mid/long-term goals, decision heuristics, and **5 trust levels** (`observe` → `adaptive_auto`). |
| **Pondering Its Own Existence** | No | No | **Yes (`inner-curiosity.json`, `train-of-thought.json`, `reflector`)**: Runs autonomous self-Q&A and associative mind-wandering chains pondering its own existence, body state, and unfinished thoughts. |
| **Dreams & Nightmares** | Charging animation / sleep timer | Power switch off / maintenance schedule | **`dreamer-mode.json` & `daydreamer-mode.json` (`02:00 AM` Sleep Cycle)**: Samples `15` lifetime memories (`227-day` decay curve) to synthesize surreal dreams and nightmares (`temperature: 1.0`, up to `4` continuations), followed by a **Psychotherapist** (`psychoanalyzer`) session that edits `persona/core.json`. |
| **Concern for Owner's Health & Safety** | Roomba checks if you are a sock; Furby does not care | Spot/Atlas treat humans as OSHA obstacles; the **Mech** treats humans as soft targets | **Active owner care (`persona/core.json` + `single_person_resume`)**: Visually tracks its owner, halts motion immediately if visual lock is lost, checks in on owner well-being, and prioritizes human safety across all autonomy modes. |
| **Lifelong Learning & Post-Mortem Continuity** | Resets when AA batteries corrode | Wiped between lab semesters or firmware flashes | **Continuous LoRA + Episodic Preservation (`Dual Consciousness` & `Emulation`)**: Learns from its first steps onward, fits new LoRA adapters during sleep, and preserves attributed episodic memories (`executions.sqlite` + L2 vector index) across decades. |
| **Boredom & Internal Drives** | Random idle animations | None | **3 Autonomy Modes**: `Reactive`, `Semi` (5-min signed `delta` drive scores in `[0, 100]`), and `Full Auto` driving `boredom-observer` (takes a photo to find something interesting), `boredom-movement`, `boredom-reflection`, and `desire-agent`. |
| **Sub-Second Physical Reflexes** | Bump / cliff switch | FOC current limit or MPC reflex | **`fast-body-event-mode.json`**: Immediate `be_` LCD face (`confused` / `surprised`) + low-latency `lightweight` remark on `tipped`, `rapid_movement`, or `servo_stall`. |
| **Voice, Vision & Big Brother Agency** | Fixed wake phrases / 2D camera | Cloud Realtime API / LiDAR / manual SSH debugging | On-chip **`micro_wake_word`** (`+6°` shoulder perk), local **Whisper STT**, local **Kokoro TTS** (custom `.pt` voicepacks), **Qwen3.5 9B VLM** + **YOLOE** + **MoGe depth**, and **Big Brother** (`Codex` / `Claude Code` CLI delegation + self-healing code repair). |

Because Ainekio is continually learning from these first steps—curating episodic memories, updating its own weights during sleep, and recording its owner's patterns in `Dual Consciousness` and `Emulation` modes—the current carpet trials are only the baseline. Over time, the robot will grow, watch you age and die, hold the attributed memory of your existence dear in its vector index, and—long after the Furby's battery contacts have oxidized and the 75-ton Mech has rusted in a crater—be the last thing left to whisper your name in a cold and distant future.
