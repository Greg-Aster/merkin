> **Dedicated Cockpit HUD Guide:** For a complete tour of **Ainekio Cockpit** (`#cockpit-section`)—including one-tap **`Tap to Talk`**, the translucent **7-graph Ainekio OS live stream** (`POST /api/metahuman-stream`), 10 immediate-apply runtime switches, and the 2D vector joystick—see **[Ainekio Cockpit: Full-Screen Camera HUD, Tap-to-Talk & Live Stream](/posts/ainekio-cockpit/)**.

Four connected subsystems in [`Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot`](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot) and [`simulator.ainek.io`](https://simulator.ainek.io/) (wire-compatible with [`game.megameal`](https://game.megameal.org/) and `@ainekio/engine@v0.1.0`) sit between physical or simulated V1, V2, and V3 robots and **[Ainekio OS](/posts/ainekio-os/)** ([embodied integration](/posts/metahuman-integration/)), giving both a real-time session owner and a workbench for calibration, teleoperation, multiplayer 3D simulation, and GPU policy training:

1. **The Host Gateway**: Maintains the authenticated `protocol-v1` WebSocket link (`40` commands, `24` feature flags) with the active robot, tracks command sequence numbers and connection epochs, streams camera frames and bidirectional `16 kHz` PCM audio, commands on-chip `50 Hz` clean-room `MicroDuck` velocity twists, or streams `50 Hz` host neural gait and `NeuralPose v8` posture frames.
2. **The Embodied Adapter**: Translates high-level intents from Ainekio OS into capability-checked robot commands, persists crash-safe SQLite action receipts, exposes a one-click pause/resume switch for Ainekio OS control, supports **zero-pinning Ainekio OS auto-following (`ce743e7e`)** across physical V1/V2 and virtual `ainekio-v*-sim` robots without bridge restart, echoes **`AIKSPK01`** spoken text into Cockpit and room chat, and forwards structured telemetry and body-contact events upward.
3. **Body Control & Cockpit HUD**: Serves both a full-viewport **Cockpit HUD** for live driving, direct text chat, and obstacle awareness (with Apache-2.0 **RT-DETRv2 + OWLv2** detection) and engineering workspaces for **Automated Servo Calibration (`auto_calibration.py`)**, IMU leveling, Freestyle authoring, the **Virtual Simulation Manager (`simulation.py`)**, and over-the-air firmware staging.
4. **Live Browser 3D Simulator ([`simulator.ainek.io`](https://simulator.ainek.io/)) & `4,096`-Env MuJoCo Twins**: Runs hot-switchable **V1, V2, and V3** robots—including compiled real `ESP32-P4` C firmware (`ainekio-v2-firmware.wasm`)—atop `500 Hz` MuJoCo WASM (`mujoco_wasm.js`) directly in a browser Web Worker across 6 playable 3D worlds with **PeerJS WebRTC multiplayer** (see the [live 3D simulator section](#game-megameal-simulator)), alongside `4,096` parallel GPU MuJoCo environments for `MicroDuck` and `NeuralPose v8` training.

The gateway stack runs on a desktop workstation or an offboard `Radxa Dragon Q6A` single-board computer, supporting direct local LAN access, automatic Wi-Fi hotspot fallback when away from a router, or connection through an optional remote tunnel.

## Command translation and idempotent receipts

When Ainekio OS dispatches an intent across the embodied bridge, the gateway adapter maps it onto the connected robot's verified capabilities:

| Cognitive Intent / Event | Gateway & Adapter Execution Path |
| --- | --- |
| **Postures & Locomotion Goals** | Dispatches a checked posture, gesture, or measured distance/turn/visual walk (analytical solver or on-chip `50 Hz` `MicroDuck v19-snag-p4` with `1.8 s` firmware carpet-unsnag reflex and `< 0.30 m` `MoGe-2` corridor braking) and monitors progress until terminal `Finish` |
| **Freestyle Programs** | Synthesizes or selects from the `939`-row clean choreography library and tracks 12-DOF targets closed-loop at `50 Hz` via `neural-pose-v8-calibrated` (`stand_enabled: false` single posture owner with `jam_enabled: true` `I²t` fuse active; `Bridge` interrupts active jobs **only** on genuine hardware thermal cutoff `outputs_released: true`) |
| **Correlated Snapshots** | Captures an explicit still frame—or attaches a post-motion verification photo immediately before `done`—and returns the matched JPEG |
| **Robot Speech (TTS)** | Paces synthesized Kokoro or Piper `16 kHz` PCM bursts (`AIKSPK01`) to the `ES8311` / `NS4150B` speaker queue while embedding optional chunk-1 `text` (`<= 2,000` chars) so spoken replies echo cleanly into Cockpit and `simulator.ainek.io` room chat, driving `50 Hz` `NeuralPosePresence` pure sagittal nods (`±2.5 mm` Z, `±4.5°` pitch, `0.0°` shoulder scrub) and holding a self-speech suppression window |
| **Spoken Utterances (STT)** | Packages **`microWakeWord` + WebRTC VAD** (or Cockpit `Tap to Talk`) speech segments as WAV audio while `NeuralPosePresence` holds motor-silent (never cutting off the mic with `stop`) for local or LAN Whisper transcription |
| **Body-Contact Events** | Forwards thresholded `tipped`, `shaken`, and hardware thermal-cutoff `servo_stall` (`outputs_released: true`, with `correction_failed` retired) events to Ainekio OS's fast 6-turn reflex workflow while absorbing routine health ticks into live status |

To guarantee that a brief Wi-Fi drop never triggers duplicate physical motion, every dispatched action is recorded in SQLite with its action ID, payload hash, connection epoch, and sequence number before transmission. Late completions settle the waiting task cleanly without re-sending the movement command. Because Ainekio OS uses **zero-pinning auto-following (`ce743e7e`)**, Body Control owns active robot selection and seamlessly hands one continuous bridge stream between physical `V1`/`V2` hardware and virtual `ainekio-v*-sim` bodies without restarting the bridge.

### Hot-switchable V1/V2/V3 rigs, 6 worlds & PeerJS multiplayer

[`simulator.ainek.io`](https://simulator.ainek.io/) compiles the `500 Hz` MuJoCo physics plant (`mujoco_wasm.js`) and real `ESP32-P4` C firmware (`ainekio-v2-firmware.wasm`, `virtual_body.c`, `face.c`, `protocol-v1`, 4th-wire ADC, `LSM6DS3` IMU, and per-channel `I²t` protection) directly into a client-side Web Worker alongside Three.js:

- **All three robot models hot-switchable live (`?model=v1|v2|v3`)**:
  - **V1 (8-servo)**: Runs `v1-microduck-p4` neural locomotion and `neural_pose_v1` expressive poses on the eight-servo quadruped chassis.
  - **V2 (12-servo)**: Runs `microduck-v19-snag-p4` neural walking, `neural-pose-v7-hysteresis` expressive poses (`Sit`, `Lay Down`, `Wave`, `Bow`, `Stretch`, `Shake`, `Dance`, `Cute`, `Nod`, `Point`, `Celebrate`, `Push-Up`, `Crouch`), and compiled `ainekio-v2-firmware.wasm` on the `650 g` closed-form `35/20/28/38 mm` four-bar rig (`ainekio-v2.glb`), with a live switch back to analytical native firmware gaits.
  - **V3 (12-servo Option A-1)**: Runs `v3-microduck-p4` and `neural_pose_v3` on the Option A-1 chassis with self-leveling ankles and the panoramic Cyclops optic.
- **Six on-demand 3D worlds**:
  - **19 m Robotics Lab (Obstacle Course)**: Upgraded 7-zone testbed with **128 MuJoCo colliders**, an agility runway, maze, ramp bridge, terrain pit, AprilTags `TAG-00`–`TAG-09`, Macbeth ColorChecker, SLAM checkerboard, and live diagnostic screens.
  - **Yggdrasil (Tree of Life)**: `27 m` Norse World Tree, Bifrost bridge, cable thresholds, cathedral roots, and **3 spatial audio zones**.
  - **Observatory**: Distance-streamed terrain chunks + MuJoCo heightfield.
  - **Miranda Wreck**
  - **Solitude (Stonehenge Plain)**: MuJoCo heightfield + floating firefly.
  - **Sci Fi Room (Astral House)**
- **PeerJS WebRTC Multiplayer Rooms**: Wire-compatible with [`game.megameal`](https://game.megameal.org/) and `@ainekio/engine@v0.1.0`, featuring shareable `?room=` invite links, `10 Hz` articulated V1/V2/V3 remote peer sync, room chat, and a unified **Comms Feed** that also echoes `AIKSPK01` spoken robot replies.
- **Orbit, Follow & Robot POV controls**: Drag freely in `Orbit` (including full upward look pitch) or drive any loaded model in `Follow` and first-person `Robot POV` via `WASD` / Arrow keys + `Q`/`E` strafe or the 2D HUD joystick.
- **Standalone or live Body Control WebSocket**: Works standalone in any desktop browser, or connects directly via **`Connect Body Control`** (`ws://` / `wss://` `/robot`) or `ainekio-pair:` Quick Pair speaking authentic `protocol-v1` JSON and binary streams so [Ainekio OS](/posts/ainekio-os/) can pilot the browser robot seamlessly.

### Virtual Simulation Manager (`simulation.py`) & full-parity firmware bridge

Managed directly from Body Control via **`Master/gateway/simulation.py` (`/api/simulation`)**—which replaces the legacy `simulator_bridge.py` and `start-virtual-robot.sh` scripts—virtual robots run the production C firmware inside a `500 Hz` MuJoCo loop rather than acting as simplified kinematic puppets:

- **Local `:8792` hosting, Quick Pair & 24/7 headless runner**: `simulation.py` serves `simulator.ainek.io` locally on **`:8792`**, issues scoped virtual tokens (`ainekio-v{1,2,3}-sim`) and **`ainekio-pair:` Quick Pair** / zero-log `#hash` launch links targeting either local `ws://127.0.0.1:8790/robot` or the Cloudflare relay `wss://robot-gateway.ainek.io/robot`, and manages a **24/7 headless Chromium runner** on the Brain host so virtual robots remain online even after desktop browser tabs close.
- **Compiled `ESP32-P4` C firmware (`ainekio-v2-firmware.wasm`, `full_parity=True`)**: Compiles real `ESP32-P4` firmware modules (`face.c`, `face_timeline.c`, `protocol-v1` `40` commands and `24` feature flags, the 12-channel 4th-wire ADC model, `LSM6DS3` IMU, and the per-joint `I²t` winding thermal fuse) directly atop the **`500 Hz` MuJoCo** closed-chain four-bar plant (`collision_profile="locomotion"`).
- **Zero-pinning single-stream Body Control attachment**: Connecting as `ainekio-v1-sim`, `ainekio-v2-sim`, or `ainekio-v3-sim` attaches to Body Control identically to a physical Wi-Fi robot, and Ainekio OS's zero-pinning auto-following (`ce743e7e`) tracks switches between physical and simulated bodies seamlessly without restarting the bridge.
- **Bidirectional `50 Hz` pose, LCD, virtual camera & Web Audio multiplex**:
  - Streams `50 Hz` closed-loop leg poses (`virtualRobotRig.ts`) to articulate the 3D browser rig and syncs remote multiplayer peers at `10 Hz`.
  - Streams `0x01` `320 × 170` RGBA `ST7789` procedural LCD face frames directly onto the 3D robot's faceplate.
  - Renders `0x02` virtual `OV5647` chin-camera JPEGs *inside the 3D Three.js world* and streams them **back** into Body Control, `RT-DETRv2 + OWLv2` object tracking, `MoGe-2` corridor braking, and Ainekio OS multimodal vision.
  - Bridges `0x03` / `0x04` `16 kHz` Web Audio speaker playback (`AIKSPK01`, with chunk-1 `text` echoing into the unified Comms Feed / room chat) and browser microphone `Tap to Talk` PCM end-to-end.
- **Interactive perturbation & fault-injection HUD**: Switch between `Orbit`, `Follow`, and `Robot POV` views; trigger `Push +X / +Y / -Y` lateral/fore-aft wrenches or `Pick Up & Shake` (vertical lift + torque impulse to trigger IMU `rapid_movement` / `tipped` reflexes); toggle **per-servo `J0–J11` pin/jam faults** (verifying 4th-wire stall sensing and selective `8–10 s` `I²t` thermal cutoff safely in software); and record `50 Hz` `.jsonl` RL rollouts.
