Since the [first printed leg prototypes](/posts/2026-09-15-leg-prototypes/), CAD and
fit passes between September 17 and 21 simplified all three mechanical layers of
Ainekio V2: a direct four-bar leg shortened to **22 / 36 / 55 mm**, a central frame
narrowed from **103 mm to 51.2 mm**, and an outer carapace shaped around the moving
links.

![Four leg parts laid out on their printing faces: knee crank, upper carrier, connecting rod, and lower leg.](/posts/2026-09-21-design-progress/flat-parts-september-18.webp)

*September 18 print-bed layout showing the knee crank, upper carrier, curved connecting rod, and lower leg.*

## Direct crank-to-lower-leg linkage

The earlier prototype routed knee drive through an intermediate coupler and relay
rocker. Dropping that extra relay lets the **knee crank** drive the **lower leg**
directly through a single curved **connecting rod**, while the second planar servo
drives the **upper carrier** and the hip-roll servo swings the whole leg plane.

On September 18 I leveled both planar servo shafts at the same height (**12.5 mm apart
horizontally**) with a **24 mm crank, 24 mm lower-leg pickup, 41 mm connecting rod,
40 mm upper carrier, and 60 mm lower leg**. Short M3×6 screws (threading 2.7 mm past the
bearing and washer stack) replaced three long through-bolts and nut traps, paired with
two **12.4 × 4.2 × 5.25 mm** servo mounting shims.

## Comparing 24/41/60 mm and compact 22/36/55 mm geometry

On September 19 I tested a tighter **22 / 36 / 55 mm** leg (crank / connecting rod /
knee-to-foot projection) against the 24 / 41 / 60 mm baseline, keeping the upper carrier
at 40 mm and the lower-leg pickup radius at 24 mm:

| Dimension | Earlier leg | Compact revision |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Knee crank (joint centers) | 24 mm | 22 mm |
| Connecting rod (joint centers) | 41 mm | 36 mm |
| Upper carrier | 40 mm | 40 mm |
| Lower-leg pickup radius | 24 mm | 24 mm |
| Lower-leg knee-to-foot projection | 60 mm | 55 mm |

Matching nine reference poses across 520 sampled actuator pairs confirmed that the
shorter linkage preserves the full angular sweep while giving up very little foot
reach:

| Matched exercise | Earlier leg | Compact revision |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Upper-link angular sweep | 90.00° | 90.00° |
| Lower-link orientation change | 54.00° | 54.00° |
| Required crank-input sweep | 98.12° | 100.81° |
| Foot-bolt horizontal extent | 67.268 mm | 66.248 mm (−1.5%) |
| Foot-bolt vertical extent | 45.383 mm | 41.200 mm (−9.2%) |

See the [gait development guide](/posts/gait-development/#linkage-kinematics-and-workspace-geometry)
for the kinematics and workspace analysis behind this four-bar geometry.

## Fixing the upper-carrier horn cavity

When test-fitting the printed upper carrier onto a servo horn, the round hub seated
cleanly, but earlier profile edits had pinched the narrow arm slot so the horn arm
couldn't drop in. Enlarging **only the arm slot by 5% in-plane**—while leaving the
4.05 mm hub seat, pocket floor, and screw holes untouched—fixed the press fit.

![CAD render of the upper carrier showing the widened horn-arm cavity and retained cylindrical hub seat.](/posts/2026-09-21-design-progress/horn-cavity.webp)

*Upper carrier with +5% in-plane horn-arm clearance and unchanged hub seat.*

## Narrowing the chassis from 103 mm to 51.2 mm

While the [September 10 body layout](/posts/2026-09-10-v2-body-design/) explored a
wide bay for three boards, the September 17 revision narrowed the chassis from
**103 mm to 51.2 mm**—matching the nominal PCB width of the 1.9-inch display—by
translating each side assembly inward **25.9 mm**.

![Side view of the September 20 CAD assembly, with exposed red leg links, black servo housings and a pale outer shell.](/posts/2026-09-21-design-progress/assembly-side-september-20.webp)

*September 20 CAD assembly side view with red leg linkages, black servo housings, and carapace shell.*

| Feature | Earlier wide layout | September 17 narrow revision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chassis outer width | 103 mm | 51.2 mm |
| Side assemblies | Wide-body positions | Each moved inward 25.9 mm |
| Rear cover | Wide cover | 47.2 mm wide (2 mm inset per side) |
| Plate thickness | 3 mm nominal | Retained (3.2 mm at top support-contact regions) |
| Q6A board and tray | Onboard in wide packaging study | Moved offboard in this narrow variant |
| ESP32-P4 and PCA9685 | Rear side-by-side placement | Retained for narrow-body mounting |

![Top detail of the narrow chassis, showing the central plate between the paired servo assemblies.](/posts/2026-09-21-design-progress/narrow-chassis-top.webp)

*Top view of the narrowed 51.2 mm central plate between the paired shoulder assemblies.*

### Shoulder-to-board clearance and bottom camera-cable routing

A 308-pose shoulder rotation check swept all four **upper servo brackets** against the
ESP32-P4 and PCA9685 board envelopes inside the narrowed frame:

| Housing pair | Board model | Clear rotation window | Minimum clearance | Contact angle |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Rear left / right | ESP32-P4 | 19° inward to 125° outward | 1.970 / 1.967 mm | ~33.0° inward |
| Front left / right | PCA9685 | 13.01° inward to 140° outward | 0.788 mm per side | ~22.3° inward |

On September 18, the front panel's camera-cable recess was also moved from the top
center edge to the bottom center edge so the ribbon exits cleanly below the face:

| Cable opening | Earlier top-edge profile | Revised bottom-edge profile |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Position | Top center | Bottom center |
| Mouth width | 22.36 mm | 22.36 mm |
| Inner flat & depth | Narrower, 4.0 mm deep | 17.0 mm flat, 4.8 mm deep |
| Central ventilation slots | 21.0 mm high | 18.2 mm high (leaving a 2 mm bridge above recess) |

## Developing the carapace shell around the mechanism

Alongside the frame and linkage work, the outer shell progressed through front,
side, and three-quarter silhouette passes in Blender—refining the face opening,
collar, roof, shoulder fins, guards, and chin.

![September 20 CAD assembly with a pale developing shell above four articulated red legs and exposed black servo housings.](/posts/2026-09-21-design-progress/assembly-september-20.webp)

*September 20 three-quarter view of the carapace shell over the four leg assemblies.*

| Area | Issue addressed | Geometry change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Collar | Broad solid center obscured layered profile | Narrowed shoulders, recessed front edge, and enlarged rounded rear relief |
| Shoulder fins | Flattened old plates failed front/side silhouette checks | Rebuilt fin surfaces with dedicated prototype supports |
| Roof tips | Unequal outward flare (−42.75 mm vs +44.65 mm) | Matched lateral span to **±41 mm** and lowered outer edges 1 mm |
| Brow and cheeks | Harsh facet highlights and missing panel seams | Adjusted local crown and added shallow panel divisions around the face opening |
| Chin | Flat upper center | Added up to 0.6 mm convex ridge above the camera opening |
| Neck plate & roof | Dense 11,099-vertex neck mesh and deep roof walls | Simplified neck plate to a 162-vertex closed solid (~2 mm spacing) and reconstructed roof at ~99 × 41 mm per side with 1.6 mm walls, vents, and three interior hex pads per side |
