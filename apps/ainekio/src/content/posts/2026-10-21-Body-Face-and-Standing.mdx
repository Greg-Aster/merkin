Following the [October 18 fourth-wire feedback rebuild](/posts/2026-10-18-servo-feedback-and-chassis/),
the October 21 photographs show Ainekio V2 reassembled with its red front shell,
bezel, chin, cheek fangs, and side guards around the wide color display. Alongside the
mechanical assembly, we repaired a feedback ground fault to verify closed-loop
standing, ran physical walking comparisons with a learned periodic correction, wired
up host-side **BoTorch Bayesian gait optimization** and **MoGe-2** visual landmark
odometry, and added a single-call **Fast Body Event** reaction path in MetaHuman OS.

[Browse all three October 21 build photographs in the gallery](/gallery/).

## Aligned LCD face and precomputed side-light mask

The wide color LCD now renders brighter pupils and swept cyan side lights centered
within the printed bezel opening (shifted 15 px horizontally to match the physical
mask cutout). A precomputed mask supplies the permanent side-light geometry while the
eyes and expression elements remain procedural.

## Ground-repaired 4th-wire Stand verification and connection-loss hold

Initial loaded tests showed analog feedback readings pinned near their minima even
while the robot was visibly standing. Tracing and repairing a loose ground in the
feedback harness restored clean potentiometer readings across all twelve channels.

On the October 20 retest with Stand verification enabled (**400 µs** arrival
tolerance), the robot rose smoothly into Stand and held steady without jerking across
**65 snapshots over 19.22 seconds**: all twelve joints stayed well inside tolerance
with a peak filtered error of **175.74 µs** and zero corrective replans.

- **Explicit unverified receipt state:** When post-command readings are unavailable,
  firmware finishes the trajectory, holds powered targets, and reports
  `position not verified` rather than fabricating arrival or falsely declaring a jam.
- **Powered continuity across Wi-Fi drops (`0.8.113-p4-link-motion`):** Instead of
  disabling the PCA9685 and dropping the robot on a connection loss, finite motions
  now complete locally and open-ended gaits execute a graceful `Finish` into a powered
  standing hold.
- **Arrival-aware gesture player (`0.8.115-p4-gesture-arrival`) & inactivity rest:**
  Named gestures now ramp at half max joint speed (with final-approach deceleration on
  *Sit*, *Rest*, and *Crouch*) and verify calibrated shaft arrival before consuming
  hold durations. A new robot-saved **Lay down after inactivity** timer (default
  3 minutes) automatically rests the body when idle.

## Learning from physical walking: periodic correction and BoTorch loop

On October 19, we recorded commanded motion, all twelve 4th-wire shaft readings, IMU
attitude, and camera frames during floor walks, fitted a compact periodic joint
correction on the host, and evaluated it at 50% gain on the P4 across four 16-cycle
steady walking trials (with gyro heading hold enabled):

| Trial | Learned correction | Linkage tracking error (RMS) | Measured floor travel |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Baseline 1 | Off | 17.26 mm | ~18.0 in |
| Candidate 1 | 50% | **16.72 mm** | ~21.0 in (no rear sag or toe drag) |
| Baseline 2 | Off | 17.21 mm | ~21.5 in |
| Candidate 2 | 50% | **16.77 mm** | ~30.5 in (no rear sag) |

Pooled linkage tracking error dropped from **17.23 mm to 16.75 mm RMS (−2.84%)** and
eliminated rear sagging during the candidate runs.

### Host-side BoTorch optimization and MoGe-2 visual landmark tracking

To automate policy search during everyday use, recording and optimization moved into
Body Control's persistent host service (`Recordings → Learning history`):

- **BoTorch Bayesian optimizer:** Tunes gait profile parameters and feedback gains
  across forward Walk, backward Walk, left/right turns, and bounding Run. Candidate
  policies are checked against the linkage preview, evaluated in P4 RAM, and promoted
  to flash only after outperforming the incumbent across all priority directions.
- **MoGe-2 monocular depth + 3D landmark tracking:** Estimates camera motion against
  retained 3D visual landmarks (transformed into the body frame) as an alternative to
  ground-plane homography, feeding scored displacement into the learning loop.

## MetaHuman OS: sub-500 ms Fast Body Events and bridge admission fixes

To make physical reactions feel immediate when Ainekio is tipped or shaken, we added a
dedicated **Fast Body Event** workflow (`Lightweight` model category on the unadapted
**Qwen3.5 2B Q8_0** base):

1. **Immediate face preset + single-call speech:** Sets `Confused` (tipped) or
   `Surprised` (rapid movement) immediately before inference and generates a brief,
   darkly humorous spoken reaction in a single LLM call—clocking **486 ms** for tipped
   and **316 ms** for rapid movement.
2. **Motion-aware IMU event filtering (`0.8.113+`):** Body Control pairs P4 IMU
   samples with active execution context so normal accelerations during Walk, Run,
   Crawl, or Crab do not false-trigger shake reactions, while `Tipped` compares
   measured attitude against the authored pose's intended vertical.
3. **Semi Auto 5-minute inactivity timer & bridge admission:** Active Operator's Semi
   Auto mode now waits for a configurable **5-minute inactivity window** rather than
   re-triggering on its own queue bookkeeping. The Environment Bridge also removed the
   legacy payload-size cap (passing full camera + depth observations cleanly) and drops
   unsolicited sensor events when MetaHuman is offline while preserving results for
   already-admitted work.
