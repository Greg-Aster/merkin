At `5.0 V`, a stalled `MG90S` micro servo draws `700–850 mA` (`3.5–4.25 W`) into a tiny brushed coreless/iron-core rotor with almost zero thermal mass—driving an adiabatic copper-winding temperature rise of **`~2.5–2.8 °C/s`**. During bench testing, a single blocked joint (`rear_left_shoulder`) pushed against a mechanical stop outside its nominal `[500, 2800] µs` interior calibration span while waiting on an unbounded arrival checkpoint, cooking the winding over `~156 s`. Conversely, cutting global `PCA9685 OE` power the instant any foot brushes a chair leg would make the robot collapse onto its belly on every minor snag—and overly sensitive software recoil heuristics falsely clamp fast loaded swings on deep carpet.

To solve both sides cleanly, Ainekio V2 combines an **`8–10 s` hardware-enforced per-joint `I²t` winding thermal fuse** on `ESP32-P4` (`servo_feedback_model.c`, `servo_feedback_position.c`, `body.c`) with the **November 20, 2026 7-layer phantom-jam resolution** ([Milestone #20](/posts/2026-11-20-clockspring-arms-phantom-jams-opt-a1-and-megameal-sim/)) across **`NeuralPose v8` (`neural-pose-v8-calibrated`)**, **`50 Hz` `NeuralPosePresence`**, and the Host Gateway—eliminating false leg jams, inverted-carrier over-retraction, and competing recovery loops while keeping genuine locked-rotor protection 100% intact.

## 1. Three-day phantom leg-jam investigation & 7-layer resolution

Between November 18 and November 20, 2026, live carpet runs exposed an insidious failure cluster: during fast posture swings, wave transitions, and carpet stance recovery, legs occasionally froze crooked mid-air, retracted carriers past calibration, or triggered competing host-versus-firmware recovery loops even when no external obstacle was present. Tracing every event from the 12-bit `CD74HC4067` mux up to the `:8794` Freestyle bridge isolated **seven interacting root causes**—all resolved without weakening the hardware `I²t` fuse:

1. **Streaming reversal high-water unlock (`servo_feedback_position.c`):** During cyclic `50 Hz` direction reversals, high-water progress tracking could latch a stale peak across clipped or extrapolated samples. `ainekio_p4_feedback_stalled()` now enforces an **`80 µs` deadband**, resets on **`80 µs/s` directional slewing**, unlocks on **`25 µs` target shifts**, and **ignores clipped or extrapolated ADC samples** when evaluating streaming reversal progress (while full-span `[1..4094]` thermal protection stays active in `servo_feedback_model.c`).
2. **Firmware `stand_hold_recover` active-leveling guard & bounded replans (`body.c`):** Previously, continuous `208 Hz` `LSM6DS3` IMU balance trims could masquerade as posture drift and false-trigger `MOTION_POSE` recovery steps, or wait forever on an obstructed foot. Firmware now gates `stand_hold_recover()` behind **`!actively_leveling`**, caps unloaded stepping at **`STAND_MAX_REPLANS = 2`**, and enforces a **`0.8 s` (`ARRIVAL_WAIT_TIMEOUT_US = 800,000`)** checkpoint escape across `MOTION_FREESTYLE`, `MOTION_CLIP`, and `Stand` so limbs never freeze crooked mid-air (while inactivity `lay_down` always reaches `PCA9685 OE = 1`).
3. **Single posture-recovery owner (`_sync_neural_pose_servo_feedback`):** Running firmware `stand_hold_recover()` simultaneously beneath `50 Hz` host `NeuralPose` created two competing posture controllers fighting over the same 12 servos. Whenever `NeuralPose` owns the body, `_sync_neural_pose_servo_feedback` sets **`stand_enabled: false`** on firmware while keeping `50 Hz` 4th-wire telemetry and **`jam_enabled: true` (`I²t` thermal fuse)** active.
4. **Removed hardcoded Python `280 ms` `_detect_stalled_recoils` & added `mid_step_active` stance settling (`executor.py`):** The earlier `280 ms` (`14`-tick) Python recoil heuristic could not distinguish a genuinely blocked paw from transient `MG90S` tracking lag during a fast loaded swing—falsely clamping joints mid-gesture. `_detect_stalled_recoils` was deleted in favor of `NeuralPose v8`'s learned carpet-relock compliance plus **`mid_step_active` terminal stance settling**, which holds episode completion until stance error settles **`<= 5.5°`** and shoulder error settles **`<= 3.5°`** (safely inside the `7.0°` zero-heat thermal deadband).
5. **Eliminated `above_calibration` inverted-carrier over-retraction (`goals.py`):** Aggressive `~61 mm` vertical step lifts drove upper carriers past their `-30.87°` Home stop into the inverted `above_calibration` zone, looking identical to a mechanical jam. Setting **`PoseManifold.CARRIER_HOME_MARGIN_DEG = 3.0`** clamps the carrier floor **`>= -27.87°`** (`3.0°` safely inside `-30.87°` Home) and right-sizes carpet step lift from `~61 mm` to **`18–20 mm`**—achieving **`above_calibration_mask = 0`** and **`stalled_joints = 0`** on live hardware.
6. **Retired event-driven `0.8B` LLM stall/tip replanning (`library_recovery.py`):** Event-driven LLM recovery synthesis (`library_recovery.py`) was retired and **`828` synthetic recovery rows were pruned** from the Freestyle motion library (`1,767 → 939` clean choreography rows). `Bridge` now interrupts active motion jobs **only** when firmware reports a genuine hardware `I²t` thermal cutoff (**`outputs_released: true`**).
7. **Replaced legacy `20 Hz` `Master/gateway/presence/` with `50 Hz` `NeuralPosePresence` (`experiments/freestyle/neural_pose/presence.py`):** The older `20 Hz` presence loop issued `stop` on `vad_open` (which could cut off microphone capture timing) and applied nonzero lateral shoulder residuals that scrubbed feet sideways into carpet pile. `50 Hz` `NeuralPosePresence` stays **motor-silent during `vad_open` / wake-word** without issuing `stop`, and during speech drives **pure sagittal nods (`±2.5 mm` Z, `±4.5°` pitch) with `0.0°` shoulder targets** (zero lateral carpet scrub).

## 2. System 1 walking unsnag (`1.8 s`) & full-span `[1..4094]` ADC stall sensing

### Autonomous `1.8 s` reverse unsnag during forward locomotion

When a foot hooks under a power cord or raised rug transition during forward walking (`cmd_twist[0] > 0.05 m/s`), both clean-room `MicroDuck` locomotion owners execute a symmetric **autonomous reverse-unsnag reflex**:

- **On-chip `ESP32-P4` (`Slave/firmware/esp32p4-wifi6/main/body.c`):** Integrates per-joint snag duration whenever `|err_cd| > 1400.0 cd` (`14.0°`) and `vel_toward < 1000.0 cd/s` (`10.0 deg/s`). At **`1.8 s`** (`MICRODUCK_SNAG_WINDOW_US`), firmware overrides `cmd_twist` to **`[-0.45, 0.0, 0.0]`** at full stride scale (`xy_scale ≥ 1.0`) for **`1.8 s`** (`MICRODUCK_UNSNAG_DURATION_US`), backing the foot clear before resuming forward walking (`2.5 s` cooldown).
- **Host neural gait (`Master/gateway/server/service.py`):** Triggers the same `(-0.45, 0.0, 0.0)` `1.8 s` reverse maneuver (`neural_gait_unsnag`) on either 4th-wire joint lag (`≥ 14.0°` at `< 10.0 deg/s`) **or** zero visual/ground-plane odometry progress (`disp < 0.025 m` over `1.8 s` while `lag ≥ 10.0°` or `stalled_joints != 0`).
- **Ainekio OS goal alignment (`active-task.ts`):** `trackLocomotionProgress()` uses a **`5.5 s`** cognitive stall window (`stalledSamples ≥ 4 && elapsedMs ≥ 5,500`) so the `1.8 s` snag detection + `1.8 s` reverse unsnag + forward re-attempt (~`4.5 s`) finishes cleanly before Goal Review flags a blocked path.

### Full-span `[1..4094]` 12-bit ADC jam measurement

Audit of the `156 s` bench stall exposed that ADC readings outside the `[500, 2800] µs` interior calibration points had previously been discarded as out-of-range. In `servo_feedback_position.c`, `jam_measurement()` linearly extrapolates each joint's 3-point `CD74HC4067` NVS curve across the entire non-railed 12-bit ADC span (**`raw ∈ [1..4094]`**), catching hard mechanical jams near `0°` and `180°` for the `I²t` thermal model while rejecting true open/short wire faults (`0` or `4095`).

## 3. Hardware-enforced per-joint `I²t` winding thermal fuse & selective `PCA9685` cutoff

If an external clamp or wedged limb keeps a servo physically pinned past `NeuralPose v8`'s compliant posture control or `MicroDuck`'s `1.8 s` reverse unsnag, `Slave/firmware/esp32p4-wifi6/main/servo_feedback_model.c` protects the motor copper directly using a per-joint **`I²t` thermal accumulator**:

```text
Constants:
  DEADBAND      = 7.0°       # Normal standing gravity sag (<= 5.5° settled) generates ZERO heat forever
  SLEW          = 12.0 deg/s # Moving shafts (< 12°/s gate) do not accumulate locked-rotor heat
  FULL_SPAN     = 11.0°      # 18.0° stalled error = 1.0× nominal stall dissipation
  MAX_DUTY      = 1.25       # Caps extreme angular error at 1.25² = 1.56× heating rate
  TRIP_SECONDS  = 9.0 s      # Trips in 8.0–10.0 s under hard rotor lock
  COOL_SECONDS  = 12.0 s     # Linear cool-down retaining thermal memory across re-jams
  REARM_LOAD    = 0.35       # Channel stays masked (0 µs) until cooled below 35%
```

When `thermal_load[j]` reaches `1.0`:

1. **Selective single-channel cutoff (no belly-flop collapse):** `handle_thermal_cutoff()` latches `thermal_cutoff_mask |= (1U << j)` and writes `pulses[ch] = 0` (`PCA9685` register `LEDn_OFF_H = 0x10`) **only on the tripped servo channel**—leaving `PCA9685 OE` active so the **11 healthy servos continue holding the robot's stance**.
2. **Immediate wire & workflow cancellation:** Firmware terminates the active motion (`ESP_ERR_INVALID_STATE`) and emits `{"t":"event","name":"servo_stall","joint_mask":...,"outputs_released":true}`. Because non-cutoff transient stalls no longer trigger chattery software aborts, `outputs_released: true` serves as the single authoritative signal for `GatewayConnection._abort_pending_motion_on_stall()`, `Bridge`, and `EnvironmentAdapter._await_motion_with_stall_abort()` to cancel active motion futures and Freestyle jobs cleanly (while Ainekio OS also supports explicit `stop` with `detach: true` for full PWM disarm on operator command).
3. **Cooldown & smooth re-seeding:** Masked channels remain at `0 µs` across new commands until `thermal_load[j] < 0.35`, then `seed_zero_channels()` re-seeds the joint from its live 4th-wire shaft reading so re-engagement never snaps abruptly.

## 4. Hardware-in-the-loop C + Python, Automated Servo Calibration guard & `simulator.ainek.io` virtual jam verification

Two complementary harnesses verify stall sensing and `I²t` cutoff end-to-end without risking physical `MG90S` windings—paired with closed-loop safety guards during automated calibration ([Milestone #21](/posts/2026-11-23-unified-p4-firmware-v1-v3-simulator-multiplayer-and-auto-calibration/)):

- **Native C + Python HIL suite (`tests/test_p4_jam_and_thermal.py`):** Compiles production `body.c`, `pca9685.c`, and `servo_feedback_*.c` into a shared library wired to a simulated `I2C` `PCA9685` register bank and 12-bit `CD74HC4067` ADC.
- **Live [`simulator.ainek.io`](https://simulator.ainek.io/) 3D browser simulator ([simulator guide](/posts/gateway-and-simulator/)):** Runs the same compiled `ESP32-P4` C firmware atop `500 Hz` MuJoCo WASM across **V1**, **V2**, and **V3** with interactive **per-servo `J0–J7` (V1) and `J0–J11` (V2/V3) pin/jam fault injection**, letting operators pin individual joints mid-walk or mid-pose in the browser and watch `thermal_load[j]` trip `LEDn_OFF_H = 0x10` in `8–10 s` while the healthy joints keep the robot standing.
- **Automated Servo Calibration (`auto_calibration.py`) `25.0°` tilt abort guard & schema cleanup:** During automated 3-point potentiometer characterization on the bench, `auto_calibration.py` continuously monitors `LSM6DS3` attitude and immediately aborts the sweep if body tilt exceeds **`25.0°`**, preventing runaway sweeps if the robot slips off its calibration stand; alongside the phantom-jam cleanup, the legacy `correction_failed` telemetry field was retired in favor of the single authoritative `outputs_released: true` thermal cutoff contract.
