Ainekio V2 ([`Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot`](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot)) is a `650 g`, twelve-servo, closed-loop quadruped body built around the **Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6**. Where the eight-servo V1 prototype moved each leg in a single plane with two open-loop motors, V2 adds a third actuator per leg for lateral hip roll (`±h`), a compact four-bar linkage for upper-carrier (`alpha`) and knee (`theta`) actuation with curved rocking soles, 4th-wire potentiometer feedback on all twelve joints, a `208 Hz` inertial measurement unit, on-chip `50 Hz` Core 1 neural gait execution inside the `ESP32-P4`'s `128 KB` L2 cache, and a modular **Wireless Keeper Bridge** `2S` power carrier.

## Compact leg linkage geometry

Each V2 leg combines a shoulder roll axis with a planar four-bar linkage mechanism that keeps both flexion motors mounted high inside the upper servo bracket near the body center of mass:

- **Upper carrier (`alpha`)**: `35 mm` pivot-to-pivot link driven directly by the carrier servo.
- **Input crank (`theta`)**: `20 mm` servo horn crank driving a `38 mm` connecting rod.
- **Lower-leg pickup, distal segment & sole**: The `38 mm` rod drives a `28 mm` pickup radius on the `55 mm` distal lower leg (`beta`), terminating in a 3D-printed curved rocking sole.
- **Workspace enforcement**: Because the carrier and crank angles couple nonlinearly, onboard firmware projects requested foot targets onto the valid Cartesian linkage boundary via analytical inverse kinematics and 8-pass Gauss-Newton refinement before driving the servos, ensuring out-of-reach targets settle cleanly on the reachable envelope rather than stalling against mechanical stops.

The progression from the initial OpenHarmony Puppy reference through the short-rod studies (`2026-09-09`), early printed prototypes (`2026-09-15`), `22/36 mm` compact leg (`2026-09-21`), and `35/20/28/38/55 mm` production geometry (`2026-10-06`) is covered in the [Gait Development Guide](/posts/gait-development/).

### PCA9685 PWM driver wiring

Motor power branches directly from the `Mini560` `5 V / 5 A` regulator into the `PCA9685` servo rail, bypassing the `ESP32-P4` logic supply while sharing a solid common ground reference:

![PCA9685 Wiring](/gallery/diagram-pca9685-wiring-960.webp)

### CD74HC4067 4th-wire feedback multiplexer wiring

Every `MG90S` servo is opened on the bench to solder a 4th wire onto its internal potentiometer wiper. Those twelve analog lines route into channels **`C4–C15`** of a `CD74HC4067` multiplexer—addressed via select lines `S0–S3` on `GPIO28–31` and sampled at `50 Hz` into `ESP32-P4` **`GPIO49` (`ADC2_CH0`)**—allowing the controller to measure real shaft positions, detect stalls or jams, and verify target arrival (`Stand` within `2 mm` and closed-loop `Walk` synchronization):

![Multiplexer Wiring](/gallery/diagram-multiplexer-wiring-960.webp)

## Power architecture and Wireless Keeper Bridge

Earlier design studies tested heavy external USB-PD power banks (`Anker` 10,000 mAh and `SHARGE`). Both power-bank approaches were retired to keep total mass down to `650 g`. V2 packages onboard power and board retention together in a **battery-mounted Wireless Keeper Bridge** and rear carrier assembly:

1. **Battery pack**: `2S` (`7.4 V` nominal / `8.4 V` full charge) Li-ion pack (`73.6 × 46 × 22 mm` envelope) seated in the lower cradle beneath the Keeper Bridge.
2. **Step-down regulation**: A `Mini560` `5 V / 5 A` synchronous buck converter steps the `2S` pack down to a regulated `5 V` rail capable of sustaining twelve-servo standing and walking loads.
3. **Onboard USB-C charging & snap-in PCB clips**: A `HiLetgo` `2S` USB-C boost charger board charges the `2S` pack directly from standard `5 V` USB-C input, while dedicated printed clips retain the `ESP32-P4-WIFI6`, `PCA9685`, `HiLetgo` charger, and `LSM6DS3` gyro carrier securely during dynamic walking bouts.

## Design evolution from V1 to V2

![Illustrated comparison of Ainekio V1 and a prospective V2 hybrid with a stout body and serrated red legs.](/assets/ainekio/v2/evolution-concept.webp)

| Subsystem | V1 (Eight-Servo S3) | V2 (Twelve-Servo P4) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Legs** | 2 joints per leg; 8× `MG90S` | 3 joints per leg; 12× feedback-modified `MG90S` |
| **Foot placement** | Fore/aft and vertical only | Full 3D foot workspace including lateral hip roll and curved rocking soles |
| **Chassis** | Open Sesame-derived frame | Custom enclosed shell (`245.85 × 182.79 × 109.06 mm`) with modular front face and Wireless Keeper Bridge |
| **Controller** | `ESP32-S3 N16R8` (direct MCPWM) | `Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6` (`50 Hz` Core 1 `107.8 KB` L2 neural gait) + `PCA9685` PWM + `CD74HC4067` (`C4–C15` → `GPIO49`) ADC mux |
| **Host compute** | Desktop over Wi-Fi | Desktop or `Radxa Dragon Q6A` over Wi-Fi running **[Ainekio OS](/posts/ainekio-os/)** |
| **Power** | `2S 1000 mAh` LiPo + dual `Mini560` | `2S` pack + `Mini560` `5 V / 5 A` + `HiLetgo` `2S` USB-C charger in Wireless Keeper Bridge |
