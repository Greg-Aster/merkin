Ainekio V2 is a twelve-servo, closed-loop quadruped body built around the **Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6**. Where the eight-servo V1 prototype moved each leg in a single plane with two open-loop motors, V2 adds a third actuator per leg for lateral hip roll (`±h`), a compact coupled linkage for upper-carrier (`alpha`) and knee (`theta`) actuation, fourth-wire potentiometer feedback on all twelve joints, a `208 Hz` inertial measurement unit, and an integrated `2S` rear power carrier.

![Ainekio V2 Assembly Reference](/gallery/diagram-assembly-reference-960.webp)

## Mechanical specification at a glance

| Parameter | V2 Specification |
| --- | --- |
| **Assembled mass** | `650 g` (complete body with 12 feedback servos, shell, LCD, camera, and 2S battery carrier) |
| **Overall envelope** | `245.85 × 182.79 × 109.06 mm` (`L × W × H`, Blender assembly reference) |
| **Degrees of freedom** | 12 actuated joints (3 per leg: shoulder roll, upper-carrier pitch, knee linkage crank) |
| **Compact linkage geometry** | `35 mm` upper carrier, `20 mm` input crank, `28 mm` lower-leg pickup radius, `38 mm` connecting rod, `55 mm` distal lower leg |
| **Actuators** | 12× Miuzei `MG90S` metal-gear micro servos modified with a 4th analog wiper feedback wire |
| **PWM driver** | `HUAREW / Adafruit PCA9685` 16-channel 12-bit PWM controller over I2C (`50 Hz` frame rate with staggered channel phase offsets) |
| **Position feedback** | `CD74HC4067` 16-channel analog multiplexer routing 12 servo wiper voltages into the `ESP32-P4` ADC |
| **Main controller** | `Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6` (dual-core 360 MHz RISC-V, 32 MB PSRAM, 32 MB flash) + onboard `ESP32-C6` Wi-Fi 6 coprocessor |
| **Inertial sensor** | `LSM6DS3` 6-axis accelerometer/gyroscope (`208 Hz` sampling, stationary bias calibration, local attitude leveling) |
| **Face display & camera** | 1.9" `ST7789` IPS LCD (`320 × 170`, SPI) + `OV5647` MIPI-CSI camera (mounted upside-down with 180° rotation in front carrier) |
| **Audio subsystem** | Onboard `ES8311` codec + `NS4150B` Class-D amplifier, microphone, `20 × 30 mm` `8 Ω 2 W` speaker, and `WakeNet9s` |
| **Power & charging** | Rear two-level carrier: `2S` (`8.4 V` max) Li-ion pack (`73.6 × 46 × 22 mm`), `Mini560` `5 V / 5 A` buck converter, `HiLetgo` 2S USB-C boost charger |

## Compact leg linkage geometry

Each V2 leg combines a shoulder roll axis with a planar linkage mechanism that keeps both flexion motors mounted inside the upper leg housing near the body center of mass:

- **Upper carrier (`alpha`)**: `35 mm` pivot-to-pivot link driven directly by the carrier servo.
- **Input crank (`theta`)**: `20 mm` servo horn crank driving a `38 mm` connecting rod.
- **Lower-leg pickup & distal segment**: The `38 mm` rod drives a `28 mm` pickup radius on the `55 mm` distal lower leg (`beta`).
- **Workspace enforcement**: Because the carrier and crank angles couple nonlinearly, firmware (`0.8.150+`) projects requested foot targets onto the valid Cartesian linkage boundary before inverse kinematics so out-of-reach targets settle cleanly on the reachable envelope rather than stalling against mechanical stops.

The evolution from the initial OpenHarmony Puppy reference through the short-rod studies (`2026-09-09`), early printed prototypes (`2026-09-15`), `22/36 mm` compact leg (`2026-09-21`), and `35/20/28/38/55 mm` production geometry (`2026-10-06`) is detailed in the [Gait Development Guide](/posts/gait-development/).

### PCA9685 PWM driver wiring

Motor power branches directly from the `Mini560` `5 V / 5 A` regulator into the `PCA9685` servo rail, bypassing the `ESP32-P4` logic supply while sharing a solid common ground reference:

![PCA9685 Wiring](/gallery/diagram-pca9685-wiring-960.webp)

### CD74HC4067 4th-wire feedback multiplexer wiring

Every `MG90S` servo has a fourth wire soldered to its internal potentiometer wiper. Those twelve analog lines route into channels `C0–C11` of a `CD74HC4067` multiplexer, allowing the `ESP32-P4` to sample real shaft positions, detect stalls or jams, and verify target arrival (`Stand` within `2 mm` and closed-loop `Walk` synchronization):

![Multiplexer Wiring](/gallery/diagram-multiplexer-wiring-960.webp)

## Power architecture and rear carrier

Earlier design iterations evaluated heavy external USB-PD power banks (`Anker` 10,000 mAh and `SHARGE`). Both power-bank approaches are retired. V2 uses a dedicated, lightweight onboard power system packaged in a **rear two-level printed power carrier**:

1. **Battery pack**: `2S` (`7.4 V` nominal / `8.4 V` full charge) Li-ion pack (`73.6 × 46 × 22 mm` envelope) seated in the lower cradle.
2. **Step-down regulation**: A `Mini560` `5 V / 5 A` synchronous buck converter steps the `2S` pack down to a regulated `5 V` rail capable of sustaining twelve-servo standing and walking loads.
3. **Onboard USB-C charging**: A `HiLetgo` `2S` USB-C boost charger board mounted on the upper carrier deck charges the `2S` pack directly from standard `5 V` USB-C input.

## Design evolution from V1 to V2

![Illustrated comparison of Ainekio V1 and a prospective V2 hybrid with a stout body and serrated red legs.](/assets/ainekio/v2/evolution-concept.webp)

| Subsystem | V1 (Eight-Servo S3) | V2 (Twelve-Servo P4) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Legs** | 2 joints per leg; 8× `MG90S` | 3 joints per leg; 12× feedback-modified `MG90S` |
| **Foot placement** | Fore/aft and vertical only | Full 3D foot workspace including lateral hip roll |
| **Chassis** | Open Sesame-derived frame | Custom enclosed shell (`245.85 × 182.79 × 109.06 mm`) with modular front face and rear power carriers |
| **Controller** | `ESP32-S3 N16R8` (direct MCPWM) | `Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6` + `PCA9685` PWM + `CD74HC4067` ADC mux |
| **Host compute** | Desktop over Wi-Fi | Desktop or `Radxa Dragon Q6A` over Wi-Fi (with onboard Q6A / USB retained as an optional study) |
| **Power** | `2S 1000 mAh` LiPo + dual `Mini560` | `2S` pack + `Mini560` `5 V / 5 A` + `HiLetgo` 2S USB-C charger in rear carrier |
