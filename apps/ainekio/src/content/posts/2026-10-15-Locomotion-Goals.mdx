# A movement needs a meaningful endpoint

A request to move a particular distance or reach a visible destination needs
more than a gait start. The October 15 locomotion work gives MetaHuman a
goal-bearing movement interface, connects it to measured progress and preserves
the result of finishing that same gait.

The application and P4 support have recorded installations. Reliable model
selection, real camera calibration and physical goal accuracy remain separate,
unfinished qualification steps.

## What a locomotion goal describes

| Goal | Evidence relevant to progress |
| --- | --- |
| Distance | Measured displacement in an identified reference frame |
| Relative turn | Signed orientation change from the starting reference |
| Approach | A specified relationship to an identified target |
| Follow | An ongoing target relationship with an ending condition |
| Visual endpoint | Image evidence evaluated against the selected completion criteria |

The model selects the goal, target, tolerance and relevant visual behavior
alongside gait controls. Speed percentages are actuation settings; they are not
metres per second. Finishing a number of gait cycles or running for a duration
does not establish arrival at a real-world destination.

MetaHuman's advertised contract now requires these goals for locomotion instead
of bare Walk, Run, Turn, Crab or Crawl starts. Body Control's manual gait
primitives remain available through their existing controls, and finite gestures
such as Bow retain ordinary command handling.

## How progress returns to the task

Core's existing Active Task owner executes the selected movement and tracks
progress. The Environment Bridge carries sensor evidence and the movement's
start, update and Finish results. Sensor updates can resume that execution
without another Intent or Task-selection call. Visual and follow goals use the
existing behavior evaluator and its selected observation cadence.

Endpoint evidence, requesting Finish, acknowledgment of settings and the original
gait's actual terminal result remain distinct. A spoken claim of arrival does
not substitute for any of them.

The owner-approved handling of missing measurements is explicit: report the goal
incomplete without starting if its required measurement is unavailable. If that
measurement disappears during movement, request Finish on the same gait and
retain the outcome for the planner. The system does not substitute timed travel,
invent a new target or automatically restart the movement.

## What the sensors can and cannot establish

Position, orientation, camera evidence and target measurements retain their
source, reference, time, units and uncertainty. Reading an old sample again does
not make it fresh. Camera availability and orientation availability are separate;
missing metric camera calibration does not erase valid orientation evidence.

Metric camera estimates need real intrinsics, a measured camera-to-body transform
and a measured reference on the ground plane. That estimator tracks the plane;
it is not general depth sensing, inertial odometry or room navigation. A metric
target needs an explicit ground-contact point in retained evidence. The bottom
of a detection box is not automatically a floor contact.

An image-relative relationship can remain image-relative rather than being
presented as metres. Visual-only goals use camera evidence without requiring
metric calibration or YOLO. A slow visual inference is also different from a
camera that has stopped supplying evidence. Commanded foot geometry remains a
prediction, not measured ground displacement.

## Sideways correction and installation

The earlier side-crab primitive could not correct sideways overshoot with only
forward and turn controls. Signed lateral control now extends the same P4 motion
owner, protocol and Core controller.

The October 15 record verifies installation of **0.8.51-p4-lateral**, capability
negotiation with Body Control, unchanged saved servo calibration, and application
activation with goal and position/yaw/camera evidence reaching the Bridge.
The subsequent [0.8.52 Wave installation](/posts/2026-10-15-motion-and-leveling/)
preserves that lateral work. This is installation and data-delivery evidence,
not measured sideways correction accuracy.

## The models still need qualification

In an initial eight-case replay without action dispatch, the existing 0.8B Task
adapter failed all six locomotion cases while retaining the finite-Bow and
discussion controls. The 9B Planner selected valid measured goals in four explicit
distance/turn cases, but fabricated visual target evidence in another case.
Neither result qualifies complete autonomous movement.

Task training for the new contract was authorized; the reviewed record does not
establish a qualified replacement adapter. The separate
[9B autonomy/persona diagnostics](/posts/2026-10-15-learning-diagnostics/)
also retained failures under serving tests.

A destination does not establish a traversable route. Obstacle avoidance,
drop-off handling, actual camera calibration and physical goal completion remain
open. This update documents the machinery for selecting and evaluating movement
without presenting it as demonstrated navigation.
