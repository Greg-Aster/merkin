# Better motion tools for the twelve-servo body

V2 now has revised locomotion paths, a faster seated Wave and more informative
self-leveling controls. The October 15 journal records Wi-Fi installation and
boot verification through **0.8.52-p4-wave**. That establishes deployed firmware;
the new gait performance and expressive motion still need loaded physical tests.

The revisions use the last-confirmed installed linkage: 35 mm carrier, 20 mm
crank, 28 mm pickup, 38 mm rod and 55 mm lower leg, with the bare lower-leg part
touching the floor. Saved joint references and inversions are preserved. These
are V2/P4 changes; the original eight-servo V1 remains a separate supported body.

## See what self-leveling is doing

Body Control now shows measured tilt even while self-leveling is Off, error
relative to the saved target, the commanded body correction, and modeled
extension and joint-angle changes for each physical leg. Reduced correction is
also visible. **Per-leg values are commanded/model data**, not sensed joint
positions or confirmed foot contact.

| Control or readout | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Measured roll and pitch | Current orientation from the IMU |
| Target offset | Difference from the saved attitude reference |
| Commanded correction | The adjustment requested by the controller |
| Per-leg changes | Modeled extension and joint-angle changes relative to the nominal motion |
| Use current pose as level reference | Copy measured roll/pitch into the target fields |
| Save on robot | Persist the selected targets and existing response settings |
| Calibrate stationary gyro bias | Collect 256 fresh raw samples, save their mean bias and apply it without rebooting |

Capturing a level reference does not change sensor axes or gyro calibration;
saving is explicit. Gyro calibration requires a still robot and reports sample
variation and storage errors. It does not calibrate accelerometer scale or
measure the physical direction of a servo.

Enabled and active remain different states. The recorded Stop command clears
the standing balance hold, so leveling can be On while inactive. Stand re-enters
the supported hold. A supported chassis may also keep the same measured tilt
despite leg correction, allowing the integrator to approach its modeled reach.
That explains why commanded adjustment alone cannot prove successful leveling.

The 0.8.48 installation and desktop/mobile controls were checked in the source
journal. Physical correction direction and loaded disturbance rejection remain
open; this website update did not change the robot's settings.

## Walk: longer steps and a broader lift

Walk retains staggered footfalls and its existing clock. The foot's swing now
uses a broader arch, clearing the floor earlier at the same peak height, while
the support transfer is retimed around the moving leg.

| Commanded Walk parameter | Previous | Revised |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Full planted sweep | 32 mm | 37 mm |
| Low/full-gain lift | 4 / 8 mm | 6 / 10 mm |
| Stance fraction | 80% | 78% |
| Lateral support shift | 9 mm | 7 mm |
| Cycle at Speed 100 | 0.9 s | 0.9 s |
| Planned travel at Speed 100 | 44.44 mm/s | 52.71 mm/s |

The planned travel increase is **18.6%**. It is not a measured increase in ground
speed. The sampled straight-path study has only 0.064 mm minimum modeled dynamic
support margin at one speed, with actual center of mass and friction unmeasured.
This is a candidate trajectory, not robust stability qualification.

The PCA9685 driver also staggers the start phases of servo pulses while retaining
their widths and one frame transaction. Electrical benefit still needs current
measurement and an oscilloscope check; distributing pulse edges does not by
itself prove reduced motor-current peaks.

## Run, Crawl and Crab

Run keeps its front/rear paired bound and flight intervals. Crawl and Crab now
use the installed bare-leg contact surface instead of the historical boot/toe
profile. Their revised swing and body shifts transfer modeled support while
preserving planted anchors and the gait clock.

| Commanded parameter | Previous | Revised |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Run planted stroke | 40 mm | 44 mm |
| Run lift range | 2–8 mm | 5–12 mm |
| Crawl planted stroke | 18 mm | 32 mm |
| Crawl lift range | 2–4 mm | 5–8 mm |
| Crab fore/aft stroke | 36 mm | 40 mm |
| Crab sideways stroke | 24 mm | 32 mm |
| Crab stance width | 190 mm | 160 mm |
| Crab lift range | 6–12 mm | 8–14 mm |

At full gain, planned Crawl cycle travel increases about 59.5%; Crab sideways
travel increases 25%. Stroke, stance fraction and cycle time all contribute to
travel, so these are not measured speed claims.

The support study assumes a centered mass distribution. Crawl's sampled support
margin stays positive in the studied cases; Crab still has negative margins
during full turns and a small negative margin in one backward case. The
three-foot support model does not qualify the dynamics of Run's paired contacts
and flight. Friction, actual mass distribution, collision clearance, motor load
and tracking remain to measure.

## A quicker, larger seated Wave

Wave keeps its seated character, lifts the arm promptly and makes three larger
waves before replacing the foot and returning to standing.

| Authored timing at 1× | Previous | Revised |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Seated entry complete | 3.0 s | 1.2 s |
| Arm lift complete | 4.2 s | 1.6 s |
| Shoulder amplitude | ±18° | ±42° |
| Standing recovery complete | 15.2 s | 6.0 s |
| Clip duration | 16.0 s | 6.2 s |

Wave has its own bare-leg contact geometry; the separate Sit command is unchanged.
The other three legs and body pose stay constant during the waving portion.
Offline checks show positive assumed support margin with the arm airborne, but
the inherited seating/recovery transition still has a negative modeled margin.
That transition, the larger swing and whole-body clearance need physical testing.

The journal records 51 native tests, 24 compiler/handoff checks and a successful
firmware build for Wave, followed by verified installation of 0.8.52. No Wave
command was sent during that installation record. The website's older Blender
images and showcase remain dated; they do not depict this revised motion.

## Goals above the gait controller

These body trajectories supply movement to MetaHuman's new
[locomotion goal interface](/posts/2026-10-15-locomotion-goals/). That interface
adds distance, turn, approach, follow and visual endpoints with actual progress
evidence. The intervening 0.8.51 update installed signed lateral control, retained
by 0.8.52. Calibrated navigation and physical arrival accuracy remain unqualified.

## Evidence and next work

The [gait journal](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot/blob/main/docs/v2-12servo/GAIT_RESEARCH_JOURNAL.md)
is the engineering owner for these dated comparisons. This report uses the
reviewed local October 15 snapshot, whose changes may precede the repository's
published branch. Its hash and supporting source files are listed in the
[snapshot manifest](/posts/2026-10-15-progress/sources.json).

Firmware installation, mathematical reach and passing software checks do not
establish loaded movement quality. The next evidence needed is actual foot
clearance, body support, displacement, current demand and response to tilt.
Earlier experiments remain in the [continuing gait history](/posts/gait-development/).
