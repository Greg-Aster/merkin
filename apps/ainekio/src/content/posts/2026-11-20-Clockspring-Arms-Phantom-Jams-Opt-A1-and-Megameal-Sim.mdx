The past three days on the workbench split across four tightly connected fronts—two mechanical CAD leaps in `v3-2.blend`, a deep multi-layer firmware and reinforcement-learning hunt to eliminate phantom leg jams on physical carpet, and bringing a full-physics 3D web robot simulator live at **[`simulator.ainek.io`](https://simulator.ainek.io/)** (since expanded to all three robots and multiplayer in [Milestone #21](/posts/2026-11-23-unified-p4-firmware-v1-v3-simulator-multiplayer-and-auto-calibration/)):

1. **[Ainekio V1](/posts/ainekio-v1/) received a vertical-knee arm setup and concentric under-arm 4-wire clock-springs (`174.8 × 155.5 × 84.1 mm`, `174` active meshes):** `R2V10` upper arms were shortened and squared (`36.17 × 36.17 × 37.50 mm`) to hold the knee `MG90S` servos vertically at the elbow, paired with standardized `21,216`-vertex sculpted spiked lower legs and a **3-part rotary clock-spring assembly** under each arm that winds and unwinds all four conductors (`PWM + 5V + GND + 4th-wire sense`) internally without exposed wire loops.
2. **Seven interacting causes of "phantom leg jams" were traced and eliminated across ESP32-P4 C firmware and `NeuralPose` (`v5` → `v8-calibrated`):** What looked on the floor like mysterious leg stalls and crooked post-walk freezes turned out to be seven stacked interactions between C-side balance trims, inverted-carrier calibration ceilings (`above_calibration`), duplicate Python stall detectors, and re-locking carpet pile—now consolidated into **`neural-pose-v8-calibrated`** (`2.46M` GPU MuJoCo steps, Schmitt-trigger single-leg carpet stepping, `above_calibration_mask = 0`, `stalled_joints = 0`) and **`50 Hz` `NeuralPosePresence`**, while keeping the hardware **`I²t` winding thermal fuse** (`8–10 s` per-channel cutoff) 100% intact.
3. **[Ainekio V3](/posts/ainekio-v3-preview/) adopted the `Option A-1` compact 5-link pantograph (`54 / 22 / 46 / 56 / 135 mm`) and unified printable armor (`402.0 × 236.5 × 343.1 mm`, `537` active meshes):** After evaluating over `5,000` linkage geometries against `DS3235` torque/speed curves, V3 replaced its tall baseline legs with `Option A-1`—delivering **`+120%` vertical lift**, **`+116%` running thrust**, **`0.93:1` dual-servo load balance**, and **`-48.6 mm` shorter rear overhang**—alongside a `3,860`-vert lap-jointed belly chassis tub, `40°` sloped `F688ZZ` rear bearing undertray, functional `Clip 26` leaf-spring clamshell latches, and recessed `AINEKIO V-003` front fender arches.
4. **[`simulator.ainek.io`](https://simulator.ainek.io/) 3D Web Robot Simulator (Live Browser Alpha — Evolved from `game.megameal`):** What started two days ago as a local `game.megameal` (`?scene=v2_lab_runtime`) daemon proof-of-concept is now live as a **zero-install browser alpha at [`simulator.ainek.io`](https://simulator.ainek.io/)**. It compiles the real `ESP32-P4` C firmware (`ainekio-v2-firmware.wasm`) and **`500 Hz` MuJoCo physics (`mujoco_wasm.js`)** directly into a browser Web Worker alongside Three.js, quantized **`microduck-v19-snag-p4`** neural walking, **`NeuralPose`** emotes, six playable 3D worlds (including **Yggdrasil**, **Observatory**, **Miranda Wreck**, **Solitude**, **Sci Fi Room**, and the **V2 Robotics Lab**), live `320 × 170` `ST7789` LCD faces, virtual `OV5647` chin-cam vision, force-wrench perturbations, and `J0–J11` servo pin/jam tests—with optional WebSocket attachment back to local Body Control / Ainekio OS.

### Key Mechanical Changes in `Scene 'v1'`
- **Compact Vertical-Knee Upper Arm (`R2V10`):** Shortened and squared from `[37.32, 60.43, 37.50 mm]` (horizontal knee pocket) to **`36.17 × 36.17 × 37.50 mm`** (`1,419` vertices), orienting each knee `MG90S` (`L3, R3, L4, R4`) **vertically** at `(±59.14, ±67.30, 74.48 mm)` with recessed hip-horn pockets and fastener clearances while keeping `683ZZ` counter-bearings on all four hip axes (`(±48.35, ±38.50 mm)`).
- **3-Part Coaxial Under-Arm Clock-Spring:** Replaced airborne wire arches and external snap-clips with a rotary clock-spring concentric with each Hip-Z shaft:
  1. **`Clockspring Rotor Cup (Under-Arm)`** (`33.90 × 33.79 × 5.30 mm`, `r = 9.30..19.88 mm`): Mounts underneath the rotating `R2V10` upper arm with a tangential side-exit slot feeding the vertical knee servo.
  2. **`Clockspring Snout Stator`** (`26.45 × 17.90 × 5.13 mm`, `r = 6.07..17.79 mm`): Anchors to the stationary hip servo snout and guides the inner harness cleanly into the `Frame7` chassis.
  3. **`4-Wire Spiral Ribbon Harness`** (`15,520` vertices per corner): Coils `PWM + 5V + GND` plus the white **4th-wire potentiometer tap** inside the enclosed rotor/stator cavity so hip rotation winds and unwinds the spiral internally with zero exposed slack.
- **Clock-Spring Bay `Frame8` Cap & Sculpted Lower Legs:** The `22.0 mm`-tall `Frame8` enclosure cap (`121.7 × 79.7 × 22.0 mm`, `1,045` vertices) adds four scalloped corner clock-spring clearance bays (`r = 32.5..66.5 mm`), while all four lower legs standardize on a unified **`21,216`-vertex (`39.30 × 5.30 × 79.17 mm`)** sculpted spiked profile (`knee` pivot at `(±72.92, ±53.53, 70.12 mm)`).

## 2. Three Days Hunting Phantom Leg Jams & Reworking Neural Gait (`NeuralPose v5` → `v8`)

On physical carpet runs with [Ainekio V2](/posts/ainekio-v2/), fast limb swings, overhead-stretch returns, and post-walk stops intermittently triggered **phantom leg-jam recoveries**, infinite unloaded stepping loops, or crooked mid-air freezes—even when no physical obstacle was blocking the leg.

Tracing every state owner from the `ESP32-P4` ADC/PWM loop up through `Body Control` and `Freestyle` uncovered **seven distinct, interacting root causes** masquerading as a single "jam" bug:

| **2. ### Four Iterations of Neural Gait & `NeuralPose` (`v5` → `v6` → `v7` → `v8-calibrated`)
Alongside retiring legacy `pupper-mjx` patches in favor of clean-room **MicroDuck PPO** (`microduck-v18-p4` & `microduck-v19-snag-p4` `107.8 KB` `int8` L2-cached policy + `1.8 s` firmware reverse-unsnag reflex) and Apache-2.0 **RT-DETRv2 + OWLv2** local vision (`detector_backend.py`), `NeuralPose` (`826,776` parameters, `50 Hz`) went through four full GPU MuJoCo training cycles:

1. **`v5-adaptive` (`1.23M` steps):** Introduced unilateral shoulder friction snags, but unlocked carpet pile permanently on first lift.
2. **`v6-relock` (`1.35M` steps):** Modeled **re-locking carpet pile** (lifting frees the foot, but touching down with `>2.6°` error re-locks pile at the landed angle), exposing sub-`6.5°` continuous micro-stepping ("drunk wobble") and `+61 mm` `above_calibration` carrier over-retraction on the physical robot.
3. **`v7-hysteresis` (`1.23M` steps) → `neural-pose-v8-calibrated` (`2.46M` steps, active):**
   - **Stand-Before-Step Gate:** Blocks shoulder stepping while any stance carrier error exceeds `14°`, forcing folded or crouched legs to stand on all four feet before lifting a paw.
   - **Schmitt-Trigger Single-Leg Sequencer:** Initiates a single-leg step strictly when shoulder splay exceeds **`6.5°`** and latches that leg down to **`<= 3.0°`**; sub-`6.5°` splays hold calm zero-lift stance inside firmware's `7.0°` zero-heat band.
   - **Tripod CoM Shift & Wide Friction Envelope:** Combines `±14 mm` lateral and `±9 mm` longitudinal CoM shifts, `-8 mm` diagonal brace press, `18–20 mm` calibrated Z lift (`carrier >= -27.87°`), decoupled loaded servo damping (`135–210°/s`), and floor friction `µ = 0.10..2.05` (slick tile through deep carpet), passing **`12/12` 3D MuJoCo qualification scenarios with `0` falls** and **`stalled_joints = 0`** on live hardware.

## 3. Ainekio V3: `Option A-1` Compact 5-Link Pantograph & Printable Hull Engineering

### `>5,000`-Candidate 5-Link Optimization → `Option A-1` (`54 / 22 / 46 / 56 / 135 mm`)
Sweeping more than `5,000` candidate 5-link geometries across full `270°` `DS3235` travel (`Part_009` `22.0 mm` flanged crank, `Part_023` `54.0 mm` trussed carrier, `-35°` angled bellcrank, `Part_026` `56.0 mm` trussed rod, and `Part_027` `109.0 mm` angled lower leg / `135 mm` effective foot vector) transformed V3's stance and actuator margin:
- **`+120%` vertical support lift** (`56 N·mm/N` vs. `105 N·mm/N` bottleneck torque) and **`+116%` running push-off thrust** (`65 N·mm/N` vs. `123 N·mm/N`), shifting bounding load from the slow, hot `84%` stall zone right into the **`46%` peak mechanical output power zone (`99% P_max`)**.
- **`0.93:1` dual-servo load sharing** between the `Part_005` crank and `Part_006` carrier servos, exploiting up to `215°–268°` of `DS3235` shaft travel across running and emotes with **`8.4 mm` minimum fender clearance** and **`-48.6 mm` less rear overhang**.

### What Is Working Live Today on [`simulator.ainek.io`](https://simulator.ainek.io/) (Nov 21 Update)
Rather than faking animations or approximating closed-loop four-bar linkages with game-ragdoll hinges, the live alpha compiles the **same `ESP32-P4` C firmware and `500 Hz` MuJoCo physics** into WebAssembly inside a dedicated browser Web Worker:

1. **100% Client-Side `500 Hz` MuJoCo WASM + Compiled `ESP32-P4` Firmware (`mujoco_wasm.js` + `ainekio-v2-firmware.wasm`):** Executes real `ESP32-P4` firmware modules (`virtual_body.c`, `face.c`, `face_timeline.c`, `protocol-v1` commands, 4th-wire `CD74HC4067` ADC feedback, `LSM6DS3` IMU attitude, and per-channel `I²t` winding thermal cutoff) inside a browser worker—paired with analytical closed-form `35 / 20 / 28 / 38 mm` four-bar loop closure on `/assets/robots/ainekio-v2.glb`.
2. **In-Browser Neural Gait & `NeuralPose` Emotes (Plus Classic Firmware Gaits):** Runs **`microduck-v19-snag-p4`** neural walking and **`neural-pose-v7-hysteresis`** expressive motions (`Sit`, `Lay Down`, `Wave`, `Bow`, `Stretch`, `Shake`, `Dance`, `Cute`, `Nod`, `Point`, `Celebrate`, `Push-Up`, `Crouch`) directly in the browser, with a live toggle back to native analytical firmware gaits (`Walk`, `Run`, `Crab`).
3. **Six On-Demand 3D Worlds Adapted from `game.megameal`:**
   - **V2 Robotics Lab:** `1:1` metric testbed with `15/16 mm` cable strips, corridor curb, and optical landmarks.
   - **Yggdrasil (Tree of Life):** Colossal `27 m` Norse World Tree with the rainbow Bifrost bridge, cable thresholds, stone rune archway, cathedral roots, ramps, slalom, and spatial audio wells.
   - **Observatory:** Coastal stone observatory with chunked `LOD0` terrain and a real MuJoCo heightfield.
   - **Miranda Wreck**, **Solitude (Stonehenge Plain)**, and **Sci Fi Room:** Full-collision exploration decks, rolling grassy plains, and biomechanical lab interiors loaded lazily on demand.
4. **Full Camera Suite, Keyboard Driving & Interactive Fault Injection:**
   - **`Orbit`**, **`Follow V2`**, and **`Robot POV`**: Drag smoothly in Orbit (including looking upward at tall structures like Yggdrasil without floor clipping), or drive directly from `Follow V2` / `Robot POV` using **`WASD` / Arrow keys + `Q`/`E` strafe** synced to the HUD joystick.
   - **Live `ST7789` LCD Face, Virtual `OV5647` Chin Cam & Audio:** Renders procedural `320 × 170` facial expressions onto the 3D faceplate and HUD, captures live chin-cam snapshots, and plays spatial world audio with `Tap to Talk` microphone support.
   - **Perturbations & `J0–J11` Pin/Jam Grid:** Click `Push +X / +Y / -Y` or **`Pick Up & Shake`** to trigger firmware IMU reflexes, or click any of the 12 servo bars (`J0–J11`) to pin/jam a joint and watch 4th-wire stall detection and `I²t` protection trip safely in software.
5. **Dual-Mode Transport (`Standalone` or Live `Body Control` WebSocket):** Works standalone with zero local software, or click **`Connect Body Control`** to link the browser robot over `ws://` / `wss://` (`protocol-v1` JSON + binary frames) to a local Body Control / [Ainekio OS](/posts/ainekio-os/) instance—the same bridge architecture proved in the original `game.megameal` `?scene=v2_lab_runtime` daemon PoC below.
