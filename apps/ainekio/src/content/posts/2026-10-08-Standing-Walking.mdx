# The robot is on its feet

The robot can now **stand, walk and respond to commands**. Two days ago, the
printed chassis could not stand on its MG90 servos. That was the starting point
for the lighter rebuild, material removal and shorter-carrier work described in
the [October 6 entry](/posts/2026-10-06-lighter-rebuild/).

The carapace pieces are not attached yet, so the robot is still “naked.”
**I have not tested Active Operator.**

## Two days of build progress, side by side

| Question | October 6 report | October 8 report |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Can the assembled robot stand? | The printed chassis could not stand on its MG90 servos | The robot can stand |
| Can it walk? | Revised parts were still printing; the new assembly had not been tested | Walking |
| Does it respond to commands? | The entry focused on the failed standing attempt and rebuild | Responds to commands |
| What is fitted? | Chassis printed; LCD and shell pieces not printed for that attempt | Photo shows the exposed assembly and illuminated LCD; carapace pieces are not attached |
| Active Operator | No physical acceptance result | Explicitly not tested |
| Weight and lifting force | Approximately 20% lighter and 20% more lifting force were estimates | No new measurements supplied; those estimates remain estimates |
| Battery endurance | Not measured | No runtime measurement supplied |

Still to measure: walking speed and distance, behavior on different surfaces,
endurance, and loaded gestures. I also need to record the installed calibration
and part revisions.

exports. Those are still uncommitted at this review. They are not included in

The [parts and assembly guide](/posts/ainekio-v2/) retains earlier options,
prototype photographs and print downloads. The
[compact-linkage study](/posts/2026-10-06-compact-linkage/) explains the
35/20/28 mm candidate and its remaining reach/travel questions. The new walking
report does not identify the exact firmware/model revision used, so it should
not be used to declare every offline motion or clearance check complete.

## The face is now part of the physical build

The photograph shows the wide LCD displaying its cyan resting expression.
The firmware records also document October 6 LCD/camera orientation fixes,
a working-speaker report, and wake-word transport checks. The
[face and media update](/posts/2026-10-08-face-and-media/) separates those device
records from the newer local expression refinements.

MetaHuman and Body Control have also changed: request handling, conversation,
task planning and connection selection are being developed together. The
[responsive-command update](/posts/2026-10-08-responsive-commands/)
describes that software work and its current test boundaries.

## What remains to document

The carapace still needs attaching, and Active Operator needs its own test.
Useful next measurements are the assembled mass, exact power arrangement,
walking settings and duration, and the flashed firmware/calibration revision.
None of those values is inferred from the photograph.
