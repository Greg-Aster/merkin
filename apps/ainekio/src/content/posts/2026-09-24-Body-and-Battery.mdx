*Cover: a frame from the September 23 Blender motion export showing the red mask,
carapace, and exposed linkage.*

Most of the recent design work has focused on the body itself: how the mask
attaches, how the end plates fit around the bearings, how printed mounts survive
assembly, and where the battery can sit while the legs move. The initial battery
candidate is an **Anker PowerCore Slim 10,000 mAh** (**155 × 72 × 16 mm** envelope),
chosen because it is already on hand. Rather than treating battery packaging as an
afterthought once the shell is finished, the underside is being designed around a
removable pack from the start.

The [V1 vs V2 comparison](/posts/v1-v2-comparison/) brings the
mechanics, processing, and power arrangements together, while the
[gait development log](/posts/gait-development/#september-24-2026--continuous-gaits-and-controller-integration)
covers the new continuous gait controls and September 24 firmware deployment.

## Chassis width and structural updates

The modeling scene now measures **80.0 mm between the exterior walls of the
center supports**, widened from 75 mm by translating each support and its leg
mechanisms 2.5 mm outward (increasing shoulder-center spacing from 56 mm to 61 mm).

| Area | September 22–24 change | Next focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Face and mask | Shorter front plate, concealed upper mask receivers, and inclined lower screw passages | Fastener length, print fit, and tool access |
| Rear panel | Matching upper/lower corner tapers (~44 mm at flat edges, 63.2 mm around bearings) | Align end plates with the 80 mm support spacing |
| Center-support tabs | Flared roots and 1.5 mm screw pilots after four printed tabs split along layer lines during assembly | Reprint and test layer strength |
| Electronics | Compared flat fore/aft board placement against centered stacked and upright alternatives | Finalize board mounts with connector and cable bend allowances |
| Underside | Packaged the rounded Anker bank beneath the moving leg assemblies | Build cradle structure and verify full-motion clearance |

### Mask attachment and reinforced center-support tabs

![CAD comparison of the previous raised front brackets and the compact mask screw mounting arrangement.](/posts/2026-09-24-progress/front-panel-mounts.webp)

*September 23 CAD review replacing raised upper brackets with compact screw mounts, using the LCD-facing surface as the print bed.*

The raised upper brackets and lower locating pins were replaced by screw
mounts. Upper screws pass through 2.2 mm clearance holes into 1.6 mm blind mask
pilots, while the lower passages incline ~19.8° to clear the LCD and retain
cheek wall thickness (sized for provisional M2×10 upper and M2×14 lower screws).
The lower plate corners also taper from 63.2 mm to 51.4 mm at the bottom so they tuck
cleanly inside the mask silhouette.

![Reinforced center-support roots and recessed fastener seats in the temporary X brace.](/posts/2026-09-24-progress/center-mount-tab-reinforcement.webp)

*Reinforced center-support roots and recessed fastener seats in the underside X brace.*

After all four original lower tabs split along print layers during linkage fastener
assembly, the revised support adopted the smaller screws that worked well in
Part_003: **1.5 mm pilots, 4 mm deep**, backed by rounded, flared roots. The mating
brace uses 2.2 mm clearance holes with **5.4 × 2.2 mm** head recesses, leaving 2 mm
of material beneath each screw head.

![Rear-panel CAD comparison showing the revised upper taper and relocated mounting holes.](/posts/2026-09-24-progress/rear-panel-y-taper.webp)

*September 23 rear-panel taper preserving full bearing support width while trimming the outer corners.*

### Electronics placement options

A September 23 packaging study compared the flat fore/aft board layout against two
centered alternatives. Standing the boards on edge saves ~10 mm of vertical height
compared to a horizontal stack and leaves an 8 mm central wiring channel between
component envelopes, though the flat fore/aft layout remains the baseline while
connector clearance is finalized for the physical HUAREW PCA9685 board.

## Battery packaging and clearance studies

| Power stage | Original V1 | September 24 V2 baseline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Battery | 1,000 mAh 2S LiPo (7.4 V) | Anker PowerCore Slim 10,000 mAh (155 × 72 × 16 mm) |
| Conversion | External buck converter | Regulated bank output |
| Charging | External balance charger | Integrated USB charging in removable bank |

Three successive CAD clearance checkpoints evaluated how high the rounded bank can
sit beneath the moving housings:

| Checkpoint | Battery height in CAD | Clearance findings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 75 mm / Z54 baseline | Upper face Z54.0 mm (center Z46.0 mm) | Checked across 15,627 integer-frame poses (25 sequences) + 690 quarter-frame samples with zero bank mesh intersections; minimum ground clearance 31.29 mm in Walk, 16.07 mm in Walk–Run–Walk, 20.00 mm in Crab, and **2.29 mm in Crawl** |
| 75 mm vs 80 mm width study | Z54.590 mm (75 mm) vs Z55.956 mm (80 mm) | Widening the support walls from 75 mm (69 mm inner) to 80 mm (74 mm inner) allows raising the battery 1.37 mm while retaining a 0.5 mm housing gap (leaving 2.755 mm below the center-mount undersides) |
| 17° front-left pose check | Upper face Z56.792 mm (center Z48.792 mm) | Lowered 0.501 mm from the contact limit to clear the Part_003 housing at front-left shoulder `h=17°, carrier=0°, crank=0°` |
