While the multi-panel **Body Control** dashboard (`Master/gateway/dashboard/`) exposes engineering calibration curves, SD card downloads, and OTA firmware staging, day-to-day floor operation and embodied debugging happen inside **Ainekio Cockpit** (`#cockpit-section`, `data-workspace-panel="cockpit"`). Accessible from the persistent top-bar `Cockpit` button in Body Control, Cockpit condenses live `OV5647` camera vision, voice capture, a translucent **[Ainekio OS](/posts/ainekio-os/)** conversation/workflow stream, neural gait switching, Freestyle choreography, and 2D vector driving onto a single viewport without scrolling.

![Body Control operator interface showing the top-bar Cockpit launcher, live camera viewport, MetaHuman/Ainekio OS control switch, and protocol-v1 robot status telemetry](/posts/2026-11-16-progress/body-control-ui.webp)

## 1. Top HUD cluster & one-tap `Tap to Talk` voice bypass

Across the top edge of the camera feed (`cockpit-hud-top`), Cockpit surfaces real-time link health and immediate voice/safety controls:

- **Live posture & attitude pills:** Connection state, active controller state (`STAND`, `WALK`, `FREESTYLE`, `PASSIVE`), `208 Hz` `LSM6DS3` roll and pitch readout (`#cockpit-attitude-readout`), and status toast notifications.
- **`Tap to Talk` (`#cockpit-talk-button`) + live mic meter (`#cockpit-mic-level`):** Normally, the `ESP32-P4` listens behind onboard wake-word gating (`WakeNet9s` / `microWakeWord`). Tapping `Tap to Talk` saves the current microphone gate (`wake` or `vad`), sends `{ on: true, gate: "open" }` to the robot, and displays a floating status banner (`"Listening — speak now, then tap Done to send..."`) alongside a live `16 kHz` PCM level meter. Tapping `Done · Send` restores the prior `wake` or `vad` gate, causing the `ESP32-P4` to seal and flush the recorded PCM utterance immediately through `Master/gateway` to Whisper (`:9883`) and Ainekio OS.
- **Camera, `Stream`, `Settings`, and direct `STOP`:** One-click `Cam` stream switch, `Snap` still capture, `Stream` overlay toggle (`localStorage["ainekio-cockpit-stream-open"]`), `⚙ Settings` drawer, and high-priority `STOP` (`stop` preempting active motion and coordinator leases).

## 2. Live Ainekio OS message stream (`#cockpit-stream-panel` & `Body Control Text Out`)

Previously, driving Ainekio from Body Control while conversing with or debugging **Ainekio OS** required keeping two browser tabs open side by side. Cockpit now renders a translucent HUD message stream (`width: clamp(230px, 34vw, 320px)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(6px)`) anchored along the right edge of the live camera viewport, backed by an end-to-end durable text projection pipeline between `ainekio-os` and `Ainekio-bot`:

| Pipeline Stage | Owner & File | Behavior |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`Body Control Text Out` node** | `ainekio-os` `packages/core/src/nodes/environment/send-text.node.ts` (alias `body_control_text_out`) | `any-input` output node wired into **7 cognitive graphs** (`environment-conversation-mode`, `agent-mode`, `dual-mode`, `emulation-mode`, `big-brother-response-mode`, `big-brother-tool-mode`, and `robot-wake-up-mode`). Emits durable `environment_text_stream` outbox effects **without claiming a `bodyLease`** or queuing physical robot commands. |
| **Coordinator outbox & SSE relay** | `coordinator-outbox.ts`, `ExecutionStore` (`completeProjection`), `environment-interface/store.ts` | Buffers up to `64` `pendingTextStream` items per session (deduplicated across `128` delivered IDs), streams `event: messages` batches over `/api/environment-bridge/stream`, and forwards `{ type: "environment.text_stream", version: 1 }` frames over the bridge WebSocket. |
| **Gateway `EnvironmentAdapter._text_stream`** | `Ainekio-bot` `Master/gateway/environment_adapter/server.py` (`POST /api/metahuman-stream`) | Merges three live sources into an 80-message deduplicated ring (`160`-ID history): **(1)** transcribed user voice utterances (`id: "voice:<utteranceId>"`, `role: "user"`, `source: "voice"`), **(2)** cognitive workflow & Big Brother replies (`environment.text_stream`), and **(3)** direct bridge text actions (`id: "action:<action_id>"`). |
| **Cockpit `#cockpit-stream-panel`** | `Master/gateway/dashboard/static/{dashboard.html,js,css}` | Displays glowing online bridge status, role/mode badges (`You · voice`, `Ainekio OS`, `Big Brother`), `HH:MM:SS` timestamps, sticky bottom auto-scroll (`≤ 28 px` threshold), and one-click `Clear` (`{"op": "clear"}`). |

## 3. Ten immediate-apply runtime controls (`#cockpit-settings-drawer`)

Opening `⚙ Settings` inside Cockpit exposes ten live hardware, neural, perception, and autonomy switches that apply immediately without leaving the camera view:

1. **Auto-leveling (`#cockpit-balance-toggle`):** Enables/pauses tapered `208 Hz` `LSM6DS3` roll/pitch self-leveling (`≤ 0.5°` bus-quiet deadband with linkage-Jacobian anti-windup).
2. **Jam detection (`#cockpit-jam-toggle`):** Enables target-aware 4th-wire potentiometer stall monitoring across all 12 `CD74HC4067` channels (see [Jam Protection & Winding Thermal Fuse](/posts/jam-protection-and-thermal-safety/)).
3. **Freestyle jam & fall auto-recovery (`#cockpit-recovery-toggle`):** Enables closed-loop `280 ms` joint recoil, `0.85 s` Return-to-Stand, and `1.8 s` walking reverse unsnag.
4. **Living presence — System 3 (`#cockpit-presence-toggle`):** Toggles the `20 Hz` autonomic `PresenceController` (`POST /api/presence`) for speech-envelope body motion, stance compliance chatter relief, and person-aware inactivity rest (see [Three-System Neural Motion Architecture](/posts/three-system-neural-architecture/)).
5. **Auto self-right & gyro reflex (`#cockpit-self-right-toggle`):** Feeds live IMU gyro/tilt state into System 3 (`imu_fed_to_policy`) and enables pickup passive safety (`calm_then_release`) and automatic self-righting.
6. **Walking heading correction (`#cockpit-walking-feedback-toggle`):** Closes IMU yaw drift during straight-line walking bouts.
7. **Host neural gait vs. On-chip MicroDuck (`#cockpit-walk-system-toggle`):** Switches live walking between **Core 1 on-chip `107.84 KiB` `int8` MicroDuck** (`source="native"`, zero Wi-Fi step jitter) and **host-streamed `50 Hz` neural policies** (`source="neural"`).
8. **Host neural policy selector (`#cockpit-neural-model-select`):** Hot-swaps runnable `Pupper MJX` (`200M`–`800M`) and `MicroDuck` (`v16`–`v19-snag-p4`) checkpoints on `:8796`.
9. **Vision navigation source (`#cockpit-depth-source`):** Switches obstacle and odometry estimation between `ground-plane` (calibrated floor homography + pyramidal Lucas-Kanade optical flow) and `moge-open3d` (`MoGe-2 ViT-L` metric depth + Open3D 3D odometry + `8×8` `< 0.30 m` corridor braking).
10. **Ainekio OS autonomous control (`#cockpit-metahuman-toggle`):** Permits or pauses autonomous `environment-bridge` actions while keeping telemetry and conversation active.

## 4. 2D vector joystick, 25-emote catalog & custom Freestyle prompt

Along the bottom of the viewport, Cockpit pairs continuous locomotion steering with expressive pose synthesis:

- **Pointer-captured 2D virtual joystick (`#cockpit-joystick`):** Features a `14%` center deadzone, `70 ms` / `8%` vector delta throttling (`driveHeldVector(forward, turn)`), a `Stride 0–100%` range slider (`#cockpit-speed`), and `Finish Walk` (`#cockpit-walk-finish`) for clean stance phase completion—working alongside `WASD`/arrow keys and USB/Bluetooth gamepads.
- **Quick posture pad & 25-button emote drawer (`#cockpit-emotes-drawer`):** Four immediate stances (`Stand`, `Sit`, `Neutral`, `Rest`) plus 25 named gestures (`wave`, `stretch`, `dance`, `bow`, `celebrate`, `cute`, `curious`, `nod`, `shake`, `shrug`, `crouch`, `lay_down`, `dead`, `pushup`, `point`, `swim`, `surprised`, `sad`, `salsa`, `macarena`, `worm`, `freaky`, `number_one` `#1 · hydrant`, and `number_two` `#2 · squat`), switchable between compiled C splines (`Legacy`) and `50 Hz` `NeuralPose` (`Freestyle`).
- **Inline Freestyle prompt (`#cockpit-freestyle-form`):** Dispatches free-text natural-language choreography prompts (`POST /api/request-motion`) to the local `:8794` `Qwen3.5-0.8B` + `freestyle-curriculum-adapter.F16.gguf` generator (`1,812` curated examples) and executes the resulting multi-frame trajectory through `NeuralPose` with 4th-wire shaft-arrival checks.

## 5. 8-sector spatial curiosity feed (`spatial_curiosity.py`)

Alongside human teleoperation in Cockpit, `Master/gateway/spatial_curiosity.py` continuously fuses live `OV5647` detections and `LSM6DS3` yaw into an **8-sector (`8 × 45°`, `65°` camera HFOV) spatial curiosity map** published over `/environment`:

- Attributes open-vocabulary `YOLOE` detections and `YuNet`/`SFace` person identities to compass-relative body yaw sectors, tracking `lastSeenSecAgo`, `freeFloorMeters` (from `GroundPlane` or `MoGe-2`), `secondsStationary`, `secondsSinceHumanActivity`, and `trackedObjects` with `inspected` flags (auto-marked when an object is framed near center at `area ≥ 0.08`).
- Resets sector placement automatically when `tipped` or `rapid_movement` indicates the robot was picked up and relocated.
- Feeds Ainekio OS's **System 1 single-hop Embodied Curiosity** (`embodied-curiosity.ts`), allowing Full Auto mode to turn toward uninspected sectors, step into open floor clearance, and escalate genuine visual discoveries to System 2 memory and speech.
