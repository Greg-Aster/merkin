# Eight servos versus twelve

V2 changes the mechanism, controller and power arrangement. The third actuator
on each leg adds sideways placement; the new software can vary a gait while it
runs. This comparison separates those implemented capabilities from the
physical results still to be measured. **Build options, controls and power are
updated through October 14. The downloadable motion-range dataset remains
the September 24 snapshot**, before the later gait and chassis changes.

**V2 can now stand, walk and respond to commands**, according to the owner's
October 8 report. At that time its carapace was not attached and Active Operator
had not been tested. Exterior printing has since begun; no newer physical
Active Operator result was supplied. This supersedes the [October 6 standing failure](/posts/2026-10-06-lighter-rebuild/)
as current status. The earlier 20% weight/lifting-force estimates remain
estimates. The October 13 owner report adds 566 g mass and 5 V supply for that assembly; it does not measure a before/after lifting-force improvement.

**Wireless**, with the Q6A offboard over Wi-Fi, is the current priority.
**Wired** retains the onboard-Q6A/native-USB alternative. The
[software update](/posts/2026-10-06-steering-sensing-connections/) compares their
status and separates implemented controls from hardware qualification.

## Mechanics and motion capability

| Comparison | V1 · eight servos | V2 · twelve servos |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Actuators per leg | Two MG90S | Three MG90-family servos; the owner is retaining MG90 for the lighter revision |
| Mechanism | Sesame-derived leg and open chassis | Shoulder roll plus coupled carrier/crank linkage; purpose-built supports, shell and underside |
| Foot placement | Primarily forward/backward and up/down | Adds sideways placement and variable stance width |
| Motion generation | Stored poses and timed sequences; 38 named assets | Continuous Walk, Run, Crawl and six-direction Crab, plus 23 declared gestures/postures |
| Speed controls | Timing of existing sequences | Gait Speed or advanced stride/rate; separate named-motion multiplier; shared joint-speed limit can retime automatic motion while retaining the path |
| Turns | Stored 45°, 90° and 180° left/right clips; open-loop heading estimates | Continuous forward/turn updates during an admitted Walk; loaded steering remains to measure |
| Expressive movement | Wave, bow, dances and other prepared gestures | Retargeted gestures plus reviewed Worm, seated Shrug, Play Dead, Lay Down and experimental Upright |
| Balance and terrain | No measured adaptive balance result | Live IMU telemetry and configurable local leveling implemented; loaded correction and adaptive terrain response remain unqualified |
| Physical evidence | Working complete prototype; movement and audio demonstrated | October 8 owner reports standing, walking and command response; illuminated LCD visible, carapace not attached, Active Operator untested |

The counts describe different things: one V2 gait can continue indefinitely and
change settings, while a V1 asset is a finite stored sequence. Twenty-three
finite gestures therefore does not mean V2 has fewer movement options.

## Movement ranges: compare the quantities first

**Dataset date: September 24.** The numbers below are preserved for comparison;
they have not been regenerated for the later controller revisions or the
October 5–6 compact-linkage candidate. The
[latest motion entry](/posts/2026-10-06-compact-linkage/)
compares that candidate with the shared model and records its changed poses.

**A commanded angle is not a measured mechanical limit.** V1 uses logical
controller angles. V2 uses signed Blender angles for three coupled inputs. Their
zeros, joint axes and mechanisms differ; subtracting the numbers does not give
a meaningful percentage increase in reachable workspace.

| Range or movement | V1 | V2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Software angle convention | Logical 0–180°; individual assets use subsets | Signed Blender angles for shoulder, carrier and crank, followed by per-joint calibration |
| Stored/sample envelope | Combined explicit targets span 0–180° across the eight channels | Shoulder −96.99 to +17.57°; carrier −79.02 to +135.37°; crank −128.11 to +101.29° |
| Walking data | V1 forward-walk targets use 45–90° excursions on individual channels; detailed table in the journal | September 24 sampled forward-walk recording: shoulder −4.47 to +4.25°, carrier −46.05 to +22.88°, crank −107.48 to +29.56° |
| Sideways travel | No dedicated third leg actuator; no measured lateral workspace supplied | Crab planner specifies 32 mm sideways travel per full-stride cycle |
| Foot travel in walking | No measured distance-per-cycle result in this review | 92 mm full planted sweep; nominal 131.43 mm advance per cycle at full stride |
| Whole-body usable limits | Installed stops, loaded workspace and travel measurements not supplied | Shoulder/body clearance, tracking, load and all-settings motion envelope remain unqualified |

The September 24 V2 envelope above was extracted from **23 declared gesture sources,
14 locomotion recordings and the Run recording**, pooling all four legs by
input type. It excludes the retired fixed-angle turn recordings. Its shoulder
maximum therefore differs from older audits that included those turns.
The forward-walk row includes its recorded Speed changes, advanced settings and
Finish; it is not the extrema of every possible walking control setting.

[Download the per-recording, per-joint CSV](/posts/2026-09-24-progress/motion-ranges.csv)
and its [read-only extraction script](/posts/2026-09-24-progress/extract-motion-ranges.py).
The CSV includes source hashes, preserves the two angle conventions and also
lists all V1 explicit target ranges. The [gait journal](/posts/gait-development/)
retains the original V1 channel table and earlier linkage studies.

### The October 5–6 compact candidate

The shared model at `13fc3e4` and the offline candidate are distinct:

| Dimension | Shared model | Compact candidate |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Carrier | 40 mm | 35 mm |
| Crank | 24 mm | 20 mm |
| Pickup radius | 24 mm | 28 mm |
| Connecting rod | 38 mm | 38 mm |
| Distal leg | 55 mm | 55 mm |

The candidate remaps all 23 named motions. Its selected Upright fit reaches
**76.585 mm**, compared with **90.339 mm** in the baseline. Combined front-crank
travel is **243.868°**, exceeding the **225°** span in the provisional servo
mapping used by that study. Usable physical shaft travel remains unmeasured.
These are pose/model comparisons, not approved mechanical limits. The candidate
has not replaced the active shared model or been installed in firmware.

Earlier pulse-endpoint and assembly-mark studies retain their own dates in
the [gait journal](/posts/gait-development/). They should not be combined into
a new calibration instruction without checking the actual installed assembly.

## Controller and processing

| Comparison | V1 | V2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Board | Freenove ESP32-S3-WROOM CAM, N16R8 | Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6; tested P4 revision 1.3 |
| Main processor | Dual-core Xtensa LX7 | Dual-core high-performance RISC-V |
| Checked firmware clock | **160 MHz** in local S3 configuration | **360 MHz** in local P4 configuration |
| Flash / PSRAM | 16 MB / 8 MB | 32 MB / 32 MB |
| Wireless | S3's own 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi | Separate ESP32-C6 coprocessor over SDIO |
| Servo signals | Eight direct hardware PWM outputs | PCA9685 driver for the twelve channels |
| Camera/audio hardware | OV3660 parallel camera; external microphone and I2S amplifier | Kit specifies OV5647 MIPI camera; onboard codec/audio path; installed peripheral operation needs its own evidence |
| Companion computer | MetaHuman OS on a separate computer | Q6A onboard for Wired or offboard for Wireless; QCS6490 eight-core CPU, Adreno 643 and Hexagon; audited unit has 11.288 GiB Linux-visible RAM |
| ROS 2 | No V1 integration in this review | Jazzy installed on Q6A; desktop messaging, recording and RViz checks recorded; robot adapter remains planned |
| Conversation and reasoning | MetaHuman OS on the host | MetaHuman OS remains separate from P4 motion deadlines; ROS does not replace it |

Processor families and board interfaces follow
[Espressif's S3 description](https://www.espressif.com/en/products/socs/esp32-s3)
and the [Waveshare board documentation](https://docs.waveshare.com/ESP32-P4-WIFI6).
Memory and clock values above also follow the local board records and checked
`sdkconfig` files. The S3 silicon supports up to 240 MHz; this checkout selects
160 MHz. These clock rates and memory capacities are **not an application
benchmark** or evidence of longer battery life.

The P4 solves the leg linkage directly and plays compact gesture curves through
one output controller. The September 28 website's deployment record reports a
**2,370,848-byte application**, verified flash and boots, retained twelve-joint
calibration and saved motion speed after reboot. Hardware checks cover startup
Home and settings; loaded twelve-servo timing, tracking and power remain
separate measurements.

The Q6A figures come from the September 28
[resource audit](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot/blob/270537fa56c8d77efd72ff694f4294d15381de52/docs/v2-12servo/RESOURCE_BUDGET.md).
More RAM and different processors allow different workloads, but their
specifications are not a measured speedup over V1. [Live IMU telemetry and saved
leveling controls](/posts/2026-10-14-startup-and-leveling/) now have dated firmware
installation records. Loaded leveling, the ROS adapter and native-USB body path
remain unqualified. [SD video and Wi-Fi maintenance](/posts/2026-10-14-recording-and-updates/)
are additional P4 features, with controller tests separated from loaded motion.

## Power supply, battery, charging and running time

**October 12:** the owner has switched to a **4 A power supply**. This rating
is not a battery capacity, measured consumption or runtime. The October 13
owner report specifies **5 V**; the complete installed rail arrangement remains
to document. The earlier battery decisions
below remain part of the iteration record.

| Comparison | V1 | V2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current supply report | Original LiPo arrangement | 4 A supply, owner-reported October 12; voltage unspecified |
| Battery | 1,000 mAh LiPo, owner-reported | October 6 separate-cell/charging-board direction; final untethered arrangement after the supply change remains undocumented |
| Voltage / energy record | Earlier parts record: 2S, 7.4 V; nominal 7.4 Wh by multiplication | New pack voltage and usable energy not yet documented |
| Conversion arrangement | External buck converter | Final power path to be documented; the earlier no-separate-buck bank plan is historical |
| Charging | External charger | Onboard charging circuit boards planned; exact boards and implementation not yet documented |
| Packaging | Existing robot arrangement | Exterior printing begun; owner reports 566 g on October 13, with final shell configuration still to document |
| Build-dependent battery load | Existing V1 body | Wired carries Q6A load; Wireless powers Q6A separately offboard |
| Measured run time | **Not measured** | **Not measured for either option** |
| Measured loaded consumption | Not supplied | Whole-system consumption and twelve-servo peaks still to measure |

The revised V2 battery capacity is unspecified, so there is no current
capacity-ratio comparison. A useful runtime estimate requires
usable delivered energy in watt-hours and average whole-robot power:
**runtime in hours ≈ delivered Wh ÷ average W**. Idle, walking and expressive
motions need separately identified test conditions.

The [September 24 Anker study](/posts/2026-09-24-body-and-battery/) and
[September 28 SHARGE budget](/posts/2026-09-28-wired-wireless-ros2/) preserve
the earlier removable-bank iterations, including the proposed two-port
5 V / 3 A branch allocation. That allocation is historical, not a specification
for the new batteries and charging boards. The
[October 6 rebuild](/posts/2026-10-06-lighter-rebuild/) records why the bank
was dropped and what remains to be selected and measured.
