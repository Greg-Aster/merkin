Ainekio has been developed across two physical quadruped generations. **V1** established the end-to-end voice, vision, gateway, and MetaHuman OS loop on a compact eight-servo, open-loop `ESP32-S3` chassis derived from the open-source Sesame robot. **V2** is a complete mechanical, electrical, and control redesign around the **`Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6`**, adding lateral hip roll joints (twelve servos total), compact five-bar/four-bar leg linkages, 4th-wire potentiometer feedback on every actuator, a `208 Hz` IMU, a 1.9-inch color face LCD, and closed-loop / neural locomotion.

## Mechanical & motion architecture

| Feature | V1 · Eight-Servo Baseline | V2 · Twelve-Servo Closed-Loop Body |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Actuators** | 8× `MG90S` (2 per leg, open-loop PWM) | 12× Miuzei `MG90S` (3 per leg) modified with 4th-wire potentiometer wiper feedback |
| **Leg mechanism** | Direct planar joints in an open Sesame-derived chassis | Shoulder roll (`±h`) + compact planar linkage (`35 mm` carrier, `20 mm` crank, `28 mm` pickup, `38 mm` rod, `55 mm` lower leg) |
| **Assembled mass & envelope** | Open-frame micro quadruped | `650 g` complete; `245.85 × 182.79 × 109.06 mm` (`L × W × H`) |
| **Foot workspace** | 2D fore/aft and vertical arc per leg | 3D Cartesian workspace per leg with `0.8.150` boundary projection and adjustable stance width |
| **Position verification** | None (open-loop elapsed timer) | 12-channel `CD74HC4067` analog multiplexer into P4 ADC; `2 mm` `Stand` settle and verified arrival |
| **Inertial sensing** | None | Onboard `LSM6DS3` 6-axis IMU (`208 Hz`), stationary gyro bias calibration, local attitude self-leveling, and tip/shake events |
| **Motion repertoire** | 38 fixed `.amot` keyframe assets (`walk_forward`, fixed turns, emotes) | Continuous analytic solver (`Walk`, `Run`, `Crawl`, 6-dir `Crab`), 23 compiled gestures, **349-example SQLite Freestyle library**, **`50 Hz` neural policy (`walk_nominal_v1`)**, and **MuJoCo MJX RL** |

## Controller, compute, and peripherals

| Subsystem | V1 (`ESP32-S3`) | V2 (`ESP32-P4` + `C6`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Controller board** | Freenove `ESP32-S3-WROOM CAM` (`N16R8`) | `Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6` (Rev 1.3) |
| **Main CPU & clock** | Dual-core Xtensa LX7 @ `160 MHz` | Dual-core RISC-V @ `360 MHz` |
| **Flash / PSRAM** | `16 MB` Flash / `8 MB` Octal PSRAM | `32 MB` NOR Flash / `32 MB` PSRAM |
| **Wireless** | Integrated 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi | Dedicated onboard `ESP32-C6` coprocessor over SDIO |
| **Servo PWM generation** | 8× direct `MCPWM` GPIO outputs | `PCA9685` 16-channel 12-bit I2C PWM driver with staggered phase offsets |
| **Face display** | 0.96" `SSD1306` monochrome I2C OLED (`128 × 64`) | 1.9" `ST7789` color IPS SPI LCD (`320 × 170`, 58 animated expressions) |
| **Camera** | `OV3660` DVP parallel camera (`XGA` stills) | `OV5647` MIPI-CSI camera (180° inverted mount, `10,000 µs` motion-safe exposure ceiling, SD video recording) |
| **Audio & wake word** | External `INMP441` mic + `MAX98357A` I2S amp; experimental TFLite Micro pilot | Onboard `ES8311` codec + `NS4150B` amp, `20 × 30 mm` speaker, **Espressif `WakeNet9s`**, 5 VAD modes, `15 s` window, `external_speech_ms` TTS suppression |
| **Host gateway & brain** | Desktop running `Master/gateway` + MetaHuman OS | Desktop or **Radxa Dragon Q6A** (`QCS6490`) running Body Control + unified `llama-cpp.service` (`:8080`) |

## Power, battery, and charging

Both generations isolate high-current `5 V` motor power from logic power while sharing a common ground reference:

| Power Component | V1 Configuration | V2 Rear Two-Level Power Carrier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Battery pack** | Sea Jump `2S` (`7.4 V` nominal), `1000 mAh` LiPo | `2S` (`8.4 V` max) Li-ion pack (`73.6 × 46 × 22 mm` envelope) |
| **Voltage regulation** | Dual `Mini560` `5 V / 5 A` buck converters | `Mini560` `5 V / 5 A` step-down converter feeding the `PCA9685` servo rail |
| **Charging** | External or `HiLetgo` 2S USB-C boost charger | Integrated `HiLetgo` 2S USB-C boost charger in the rear printed carrier (earlier Anker and SHARGE power banks retired) |

## Commanded joint range comparison

Because V1 uses logical `0–180°` direct servo targets and V2 uses signed Blender geometric angles (`h`, `alpha`, `theta`) mapped through coupled four-bar/five-bar linkages, their raw angle spans represent different physical coordinates:

| Metric | V1 (8 Logical Channels) | V2 (12 Coupled Linkage Channels) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Angle convention** | Logical `0–180°` around `90°` neutral | Signed geometric offsets (`h`, `alpha`, `theta`) from CAD neutral, mapped via per-joint calibration |
| **Full gesture/gait envelope** | `0–180°` across all 38 stored `.amot` assets | Shoulder `h`: `-96.99° to +17.57°`; Carrier `alpha`: `-79.02° to +135.37°`; Crank `theta`: `-128.11° to +101.29°` |
| **Forward walk excursion** | `45–90°` channel excursions (`920 ms` 6-group cycle) | `92–96 mm` planted foot sweep (`131.43 mm` nominal body advance per full-stride cycle) |
| **Lateral travel** | None (2-DOF planar legs) | `32 mm` sideways travel per full-stride `Crab` cycle + dynamic roll leveling |
