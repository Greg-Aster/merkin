Ainekio ([`Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot`](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot)) runs across **two active, functioning physical quadruped generations**—with both robots under active improvement and a third industrial armored Linux SBC generation modeled in `537`-mesh Blender CAD ([Milestone #20](/posts/2026-11-20-clockspring-arms-phantom-jams-opt-a1-and-megameal-sim/)):

- **[Ainekio V1](/posts/ainekio-v1/)** (Active 8-Servo Robot) runs a functioning eight-servo `ESP32-S3` spider-stance floor body with onboard `OV3660` vision, I2S room audio, a `0.96"` `SSD1306` OLED (**`59` V2-derived procedural faces / `303` frames** via `derive_v1_oled.py`), 38 `.amot` motions, and **[Ainekio OS](/posts/ainekio-os/)** ([`Greg-Aster/ainekio-os`](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/ainekio-os)) over `protocol-v1`—and is actively advancing through its **`Scene 'v1'` hardware & CAD upgrade (`174` active meshes, `174.8 × 155.5 × 84.1 mm` neutral CAD envelope)** adding a lowered `Frame8` enclosure cap (`121.7 × 79.7 × 22.0 mm`) with corner clock-spring clearance bays, squared vertical-knee `R2V10` upper arms (`36.17 × 36.17 × 37.50 mm`), unified cobalt-blue sculpted spiked lower legs (`39.30 × 5.30 × 79.17 mm`), concentric 3-part coaxial under-arm 4-wire rotary clock-springs, `4× 683ZZ` hip counter-bearings, and an `ESP32-P4-WIFI6` + `PCA9685` + 8× 4th-wire feedback + `GY-LSM6DS3` + `Arducam B0066` deck.
- **[Ainekio V2](/posts/ainekio-v2/)** (Active 12-Servo Robot) is a `650 g` mechanical, electrical, and control build around the **`Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6`**, adding lateral hip roll joints (twelve servos total), compact `35/20/28/38/55 mm` four-bar leg linkages with curved rocking soles, 4th-wire potentiometer feedback (`CD74HC4067` `C4–C15` → `GPIO49`, `S0–S3` on `GPIO27–30`), a `208 Hz` IMU, a 1.9-inch **59-expression** color LCD, open **`microWakeWord` + WebRTC VAD** audio, an **`8–10 s` `I²t` winding thermal fuse**, a `2S` **Wireless Keeper Bridge**, and a commercial-clean **3-system neural motion stack (`50 Hz` on-chip `MicroDuck v19-snag-p4` + `50 Hz` `NeuralPose v8` across `939` clean Freestyle rows + `50 Hz` `NeuralPosePresence`)**.
- **[Ainekio V3](/posts/ainekio-v3-preview/)** (In Development) scales up into a **`537`-mesh industrial armored `DS3235` CAD assembly (`402.0 × 236.5 × 343.1 mm` w/ whip antenna; `~260.6 mm` to ROPS/cargo roofline)** with **`Option A-1` (`54 / 22 / 46 / 56 / 135 mm`, `-35°` angled bellcrank)** compact high-torque 5-link pantograph crawler legs (`+120%` vertical lift, `+116%` running thrust, `0.93:1` dual-servo torque balance, `-48.6 mm` shorter rear overhang), a continuous `3,860`-vert armored belly chassis tub housing a low-CG `4× 21700` (`2S2P`) pack, recessed `AINEKIO` / `V-003` armor badging, a **recessed `2.8"` `MSP2807` (`ILI9341`) vault TFT behind a swept ROPS bull-bar**, a functional `Clip 26` leaf-spring clamshell latch + `72 mm` `Ø2.0 mm`-pin rear piano hinge, and an **onboard `Radxa Dragon Q6A` Linux SBC + `ESP32-P4-WIFI6`** inside the hull.

| Feature | V1 · Active 8-Servo Quadruped & `Scene 'v1'` Upgrade | V2 · Active 12-Servo Closed-Loop Body |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Actuators** | 8× `MG90S` (2 per leg; open-loop PWM on S3 physical build; vertically mounted knees + 8× 4th-wire `J0–J7` via coaxial under-arm clock-springs in Nov 20 `Scene 'v1'` CAD) | 12× Miuzei `MG90S` (3 per leg) modified with 4th-wire potentiometer wiper feedback |
| **Leg mechanism & bearings** | Planar hip sweep (`4× 683ZZ` counter-bearings) + squared vertical-knee `R2V10` arms (`36.17 × 36.17 × 37.50 mm`), concentric 3-part under-arm 4-wire clock-springs, and unified sculpted spiked lower legs (`39.30 × 5.30 × 79.17 mm`) in `Frame7`/`Frame8` | Shoulder roll (`±h`) + compact four-bar linkage (`35 mm` carrier, `20 mm` crank, `28 mm` pickup, `38 mm` rod, `55 mm` lower leg) + curved rocking soles |
| **Assembled mass & envelope** | Micro 8-servo quadruped (`174` active meshes, `174.8 × 155.5 × 84.1 mm` neutral CAD envelope in Nov 20 `Scene 'v1'`) | `650 g` complete; `245.85 × 182.79 × 109.06 mm` (`L × W × H`) |
| **Foot workspace** | Coupled horizontal hip arc + vertical knee fold per leg | 3D Cartesian workspace per leg with 8-pass Gauss-Newton boundary projection and adjustable stance width |
| **Position verification & jam safety** | None on Summer 2026 S3 floor build (8× 4th-wire sense leads coiled inside under-arm clock-springs in `Scene 'v1'` CAD) | `CD74HC4067` multiplexer **`C4–C15`** → `ESP32-P4` **`GPIO49` (`ADC2_CH0`)** (`S0–S3` on `GPIO27–30`); `2 mm` `Stand` settle + **[`8–10 s` per-joint `I²t` thermal fuse](/posts/jam-protection-and-thermal-safety/)** |
| **Inertial sensing** | None on Summer 2026 S3 floor build (`GY-LSM6DS3` on `I2C0` in `Scene 'v1'` CAD) | Onboard `LSM6DS3` 6-axis IMU (`208 Hz`), stationary gyro bias calibration, local attitude self-leveling, and tip/shake events |
| **Motion repertoire** | 38 fixed `.amot` keyframe assets (`walk_forward`, timed turns, 22 emotes/dances) + `motion_plan_v1` (`1–32` steps) | **On-chip `50 Hz` `MicroDuck v19-snag-p4`** (`107.8 KB` L2-cached RL gait), **`50 Hz` `NeuralPose v8`** (`neural-pose-v8-calibrated` + **`939`-row** clean Freestyle library & `Qwen3.5-0.8B` LoRA), **`50 Hz` `NeuralPosePresence`**, continuous analytic gaits (`Walk`, `Run`, `Crawl`, `Crab`), and `8–10 s` `I²t` jam protection |

## Controller, compute, and peripherals

| Subsystem | V1 (Summer 2026 `ESP32-S3` · Nov 20 `Scene 'v1'` CAD) | V2 (`ESP32-P4` + `C6`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Controller board** | Physical: Freenove `ESP32-S3-WROOM CAM` (`N16R8`) · `Scene 'v1'` CAD: `Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6` | `Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6` (Rev 1.3) |
| **Main CPU & cache** | Dual-core Xtensa LX7 @ `160 MHz` (S3 build) / Dual-core RISC-V @ `360 MHz` (`Scene 'v1'` CAD) | Dual-core RISC-V @ `360 MHz` (`128 KB` L2 cache running Core 1 `50 Hz` `int8` `MicroDuck v19-snag-p4`) |
| **Flash / PSRAM** | `16 MiB` Flash / `8 MiB` Octal PSRAM (`GPIO33–37` reserved on S3) | `32 MB` NOR Flash / `32 MB` PSRAM |
| **Wireless** | Integrated 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi (S3) / `ESP32-C6` Wi-Fi 6 (`Scene 'v1'` CAD) | Dedicated onboard `ESP32-C6` Wi-Fi 6 / Bluetooth coprocessor over SDIO |
| **Servo PWM generation** | 8× direct `MCPWM` GPIO outputs on S3 build (`PCA9685` on `I2C1+OE` in `Scene 'v1'` CAD) | `PCA9685` 16-channel 12-bit I2C PWM driver with staggered phase offsets & selective jam cutoff |
| **Face display** | `0.96"` `SSD1306` monochrome I2C OLED (`128 × 64`, **`59` V2-derived 1-bit expressions / `303` frames (`faces-v1.json`)** via `derive_v1_oled.py` `2.5×` isotropic crop + `talk` overlay) | `1.9"` `ST7789` color IPS SPI LCD (`320 × 170`, **59 animated expressions**) |
| **Camera** | Physical: `OV3660` DVP camera (`XGA` `1024×768` stills, `1/60 s`) · `Scene 'v1'` CAD: `Arducam B0066` MIPI-CSI | Chin-mounted `OV5647` MIPI-CSI camera below the LCD (`180°` inverted mount, `10,000 µs` motion-safe exposure ceiling, microSD clip recording) |
| **Audio & wake word** | Physical: `INMP441` mic + `MAX98357A` I2S amp + **`vadnet1_medium`** VAD (`20×30 mm` speaker + cradle in `Scene 'v1'` CAD) | Onboard `ES8311` codec + `NS4150B` amp, `20 × 30 mm` speaker, open **`microWakeWord` (`int8` TFLite Micro) + WebRTC VAD** (plus `WakeNet9s`), `15 s` window, self-speech suppression |
| **Host gateway & mind** | Desktop running `Master/gateway` + Ainekio OS (formerly MetaHuman OS) | Desktop or **Radxa Dragon Q6A** running Body Control / Cockpit HUD + **[Ainekio OS](/posts/ainekio-os/)** (`Greg-Aster/ainekio-os`) |

## Power, battery, and charging

Both generations isolate high-current `5 V` motor power from logic power while sharing a common ground reference:

| Power Component | V1 Configuration | V2 Wireless Keeper Bridge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Battery pack** | Sea Jump `2S` (`7.4 V` nominal), `1000 mAh` `10C` **Li-ion** (`2× 14500`, `SM-3P`; dual flank packs in `Scene 'v1'` CAD) | `2S` (`8.4 V` max) **Li-ion** pack (`73.6 × 46 × 22 mm` envelope) seated beneath the battery-mounted **Wireless Keeper Bridge** |
| **Voltage regulation** | Physical S3 build: dual `MINI560 PRO` `5 V / 5 A` bucks (`1000 µF` servo cap + logic rail) · `Scene 'v1'` CAD: `Mini560` `5 V / 5 A` buck feeding `PCA9685` | `Mini560` `5 V / 5 A` step-down converter feeding the `PCA9685` servo rail |
| **Charging & retention** | `HiLetgo` `2S` USB-C boost charger + master rocker switch (`KCD1` physical / `19×12 mm` in `Scene 'v1'`) + `GPIO3` telemetry-only ADC divider | Integrated `HiLetgo` `2S` USB-C boost charger + snap-in `ESP32-P4`, `PCA9685`, `HiLetgo`, and `LSM6DS3` PCB clips (retiring earlier Anker and SHARGE power banks) |

## Commanded joint range comparison

Because V1 uses logical `0–180°` direct servo targets and V2 uses signed Blender geometric angles (`h`, `alpha`, `theta`) mapped through coupled four-bar linkages, their raw angle spans represent different physical coordinates:

| Metric | V1 (8 Logical Channels) | V2 (12 Coupled Linkage Channels) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Angle convention** | Logical `0–180°` around `90°` (`1500 µs`) neutral | Signed geometric offsets (`h`, `alpha`, `theta`) from CAD neutral, mapped via per-joint calibration |
| **Full gesture/gait envelope** | `0–180°` across all 38 stored `.amot` assets | Shoulder `h`: `-96.99° to +17.57°`; Carrier `alpha`: `-79.02° to +135.37°`; Crank `theta`: `-128.11° to +101.29°` |
| **Forward walk excursion** | `45–90°` channel excursions (`920 ms` 6-group cycle) | `92–96 mm` planted foot sweep (`131.43 mm` nominal analytic cycle; up to `+0.213 m/s` under on-chip `50 Hz` MicroDuck gait) |
| **Lateral travel** | None (2-DOF planar legs) | `32 mm` sideways travel per full-stride `Crab` cycle + `±0.08 m/s` neural strafe + dynamic roll leveling |
