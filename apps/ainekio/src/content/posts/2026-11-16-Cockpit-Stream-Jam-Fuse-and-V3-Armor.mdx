Between November 12 and November 16, work across [`Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot`](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot), [`Greg-Aster/ainekio-os`](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/ainekio-os), and the active Blender `V3` scene (`v3-2.blend`) closed out five major engineering tracks:

1. **[Ainekio Cockpit](/posts/ainekio-cockpit/)** gained one-tap **`Tap to Talk`** wake-word bypass, 10 immediate-apply runtime toggles, and a translucent **live Ainekio OS message stream** fed by a durable `Body Control Text Out` node across 7 cognitive graphs.
2. **[Three-System Neural Motion](/posts/three-system-neural-architecture/)** expanded beyond walking (`MicroDuck v18` System-ID & `v19-snag-p4` swing-foot snag reflex + `ESP32-P4` RISC-V PIE coprocessor fixes) to include `50 Hz` delay-tolerant `NeuralPose` (`1,812`-example Freestyle curriculum) and **`20 Hz` System 3 Autonomic Living Presence** (`56 KB` `int8`, `99.69%` expression accuracy).
3. **[Recovery-First Jam Protection & Per-Joint `I²t` Winding Fuse](/posts/jam-protection-and-thermal-safety/)** replaced fragile LLM stall re-prompting with deterministic `280 ms` pose recoil, `1.8 s` reverse walking unsnag, `800 ms` deadlock-bounded arrival waits, and an `8–10 s` per-joint `PCA9685` thermal cutoff that leaves the 11 healthy servos holding the robot upright.
4. **[Ainekio OS](/posts/ainekio-os/)** added **8-sector single-hop Embodied Curiosity**, `MoGe-2` `8×8` `< 0.30 m` forward corridor braking, `ExecutionStore` v2 fast synchronous `JsonPlus` checkpointing, pre-warmed `GeminiAcpServer` (`gemini-4-hydrogen-low`), and VRAM-aware `Kokoro` CUDA↔CPU auto-switching during GPU training runs.
5. **[Ainekio V3](/posts/ainekio-v3-preview/)** underwent a full heavy-equipment industrial CAD redesign in Blender (`440.8 × 234.4 × 307.8 mm` ROPS body / `381.4 mm` with whip antenna), pairing `12×` waterproof `DS3235` servos and shrouded `87.7 / 40.0 / 93.6 / 158.4 mm` excavator legs with a recessed `2.8"` `MSP2807` vault screen, `Radxa Dragon Q6A` SBC, `ESP32-P4`, and `4× 21700` belly pack.

## 2. Three-system neural stack (`v18` Sys-ID, `v19` snag plant, `NeuralPose` & System 3 Presence)

Three untethered November 12 floor runs on `ainekio-p4-e6ebe5` (`220 MB` of `50 Hz` telemetry) directly drove the next two on-chip `MicroDuck` generations, an ESP-IDF RISC-V vector build patch, and a third neural controller dedicated to conversational body language:

![Three-System Neural Motion Architecture and ESP32-P4 RISC-V PIE build patch](/posts/2026-11-16-progress/three-system-neural-stack.webp)

- **`microduck-v18-p4` & `microduck-v19-snag-p4`:** Modeling measured `-19.1°` `J3` shoulder compliance and `41–45%` `RL` gain sag lifted the 10-scenario composite score from `-1.2871` (`1` fall) to **`+1.3246` (`0` falls)** (`+69.5%` weak-RL speed, `+79.7%` over `14–17 mm` cables). `v19-snag-p4` adds a `500 Hz` unilateral `22–27 mm` swing-foot snag wrench (`28%` episode probability) so the `107.84 KiB` L2-cached policy reflexively high-steps over cords and rug lips.
- **`ESP32-P4` RISC-V PIE Coprocessor Patch (`idf_pie_coproc.cmake`):** Fixes `esp.lp.setup` hardware-loop interrupt PC corruption (`cpair = 99`), lazy FreeRTOS `CSR 0x7f2` ownership, and omitted `q3` / swapped `SAR` / 8-byte `RV_PIE_XACC` context-frame bugs inside `${CMAKE_BINARY_DIR}/ainekio-idf` with zero SDK mutation.
- **System 2 (`NeuralPose` + `1,812`-row Freestyle) & System 3 (`20 Hz` Living Presence):** `freestyle_curriculum.py` unified `1,812` motion and recovery examples (adding unloaded rear-leg `sit_brace_enter/exit` for seated two-paw poses) behind `neural-pose-v2-delay` (`0.52°–1.33°` MAE @ `180 ms` Wi-Fi delay) and `v3-jam-compliant`. Between commands, **`ainekio-presence-v1`** (`56,094` params / `56 KB` `int8`, `99.69%` expression accuracy) drives `±8 mm` speech-RMS body motion and `±1.8 mm` stance chatter-relief trims while staying **100% silent during microphone VAD capture**. Full details in [Three-System Neural Motion Architecture](/posts/three-system-neural-architecture/).

## 3. 3-tier recovery-first jam protection & per-joint `I²t` winding thermal fuse

After diagnosing a `~156 s` bench stall on `rear_left_shoulder` (`3.5–4.25 W` / `~2.5–2.8 °C/s` winding rise), jam handling was rebuilt from end to end around non-collapsing hardware and closed-loop reflexes:

![3-Tier Jam Protection and Per-Joint I²t Winding Thermal Fuse](/posts/2026-11-16-progress/jam-protection-and-thermal-fuse.webp)

- **`280 ms` pose recoil & `1.8 s` walking reverse unsnag:** `NeuralPose` backs obstructed joints `2.0°` onto the four-bar manifold after `14` ticks (`280 ms`) and runs an automatic `0.85 s` Return-to-Stand if a final pose jams; walking controllers on both P4 C firmware and the Host Gateway execute a `1.8 s` `-0.45 m/s` reverse unsnag (`5.5 s` Ainekio OS goal window).
- **Full-span `[1..4094]` ADC extrapolation, `800 ms` deadlock bounds & `8–10 s` `I²t` cutoff:** Extrapolates 3-point `CD74HC4067` curves to catch extreme end-stop jams, bounds all four firmware arrival-wait paths (`800 ms`, `STAND_MAX_REPLANS = 2`), and integrates locked-rotor heat above a `7.0°` deadband (`0.0` heat forever under normal standing sag)—cutting `PCA9685 LEDn_OFF_H = 0x10` **only on the jammed servo** at `8–10 s` so the 11 healthy servos hold stance. Verified across 5 hardware-in-the-loop suites in `tests/test_p4_jam_and_thermal.py`; see [Jam Protection & Winding Thermal Fuse](/posts/jam-protection-and-thermal-safety/).

## 4. Ainekio OS embodied curiosity, `ExecutionStore` v2 & V2 STL refinements

Alongside the robot runtime changes, six commits (`2cdd0674` → `95658151`) and today's bridge updates landed in **[Ainekio OS](/posts/ainekio-os/)**:

- **System 1 / System 2 Embodied Curiosity (`embodied-curiosity.ts`):** Consumes `Master/gateway`'s 8-sector `SpatialCuriosity` map (`8 × 45°` IMU yaw sectors, `YOLOE` objects, `freeFloorMeters`) to run a **single-hop System 1 physical instinct** (`precomputedEnvironmentPlan` bypassing Intent/Task LLM hops) and escalates visual discoveries to **System 2** (`environment.curiosity-escalation`) for episodic memory, Desire updates, and speech.
- **`MoGe-2` `8×8` `< 0.30 m` corridor brake & retired `robotCommand` shim:** `summarizeDepthSpatialClearance()` halts forward distance goals immediately when center corridor clearance drops below `0.30 m`, and legacy `robotCommand` catalogs were retired in favor of unified `move` (`locomotionGoal`), `requestMotion`, and `stop` (`detach: true` supported for full PWM release).
- **Runtime speedups & VRAM-aware `Kokoro` TTS:** `ExecutionStore` v2 replaced async LangChain serialization with synchronous `JsonPlus` + prepared-statement caching; `GeminiAcpServer` (`gemini-4-hydrogen-low` default) pre-warms its 2-session pool on startup and purges closed session artifacts; and `kokoro_server.py` checks `nvidia-smi` (`< 2560 MiB` free VRAM or active LoRA/RL training processes) to switch dynamically between CUDA and CPU ONNX sessions.
- **Repository consolidation, licensing & 17 V2 Red STLs:** Retired `2.1M` lines of legacy `Emulator`/`Sesame` code, published CAD and model weights in [`Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot`](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot) under `PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0` (code/weights) and `CC BY-NC-SA 4.0` (hardware/docs), and exported **17 updated V2 Red STLs** (`Slave/hardware/v2-12servo/STL/Red/`: `38 mm` styled rods, `35 mm` `4.9 mm` outside-horn plain-bore carriers, carapace side protectors, `Mini560`/charger retainers, compact front panel, and `MG90S` lower housing).
