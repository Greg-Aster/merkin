Ainekio is a quadruped robot companion combining a 3D-printed, twelve-servo closed-loop body ([`Ainekio-bot`](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot)) with **[Ainekio OS](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/ainekio-os)** (formerly MetaHuman OS; [system reference](/posts/ainekio-os/)) for conversation, persistent memory, perception, personality, and autonomous task planning. The physical `ESP32-P4` body handles real-time motor control, `50 Hz` on-chip neural gait execution, joint-position verification, inertial sensing, vision capture, wake-word listening, speaker playback, and facial expression, while Ainekio OS and the host gateway coordinate cognition, locomotion training, and long-horizon goals.

## What Ainekio is built to do

Ainekio is designed as a persistent robot familiar with continuity across interactions:

| Function | Role in the system |
| --- | --- |
| **Conversation & Standing Persona** | Maintain a consistent identity (`persona/core.json`), converse naturally across web, CLI, Android, or voice, and ground replies in current context and action outcomes |
| **Perception & Expression** | Connect camera observations, optical-flow obstacle range, wake-gated speech, and IMU/joint sensing to spoken responses, 59 animated LCD faces, and physical posture |
| **Persistent Memory** | Retain episodic context and long-term experiences with clear source attribution, distinguishing direct observation from internal imagination |
| **Locomotion & Freestyle Motion** | Execute closed-loop standing, continuous gaits (`Walk`, `Run`, `Crawl`, `Crab`), on-chip `50 Hz` `107.8 KB` MicroDuck neural walking (`0.8.159-p4-microduck-v17`), host Pupper V3 MJX policies, and 349+ Freestyle motion programs (`489` training rows with native-`stand` recovery) |
| **Bounded Autonomy** | Operate across 4 Cognitive Modes (`Dual`, `Agent`, `Emulation`, `Environment`) and 3 Robot Autonomy Modes (`Reactive`, `Semi`, `Full Auto`) |
| **Delegation & Review** | Escalate complex coding, diagnostics, or desktop workflows to Big Brother (`codex`, `claude-code`, and warm 2-session `GeminiAcpServer`) and consolidate memories, LoRAs, and dreams during the 8-stage `02:00` Sleep Cycle |

## System architecture at a glance

The runtime is divided into three cooperating layers so hard real-time motor and safety loops never block on language-model inference:

### 1. Onboard Body (`Slave/firmware/esp32p4-wifi6`)

- **Compute, Wireless & On-Chip Neural Inference**: `Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6` (dual-core 360 MHz RISC-V, 32 MB PSRAM, 32 MB flash) paired with an onboard `ESP32-C6` Wi-Fi 6 / Bluetooth coprocessor over SDIO. Core 1 runs a DAgger-distilled **`110,432-byte` (`107.8 KB`) `int8` MicroDuck neural gait policy** (`720 → 128 → 64 → 32 → 12`, `0.8.159-p4-microduck-v17`) natively at `50 Hz` inside the P4's `128 KB` L2 cache.
- **Actuation & Closed-Loop Feedback**: Twelve modified Miuzei `MG90S` servos (three per leg: shoulder roll, upper-carrier pitch, and five-bar/four-bar knee linkage crank) driven by a `PCA9685` 16-channel PWM controller, with 4th-wire potentiometer wiper feedback read through a `CD74HC4067` 16-channel analog multiplexer and smoothed by a One-Euro velocity filter.
- **Face & Vision**: 1.9-inch `ST7789` IPS LCD (`320 × 170`, 59 expression animations in a recessed bezel with 4 wireless spacers) and an `OV5647` MIPI-CSI camera mounted in the front face carrier with 180° rotation and a `10,000 µs` motion-safe exposure ceiling.
- **Inertial & Audio Peripherals**: `LSM6DS3` 6-axis IMU sampled at `208 Hz` (`FusionAhrs` yaw-zeroed gravity projection, self-leveling, and tip/shake detection); `ES8311` codec and `NS4150B` amplifier with onboard microphone, speaker, and Espressif `WakeNet9s` wake-word detection.
- **Mass & Power**: `650 g` assembled mass in a `245.85 × 182.79 × 109.06 mm` envelope, powered by a redesigned battery-mounted **Wireless Keeper Bridge** housing a `2S` (`8.4 V`) Li-ion pack, `Mini560` `5 V / 5 A` step-down converter, `HiLetgo` 2S USB-C boost charger, and dedicated PCB clips.

### 2. Host Gateway & Body Control (`Master/gateway`)

- Runs on the desktop workstation or an offboard/onboard **Radxa Dragon Q6A** (`QCS6490`), with optional no-login local operator access (`AINEKIO_DASHBOARD_NO_LOGIN`) and automatic hotspot fallback when Wi-Fi infrastructure is absent.
- Maintains the authenticated `protocol-v1` WebSocket session with the robot, manages action receipts and connection epochs, streams camera frames and PCM audio, and serves the **Body Control** workspace and full-viewport **Cockpit HUD** (live **Lucas-Kanade pyramidal optical-flow + Depth-Anything obstacle contact range**, **On-chip MicroDuck vs. Host neural gait** toggle, and top-bar **Ainekio OS control** switch).
- Hosts the SQLite **Freestyle** motion library (`349` curated examples / `489`-row fault-recovery dataset), `4,096`-environment GPU hardware-twin RL trainer (`tensor_sensors.py` + `tensor_body.py`), `50 Hz` host neural policy streamer, YOLOE/ByteTrack object tracker, and OpenGV / MoGe visual depth odometry tools.

### 3. Ainekio OS Mind (`Greg-Aster/ainekio-os`)

- **Standalone & Embodied Personal AI OS**: Runs standalone across Astro/Svelte (`123` unified API modules + `/architecture` `@xyflow/svelte` map), CLI (`mh`), and Android, or embodied in Ainekio V2 via `environment-bridge` (LAN, SSH, or `bridge.ainek.io` Cloudflare tunnel with `followRobot`). See [Ainekio OS Architecture](/posts/ainekio-os/).
- **`ServerStatus` Compute & Voice Control Plane**: Routes **12 specialist model roles** across `Local`, `Hybrid`, `BB-Codex`, and `BB-Gemini` presets—combining a shared `llama-cpp.service` (`:8080`, 5-minute idle eviction, per-request LoRAs, remote `llama.cpp` discovery) with Big Brother CLI (`codex`, `claude-code`, warm 2-session `GeminiAcpServer`) and toggleable **local ↔ remote** CUDA **Whisper (`:9883`)** & **Kokoro (`:9882`)** voice servers plus fast local **Piper ONNX** fallback.
- **47 Cognitive Graphs & SQLite Durable Execution**: Executes **47 editable JSON workflows** over a 14-table WAL `executions.sqlite` engine (`Scheduler Contract v1`, replay-safe `waitForEvent`).
- **Identity, Agency & Sleep**: Anchored by **Standing Persona** (`persona/core.json`, edited autonomously only by the Psychotherapist during the 8-stage `02:00` Sleep Cycle), **Desire Agent**, varied 6-turn `fast-body-event-mode.json` reactions on tip/shake/stall, and hosted static operator UI (`https://mh.ainek.io`).

## The two hardware generations

| Feature | V1 (Eight-Servo Baseline) | V2 (Twelve-Servo Closed-Loop Body) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Actuators** | 8× MG90S (2 per leg, open-loop PWM) | 12× MG90S (3 per leg, 4th-wire analog position feedback via `CD74HC4067`) |
| **Controller** | Freenove `ESP32-S3-WROOM CAM` (`N16R8`) | `Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6` + `ESP32-C6` + `PCA9685` |
| **Mechanics** | Open Sesame-derived frame, planar legs | Enclosed shell (`245.85 × 182.79 × 109.06 mm`), hip roll + `35/20/28/38/55 mm` linkage legs |
| **Display & Camera** | 0.96" `SSD1306` OLED + `OV3660` | 1.9" `ST7789` color LCD (59 expressions) + `OV5647` MIPI-CSI |
| **Inertial & Audio** | External `INMP441` + `MAX98357A`, no IMU | `LSM6DS3` 208 Hz IMU + `ES8311` / `NS4150B` + `WakeNet9s` |
| **Locomotion & Control** | 38 fixed `.amot` keyframe sequences | On-chip `50 Hz` `107.8 KB` MicroDuck neural gait, closed-loop `Stand`/`Walk`, 349-example Freestyle library, Pupper V3 / MJX RL |

V1 remains documented and supported alongside V2. See the [V1 vs. V2 comparison](/posts/v1-v2-comparison/) for detailed mechanical, electrical, and motion data.

## End-to-end command and perception path

1. **Observation**: Spoken utterances (via `WakeNet9s` and 5-mode VAD), camera snapshots, or physical body events (`tipped`, `shaken`, `jammed`, `boot`) arrive at the Host Gateway and flow through `environment-bridge` into Ainekio OS.
2. **Cognition & Routing**: The Work Coordinator runs `environment-mode.json` (or `fast-body-event-mode.json` with 6-turn reaction memory for rapid physical reflexes). Specialist adapters select semantic robot commands, locomotion goals, Freestyle target programs, or Big Brother delegations while the Standing Persona owns outward speech.
3. **Closed-Loop & On-Chip Execution**: The gateway dispatches checked commands or velocity twists to the `ESP32-P4`. Firmware enforces joint calibration and 8-pass Gauss-Newton Cartesian workspace projection (`0.8.150+`), executes either the analytical solver or Core 1's `50 Hz` `int8` MicroDuck neural actor (`0.8.159-p4-microduck-v17`), drives the `PCA9685`, verifies physical shaft arrival via the `CD74HC4067` multiplexer, and applies `LSM6DS3` attitude corrections.
4. **Settlement & Review**: Correlated action receipts, shaft-arrival telemetry, and fresh post-action camera frames return to the waiting workflow so Goal Review and the Desire Agent can verify physical outcomes before advancing.
