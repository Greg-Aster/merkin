# Ainekio

Ainekio is my four-legged robot familiar project. The body has a camera,
microphone, speaker, and display. [MetaHuman OS](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/metahuman-os)
handles conversation, memory, perception, and task planning; the body controller
handles motors and movement limits.

## Project intent

Ainekio is being built as a persistent companion with a recognizable personality
and continuity between interactions. The aim is to converse, notice the
surroundings, remember useful experiences, express itself, choose activities
and improve through reviewed learning. A request should carry through actions
and corrections without losing its objective.

## Understanding the companion

| Function | Its part in Ainekio |
| --- | --- |
| Conversation and personality | A consistent identity, with words and expression appropriate to the situation |
| Perception and expression | Camera, listening and body sensing connected to speech, faces and supported movement |
| Memory | Recent context and longer-term experiences that can inform later interactions |
| Goals and autonomy | Respond to requests, act on configured triggers, or choose and pursue activities according to the selected mode |
| Inner activity | Reflection, dreams, daydreams and curiosity, retained as internal content with its origin identified |
| Learning | Review experience and corrections, train specialist skills or conversational personality, then evaluate the changes |

These describe the intended connected experience. Components are implemented
and under test, but reliable recall, sustained autonomy and the complete
learning loop remain unfinished. [Meet Ainekio as a companion](/posts/ainekio-companion/)
for a plain-language explanation of how the functions fit together; the
[status page](/posts/current-status/) records the latest results.

## The two builds

| | Original V1 | Current V2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Servos | Eight MG90S; two per leg | Twelve; three per leg |
| Controller | ESP32-S3 | ESP32-P4 |
| Mechanics | Sesame-derived body | Redesigned chassis and compact linkages with sideways leg placement |
| Motion | Scripted poses and sequences | Walk, Run, Crawl, six-direction Crab, and 23 finite gestures |

V1 remains supported. [Design history](/project-guide/#original-ainekio-and-ainekio-v2)
and [V1/V2 comparison](/posts/v1-v2-comparison/).

I have begun printing the exterior shell, made further weight modifications,
and switched to a **4 A power supply**. Current work:

- [Shell, power, and motion](/posts/2026-10-12-shell-power-and-motion/): Blender revisions, 27 prototype STLs, and revised Lay Down / Play Dead foot placement.
- [YOLOE tracking](/posts/2026-10-12-yoloe-tracking/): a stationary chair retained one track across eight frames; camera-stream recovery still needs work.

The lighter robot **stood, walked, and responded to commands on October 8**.
The [build photograph](/posts/2026-10-08-standing-walking/) shows it without
the carapace. Later Active Operator component checks are recorded in the October 15 report; complete autonomous behavior remains unqualified. The roughly 20% weight and
lifting-force improvements from the rebuild are estimates. The later owner-reported
mass is 566 g; loaded gait performance and endurance still need measurement.

I dropped the heavy **10,000 mAh battery bank** on October 6 in favor of separate
batteries and charging boards. The final battery arrangement is still open.

## Wired and wireless

**Wireless** is the current priority: Q6A offboard over Wi-Fi. **Wired** keeps the
Q6A on the robot with a native USB connection to the P4.

The P4 now supplies live IMU evidence and configurable local balance correction.
Loaded leveling and the robot ROS 2 adapter remain unqualified. A dated short
Wi-Fi recovery run passed; sustained wireless reliability still needs testing.

## Command path

1. MetaHuman's Work Coordinator admits the request and selects a supported action.
2. The Environment Bridge passes it through Ainekio's adapter and authenticated gateway.
3. The controller checks movement limits and executes the command.
4. Results return to MetaHuman's task workflow.

Workflows retain requests, corrections, and results across waits and restarts.
Gateway recovery reconnects delayed results to their original command without
repeating movement. The September live run exposed stale goals; subsequent
repairs have controlled-test coverage and still need connected-system testing.
