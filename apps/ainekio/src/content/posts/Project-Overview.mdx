Ainekio pairs a custom `650 g`, 3D-printed twelve-servo quadruped body (`~$85` in parts, [`Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot`](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot)) with **[Ainekio OS](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/ainekio-os)** (formerly MetaHuman OS; [architecture guide](/posts/ainekio-os/)), a local-first AI operating system for conversation, long-term memory, perception, and bounded autonomy.

<ProjectStatus />

## What Ainekio does

Rather than treating the robot as a stateless remote-controlled toy that forgets everything on power-off, Ainekio splits work cleanly between real-time embedded control on the body and persistent memory and planning in Ainekio OS:

| Capability | Role in the system |
| --- | --- |
| **Conversation & Standing Persona** | Holds natural voice or text conversations through a single persistent identity grounded in live room context and verified action outcomes |
| **Perception & Expression** | Connects camera snapshots, Apache-2.0 `RT-DETRv2 + OWLv2` open-vocab detection, optical-flow obstacle range, wake-word speech capture, and IMU/joint sensing to spoken replies, 59 animated LCD facial expressions, and physical posture |
| **Attributed Memory** | Remembers conversations, camera observations, and task outcomes across days while tagging each memory's source so imagined thoughts are never confused with physical facts |
| **Closed-Loop & Neural Locomotion** | Runs closed-loop standing and analytical gaits (`Walk`, `Run`, `Crawl`, `Crab`), an on-chip `50 Hz` `107.8 KB` clean-room `MicroDuck` neural walking policy, `50 Hz` `NeuralPose v8` (`neural-pose-v8-calibrated`) choreography over `939` clean Freestyle rows, and `50 Hz` `NeuralPosePresence` |
| **Bounded Autonomy & 3D Web Simulation** | Operates across Reactive, Semi Mode, and Full Auto modes—either on physical hardware or live inside **[`simulator.ainek.io`](https://simulator.ainek.io/)** (V1, V2 & V3 running `500 Hz` MuJoCo WASM + compiled `ESP32-P4` C firmware + PeerJS multiplayer + 19 m Robotics Lab, powered by `@ainekio/engine`; [simulator guide](/posts/gateway-and-simulator/))—while keeping language models strictly behind geometric and electrical safety checks |
| **Overnight Consolidation** | Reviews recent interactions, updates specialist adapters, runs symbolic dreaming, and evaluates persona drift during a scheduled `02:00` software sleep cycle |

### 1. Onboard `ESP32-P4` Body

- **Controller, RISC-V PIE Patch & On-Chip Neural Gait**: A `Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6` board (dual-core `360 MHz` RISC-V with `32 MB` PSRAM, `32 MB` flash, an onboard `ESP32-C6` Wi-Fi 6 coprocessor, and build-local RISC-V PIE vector coprocessor fixes) runs body safety and peripheral control. Core 1 executes a clean-room distilled `107.84 KiB` `int8` `MicroDuck` neural walking policy (`microduck-v18-p4` System-ID & `microduck-v19-snag-p4` swing-foot snag reflex) natively at `50 Hz` inside the processor's `128 KB` L2 cache, paired with a `1.8 s` `-0.45 m/s` autonomous carpet-unsnag reflex.
- **12-Servo Closed-Loop Actuation & Per-Joint `I²t` Thermal Fuse**: Twelve modified `MG90S` metal-gear micro servos (three per leg: shoulder roll, upper-carrier pitch, and four-bar knee crank) are driven by a `PCA9685` PWM board. A soldered 4th wire on every servo's internal potentiometer routes through channels `C4–C15` of a `CD74HC4067` analog multiplexer (`S0–S3 = GPIO27–30`) into `GPIO49` (`ADC2_CH0`) across the full `[1..4094]` ADC span, feeding an **`8–10 s` per-joint `I²t` winding thermal fuse** (`7.0°` gravity-sag deadband, `< 12°/s` slew gate) that cuts `PCA9685 LEDn_OFF_H = 0x10` **only on a locked channel** while leaving the 11 healthy servos energized to hold stance.
- **Face, Chin Camera, IMU & Audio**: The front carrier houses a `1.9"` `ST7789` IPS color LCD (`320 × 170`) rendering **59 animated expressions**, with an `OV5647` MIPI-CSI camera mounted in the lower-jaw / chin mount below the display (`180°` rotated in software with a `10,000 µs` motion-safe exposure cap). Inside the shell sit a `208 Hz` `LSM6DS3` 6-axis IMU for self-leveling and tip/shake detection, plus onboard `ES8311` / `NS4150B` audio with open `microWakeWord` (`int8` TFLite Micro) + WebRTC VAD listening.
- **Chassis & Power**: The complete robot weighs `650 g` (`244.9 × 141.5 × 103.4 mm` neutral CAD envelope; `245.9 × 182.8 × 109.1 mm` splayed physical stance) and runs untethered from a battery-mounted **Wireless Keeper Bridge** carrying a `2S` (`8.4 V` max) Li-ion pack, a `Mini560` `5 V / 5 A` buck regulator, and an onboard `2S` USB-C boost charger.

### 2. Host Gateway, 3-System Neural Motion, Cockpit HUD & `simulator.ainek.io`

- Runs on a nearby desktop workstation or an offboard `Radxa Dragon Q6A` single-board computer, maintaining the authenticated `protocol-v1` WebSocket session (`40` commands, `24` feature flags) with the physical robot—or with V1, V2, and V3 browser twins live on **[`simulator.ainek.io`](https://simulator.ainek.io/)** (`500 Hz` MuJoCo WASM + compiled `ESP32-P4` C firmware + PeerJS multiplayer + 19 m Robotics Lab, powered by `@ainekio/engine`; [gateway & simulator guide](/posts/gateway-and-simulator/)).
- Serves the **Body Control** workbench and full-viewport **[Ainekio Cockpit HUD](/posts/ainekio-cockpit/)**, combining live camera video, Apache-2.0 **`RT-DETRv2 + OWLv2`** (`detector_backend.py`) open-vocab detection, `MoGe-2` `8×8` / ground-plane corridor clearance, one-tap **`Tap to Talk`** wake-word bypass, a **translucent live Ainekio OS message stream** (`POST /api/metahuman-stream`), 10 immediate-apply runtime toggles, and a 2D vector joystick.
- Coordinates **[System 2 (`50 Hz` `neural-pose-v8-calibrated`)](/posts/three-system-neural-architecture/)** (`2.46M` GPU MuJoCo steps, `939` clean Freestyle choreography rows, Schmitt-trigger single-leg carpet stepping `> 6.5°` / `<= 3.0°`, `CARRIER_HOME_MARGIN_DEG = 3.0` clamping carrier `>= -27.87°`, `12/12` MuJoCo suites w/ `0` falls) and **[System 3 (`50 Hz` `NeuralPosePresence`)](/posts/three-system-neural-architecture/)** (motor-silent during `vad_open`/wake-word without cutting off the mic, plus pure sagittal speech nods `±2.5 mm` Z / `±4.5°` pitch with `0.0°` shoulder scrub), alongside an **8-sector (`8 × 45°`) spatial curiosity tracker**.

### 3. Ainekio OS Mind

- Runs as a standalone local-first personal AI system across its local web workbench, terminal CLI, and Android app, or connects to the robot through its embodied bridge (locally, over SSH, or across an optional Cloudflare tunnel) while projecting live workflow and Big Brother replies to Cockpit via `Body Control Text Out` across 7 cognitive graphs.
- Pairs **System 1 single-hop Embodied Curiosity** (`embodied-curiosity.ts`) with **12 specialist cognitive roles** (including deduplicated `v26` Intent & Task Action Selector LoRAs trained over `2,941` dev records on `Qwen3.5-0.8B`) across local `llama.cpp` / Ollama models, offline-first local or LAN-shared Whisper speech-to-text, VRAM-aware Kokoro (CUDA↔CPU auto-switching) or Piper text-to-speech, and pre-warmed **Big Brother** coding sessions (`Codex`, `Claude Code`, and `GeminiAcpServer`).
- Executes **47 inspectable node-based cognitive graphs** (`203` registered node definitions) backed by **`ExecutionStore` v2** synchronous `JsonPlus` SQLite checkpoints so multi-step physical tasks survive movement waits or brief disconnects without repeating actions twice.

## How a spoken request becomes physical motion

1. **Sense**: A spoken phrase (captured after the `microWakeWord` wake-word trigger or Cockpit `Tap to Talk`), a camera snapshot, or a physical body event (`tipped`, `shaken`, `servo_stall`) reaches the Host Gateway and forwards to Ainekio OS.
2. **Decide**: Ainekio OS classifies the turn—routing sudden physical jostles to a fast reflex path with varied vocal/LCD reactions, or routing multi-step requests through specialist models that pick bounded robot commands, locomotion goals, or Freestyle posture programs while the Standing Persona owns outward speech.
3. **Move & Verify**: The gateway sends checked commands, velocity twists, or `50 Hz` `NeuralPose v8` foot targets to the `ESP32-P4`. Onboard firmware projects foot targets onto the valid four-bar workspace, runs either the analytical solver or Core 1's `50 Hz` `MicroDuck` neural policy, verifies physical shaft arrival via the `CD74HC4067` feedback mux, and enforces the `8–10 s` per-joint `I²t` winding thermal fuse.
4. **Confirm**: Action receipts, shaft-arrival status, and a fresh post-motion camera frame return to Ainekio OS so it can verify whether the physical goal actually succeeded before deciding what to do next.
