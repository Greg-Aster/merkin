Ainekio is a quadruped robot companion combining a 3D-printed, twelve-servo closed-loop body with [MetaHuman OS](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/metahuman-os) for conversation, persistent memory, perception, personality, and autonomous task planning. The physical body handles real-time motor control, joint-position verification, inertial sensing, vision capture, wake-word listening, speaker playback, and facial expression, while MetaHuman OS and the host gateway coordinate cognition, locomotion learning, and long-horizon goals.

## What Ainekio is built to do

Ainekio is designed as a persistent robot familiar with continuity across interactions:

| Function | Role in the system |
| --- | --- |
| **Conversation & Standing Persona** | Maintain a consistent identity (`persona/core.json`), converse naturally, and ground replies in current context and action outcomes |
| **Perception & Expression** | Connect camera observations, wake-gated speech, and IMU/joint sensing to spoken responses, 58 animated LCD faces, and physical posture |
| **Persistent Memory** | Retain episodic context and long-term experiences with clear source attribution, distinguishing direct observation from internal imagination |
| **Locomotion & Freestyle Motion** | Execute closed-loop standing, continuous gaits (`Walk`, `Run`, `Crawl`, `Crab`), neural policies, and 349+ model-generated Freestyle motion programs |
| **Bounded Autonomy** | Operate in Reactive, Semi-Autonomous (drive-delta interval), or Full Auto (continuous high-reasoning activity selection) modes |
| **Delegation & Review** | Escalate complex coding, diagnostics, or desktop workflows to Big Brother (`TerminalChat.svelte` / `big-brother-tool-mode.json`) and review experience during Sleep |

## System architecture at a glance

The runtime is divided into three cooperating layers so hard real-time motor and safety loops never block on language-model inference:

### 1. Onboard Body (`Slave/firmware/esp32p4`)

- **Compute & Wireless**: `Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6` (dual-core 360 MHz RISC-V, 32 MB PSRAM, 32 MB flash) paired with an onboard `ESP32-C6` Wi-Fi 6 / Bluetooth coprocessor over SDIO.
- **Actuation & Closed-Loop Feedback**: Twelve modified Miuzei `MG90S` servos (three per leg: shoulder roll, upper-carrier pitch, and five-bar/four-bar knee linkage crank) driven by a `PCA9685` 16-channel PWM controller, with 4th-wire potentiometer wiper feedback read through a `CD74HC4067` 16-channel analog multiplexer.
- **Face & Vision**: 1.9-inch `ST7789` IPS LCD (`320 × 170`, 58 expression animations) and an `OV5647` MIPI-CSI camera mounted in the front face carrier with 180° rotation and a `10,000 µs` motion-safe exposure ceiling.
- **Inertial & Audio Peripherals**: `LSM6DS3` 6-axis IMU sampled at `208 Hz` for attitude estimation, self-leveling, and tip/shake detection; `ES8311` codec and `NS4150B` amplifier with onboard microphone, speaker, and Espressif `WakeNet9s` wake-word detection.
- **Mass & Power**: `650 g` assembled mass in a `245.85 × 182.79 × 109.06 mm` envelope, powered by a rear two-level carrier housing a `2S` (`8.4 V`) Li-ion pack, `Mini560` `5 V / 5 A` step-down converter, and `HiLetgo` 2S USB-C boost charger.

### 2. Host Gateway & Body Control (`Master/gateway`)

- Runs on the desktop workstation or an offboard/onboard **Radxa Dragon Q6A** (`QCS6490`).
- Maintains the authenticated `protocol-v1` WebSocket session with the robot, manages action receipts and connection epochs, streams camera frames and PCM audio, and serves the **Body Control** operator dashboard.
- Hosts the SQLite **Freestyle** motion library (`349` curated examples), gait telemetry logger, neural policy streamer (`50 Hz`), YOLOE/ByteTrack object tracker, and OpenGV / MoGe visual depth odometry tools.

### 3. MetaHuman OS Brain (`metahuman-os`)

- **Unified Model Router**: A single `llama-cpp.service` instance on `:8080` (`load-on-startup = false`, 5-minute idle unload) serving `Qwen3.5 9B` (conversation/vision/autonomy), `Environment 0.8B/2B` specialists with per-request LoRAs, `Freestyle Qwen` motion generation, and CPU-only embedding models (`Qwen3 Embedding 0.6B` and `Nomic Embed v1.5`).
- **Single Bridge & Unified Graph**: One consolidated `environment-bridge` with multi-host following feeds observations into the unified `environment-mode.json` cognitive graph.
- **Identity & Agency**: A single **Standing Persona** (`persona/core.json`, edited autonomously only by the Psychotherapist agent) works alongside the **Desire Agent** (which adjusts desire strengths), `fast-body-event-mode.json` (`be_` token-matched facial expressions and brief reactions on tip/shake/stall), and **Big Brother** (`big-brother-tool-mode.json` and `system-code-review`) for asynchronous computer tasks.
- **Web & Operator Surface**: Accessible locally or via the hosted static interface at `https://mh.ainek.io`.

## The two hardware generations

| Feature | V1 (Eight-Servo Baseline) | V2 (Twelve-Servo Closed-Loop Body) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Actuators** | 8× MG90S (2 per leg, open-loop PWM) | 12× MG90S (3 per leg, 4th-wire analog position feedback via `CD74HC4067`) |
| **Controller** | Freenove `ESP32-S3-WROOM CAM` (`N16R8`) | `Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6` + `ESP32-C6` + `PCA9685` |
| **Mechanics** | Open Sesame-derived frame, planar legs | Enclosed shell (`245.85 × 182.79 × 109.06 mm`), hip roll + `35/20/28/38/55 mm` linkage legs |
| **Display & Camera** | 0.96" `SSD1306` OLED + `OV3660` | 1.9" `ST7789` color LCD (58 expressions) + `OV5647` MIPI-CSI |
| **Inertial & Audio** | External `INMP441` + `MAX98357A`, no IMU | `LSM6DS3` 208 Hz IMU + `ES8311` / `NS4150B` + `WakeNet9s` |
| **Locomotion & Control** | 38 fixed `.amot` keyframe sequences | Closed-loop `Stand`/`Walk`, continuous solver, 349-example Freestyle library, 50 Hz neural policies, MuJoCo/MJX RL |

V1 remains documented and supported alongside V2. See the [V1 vs. V2 comparison](/posts/v1-v2-comparison/) for detailed mechanical, electrical, and motion data.

## End-to-end command and perception path

1. **Observation**: Spoken utterances (via `WakeNet9s` and 5-mode VAD), camera snapshots, or physical body events (`tipped`, `shaken`, `jammed`, `boot`) arrive at the Host Gateway and flow through `environment-bridge` into MetaHuman OS.
2. **Cognition & Routing**: The Work Coordinator runs `environment-mode.json` (or `fast-body-event-mode.json` for rapid physical reflexes). Specialist adapters select semantic robot commands, locomotion goals, Freestyle target programs, or Big Brother delegations while the Standing Persona owns outward speech.
3. **Closed-Loop Execution**: The gateway dispatches checked commands to the `ESP32-P4`. Firmware enforces joint calibration and Cartesian workspace boundaries (`0.8.150`), drives the `PCA9685`, verifies physical shaft arrival via the `CD74HC4067` multiplexer, and applies `LSM6DS3` attitude corrections.
4. **Settlement & Review**: Correlated action receipts, shaft-arrival telemetry, and fresh post-action camera frames return to the waiting workflow so Goal Review and the Desire Agent can verify physical outcomes before advancing.
