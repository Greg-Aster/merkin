Most build entries on this site record *chronological bench work*—individual linkage revisions, wiring fixes, reinforcement-learning failure modes, and firmware milestones. This reference pulls the entire active **Ainekio V2** system together into a single capability and specification inventory derived directly from the live [`Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot`](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot) (hardware, `ESP32-P4` firmware, Body Control gateway, and motion stacks) and [`Greg-Aster/ainekio-os`](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/ainekio-os) (cognitive runtime, persistent memory, and autonomy engine) repositories.

The engineering goal of Ainekio is specific: pair an ultra-low-cost (`~$85` BOM, `650 g`) 3D-printed quadruped that has **measured closed-loop joint feedback and onboard `50 Hz` neural locomotion** with a **persistent, local-first AI operating system** that remembers interactions across days, perceives people and objects in the room, executes verified physical goals, and consolidates its own memory and specialist adapters overnight.

## 2. Physical Hardware, Kinematics & Closed-Loop Actuation

Unlike open-loop hobby quadrupeds that fire blind PWM pulses and hope a foot reaches the floor, V2 modifies commodity `$1.50` micro servos into closed-loop position sensors while staying inside a `650 g` printed envelope.

### 2.1 Mechanical, Electrical & Compute Specifications

| Parameter | Active V2 Specification (`v2-12servo`) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Envelope & Weighed Mass** | **`245.85 × 182.79 × 109.06 mm`** (`375`-mesh Blender assembly) · **`650 g` (`0.650 kg`)** total mass with `2S` pack |
| **Shoulder Pivot Grid** | Fore-aft spacing **`173.434 mm`** · Lateral spacing **`63.500 mm`** · Vertical datum **`Z = 80.962 mm`** |
| **Leg Kinematics (`20` coords)** | Closed-chain four-bar linkage (`compact-35-20-28-38-55`): Carrier **`35.0 mm`**, Crank **`20.0 mm`**, Pickup **`28.0 mm`**, Rod **`38.0 mm`**, Lower leg **`55.0 mm`** + `92`-vertex `Part027` sole (`<0.15 mm` fit bound); **12 actuated** (`shoulder`, `carrier`, `crank` × 4) + **8 passive** (`pickup`, `rod` × 4) hinges |
| **Primary MCU & Memory** | **Waveshare `ESP32-P4-WIFI6`**: dual-core `360 MHz` RISC-V (`rev 1.3`), **`128 KB` L2 cache**, **`32 MiB` hex-SPI PSRAM (`200 MHz`)**, **`32 MiB` QIO flash** (`2× 8 MiB` OTA slots + `15.875 MiB` LittleFS) |

### 2.2 Firmware Safety, Startup Slew & Recovery-First Stall Handling

Onboard FreeRTOS splits supervision and control across cores so servo power is protected even if Wi-Fi drops:

- **Core 0 `output_guard` (`5 ms` tick)**: Monitors `/robot` control lease freshness and pulls `PCA9685 OE` high (disarming servo drive) if control/heartbeats go silent for **`> 4,000 ms`**.
- **Sequential Startup & Staggered Stand**: Fault, panic, or brownout resets inhibit automatic startup motion. On clean engage, channels home **`200 ms` apart** at a `1,000 µs/s` slew limit, then execute a front/rear **`100 ms`-staggered rise** (`1.2 s` lift + `2 mm / 200 ms` settle) verified against 4th-wire shaft landmarks.
- **One-Euro Feedback & Non-Releasing Stall Detection**: Raw 12-bit wiper counts pass through 3-point NVS piecewise calibration (`500 / 1650 / 2800 µs`) and an adaptive **One-Euro filter** (`min_cutoff = 1.5 Hz`, `beta = 0.002`) producing real-time `filtered_model_deg`, `velocity_deg_s`, and health bitmasks (`usable`, `clipped`, `unavailable`). When an active joint outside its `30 µs` target tolerance makes **`< 5 µs` directional progress for `1,000 ms`**, firmware emits `event: "servo_stall"` with **`outputs_released: false`**—keeping the stance legs energized so the host can actively lift and clear the snagged foot instead of collapsing.
- **Tapered IMU Leveling & Local 180 s Auto-Rest**: Stationary balance holds bus-quiet inside `≤ 0.5°` roll/pitch error, wakes above `1.0°` with rate `2 + 6r²/(1+r²) s⁻¹`, and projects 4th-wire tracking lag outside `±120 µs` through the linkage Jacobian for anti-windup. After **`180 s`** of inactivity (deferred while moving, listening, or speaking), `idle_rest.c` locally runs `lay_down` and disarms `PCA9685 OE` without needing a host connection.

## 4. Freestyle Choreography & 50 Hz Neural Pose Control

Walking policies only handle cyclic stepping; expressive room behavior and fault recovery require arbitrary whole-body postures. Body Control pairs a natural-language **Freestyle** synthesizer (`:8794`) with a closed-loop **50 Hz Neural Pose & Emote runtime** (`experiments/freestyle/neural_pose/`).

### 4.1 The 1,767-Example Freestyle Library & 11 DSL Primitives

Freestyle runs a fine-tuned **`Qwen3.5-0.8B` GGUF LoRA** (`rank=16`, `alpha=32` on the shared `:8080` `llama.cpp` server, with optional Big Brother escalation) backed by a curated SQLite motion library containing **`1,767` active programs** (**`939` ordinary motions** across `109` choreography families plus **`828` fault-recovery trajectories** across `16` stall/slip/dropout families, validated against `86` ordinary and `404` recovery probe splits).

Instead of emitting raw unvalidated angles, the model composes programs from **`11` geometry-checked `motion-targets-v1` primitives**:

| Primitive | Signature & Parameters | Physical Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `body` | `(translation_mm[3], rotation_deg[3], legs[], foot_offsets_mm[], speed_pct, taper_deg)` | 6-DOF torso COM shift and roll/pitch/yaw posture over planted feet (essential before lifting a loaded `MG90S` leg) |
| `feet` | `(legs[], offsets_mm[], speed_pct, taper_deg)` | Robot-frame Cartesian foot IK (`+Z` vertical unload → horizontal sweep → `0` placement) |
| `pose` / `move` | `(joints[], angles_deg[], reference, speed_pct, taper_deg)` | Multi-joint coordinated or single-joint angular target (`current` / `home` / `absolute`) with endpoint taper |
| `oscillate` | `(joint, amplitude_deg, cycles, speed_pct, taper_deg)` | 4-phase cyclic wave/wag/nod (`+A → 0 → -A → 0`) |
| `gait` | `(command, speed_pct, cycles)` | Bounded analytical step/turn segments (auto-chunked to `≤10` cycles per intent) |
| `hold` / `stand` / `stop` / `release` / `face` | `(seconds)` · `()` · `()` · `()` · `(expr[59])` | Timed dwell, closed-loop neutral recovery, immediate hold-stop, `OE` PWM disarm, and synchronized LCD expression |

When `servo_stall`, `servo_feedback_fault`, `tipped`, or `rapid_movement` interrupts a motion, `bridge.py` feeds the original `motion_goal`, `interrupted_action`, `remaining_program`, and per-joint 4th-wire progress back into Freestyle so it synthesizes an unloading recovery path rather than abandoning the user's request.

### 4.2 Closed-Loop 50 Hz Neural Pose & Emote Controller (neural_pose)

To replace open-loop keyframe interpolation during poses, 3-leg waves, and seated 2-paw mimes, Body Control automatically activates `neural_pose` (`policy.onnx`, **`3.17 MiB`**, **`826,776` parameters**, `(512, 256, 128)` MLP trained across `512` GPU MuJoCo 3.10 environments on a `2,048`-pose goal bank):

- **`1,290`-Float Fault-Tolerant Observation (`15 × 86` @ `20 ms`)**: Combines `joint_rad[12]`, `joint_vel_rad_s[12]`, **`usable_flags[12]`** (automatically substituting commanded angles and zeroing velocity if any 4th-wire ADC channel clips or drops out—trained with **`35%` sensor-faulted episodes** and **`30%` suspended-in-air episodes**), IMU gyro/accel/gravity`[9]`, `goal_joint_rad[12]`, `goal_foot_norm[12]`, `leg_support[4]`, `speed_scale[1]`, and `prev_action[12]`.
- **`0.46 ms` Host Inference & Ray-Connected IK**: Outputs `12` foot targets scaled across **`(95, 155, 180) mm`**—wide enough to span full `Stand`, `Sit`, `Rest`, high waves, and seated rear-braced gestures—decoded via closed-form `65 × 17` ray-connected 4-bar IK (`zero` Cartesian IK exceptions) and streamed at `50 Hz` over `walk_nominal_v1`, advancing segments early as soon as 4th-wire error settles (`≤ 4.5°`, `≤ 22°/s`).

## 6. Wire Protocols, Action Inventory & Closed-Loop Locomotion Goals

Across `/robot` (`protocol-v1`, **27 capability flags** and **39 JSON/binary message types**) and `/environment` (Environment Bridge v1), Ainekio OS controls the physical body exclusively through **`12` typed, schema-validated actions** backed by a **`7,206`-case Action Selector training corpus** (`1,814` frozen evaluation + `197` regression cases):

| Outbound Action | Wire / Execution Path | Behavior, Parameters & Safety Contract |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`move`** *(requires `EnvironmentLocomotionGoal`)* | `/environment` → `intent: walk` + `environment.action.update` (`validForMs ≤ 2000 ms`) | Bare open-loop gait starts are rejected. ## 7. Cognitive Engine, Persistent Memory, Autonomy & Sleep (Ainekio OS)

When connected—or when running standalone on a workstation, CLI (`mh`), or mobile client while the physical robot is powered off—**[Ainekio OS](/posts/ainekio-os/)** provides the persistent cognitive architecture behind those actions:

1. **`47` Node-Based Cognitive Graphs (`176` `.node.ts` implementations) & `12` Specialist Model Roles**:
   - Every turn executes a deterministic demand-driven graph (`environment-mode.json` has `43` nodes / `172` edges; `robot-autonomy-controller-mode.json` has `26` nodes / `62` edges) across **`12` decoupled roles**: `orchestrator`, `environmentVision`, `persona`, `autonomy`, `environmentActionSelector`, `lightweight`, `curator`, `coder`, `planner`, `summarizer`, `psychotherapist`, and `embedder`.
   - Shared native `llama.cpp` (`:8080`) demand-loads `Qwen3.5 9B` (multimodal vision + `262K` context), `Qwen3.5 0.8B` / `2B` per-request Intent/Task LoRAs, and `Qwen3.5 4B` (`Q4_K_M`) personalization LoRAs (unloading after `5 min` idle), alongside CPU-pinned **`Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B` (`1024-D`)** so vector search never competes for GPU VRAM. Any role can also route to a LAN GPU server, cloud APIs, or **Big Brother** CLI sessions (**`codex`**, **`claude-code`**, and warm **`GeminiAcpServer`** pools).
2. **Crash-Safe SQLite Durable Execution (`executions.sqlite`)**:
   - Checkpoints graph state across **`17` WAL tables** with `30,000 ms` writer-lease fencing, content-addressed `mh-blob:<sha256>` camera frame deduplication, `waitForEvent` zero-CPU suspension, and a transactional outbox correlated to Body Control's `ActionReceipts`—guaranteeing reconnects or restarts **never double-execute physical robot motions**.
3. **Provenance-Tagged Memory & Protected Standing Persona**:
   - Stores **`12` canonical event types** (`conversation`, `observation`, `task`, `inner_dialogue`, `reflection`, `dream`, `daydream`, `tool_invocation`, `file_read`, `file_write`, `summary`, `operator_feedback`) with optional **`AES-256-GCM` or VeraCrypt** encryption and hybrid retrieval (`70%` cosine + `30%` lexical across `shallow` / `normal` / `deep` / `exhaustive` scopes).
   - Identity lives in a read-protected **Standing Persona** (`core.json` + switchable **Facets** and `mood-review.json`); only the evidence-gated **Psychotherapist** (`psychoanalyzer`, requiring valid `evidenceIds` across `30` days of non-echo-chamber memories) or the human owner can mutate core traits, values, or standing goals.
4. **Three Autonomy Modes (`Reactive` / `Semi` / `Full`), `1–10` Step Desires & System Code Review**:
   - **Active Operator** schedules **`15` autonomous operations**—including `boredom-observer` (visual curiosity), `boredom-movement` (posture shifts), `inner-curiosity` & `train-of-thought` (`≤5` associative hops), `curiosity-researcher`, `mood`, and **`system-code-review`** (ingesting runtime errors into `HelpTicket`s for read-only root-cause diagnosis, or live code repair at `adaptive_auto` trust).
   - Longer objectives enter the **Agency** pipeline (`10` weighted sources from `0.25` to `1.00`, `0.03/day` decay, auto-archival at `0`), compiling strict **`1–10` step `DesirePlan`s** where each step targets `'operator'` (digital) or `'robot'` (child `environment-mode` execution) across **5 trust tiers** (`observe` → `suggest` → `supervised_auto` → `bounded_auto` → `adaptive_auto`, with up to `2` levels of earned reduced inhibition).
5. **The `8`-Stage `02:00` Overnight Sleep Cycle**:
   - Suppresses autonomous motion at `02:00` to run `organize-memory` → `curate-memory` → `curate-environment-decisions` → `train-personalization` → `run-desire-agent` → `dream` (associative symbolic dreams tagged with `sleepSessionId`) → `review-persona` (`psychoanalyzer`) → `rebuild-index`, aborting immediately (`wakeSleepSession`) if a person speaks to Ainekio.

## 8. Operator Workbenches & Current Engineering Limits

Both halves expose full operator inspection rather than hiding behavior inside a black box:

- **Body Control (`:8791` / `:8795`) & `/cockpit` HUD**: Live MIPI-CSI stream with YOLOE/face-gallery overlays, keyboard/gamepad 3D twist teleop, instant `native` (`ESP32-P4` L2) vs. `neural` (host) gait switching, per-joint PWM & 3-point 4th-wire ADC calibration, checkerboard ground-plane calibrator, `1,767`-item Motion Library editor, microSD `.avi` clip browser, and Bearer-authenticated **Wi-Fi HTTP OTA firmware flashing** with automatic peripheral/WebSocket health verification before rollback cancellation.
- **Ainekio OS Web Workbench**: Live SvelteFlow cognitive graph editor (`47` graphs), `/architecture` interactive system map, multi-channel conversation & Inner Dialogue inspector, Memory & Vector Index browser, Desire/Agency reviewer, Training curator, and `ServerStatus` per-role model/voice switcher.

### Documented Hardware & Software Constraints

- **Hobby-Servo Torque Ceiling (`0.18 N·m`)**: `$1.50` geared `MG90S` servos cannot brute-force sideways foot drags on deep-pile carpet; reliable non-walking motion requires `body` COM shifts and `+Z` vertical foot unloads before sweeping.
- **No Onboard `Vbat` Telemetry**: V2 isolates the `5 V` `Mini560` servo rail from `3.3 V` logic without a battery divider (`battery_available = false`), so pack depletion is currently inferred from under-load servo tracking lag rather than cell voltage.
- **Provisioned Wake Model & Metric Depth Calibration**: Firmware compiles in the TFLite Micro `microWakeWord` engine (`esp-sr` WebRTC VAD works out of the box), while the `.tflite` wake checkpoint is provisioned separately to LittleFS (`wake_ready = false` on an unprovisioned flash). Similarly, `MoGe-2` visual-IMU depth reports `metricAccuracyVerified: false` until paired with a 3-view checkerboard ground-plane calibration for that camera mount.
