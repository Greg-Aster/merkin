Most build entries on this site record *chronological bench work*—individual linkage revisions, wiring fixes, reinforcement-learning failure modes, and firmware milestones. This reference pulls the entire active **Ainekio V2** system together into a single capability and specification inventory derived directly from the live [`Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot`](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot) (hardware, `ESP32-P4` firmware, Body Control gateway, commercial-clean vision/motion stacks, and [`game.megameal` 3D simulator bridge](/posts/gateway-and-simulator/#game-megameal-simulator)) and [`Greg-Aster/ainekio-os`](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/ainekio-os) (cognitive runtime, persistent memory, and autonomy engine) repositories.

The engineering goal of Ainekio is specific: pair an ultra-low-cost (`~$85` BOM, `650 g`) 3D-printed quadruped that has **measured closed-loop joint feedback and onboard `50 Hz` neural locomotion** with a **persistent, local-first AI operating system** that remembers interactions across days, perceives people and objects in the room, executes verified physical goals, and consolidates its own memory and specialist adapters overnight.

## 2. Physical Hardware, Kinematics & Closed-Loop Actuation

Unlike open-loop hobby quadrupeds that fire blind PWM pulses and hope a foot reaches the floor, V2 modifies commodity `$1.50` micro servos into closed-loop position sensors while staying inside a `650 g` printed envelope.

### 2.1 Mechanical, Electrical & Compute Specifications

| Parameter | Active V2 Specification (`v2-12servo`) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Envelope & Weighed Mass** | **`245.85 × 182.79 × 109.06 mm`** (`375`-mesh Blender assembly) · **`650 g` (`0.650 kg`)** total mass with `2S` pack |
| **Shoulder Pivot Grid** | Fore-aft spacing **`173.434 mm`** · Lateral spacing **`63.500 mm`** · Vertical datum **`Z = 80.962 mm`** |
| **Leg Kinematics (`20` coords)** | Closed-chain four-bar linkage (`compact-35-20-28-38-55`): Carrier **`35.0 mm`**, Crank **`20.0 mm`**, Pickup **`28.0 mm`**, Rod **`38.0 mm`**, Lower leg **`55.0 mm`** + `92`-vertex `Part027` sole (`<0.15 mm` fit bound); **12 actuated** (`shoulder`, `carrier`, `crank` × 4) + **8 passive** (`pickup`, `rod` × 4) hinges |
| **Primary MCU & Memory** | **Waveshare `ESP32-P4-WIFI6`**: dual-core `360 MHz` RISC-V (`rev 1.3`), **`128 KB` L2 cache**, **`32 MiB` hex-SPI PSRAM (`200 MHz`)**, **`32 MiB` QIO flash** (`2× 8 MiB` OTA slots + `15.875 MiB` LittleFS) |

### 2.2 Firmware Safety, Startup Slew & 7-Layer Jam / Thermal Protection

Onboard FreeRTOS splits supervision and control across cores so servo power is protected even if Wi-Fi drops:

- **Core 0 `output_guard` (`5 ms` tick)**: Monitors `/robot` control lease freshness and pulls `PCA9685 OE` high (disarming servo drive) if control/heartbeats go silent for **`> 4,000 ms`**.
- **Sequential Startup & Staggered Stand**: Fault, panic, or brownout resets inhibit automatic startup motion. On clean engage, channels home **`200 ms` apart** at a `1,000 µs/s` slew limit, then execute a front/rear **`100 ms`-staggered rise** (`1.2 s` lift + `2 mm / 200 ms` settle) verified against 4th-wire shaft landmarks.
- **7-Layer Phantom-Jam Resolution & Per-Joint `I²t` Winding Thermal Fuse**: Raw 12-bit wiper counts pass through 3-point NVS piecewise calibration (`500 / 1650 / 2800 µs`, extrapolated across `[1..4094]` for thermal protection while ignoring clipped/extrapolated samples during streaming reversal high-water unlock: `80 µs` deadband, `80 µs/s` slew reset, `25 µs` target-shift unlock) and an adaptive **One-Euro filter** (`min_cutoff = 1.5 Hz`, `beta = 0.002`). Firmware bounds arrival waits at **`800 ms`** (`!actively_leveling` guard, `STAND_MAX_REPLANS = 2`) and executes a **`1.8 s` `-0.45 m/s` reverse unsnag** during walking, while host `NeuralPose v8` acts as the **single posture owner** (`stand_enabled: false`, `jam_enabled: true`, `CARRIER_HOME_MARGIN_DEG = 3.0` clamping carrier `>= -27.87°`, `18–20 mm` step lift, and `mid_step_active` settling `<= 5.5°` / `<= 3.5°`—with the false-clamping `280 ms` `_detect_stalled_recoils` heuristic and `library_recovery.py` retired). Genuine rotor locks trip the **`8.0–10.0 s` per-joint `I²t` thermal fuse** (`DEADBAND = 7.0°`, `< 12°/s` slew gate), setting `PCA9685 LEDn_OFF_H = 0x10` **only on the locked servo** (`outputs_released: true`) while the 11 healthy servos hold the body upright ([full guide](/posts/jam-protection-and-thermal-safety/)).
- **Tapered IMU Leveling & Local 180 s Auto-Rest**: Stationary balance holds bus-quiet inside `≤ 0.5°` roll/pitch error, wakes above `1.0°` with rate `2 + 6r²/(1+r²) s⁻¹`, and projects 4th-wire tracking lag outside `±120 µs` through the linkage Jacobian for anti-windup. After **`180 s`** of inactivity (deferred while moving, listening, speaking, or while `RT-DETRv2 + OWLv2` sees a person), `idle_rest.c` locally runs `lay_down` and disarms `PCA9685 OE`.

## 4. Freestyle Choreography, 50 Hz NeuralPose v8 & 50 Hz NeuralPosePresence

Walking policies only handle cyclic stepping; expressive social greeting, seated gestures, and splayed-carpet stance alignment require closed-loop whole-body posture control and calm conversational presence. Body Control pairs a natural-language **Freestyle** synthesizer (`:8794`) with **`50 Hz` `neural-pose-v8-calibrated`** (`experiments/freestyle/neural_pose/`) and **System 3 (`50 Hz` `NeuralPosePresence`)** (`experiments/freestyle/neural_pose/presence.py`).

### 4.1 Clean 939-Row Freestyle Library & 11 DSL Primitives

Freestyle runs a fine-tuned **`Qwen3.5-0.8B` GGUF LoRA** (`rank=16`, `alpha=32` on the shared `:8080` `llama.cpp` server, with optional Big Brother escalation) backed by a **`939`-row clean choreography library** (after retiring event-driven `library_recovery.py` replanning and pruning `828` synthetic recovery rows from `1,767 → 939`, including unloaded rear-leg seated bracing `sit_brace_enter()` / `sit_brace_exit()`).

Instead of emitting raw unvalidated angles, the model composes programs from **`11` geometry-checked `motion-targets-v1` primitives**:

| Primitive | Signature & Parameters | Physical Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `body` | `(translation_mm[3], rotation_deg[3], legs[], foot_offsets_mm[], speed_pct, taper_deg)` | 6-DOF torso COM shift and roll/pitch/yaw posture over planted feet (essential before lifting a loaded `MG90S` leg) |
| `feet` | `(legs[], offsets_mm[], speed_pct, taper_deg)` | Robot-frame Cartesian foot IK (`+Z` vertical unload → horizontal sweep → `0` placement) |
| `pose` / `move` | `(joints[], angles_deg[], reference, speed_pct, taper_deg)` | Multi-joint coordinated or single-joint angular target (`current` / `home` / `absolute`) with endpoint taper |
| `oscillate` | `(joint, amplitude_deg, cycles, speed_pct, taper_deg)` | 4-phase cyclic wave/wag/nod (`+A → 0 → -A → 0`) |
| `gait` | `(command, speed_pct, cycles)` | Bounded analytical step/turn segments (auto-chunked to `≤10` cycles per intent) |
| `hold` / `stand` / `stop` / `release` / `face` | `(seconds)` · `()` · `()` · `()` · `(expr[59])` | Timed dwell, closed-loop neutral recovery, immediate hold-stop, `OE` PWM disarm, and synchronized LCD expression |

### 4.2 Closed-Loop 50 Hz Neural Pose & Emote Controller (`neural-pose-v8-calibrated`)

Evolved across four iterations in three days (`v5-adaptive` → `v6-relock` → `v7-hysteresis` → **`neural-pose-v8-calibrated`**, **`2.46M` GPU MuJoCo steps**, **`826,776` parameters**, `(512, 256, 128)` MLP, **`12/12` 3D MuJoCo qualification scenarios passed with `0` falls**), `NeuralPose v8` serves as the single posture owner (`stand_enabled: false`, `jam_enabled: true`):

- **`1,290`-Float Fault-Tolerant Observation (`15 × 86` @ `20 ms`)**: Combines `joint_rad[12]`, `joint_vel_rad_s[12]`, **`usable_flags[12]`** (automatically substituting commanded angles and zeroing velocity if any 4th-wire ADC channel clips or drops out—trained with **`35%` sensor-faulted episodes** and **`30%` suspended-in-air episodes**), IMU gyro/accel/gravity`[9]`, `goal_joint_rad[12]`, `goal_foot_norm[12]`, `leg_support[4]`, `speed_scale[1]`, and `prev_action[12]`.
- **Re-Locking Carpet Pile, Stand-Before-Step Gate & Schmitt-Trigger Single-Leg Sequencer**: Trained against **re-locking carpet pile (`mu = 0.10..2.05`)** with equivariant DAgger warmstart, `neural-pose-v8-calibrated` enforces **`|ca_err| <= 14°`** before lifting any foot, triggers single-leg alignment steps strictly above **`6.5°` shoulder splay** (latching down to **`<= 3.0°`** and holding calm zero-lift below `6.5°` with zero "drunk wobble"), and shifts torso CoM onto the planted tripod (**`±14 mm` Y, `±9 mm` X, `-8 mm` diagonal brace**).
- **Calibrated Carrier Clamp & Terminal Stance Settling**: `PoseManifold.CARRIER_HOME_MARGIN_DEG = 3.0` (`goals.py`) clamps the carrier floor **`>= -27.87°`** above `-30.87°` Home and right-sizes carpet step lift from `~61 mm` to **`18–20 mm`** (`above_calibration_mask = 0`, `stalled_joints = 0` on live hardware), while `executor.py` holds **`mid_step_active` terminal stance settling** (`<= 5.5°` stance / `<= 3.5°` shoulder error, safely inside the `7.0°` thermal deadband).

### 4.3 System 3: `50 Hz` `NeuralPosePresence` (`presence.py`)

Replacing the legacy `20 Hz` `Master/gateway/presence/` module, **`50 Hz` `NeuralPosePresence`** (`experiments/freestyle/neural_pose/presence.py`) keeps the `650 g` body lifelike during speech without disturbing microphone capture or scrubbing carpet pile:

- **Motor-Silent Listening (`vad_open` / Wake-Word)**: Stays completely motor-silent whenever `vad_open` or wake-word capture is active—never issuing a `stop` command that could cut off microphone capture, while keeping servo gear whine off the `ES8311` mic.
- **Pure Sagittal Speech Nods (`0.0°` Shoulder Scrub)**: During `16 kHz` TTS playback, drives smooth sagittal nods (**`±2.5 mm` Z, `±4.5°` pitch**) with **`0.0°` shoulder targets**, eliminating lateral carpet scrub and shoulder heat.

## 6. Wire Protocols, Action Inventory & Closed-Loop Locomotion Goals

Across `/robot` (`protocol-v1`, **24 feature flags** and **40 commands / message types** compiled identically into physical firmware and `libgait_native.so`) and `/environment` (Environment Bridge v1), Ainekio OS controls the physical or simulated body exclusively through **`12` typed, schema-validated actions** backed by a **`7,206`-case Action Selector training corpus** (`1,814` frozen evaluation + `197` regression cases):

| Outbound Action | Wire / Execution Path | Behavior, Parameters & Safety Contract |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`move`** *(requires `EnvironmentLocomotionGoal`)* | `/environment` → `intent: walk` + `environment.action.update` (`validForMs ≤ 2000 ms`) | Bare open-loop gait starts are rejected. Every `move` carries `gait` (`walk`\|`crawl`\|`run`\|`crab`), `forward`/`turn`/`lateral` (`-100..100%`), and one of **5 closed-loop stopping contracts**: **`distance`** (`metres ± tolerance`), **`relativeTurn`** (unwrapped IMU `degrees ± tolerance`), **`approach`** (`metric` metres or `imageRelative` box height), **`follow`** (`target` + `endingCondition`), or **`visual`** (`completionCriteria`). Missing sensors abort cleanly; mid-walk target loss triggers clean gait `Finish` (`speed=0`) |
| **`requestMotion`** | `/environment` → Freestyle `:8794` → `motion_plan` / `neural_pose` (`v8`) | Synthesizes & executes natural-language postures/emotes over `939` clean choreography rows; native `Stand` maps directly to `[["stand"]]`; `Bridge` interrupts active jobs **only** on genuine hardware `I²t` thermal cutoff (`outputs_released: true`) |
| **`stop`** | `/environment` → `stop` (`detach?: bool`) | Immediate hold-stance halt (`detach: false`) or emergency `PCA9685 OE` PWM disarm (`detach: true`, also drops autonomy to `reactive`) |
| **`faceExpression`** | `/environment` → `intent: face` (`face_feedback_v1`) | Sets any of the `59` expressions on foreground or `displayBackground` slots with `displayToken` (`thinking`, `be_*`), `displayTimeoutMs`, and `displayRelease` |
| **`speak`** | `tts.robot-speech` → `AIKSPK01` → `0x10` `speaker_pcm` | Streams `16 kHz` `20 ms` PCM to robot speaker or host computer (`Off` / `Browser` / `Body Control`), returning correlated playback completion receipts |
| **`captureImage` / `visual.read`** | `/environment` → `snap` / vision worker | Captures fresh correlated frame or executes `acquire`, open-vocab `OWLv2` `search`, box `select`, `release`, or on-demand `depthDemand` |
| **`look` / `inspect` / `visualApproach` / `sendText`** | `/environment` adapter & `environment-mode.json` | Orient toward yaw/pitch or bounding box, run bounded close-up visual inspection, combine target lock with metric/visual approach, or emit operator stream text |

## 7. Cognitive Engine, Persistent Memory, Autonomy & Sleep (Ainekio OS)

When connected—or when running standalone on a workstation, CLI (`mh`), or mobile client while the physical robot is powered off—**[Ainekio OS](/posts/ainekio-os/)** provides the persistent cognitive architecture behind those actions:

1. **`47` Node-Based Cognitive Graphs (`176` `.node.ts` implementations) & `12` Specialist Model Roles**:
   - Every turn executes a deterministic demand-driven graph (`environment-mode.json` has `43` nodes / `172` edges; `robot-autonomy-controller-mode.json` has `26` nodes / `62` edges) across **`12` decoupled roles**: `orchestrator`, `environmentVision`, `persona`, `autonomy`, `environmentActionSelector`, `lightweight`, `curator`, `coder`, `planner`, `summarizer`, `psychotherapist`, and `embedder`.
   - Shared native `llama.cpp` (`:8080`) demand-loads `Qwen3.5 9B` (multimodal vision + `262K` context), `Qwen3.5 0.8B` / `2B` per-request **`v25/v26` Intent/Task specialist LoRAs** (`661` source cases / `2,941` dev + `814` eval records), and `Qwen3.5 4B` (`Q4_K_M`) personalization LoRAs (unloading after `5 min` idle), alongside CPU-pinned **`Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B` (`1024-D`)** so vector search never competes for GPU VRAM. Any role can also route to a LAN GPU server, cloud APIs, or **Big Brother** CLI sessions (**`codex`**, **`claude-code`**, and warm **`GeminiAcpServer`** pools).
2. **Crash-Safe SQLite Durable Execution (`executions.sqlite`)**:
   - Checkpoints graph state across **`17` WAL tables** with `30,000 ms` writer-lease fencing, content-addressed `mh-blob:<sha256>` camera frame deduplication, `waitForEvent` zero-CPU suspension, and a transactional outbox correlated to Body Control's `ActionReceipts`—guaranteeing reconnects or restarts **never double-execute physical robot motions**.
3. **Provenance-Tagged Memory & Protected Standing Persona**:
   - Stores **`12` canonical event types** (`conversation`, `observation`, `task`, `inner_dialogue`, `reflection`, `dream`, `daydream`, `tool_invocation`, `file_read`, `file_write`, `summary`, `operator_feedback`) with optional **`AES-256-GCM` or VeraCrypt** encryption and hybrid retrieval (`70%` cosine + `30%` lexical across `shallow` / `normal` / `deep` / `exhaustive` scopes).
   - Identity lives in a read-protected **Standing Persona** (`core.json` + switchable **Facets** and `mood-review.json`); only the evidence-gated **Psychotherapist** (`psychoanalyzer`, requiring valid `evidenceIds` across `30` days of non-echo-chamber memories) or the human owner can mutate core traits, values, or standing goals.
4. **Three Autonomy Modes (`Reactive` / `Semi` / `Full`), `1–10` Step Desires & System Code Review**:
   - **Active Operator** schedules **`15` autonomous operations**—including `boredom-observer` (visual curiosity), `boredom-movement` (posture shifts), `inner-curiosity` & `train-of-thought` (`≤5` associative hops), `curiosity-researcher`, `mood`, and **`system-code-review`** (ingesting runtime errors into `HelpTicket`s for read-only root-cause diagnosis, or live code repair at `adaptive_auto` trust).
   - Longer objectives enter the **Agency** pipeline (`10` weighted sources from `0.25` to `1.00`, `0.03/day` decay, auto-archival at `0`), compiling strict **`1–10` step `DesirePlan`s** where each step targets `'operator'` (digital) or `'robot'` (child `environment-mode` execution) across **5 trust tiers** (`observe` → `suggest` → `supervised_auto` → `bounded_auto` → `adaptive_auto`, with up to `2` levels of earned reduced inhibition).
5. **The `8`-Stage `02:00` Overnight Sleep Cycle**:
   - Suppresses autonomous motion at `02:00` to run `organize-memory` → `curate-memory` → `curate-environment-decisions` → `train-personalization` → `run-desire-agent` → `dream` (associative symbolic dreams tagged with `sleepSessionId`) → `review-persona` (`psychoanalyzer`) → `rebuild-index`, aborting immediately (`wakeSleepSession`) if a person speaks to Ainekio.

## 8. Operator Workbenches, 3D Web Simulator & Current Engineering Limits

Both halves expose full operator inspection rather than hiding behavior inside a black box:

- **Body Control (`:8791` / `:8795`) & `/cockpit` HUD**: Live MIPI-CSI stream with `RT-DETRv2 + OWLv2` and face-gallery overlays, keyboard/gamepad 3D twist teleop, instant `native` (`ESP32-P4` L2) vs. `neural` (host `MicroDuck`) gait switching, per-joint PWM & 3-point 4th-wire ADC calibration, checkerboard ground-plane calibrator, `939`-row Freestyle choreography & Motion Library editor, microSD `.avi` clip browser, and Bearer-authenticated **Wi-Fi HTTP OTA firmware flashing** with automatic peripheral/WebSocket health verification before rollback cancellation.
- **`game.megameal` 3D Web Robot Simulator ([alpha PoC](/posts/gateway-and-simulator/#game-megameal-simulator))**: Launches `ainekio-v2-sim` via `Master/start-virtual-robot.sh` (`libgait_native.so` compiled `ESP32-P4` C firmware + `500 Hz` MuJoCo plant), streaming `50 Hz` four-bar poses, `320 × 170` `ST7789` LCD frames, virtual `OV5647` camera JPEGs, and `16 kHz` Web Audio into `https://game.megameal.org/?scene=v2_lab_runtime` with interactive external wrenches and per-servo `J0–J11` pin/jam toggles (`VirtualRobotPanel.svelte`).
- **Ainekio OS Web Workbench**: Live SvelteFlow cognitive graph editor (`47` graphs), `/architecture` interactive system map, multi-channel conversation & Inner Dialogue inspector, Memory & Vector Index browser, Desire/Agency reviewer, Training curator, and `ServerStatus` per-role model/voice switcher.

### Documented Hardware & Software Constraints

- **Hobby-Servo Torque Ceiling (`0.18 N·m`)**: `$1.50` geared `MG90S` servos cannot brute-force sideways foot drags on deep-pile carpet; reliable non-walking motion relies on `NeuralPose v8`'s tripod CoM shifts (`±14 mm` Y, `±9 mm` X) and `18–20 mm` vertical step unloads before sweeping.
- **No Onboard `Vbat` Telemetry**: V2 isolates the `5 V` `Mini560` servo rail from `3.3 V` logic without a battery divider (`battery_available = false`), so pack depletion is currently inferred from under-load servo tracking lag rather than cell voltage.
- **Provisioned Wake Model & Metric Depth Calibration**: Firmware compiles in the TFLite Micro `microWakeWord` engine (`esp-sr` WebRTC VAD works out of the box), while the `.tflite` wake checkpoint is provisioned separately to LittleFS (`wake_ready = false` on an unprovisioned flash). Similarly, `MoGe-2` visual-IMU depth reports `metricAccuracyVerified: false` until paired with a 3-view checkerboard ground-plane calibration for that camera mount.
