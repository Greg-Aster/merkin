Rather than forcing a single monolithic network to walk over cables, perform seated two-paw gestures, and breathe subtly during conversation, Ainekio V2 divides learned motor control into **three specialized neural systems** coordinated across `ESP32-P4` Core 1 and the **Body Control** runtime:

| Neural Tier | Rate & Footprint | Active Policy / Adapter | Role on the Robot |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **System 1 · Locomotion** | `50 Hz` · **`107.84 KiB` `int8`** (`ESP32-P4` `128 KB` L2 cache) | `microduck-v18-p4` & `microduck-v19-snag-p4` (`720 → 128 → 64 → 32 → 12`) | Omnidirectional closed-loop walking, slick-wood/carpet seam traversal, weak-servo compensation, and swing-foot snag high-stepping |
| **System 2 · Pose & Choreography** | `50 Hz` · `826,776` params ONNX (`0.4 ms` CPU) + `Qwen3.5-0.8B` LoRA | `neural-pose-v2-delay` / `v3-jam-compliant` + `freestyle-curriculum` (`1,812` rows) | Translates natural-language prompts into multi-frame IK targets and tracks 12-DOF poses across `180 ms` Wi-Fi delay, faulted pot wires, and limb obstructions |
| **System 3 · Autonomic Living Presence** | `20 Hz` · **`56,094` params** (`56 KB` `int8`, P4 SRAM-ready) | `ainekio-presence-v1` (`320 → 128 → 96 → 30`) | Speech-RMS body gestures (`±8 mm`), stance compliance chatter relief (`±1.8 mm`), `99.69%`-accurate LCD expression logits, pickup safety, and VAD-silent listening |

## 1. System 1: On-chip `50 Hz` MicroDuck progression (`v16` → `v18` Sys-ID → `v19-snag-p4`)

Compiled into a `110,432`-byte (`107.84 KiB`) symmetric row-wise `int8` weight binary (`policy.bin`) multiplied against dynamically quantized `int16` activations (`102,784` MACs/step, `< 0.1 mm` foot-offset error vs. FP32 ONNX), System 1 runs natively on `ESP32-P4` Core 1 at `50 Hz` inside the chip's `128 KB` L2 cache (`ainekio-neural-gait-v7-proprio`: `15 × 48 = 720` floats of 4th-wire joint angles, velocities, `208 Hz` IMU gyro/accel/gravity, twist `[vx, vy, wz]`, and prior actions).

### System identification from physical floor runs (`v17` → `v18`)

On November 12, three untethered floor trials on physical unit `ainekio-p4-e6ebe5` (`220 MB` of `50 Hz` `trace.jsonl` logs across `298` commands) revealed two real-hardware asymmetries not present in nominal CAD simulation: `-19.1°` compliance sag on front-right shoulder `J3` (`J3`/`J6` diagonal flex) and `41–45%` lower effective position gain on the rear-left (`RL`) leg under load.

Injecting per-world `MG90SActuator` `effort_scale`, coupled weak-`RL` randomization (`kp 0.42–0.72×`, `effort 0.60–0.86×`), diagonal shoulder compliance, a single-leg ground-dragging penalty (`contact > 0.70 s`), and `18 mm` cable ledges produced **`microduck-v18-p4`** (`+8.01M` PPO steps):

- **10-Scenario Composite Benchmark:** Improved from `-1.2871` (`1` fall in `v17`) to **`+1.3246` with `0` falls**.
- **Weak-RL System-ID Suite:** Forward velocity jumped **`+69.5%`** (`+0.0817 m/s`), keeping 10th-percentile pelvis height at **`52.5 mm`** instead of sagging to `22.4 mm`.
- **Cable Ledges & Carpet:** Speed across `14–17 mm` cables rose **`+79.7%`** (`+0.0575 m/s`, `3.2×` straighter heading), and plush carpet speed rose **`+28.6%`** (`+0.0970 m/s`).

### Swing-foot snag obstacle plant (`microduck-v19-snag-p4`)

To prevent low-slung micro-quadruped toes from catching on cords or rug edges mid-swing, **`microduck-v19-snag-p4`** (`TASK_REVISION = 19`, compiled into `Slave/software/models/v2-12servo/motions/microduck/policy.bin`) adds a **`500 Hz` unilateral swing-foot snag wrench** (`compute_swing_snag_wrench_500hz`) in MuJoCo:

- During `28%` of episodes (`DEFAULT_SNAG_PROB = 0.28`), an invisible unilateral lip (`lip_z = 22–27 mm`) catches forward-moving swing feet below lip height with a `950 N/m` + `14 N·s/m` contact wrench (capped at `5.5 N`).
- Paired with a telescopic `snag_clearance` lift reward (`+1.80` clearance bonus) and firmware's `1.8 s` `-0.45 m/s` autonomous reverse unsnag, the policy learns to lift snagged feet cleanly up and over the lip rather than pushing stall current into the obstruction.

## 2. Fixing three ESP-IDF RISC-V PIE vector coprocessor bugs (`idf_pie_coproc.cmake`)

Enabling `ESP32-P4` RISC-V PIE SIMD acceleration alongside multi-task FreeRTOS scheduling uncovered three upstream bugs in ESP-IDF `v5.5.4` and `esp-nn`—including a live crash where an interrupt boundary corrupted `PC` into `pxPortGetCoprocArea` (`cpair = 99`). Rather than modifying the global SDK installation, `Slave/firmware/esp32p4-wifi6/cmake/idf_pie_coproc.cmake` (`753` lines, verified by `tests/test_pie_coproc.py`) applies three deterministic build-directory patches inside `${CMAKE_BINARY_DIR}/ainekio-idf`:

1. **Hardware-loop (`HWLP`) interrupt PC corruption fix:** Replaces short (`< 8`-instruction), unaligned, or vector-terminated `esp.lp.setup` hardware loops in `esp-nn` with scalar `addi + bnez` loops (which dual-issue alongside PIE vector instructions on `ESP32-P4` without hardware-loop PC state hazards across interrupts).
2. **Lazy FreeRTOS PIE ownership fix:** Strips direct `csrsi 0x7f2, 0b01` (`CSR_PIE_STATE_REG`) writes from `esp-nn` kernels so FreeRTOS's lazy coprocessor trap (`rtos_save_pie_coproc`) properly claims task ownership and preserves vector state across context switches.
3. **`portasm.S` & `rvruntime-frames.h` context-frame repair:** Saves and restores previously omitted vector register `q3` and `PIE CFG`, unswaps `SAR` versus `FFT_BIT_WIDTH` on context restore, and widens `RV_PIE_XACC` from `4` bytes to the full `8`-byte accumulator width.

## 3. System 2: `50 Hz` `NeuralPose` & unified `1,812`-example Freestyle curriculum

When Ainekio stops walking to bow, wave, brace on its haunches, or execute an operator-prompted emote, control hands off to **System 2**:

- **Unified `Qwen3.5-0.8B` Freestyle Curriculum (`experiments/freestyle/freestyle_curriculum.py`):** Consolidates `~25` earlier scripts into one generator producing **`1,812` verified examples** (`1,000` ordinary choreography programs + `812` fault-recovery trajectories, `freestyle-curriculum-adapter.F16.gguf`, `12.79 MB`). Includes unloaded rear-leg step-in bracing (`sit_brace_enter()` / `sit_brace_exit()`) so seated two-paw gestures (`celebrate`, `beg`, `clap`) keep the center of mass stably behind the rear support line without tipping forward. The `:8794` inference daemon (`ainekio-freestyle-api.service`) auto-starts and self-recovers on connection refusal.
- **`neural-pose-v2-delay` (`50 Hz` closed-loop pose tracking):** `826,776`-parameter actor (`1,290-D` observation vector, `0.38–0.44 ms` CPU inference) trained across `2.95M` PPO steps with simulated `180 ms` Wi-Fi packet jitter and `35%` dropped/faulted 4th-wire potentiometer channels—achieving **`0.52°–1.33°` joint MAE** and **`0` falls** across all 6 qualification suites.
- **`neural-pose-v3-jam-compliant` & `280 ms` manifold recoil:** Injects unilateral `jnt_range` mechanical obstructions at `30%–65%` limb travel (`28%` episode probability) with a `jam_compliance_cost` (`-2.2`), training the policy to hold a gentle **`~3.4°` (`0.060 rad`) feeler preload** against unexpected stops while the executor backs jammed joints off `2.0°` after `280 ms` (detailed in [Jam Protection & Winding Thermal Fuse](/posts/jam-protection-and-thermal-safety/)).

## 4. System 3: `20 Hz` autonomic "Living Presence" (`ainekio-presence-v1`)

Between walks and explicit emotes, a rigid robot locked at static PWM ticks either hums from inter-leg servo fighting or looks lifeless while speaking. **System 3** (`Master/gateway/presence/`) adds a lightweight autonomic neural layer running at `20 Hz` (`50 ms` period):

- **Compact SRAM-ready network:** `4 × 80 = 320` inputs → `(128, 96)` hidden layers → `30` outputs (`56,094` parameters; `224 KB` FP32 ONNX / **`56 KB` `int8`**, verified `p4_internal_sram_fit = True`). Trained on `72,000` sequences (`32` CUDA epochs in `126 s`) to `val_foot_rmse_norm = 0.0268` and **`99.69%` LCD expression validation accuracy**.
- **Three simultaneous output heads:**
  1. **`12` conversational foot residuals (`±8.0 mm`):** Driven by live **`50 ms` PCM RMS audio envelopes** computed across `16 kHz` robot/host TTS playback so Ainekio shifts weight and nods naturally in cadence with its own spoken syllables.
  2. **`12` stance compliance trim steps (`±1.8 mm`, clamped to `±3.5 mm` XY / `±2.0 mm` Z):** Bleeds off opposing four-bar shear and micro-servo buzz when planted on high-friction carpet without letting the torso sag.
  3. **`6` LCD facial expression logits:** Synchronizes subtle face shifts with posture and vocal energy.
- **Strict VAD-listening silence & `< 1 ms` preemption:** System 3 suppresses 20 Hz motor motion completely whenever microphone listening / VAD (`vad_open`) is active—ensuring servo gear noise never holds the speech detector open and leaving P4's quiet `+6°` front-shoulder `listen_frame` ear perk undisturbed. When idle for `180 s` (deferred while `YOLOE` sees a person in the room), System 3 settles into the canonical crouch `(0.0°, -23.22°, -22.03°)` (`> 18°` end-stop margin) and disarms `PCA9685 OE`, preempting in `< 1 ms` whenever a walk, gesture, or `stop` command arrives.
