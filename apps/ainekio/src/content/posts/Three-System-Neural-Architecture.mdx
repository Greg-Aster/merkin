Rather than forcing a single monolithic network to walk over cables, perform seated two-paw gestures, and breathe subtly during conversation, Ainekio V2 divides learned motor control into **three specialized neural systems** coordinated across `ESP32-P4` Core 1 and the **Body Control** runtime:

| Neural Tier | Rate & Footprint | Active Policy / Adapter | Role on the Robot |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **System 1 · Locomotion** | `50 Hz` · **`110,432 B` (`107.84 KiB`) `int8`** (`ESP32-P4` `128 KB` L2 cache) | **`MicroDuck v19-snag-p4`** (`policy.bin`, `720 → 128 → 64 → 32 → 12`) | Omnidirectional closed-loop walking, slick-wood/carpet seam traversal, weak-servo compensation, `25 mm` swing-foot snag clearance, and `1.8 s` reverse unsnag |
| **System 2 · Pose & Choreography** | `50 Hz` · `826,776` params ONNX (`0.4 ms` CPU) + `Qwen3.5-0.8B` / `2B` LoRAs | **`NeuralPose v5-adaptive`** + **`2,322`-row (`94`-family)** Freestyle LoRA + `v25/v26` Intent/Task LoRAs (`2,941` dev + `814` eval) | Synthesizes multi-step IK from `splayed_stand`/`lay_down`/`dead`/standing starts; tracks poses across `180 ms` delay, `6.7 mm` friction micro-step lift, `280 ms` recoil, and multi-joint stance jam escalation |
| **System 3 · Autonomic Living Presence** | `20 Hz` · **`56,382` params** (**`56.4 KB` INT8**, P4 SRAM-ready) | `ainekio-presence-v1` (`72,000`-seq retrain, `320 → 128 → 96 → 30`) | Speech-RMS body gestures (`±8 mm`), `64.1%` err / `31.1%` torque cut on carrier jam, `_common_mode_drift()` `14 mm` vertical yield, `99.65%` LCD expression accuracy, and `~0.42 s` silent-lock wake-word perk |

## 1. System 1: On-chip `50 Hz` MicroDuck progression (`v16` → `v18` Sys-ID → `v19-snag-p4`)

Compiled into a `110,432`-byte (`107.84 KiB`) symmetric row-wise `int8` weight binary (`policy.bin`) multiplied against dynamically quantized `int16` activations (`102,784` MACs/step, `< 0.1 mm` foot-offset error vs. FP32 ONNX), System 1 runs natively on `ESP32-P4` Core 1 at `50 Hz` inside the chip's `128 KB` L2 cache (`ainekio-neural-gait-v7-proprio`: `15 × 48 = 720` floats of 4th-wire joint angles, velocities, `208 Hz` IMU gyro/accel/gravity, twist `[vx, vy, wz]`, and prior actions).

### System identification from physical floor runs (`v17` → `v18`)

On November 12, three untethered floor trials on physical unit `ainekio-p4-e6ebe5` (`220 MB` of `50 Hz` `trace.jsonl` logs across `298` commands) revealed two real-hardware asymmetries not present in nominal CAD simulation: `-19.1°` compliance sag on front-right shoulder `J3` (`J3`/`J6` diagonal flex) and `41–45%` lower effective position gain on the rear-left (`RL`) leg under load.

Injecting per-world `MG90SActuator` `effort_scale`, coupled weak-`RL` randomization (`kp 0.42–0.72×`, `effort 0.60–0.86×`), diagonal shoulder compliance, a single-leg ground-dragging penalty (`contact > 0.70 s`), and `18 mm` cable ledges produced **`microduck-v18-p4`** (`+8.01M` PPO steps):

- **10-Scenario Composite Benchmark:** Improved from `-1.2871` (`1` fall in `v17`) to **`+1.3246` with `0` falls**.
- **Weak-RL System-ID Suite:** Forward velocity jumped **`+69.5%`** (`+0.0817 m/s`), keeping 10th-percentile pelvis height at **`52.5 mm`** instead of sagging to `22.4 mm`.
- **Cable Ledges & Carpet:** Speed across `14–17 mm` cables rose **`+79.7%`** (`+0.0575 m/s`, `3.2×` straighter heading), and plush carpet speed rose **`+28.6%`** (`+0.0970 m/s`).

### Active production actor: `25 mm` swing-foot snag obstacle plant (`MicroDuck v19-snag-p4`)

To prevent low-slung micro-quadruped toes from catching on cords or rug edges mid-swing, the active on-chip actor **`MicroDuck v19-snag-p4`** (`TASK_REVISION = 19`, compiled into the **`110,432 B` `policy.bin`** at `Slave/software/models/v2-12servo/motions/microduck/policy.bin`) adds a **`25 mm` (`lip_z = 22–27 mm`) `500 Hz` unilateral swing-foot snag plant** (`compute_swing_snag_wrench_500hz`) in MuJoCo:

- During `28%` of episodes (`DEFAULT_SNAG_PROB = 0.28`), a `25 mm` nominal unilateral lip (`22–27 mm`) catches forward-moving swing feet below lip height with a `950 N/m` + `14 N·s/m` contact wrench (capped at `5.5 N`).
- Paired with a telescopic `snag_clearance` lift reward (`+1.80` clearance bonus) and firmware's **`1.8 s` (`-0.45 m/s`) autonomous reverse unsnag**, `MicroDuck v19-snag-p4` lifts snagged feet cleanly up and over obstructions rather than pushing stall current into the snag.

## 2. Fixing three ESP-IDF RISC-V PIE vector coprocessor bugs (`idf_pie_coproc.cmake`)

Enabling `ESP32-P4` RISC-V PIE SIMD acceleration alongside multi-task FreeRTOS scheduling uncovered three upstream bugs in ESP-IDF `v5.5.4` and `esp-nn`—including a live crash where an interrupt boundary corrupted `PC` into `pxPortGetCoprocArea` (`cpair = 99`). Rather than modifying the global SDK installation, `Slave/firmware/esp32p4-wifi6/cmake/idf_pie_coproc.cmake` (`753` lines, verified by `tests/test_pie_coproc.py`) applies three deterministic build-directory patches inside `${CMAKE_BINARY_DIR}/ainekio-idf`:

1. **Hardware-loop (`HWLP`) interrupt PC corruption fix:** Replaces short (`< 8`-instruction), unaligned, or vector-terminated `esp.lp.setup` hardware loops in `esp-nn` with scalar `addi + bnez` loops (which dual-issue alongside PIE vector instructions on `ESP32-P4` without hardware-loop PC state hazards across interrupts).
2. **Lazy FreeRTOS PIE ownership fix:** Strips direct `csrsi 0x7f2, 0b01` (`CSR_PIE_STATE_REG`) writes from `esp-nn` kernels so FreeRTOS's lazy coprocessor trap (`rtos_save_pie_coproc`) properly claims task ownership and preserves vector state across context switches.
3. **`portasm.S` & `rvruntime-frames.h` context-frame repair:** Saves and restores previously omitted vector register `q3` and `PIE CFG`, unswaps `SAR` versus `FFT_BIT_WIDTH` on context restore, and widens `RV_PIE_XACC` from `4` bytes to the full `8`-byte accumulator width.

## 3. System 2: `50 Hz` `NeuralPose v5-adaptive`, `2,322`-row Freestyle LoRA & `v25/v26` specialist routing

When Ainekio stops walking to bow, wave, brace on its haunches, rise from a splayed or prone floor state, or execute an operator-prompted emote, control hands off to **System 2**:

- **`2,322`-Row (`94`-Family) `Qwen3.5-0.8B` Freestyle LoRA & `v25/v26` Specialist LoRAs:** `experiments/freestyle/freestyle_curriculum.py` generates **`2,322` geometry-verified examples across `94` choreography families** (**`1,510` ordinary programs + `812` fault-recovery trajectories**, `0.00096` train loss), explicitly covering **`splayed_stand`**, **`lay_down`**, and **`dead`** starting states alongside unloaded rear-leg seated bracing (`sit_brace_enter()` / `sit_brace_exit()`). Upstream in **[Ainekio OS](/posts/ainekio-os/)**, **`v25/v26` Intent and Task specialist LoRAs** (`661` curated source cases expanded to **`2,941` dev + `814` eval records**) route user requests cleanly into `requestMotion` and closed-loop locomotion intents, while the `:8794` Freestyle daemon (`ainekio-freestyle-api.service`) auto-starts and self-recovers on connection refusal.
- **Four-stage `50 Hz` `NeuralPose` progression (`v2-delay` → `v3-jam-compliant` → `v4-equivariant` → `v5-adaptive`):**
  1. **`v2-delay`:** `826,776`-parameter actor (`1,290-D` observation vector, `0.38–0.46 ms` CPU inference) trained with `180 ms` Wi-Fi packet jitter and `35%` dropped/faulted 4th-wire potentiometer channels (`0.52°–1.33°` joint MAE).
  2. **`v3-jam-compliant`:** Injects unilateral `jnt_range` limb obstructions (`28%` episode probability) with a `jam_compliance_cost` (`-2.2`) so stalled joints hold a gentle `~3.4°` feeler preload instead of driving full stall current.
  3. **`v4-equivariant`:** Enforces exact **left/right mirror symmetry** across paired legs so symmetric bows, sits, and alternate-paw waves never develop unilateral stance lean.
  4. **`v5-adaptive` (Active):** Trained for **`1.23M` adaptive PPO steps**, **`NeuralPose v5-adaptive`** passes **`7/7` qualification scenarios with `0` falls**, adding **`6.7 mm` ground-friction micro-step unloading** whenever carpet shear resists lateral foot motion.
- **Local `280 ms` paw recoil vs. multi-joint stance jam escalation:** Single-joint gesture contacts recoil locally by **`2.0°` in `280 ms`** (`+ 0.85 s` Return-to-Ready on final posture hold), while **`>= 2` jammed stance joints** or **`> 12°` left/right recoil asymmetry** escalates to a **one-shot `replan_recovery`** from measured 4th-wire state (detailed in [Jam Protection & Winding Thermal Fuse](/posts/jam-protection-and-thermal-safety/)).

## 4. System 3: `20 Hz` autonomic "Living Presence" (`72,000`-sequence retrain)

Between walks and explicit emotes, a rigid robot locked at static PWM ticks either hums from inter-leg servo fighting or looks lifeless while speaking. **System 3** (`Master/gateway/presence/`) adds a lightweight autonomic neural layer running at `20 Hz` (`50 ms` period), retrained on November 17–18 across **`72,000` sequences**:

- **Compact SRAM-ready network (`56,382` parameters / `56.4 KB` INT8):** `4 × 80 = 320` inputs → `(128, 96)` hidden layers → `30` outputs (verified `p4_internal_sram_fit = True`), achieving **`99.65%` LCD expression validation accuracy**.
- **Carrier-jam relief & `_common_mode_drift()` `14 mm` vertical yield:**
  1. **`12` conversational foot residuals (`±8.0 mm`):** Driven by live **`50 ms` PCM RMS audio envelopes** across `16 kHz` robot/host TTS playback so Ainekio shifts weight and nods naturally in cadence with its spoken syllables.
  2. **Jam-compliant stance trim & `14 mm` vertical yield:** Under unexpected carrier-link jams, System 3 reduces **joint tracking error by `64.1%`** and **servo torque by `31.1%`**, while `_common_mode_drift()` permits up to **`14 mm` of vertical jam yield** on an obstructed leg **without causing 4-leg torso sink**.
  3. **Expression logits:** Synchronizes subtle `ST7789` facial shifts with posture and vocal energy at **`99.65%` accuracy**.
- **`~0.42 s` silent-lock wake-word perk & `< 1 ms` preemption:** When `microWakeWord` or WebRTC VAD opens (`vad_open`), System 3 executes a **`~0.42 s` silent-lock front-shoulder perk**, switches to the **`listening` face**, and issues **`stop(detach=False)`** so holding torque stays silent throughout user speech and servo gear noise never holds VAD open. After `180 s` of room quiet (deferred while `YOLOE` sees a person), System 3 settles into the canonical crouch `(0.0°, -23.22°, -22.03°)` (`> 18°` end-stop margin) and disarms `PCA9685 OE`, preempting in `< 1 ms` on any incoming command.
