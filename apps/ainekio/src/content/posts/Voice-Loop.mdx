Ainekio's voice loop connects onboard acoustic capture and speaker playback on the `ESP32-P4` V2 body ([`Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot`](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot)) to speech recognition (**Whisper**), persistent conversational reasoning, and expressive speech synthesis (**Kokoro** + **Piper** fallback) in **[Ainekio OS](/posts/ainekio-os/)** ([`Greg-Aster/ainekio-os`](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/ainekio-os)).

## Onboard V2 audio hardware (`ES8311` + `NS4150B`)

Where the eight-servo V1 build wired an external `INMP441` microphone breakout and `MAX98357A` amplifier over shared I2S jumpers, the V2 `Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6` body uses an integrated low-noise audio chain:

- **Audio codec & microphone**: Onboard **`ES8311`** low-power mono codec sampling at **`16 kHz`, 16-bit mono PCM** (`640 bytes` per `20 ms` frame), buffered in `32 MB` PSRAM.
- **Speaker amplifier & enclosure**: Onboard **`NS4150B`** Class-D amplifier driving a chassis-mounted **`20 × 30 mm` `8 Ω 2 W` speaker** seated in a dedicated 3D-printed holder inside the shell.

## Open `microWakeWord` (`TFLite Micro`), WebRTC VAD & Cockpit `Tap to Talk`

To allow hands-free room conversation without streaming continuous idle room audio over Wi-Fi—and without relying on closed wake-word blobs—V2 pairs open wake-word inference with embedded voice activity detection directly on the `ESP32-P4`:

1. **Open `microWakeWord` on TFLite Micro (`pilot-v1`)**: Runs a **`40`-mel streaming `int8` `microWakeWord` model** (`62,304 B` `pilot-v1` checkpoint provisioned to LittleFS; proprietary `WakeNet9s` was disabled in favor of open `microWakeWord`) directly on the microcontroller before opening an upstream utterance stream.
2. **System 3 `50 Hz` `NeuralPosePresence` motor-silent listening gate**: When wake detection or `vad_open` triggers, **System 3 (`50 Hz` `NeuralPosePresence`)** switches the `ST7789` display to the **`listening` face** and holds **100% motor-silent** throughout user speech without issuing `stop`—eliminating both servo gear whine on the `ES8311` mic and premature `vad_open` capture cutoffs.
3. **5-mode `esp-sr` WebRTC VAD & `15 s` utterance window**: Once woken (or when continuous VAD gating is selected in Body Control), **`esp-sr` WebRTC VAD** segments speech across **five sensitivity modes (`0–4`)** with a `100 ms` pre-roll and up to a **`15-second` (`≤ 512 KiB`)** utterance window so multi-clause requests are never clipped mid-thought.
4. **Cockpit `Tap to Talk` (`gate: "open"`)**: In noisy rooms or when testing without a wake phrase, clicking **`Tap to Talk`** in [Ainekio Cockpit](/posts/ainekio-cockpit/) saves the active `wake` or `vad` gate and sends `{ on: true, gate: "open" }`, streaming `16 kHz` PCM immediately and sealing/flushing the `AIKAUD01` WAV packet to Whisper as soon as the operator taps `Done · Send`.
5. **Self-speech suppression cooldown (`800 ms`)**: Inside a `650 g` plastic shell, speaker vibrations couple strongly toward the microphone. During TTS playback—and through a configurable post-playback tail cooldown (`800 ms` `external_speech_ms`)—the gateway and firmware suppress wake-word and VAD triggers so Ainekio never wakes on or transcribes its own voice.

## End-to-end spoken turn flow

1. **Utterance packaging & paired snapshot**: When VAD (or `Tap to Talk`) closes a spoken turn (`≥ 300 ms`), the Host Gateway ([gateway guide](/posts/gateway-and-simulator/)) packages the `16 kHz` PCM stream into an `AIKAUD01` WAV packet and attaches a time-correlated `origin: "audio"` `OV5647` chin-camera still sharing the same `utterance_id`.
2. **Offline-resilient `Whisper` speech-to-text (`:9883`)**: The [embodied bridge](/posts/metahuman-integration/) delivers the audio to Ainekio OS, where `faster-whisper` (seeded with persona hotwords and switchable live between local execution and a LAN GPU workstation) transcribes the utterance. On startup, `whisper_server.py` follows an explicit three-stage load fallback—**`local_files_only=True` → default hub lookup → anonymous `use_auth_token=False`**—so cached checkpoints boot cleanly offline and even a `401`/expired Hugging Face token never blocks startup.
3. **Silent action dispatch, single outward voice**:
   - **`v25/v26` Intent and Task specialist LoRAs** decide whether the turn asks for conversation, physical motion, or both, dispatching bounded postures, on-chip `50 Hz` `MicroDuck v19-snag-p4` walks, or `939`-row Freestyle / `50 Hz` `NeuralPose v8` gestures quietly without injecting robotic progress announcements between steps.
   - The **Standing Persona** synthesizes one natural spoken reply once context or physical verification is ready (while sudden physical tips, shakes, or thermal-cutoff stalls use the fast 6-turn reflex path).
4. **VRAM-aware `Kokoro` (CUDA↔CPU) / `Piper` TTS, LCD lip-sync & `AIKSPK01` text echo**: Ainekio OS renders speech through **`Kokoro` (`:9882`)** with custom `.pt` voicepacks (switchable between local and LAN GPU hosts). Whenever `kokoro_server.py` detects **`< 2,560 MiB` free VRAM** or active local RL/LoRA training processes via `nvidia-smi`, it **switches automatically between its cached CUDA session and CPU ONNX** without dropping speech—backed by async WAV caching and automatic fallback to local **Piper ONNX** when external voice servers are offline. Ordered `AIKSPK01` `16 kHz` `20 ms` PCM chunks stream back through the gateway to the `ESP32-P4` speaker queue—with chunk-1 `audio.speech` packets (`robot-speech.ts` / `speech-transport.ts`) embedding optional **`text` (`<= 2,000` chars) metadata** so spoken robot replies echo cleanly into [Ainekio Cockpit](/posts/ainekio-cockpit/) and [`simulator.ainek.io`](https://simulator.ainek.io/) room chat—while driving `50 Hz` `NeuralPosePresence` pure sagittal speech nods (`±2.5 mm` Z, `±4.5°` pitch, `0.0°` shoulder scrub) alongside live audio-synced speaking bars across the **59 expressions** on the `1.9"` `ST7789` LCD and returning a terminal playback receipt (`done`, `cancelled`, or `outcome_unknown`) when finished.
