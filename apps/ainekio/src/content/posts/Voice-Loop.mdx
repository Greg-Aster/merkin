Ainekio's voice loop connects onboard acoustic capture and speaker playback on the `ESP32-P4` body to speech-to-text (`Whisper`), conversational reasoning (`Qwen3.5 9B` Standing Persona), and text-to-speech synthesis (`Kokoro` + `Piper` fallback) in **[Ainekio OS](/posts/ainekio-os/)** (formerly MetaHuman OS).

## Onboard V2 audio hardware (`ES8311` + `NS4150B`)

Where the V1 `ESP32-S3` prototype wired an external `INMP441` microphone and `MAX98357A` breakout over shared I2S lines, the V2 `Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6` body uses an integrated low-noise audio path:

- **Audio codec & microphone**: Onboard **`ES8311`** low-power mono audio codec sampling at **`16 kHz`, 16-bit mono PCM** (`640 bytes` per `20 ms` frame), buffered in `32 MB` PSRAM.
- **Speaker amplifier**: Onboard **`NS4150B`** Class-D amplifier driving a chassis-mounted **`20 × 30 mm` `8 Ω 2 W` speaker** enclosed in its dedicated printed holder.

## Wake-word detection (`WakeNet9s`) and 5-mode VAD

To support hands-free room interaction without streaming continuous room audio when idle, V2 runs Espressif's embedded speech front-end directly on the `ESP32-P4`:

1. **Espressif `WakeNet9s`**: Replaces the earlier experimental TFLite Micro pilot with **`WakeNet9s`**, providing reliable onboard wake-word detection before opening an upstream utterance stream.
2. **5 VAD sensitivity modes**: Once woken (or when listening is opened continuously in Body Control), voice activity detection (`VAD`) segments speech from background room and servo noise using **five selectable sensitivity modes** (`0–4`) tunable from the operator dashboard.
3. **Configurable `15 s` recording window**: Utterance capture supports a configurable maximum recording window of up to **`15 seconds`**, alongside minimum speech and trailing-silence cutoffs, so longer multi-clause instructions are not clipped mid-sentence.
4. **`external_speech_ms` TTS self-suppression**: Because a compact quadruped chassis couples speaker vibrations toward the microphone, the gateway and firmware coordinate an explicit **`external_speech_ms` suppression window** during and immediately after TTS playback. While speaker PCM is playing—and through the configured `external_speech_ms` tail cooldown—wake detection and VAD capture are suppressed so Ainekio never wakes or transcribes its own voice.

## From spoken utterance to Standing Persona reply

1. **Utterance assembly & paired vision**: When VAD closes an utterance, the Host Gateway packages the `16 kHz` PCM stream as a WAV file and optionally attaches a contemporaneous `OV5647` camera frame sharing the same `utterance_id`.
2. **Local ↔ Remote `Whisper` transcription (`:9883`)**: `environment-bridge` delivers the utterance to Ainekio OS. In the `ServerStatus` right drawer, operators can toggle **`Whisper` STT** per-service between the local CUDA server (`:9883`) and a discovered remote GPU workstation, transcribing the audio into an `audio_utterance` observation entering `environment-mode.json`.
3. **Single outward speech owner (Standing Persona)**:
   - **Orchestrator (Intent)** determines whether the spoken turn calls for a conversational reply, a physical action, or both.
   - **Task Decisions** dispatches any requested robot commands (`Stand`, `Walk`, on-chip MicroDuck twists, `Freestyle`, camera capture, or Big Brother delegation) without injecting robotic step-by-step announcements between movements.
   - **Standing Persona (`persona/core.json`)** owns the single outward response, combining conversational context and action outcomes into Ainekio's natural voice. (For physical tip, shake, or stall reflexes, `fast-body-event-mode.json` queries the last 6 reactions and produces varied situational remarks paired with `be_` LCD faces.)
4. **Paced `Kokoro` TTS (`:9882`), `Piper` fallback, and settlement**: Ainekio OS synthesizes the reply with **`Kokoro` TTS** (`:9882`, toggleable between `local` and `remote` CUDA targets in `ServerStatus` with custom `.pt` voicepacks) or falls back automatically to fast local **`Piper` ONNX** when CUDA voice servers are offline (legacy Kitten, GPT-SoVITS, and RVC paths are retired). The gateway paces `16 kHz` PCM bursts to the `ESP32-P4` speaker queue, sets `external_speech_ms`, drives synchronized talking expressions on the 1.9-inch `ST7789` LCD, and returns the terminal playback receipt (`done`, `cancelled`, or `outcome_unknown`) to the waiting workflow.
