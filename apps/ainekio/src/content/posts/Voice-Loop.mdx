Ainekio's voice loop connects onboard acoustic capture and speaker playback on the `ESP32-P4` V2 body ([`Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot`](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot)) to speech recognition (**Whisper**), persistent conversational reasoning, and expressive speech synthesis (**Kokoro** + **Piper** fallback) in **[Ainekio OS](/posts/ainekio-os/)** ([`Greg-Aster/ainekio-os`](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/ainekio-os)).

## Onboard V2 audio hardware (`ES8311` + `NS4150B`)

Where the V1 `ESP32-S3` prototype wired an external `INMP441` microphone breakout and `MAX98357A` amplifier over shared I2S jumpers, the V2 `Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6` body uses an integrated low-noise audio chain:

- **Audio codec & microphone**: Onboard **`ES8311`** low-power mono codec sampling at **`16 kHz`, 16-bit mono PCM** (`640 bytes` per `20 ms` frame), buffered in `32 MB` PSRAM.
- **Speaker amplifier & enclosure**: Onboard **`NS4150B`** Class-D amplifier driving a chassis-mounted **`20 × 30 mm` `8 Ω 2 W` speaker** seated in a dedicated 3D-printed holder inside the shell.

## Wake-word listening (`WakeNet9s`) and 5-mode VAD

To allow hands-free room conversation without streaming continuous idle room audio over Wi-Fi, V2 runs Espressif's embedded speech front-end directly on the `ESP32-P4`:

1. **On-chip `WakeNet9s` wake detection**: Listens locally on the microcontroller for Ainekio's wake phrase (pairing detection with a subtle `+6°` front-shoulder ear perk) before opening an upstream utterance stream.
2. **5 selectable VAD sensitivity modes**: Once woken—or when continuous listening is enabled in Body Control—voice activity detection (`VAD`) segments speech from background room and servo noise across **five sensitivity levels (`0–4`)**.
3. **Configurable `15 s` utterance window**: Recording supports up to a **`15-second`** spoken window alongside minimum-speech and trailing-silence cutoffs so multi-clause requests are not clipped mid-thought.
4. **Self-speech suppression cooldown**: Inside a `650 g` plastic shell, speaker vibrations couple strongly toward the microphone. During TTS playback—and through a configurable post-playback tail cooldown (`external_speech_ms`)—the gateway and firmware suppress wake-word and VAD triggers so Ainekio never wakes on or transcribes its own voice.

## End-to-end spoken turn flow

1. **Utterance packaging & paired snapshot**: When VAD closes a spoken turn, the Host Gateway ([gateway guide](/posts/gateway-and-simulator/)) assembles the `16 kHz` PCM stream into WAV audio and can attach a contemporaneous `OV5647` chin-camera still sharing the same `utterance_id`.
2. **Local or LAN `Whisper` speech-to-text**: The [embodied bridge](/posts/metahuman-integration/) delivers the audio to Ainekio OS, where **Whisper STT**—switchable live between local execution and a LAN GPU workstation—transcribes the utterance into an attributed spoken observation.
3. **Silent action dispatch, single outward voice**:
   - Intent classification decides whether the turn asks for conversation, physical motion, or both.
   - Task selection dispatches bounded postures, on-chip `50 Hz` MicroDuck walks, or Freestyle gestures quietly without injecting robotic progress announcements between steps.
   - The **Standing Persona** synthesizes one natural spoken reply once context or physical verification is ready (while sudden physical tips, shakes, or stalls use the fast 6-turn reflex path).
4. **Paced `Kokoro` / `Piper` TTS & LCD lip-sync**: Ainekio OS renders speech using **Kokoro TTS** with custom voicepacks (switchable between local and LAN GPU hosts) or falls back automatically to fast local **Piper ONNX** synthesis when GPU voice servers are offline. Ordered `16 kHz` PCM chunks stream back through the gateway to the `ESP32-P4` speaker queue, driving live audio-synced speaking bars across the **59 expressions** on the `1.9"` `ST7789` LCD and returning a terminal playback receipt (`done`, `cancelled`, or `outcome_unknown`) when finished.
