# From the microphone to a spoken response

Ainekio records speech on the robot, sends utterances to MetaHuman for
transcription and conversation, and plays generated speech through its speaker.
V1 has produced audible playback. Reliable repeated conversation in the room
still needs testing, including wake detection, interruptions, and reconnects.

## Listening

```text
Microphone -> S3 capture -> wake gate and voice activity detection
  -> gateway utterance assembly -> WAV -> Environment Bridge
  -> Whisper -> audio_utterance observation -> conversation
```

The microphone produces 16 kHz, 16-bit mono PCM in 20 ms frames of 640 bytes.
The gateway assembles one utterance before sending it for transcription.

A pinned neural voice activity detection (VAD) model uses a minimum capture
window, a silence window, and an absolute maximum duration. A PSRAM queue absorbs
short WebSocket stalls within a fixed memory limit.

At utterance completion, the robot can take a still with the same ID. Environment
Bridge can join it with the audio before submitting the conversation turn. If
the camera is unavailable, the audio proceeds or fails under its own rules;
no image context is invented.

## Wake detection

The microWakeWord/TensorFlow Lite Micro runtime loads an owner-trained package
from LittleFS. It checks the manifest, tensor shape, audio frontend, and model
ID before reporting `wake_ready=true`.

**No production Ainekio wake model has been accepted and tracked.** The initial
configuration therefore leaves wake detection disabled and unavailable.

A pilot ran on the physical robot but produced false triggers. Its staged
threshold was much lower than the documented package default, and the negative
test set was too small. A threshold sweep provided tuning data.

The next evaluation needs hours of room negatives: robot speech, servo noise,
television, fans, and similar-sounding words. It must also record detection
confidence and rearm behavior on the installed controller.

## Speaking

```text
MetaHuman response -> Kokoro or configured synthesis service
  -> PCM artifact -> Environment Bridge -> Ainekio adapter
  -> paced gateway TTS burst -> S3 queue -> I2S speaker
  -> playback result
```

The speech artifact has size limits, session identity, and an action ID. The
adapter sends it through the gateway and returns the robot's playback result
to that action. Successful synthesis or a `sendText` acknowledgement alone
does not establish playback.

The firmware accepts PCM only during an active TTS burst. It reports queue
overflow, interruption, data outside a burst, and completion explicitly. September
adapter work also retains uncertain outcomes when playback may have started
but its final result is missing.

## Physical results and remaining faults

A bounded prebuffer and small blocks paced relative to send time produced clean
audible V1 playback. That run still recorded speaker underruns and separate
microphone transport faults. Those faults remain relevant even when the audio
sounds good.

Full acoustic echo cancellation is absent. The microphone is deliberately
suspended during speech and resumes after a cooldown. Small listening and
speaking motion cues are subject to calibration, stop, Sleep, and fail-safe
rules. Battery ADC warnings do not establish servo-supply readiness; combined
motion/audio loads need physical power tests.

MetaHuman's September changes cover audio/image joining, conversation context,
speech delivery, and saved graph outcomes. They are under integration. Earlier
playback does not validate the revised deployed path. P4 also requires its own
microphone, speaker, camera, and transport implementation.

## Next conversation test

Record a repeated room conversation from wake detection through VAD, utterance
and optional image, Whisper transcript, conversation, generated response, speech
artifact, gateway sequence, and robot playback result. Include wake rearm,
PCM drops, underruns, reconnects, and microphone-resume counters.

Repeatability across those stages is the remaining test for a reliable voice
loop. [Current Status](/posts/current-status/) records the dated progress.
