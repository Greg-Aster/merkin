## DAgger imitation learning on the 650 g Blender assembly

The simulation imports the **650 g** Blender assembly, articulated **35 / 20 / 28 /
38 / 55 mm** four-bar linkages, and boot collision meshes. Rather than predicting raw
coupled servo angles (which caused 100/100 geometric linkage rejections in an early
direct-joint experiment), the neural policy outputs **standing-relative Cartesian foot
offsets**, which the native inverse-kinematics solver converts into valid twelve-joint
targets.

Using **DAgger (Dataset Aggregation)** across 360,000 updates—rolling out the learner,
labeling the states it actually visits with the teacher demonstration's next action,
and annealing teacher intervention to zero—produced a single policy (`Master/gateway/neural_gait`)
covering **five commands**: forward, two reverse speeds, left turn, and right turn.

### Direct simulation vs Body Control + native P4 executor replay

On November 1, we replayed the selected 360k checkpoint across 16-second trials comparing
direct simulation against the full **Body Control WebSocket + native P4 executor**
loop:

| Command | Target velocity | Direct simulation | Body Control + P4 executor |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| **Forward** | `+80.0 mm/s` | `+27.82 mm/s` | `+22.45 mm/s` |
| **Reverse** | `−20.0 mm/s` | `−22.20 mm/s` | `−20.55 mm/s` |
| **Slower reverse** | `−15.0 mm/s` | `−18.28 mm/s` | `−13.48 mm/s` |
| **Left turn** | `+0.120 rad/s` | `+0.128 rad/s` | `+0.021 rad/s` |
| **Right turn** | `−0.120 rad/s` | `−0.148 rad/s` | `−0.113 rad/s` |

Neither path fell or rejected a target, but running through the real command/executor
transport exposed a sharp drop in left-turn authority (`+0.128 → +0.021 rad/s`) and a
~19% drop in forward speed (`+27.82 → +22.45 mm/s`). Tracing the adapter uncovered two
concrete causes—fractional command rounding (truncating `−18.75%` to `−19%`) and
checkpoint evaluation state carryover—both of which are now fixed as we run a 55,000-update
connected DAgger continuation directly through Body Control.

| Training experiment | Outcome | Engineering takeaway |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Higher-quality forward teacher (60 s trial) | `16.40 → 20.96 mm/s` | Teacher trajectory quality directly lifts learner speed |
| 5-command DAgger aggregation | Forward, 2× reverse, left/right turn | Single policy handles full planar command set without falling |
| Warm-started SAC after imitation | Degraded gait even at reduced LR | Untrained SAC critic perturbs the imitation actor before value estimates converge |
| Early PPO velocity reward | High reward with near-zero travel | Rewarded standing still; replaced with coupled linear/yaw tracking + bounded swing credit |

## MetaHuman OS: unified Environment Bridge and Dataset v22 context training

  <img src="/posts/2026-11-01-progress/body-connection.svg" alt="Diagram showing MetaHuman OS connecting through a single Environment Bridge to Body Control and the ESP32-P4." width="1000" height="560" loading="lazy" />

On the software side ([commit `4817fff9`](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/ainekio-os/commit/4817fff97ee0ad34ccfcc6d818b1389f0eb21f1e)):

- **Unified Environment Bridge:** Consolidated the separate Local and Remote bridge
  entrypoints into a single Environment Bridge owner handling Local, SSH, and
  Cloudflare connections, while delegating Freestyle motion generation cleanly to Body
  Control via `requestMotion`.
- **Quieter servo-health telemetry & native `llama.cpp` cancellation:** Fourth-wire
  feedback fault/restored notices now update robot health state and recovery context
  without triggering unsolicited conversational turns, and a native `llama.cpp` patch
  propagates request cancellation directly into in-flight token generation.
- **Intent Dataset Version 22 (`1,803` dev / `485` eval / `46` regression):** Added
  256 development and 128 evaluation autonomy-context examples that vary open activity,
  ongoing work, environmental interest, and inner desires across both Semi Auto and
  Full Auto modes.
