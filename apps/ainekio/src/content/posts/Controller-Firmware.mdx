> **Active V1 firmware reference:** This guide documents the eight-servo **`ESP32-S3`** firmware (`Slave/firmware/esp32s3`) running on the active **[Ainekio V1](/posts/ainekio-v1/)** robot, alongside its `Scene 'v1'` `ESP32-P4` / `PCA9685` / 4th-wire closed-loop upgrade path. For the twelve-servo `ESP32-P4` firmware and host motion stack—including 4th-wire closed-loop feedback, `8–10 s` `I²t` winding thermal protection, open **`microWakeWord` + WebRTC VAD** audio, 59 color LCD expressions, on-chip `50 Hz` `MicroDuck v19-snag-p4` neural walking, `50 Hz` **`NeuralPose v8`** (`neural-pose-v8-calibrated` across the `939`-row clean Freestyle curriculum + `v26` `661`-case / `2,941`-record specialist LoRAs), and **`50 Hz` `NeuralPosePresence`**—see **[Protocol, Telemetry & Safety](/posts/protocol-and-safety/)** and **[Ainekio V2 Hardware](/posts/ainekio-v2/)**.

On the eight-servo **[Ainekio V1](/posts/ainekio-v1/)** quadruped ([V1 vs. V2 comparison](/posts/v1-v2-comparison/)), a **Freenove `ESP32-S3-WROOM CAM`** (`N16R8`, dual-core `160 MHz` Xtensa LX7, `16 MiB` flash, `8 MiB` octal PSRAM) drives eight `MG90S` servos, an `OV3660` parallel camera, full-duplex `16 kHz` I2S audio (`INMP441` mic + `MAX98357A` speaker amp), and a `0.96"` `SSD1306` OLED face display (**59 expressions**). **[Ainekio OS](/posts/ainekio-os/)** handles conversation, memory, and planning over Wi-Fi while the `ESP32-S3` firmware validates `protocol-v1` commands and owns local safety.

When asked to `"wave"`, for example, the V1 controller checks battery and lease state in its portable C11 core, applies per-joint trim (`±30°`) and min/max angle clamps, steps all eight `MCPWM` channels through the stored `.amot` clip, captures a correlated post-action `OV3660` JPEG still, and only then emits the final `done` receipt—while keeping emergency `stop` and disconnect watchdogs completely independent of media traffic.

## Portable C11 `ainekio_core` & `MCPWM` motion engine

V1 splits its **ESP-IDF v5.5.4** codebase in [`Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot`](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot) into two strict layers:

1. **`Slave/software/core` (host-tested C11):** Owns `protocol-v1` command validation, state lifecycles, command leases, per-joint calibration, `.amot` / `motion_plan_v1` interpolation, and battery thresholds (`7.2 V` warn / `7.0 V` motion refusal / `6.8 V` critical—telemetry-only without deep-sleep cutoff) with zero FreeRTOS or ESP-IDF headers.
2. **`Slave/firmware/esp32s3/components/ainekio_platform`:** Owns FreeRTOS tasks, Wi-Fi + single-socket `protocol-v1` WebSocket transport, `MCPWM` timers, `OV3660` DVP camera, `SSD1306` OLED, full-duplex I2S audio, `LittleFS` / `SPIFFS` / `SD_MMC`, SoftAP provisioning, and dual-slot OTA.

Eight hardware **`MCPWM`** channels generate `50 Hz` servo pulses (`732–2500 µs` window, `1500 µs` = `90°` neutral, attached in staggered `20 ms` steps `R1, R2, L1, L2, R4, R3, L3, L4` at boot) so servo timing never collides with the `LEDC` `XCLK` timer required by the camera. Both the **38 stored `.amot` assets** (`3` postures, `5` continuous gaits, `8` timed `45°/90°/180°` open-loop scrub turns, and `22` gestures/dances) and dynamic **`motion_plan_v1`** plans (`1–32` keyframes, `≤ 20 s`) execute through the same clamp and instant-preemption pipeline.

## `OV3660` vision, 52→59 `SSD1306` OLED expressions, and `vadnet1_medium` I2S audio

- **Manual-exposure `OV3660` `XGA` stills before `done`:** Delivered Freenove `N16R8` boards shipped with an **`OV3660`** sensor (superseding early `OV2640` drafts). Firmware captured `XGA` (`1024×768`, JPEG `q=10`) stills using a manual **`72`-line (`~1/60 s`) indoor exposure profile** (`gainceiling=2x`) that eliminated motion blur and early high-gain cross-hatch artifacts, transmitting correlated post-action frames (`0x02` binary WebSocket prefix) *before* sending the motion's `done` acknowledgement.
- **52 Summer 2026 baseline OLED faces + deterministic `derive_v1_oled.py` pipeline across all `59` V2 expressions (`faces-v1.json`, `303` `.bin` frames):** Because free pins were scarce, the `0.96"` `128×64` OLED (`0x3C`) used `GPIO0` (`SDA`) and `GPIO43` (`SCL` / `UART0 TX`). After early boot logs completed, firmware cleanly handed `GPIO43` over to `400 kHz` I2C—automatically reverting to `UART0` serial logging if no `0x3C` panel acknowledged on the bench. Onboard `LittleFS` originally shipped with **52 `1024`-byte face bitmaps** in the Summer 2026 baseline (with `3.6–6.0 s` idle blinking and a TTS-synchronized `talk` mouth overlay). Today, the deterministic **`derive_v1_oled.py`** pipeline performs a **`2.5×` isotropic crop** from V2's procedural C `face_preview` renderer to generate **`faces-v1.json`** and all **`59` 1-bit `SSD1306` expressions (`303` animated `.bin` frames)**, giving V1 and V2 complete facial vocabulary parity over `protocol-v1`.

- **Full-duplex `16 kHz` I2S audio & neural VAD:** Assigning `GPIO19/20` to `INMP441` mic data-in and `MAX98357A` speaker data-out intentionally traded native USB-OTG (`D-/D+`) for clean digital audio while preserving the board's dedicated `CH343` USB-UART port for flashing. Incoming mic audio ran through Espressif's **`vadnet1_medium`** neural voice-activity detector (loaded from a `512 KiB` `/srmodels` `SPIFFS` partition with a `200 ms` PSRAM pre-roll ring). A TensorFlow Lite Micro (`microWakeWord`) scaffold was also compiled in, paving the way for V2's active **`microWakeWord` (`pilot-v1` `int8` on TFLite Micro) + `esp-sr` WebRTC VAD** pipeline ([Listening & Speech](/posts/voice-loop/)).

## Unified multi-model `ESP32-P4` firmware (`v1-8servo`, `v2-12servo` & `v3-12servo`)

Alongside the active `ESP32-S3` direct-`MCPWM` floor firmware documented above, `Slave/firmware/esp32p4-wifi6` now compiles as a **unified multi-model `ESP32-P4` firmware codebase** spanning all three Ainekio generations ([Milestone #21](/posts/2026-11-23-unified-p4-firmware-v1-v3-simulator-multiplayer-and-auto-calibration/)):

- **V1 target (`sdkconfig.v1-8servo` — verified binary `build-v1/ainekio_p4_body.bin`):** Pairs **`body_v1.c`** (`1,062` lines) and **`display_ssd1306.c`** (`0.96"` `128×64` I2C OLED moved to `SCL=GPIO26`, `SDA=GPIO27` so `GPIO20–23` remain free for analog input) with **8 direct `ESP32-P4` ADC channels (no `CD74HC4067` analog mux required)**: `ADC1 CH4..7` (`GPIO20..23`, `J0..J3`) and `ADC2 CH0..3` (`GPIO49..52`, `J4..J7`) routed through `Scene 'v1'`'s concentric under-arm 4-wire clock-springs, plus `PCA9685` (`I2C1+OE`), `GY-LSM6DS3` IMU (`I2C0`), `microWakeWord` + WebRTC VAD audio, and trained `v1-microduck-p4` / `neural_pose_v1` support (live in [`simulator.ainek.io`](https://simulator.ainek.io/#model=v1); see the [full 59-expression OLED contact sheet in the V1 Systems Guide](/posts/ainekio-v1/#4-esp32-s3-baseline-firmware-gpio-map-38-amot-motions--59-oled-faces)).
- **V2 default target (`v2-12servo`):** Runs **`body.c`** with 12× `MG90S` `PCA9685` channels, `CD74HC4067` 4th-wire mux (`C4–C15` → `GPIO49`, `S0–S3` on `GPIO27–30`), `ST7789` `320×170` color LCD, `I²t` per-joint thermal fuse, and Core 1 `50 Hz` `MicroDuck v19-snag-p4` ([V2 Hardware Reference](/posts/ainekio-v2/)).
- **V3 target (`sdkconfig.v3-12servo` — verified `2.1 MB` compiled binary):** Runs **`body_v3.c`** for 12× `DS3225 PRO` HV coreless servos (`Option A-1` + self-leveling crawler feet), **`display_ili9341.c`** (`2.8"` `MSP2807` `320×240` SPI TFT centering the `320×170` canvas), and the procedural panoramic **`Slave/software/faces/face_cyclops.c`** (`964` lines; live in [`simulator.ainek.io`](https://simulator.ainek.io/#model=v3)).

## SoftAP provisioning, `16 MiB` flash layout & dual-OTA rollback

On an unprovisioned boot, V1 hosted a WPA2 **`Ainekio-Setup`** SoftAP (`192.168.4.1`) with a per-device key, saving Wi-Fi credentials into A/B `NVS` namespaces so network resets never erased servo calibration trims or battery divider constants.

Flash (`16 MiB`) was partitioned into **two `3 MiB` OTA slots (`ota_0` / `ota_1`)**, **`9.375 MiB` `LittleFS` (`/assets`)**, and **`512 KiB` `SPIFFS` (`/srmodels`)**, backed by optional 1-bit `SD_MMC` rolling CRC diagnostic logs on microSD. Any newly flashed OTA image remained tentative until it completed authenticated `protocol-v1` gateway handshake, rolling back automatically on a bad boot.

## V1 `ESP32-S3 N16R8` GPIO & peripheral map

> **Warning:** The GPIO table below applies **only** to the V1 Freenove `ESP32-S3 N16R8` build. Do **not** copy these pin numbers onto the V2 `Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6` body (which uses `PCA9685` I2C PWM and `CD74HC4067` feedback on `GPIO49`; see [V2 Hardware & Pinout](/posts/ainekio-v2/)).

| V1 Subsystem | `ESP32-S3` GPIOs | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eight `MG90S` servos (`MCPWM` `50 Hz`) | `1`, `2`, `14`, `21` (hips `R1,R2,L1,L2`) · `47`, `48`, `45`, `46` (knees `R4,R3,L3,L4`) | `732–2500 µs`; remapped off `GPIO33/34` |
| `0.96"` `SSD1306` OLED (`I2C` `400 kHz`) | `SDA 0`, `SCL 43` (`0x3C`) | Runtime handoff from `UART0 TX` (`GPIO43`) |
