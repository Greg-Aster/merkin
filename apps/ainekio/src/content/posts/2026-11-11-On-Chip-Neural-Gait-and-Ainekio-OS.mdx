Between November 7 and November 11, development across [`Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot`](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot) and [`Greg-Aster/ainekio-os`](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/ainekio-os) crossed two major architectural thresholds:

1. **Neural locomotion moved off the Wi-Fi link and directly onto the `ESP32-P4` microcontroller (`0.8.159-p4-microduck-v17`)**: Our GPU-trained **MicroDuck** proprioceptive policy (`720` inputs from `300 ms` of 4th-wire potentiometer and `208 Hz` `LSM6DS3` IMU history) is now compiled to symmetric `int8` (`policy.bin`) and distilled via **2-round DAgger** from `547,040 B` (`512-256-128`) down to **`110,432 B` (`107.8 KB`, `128-64-32`)**—fitting completely inside the **`ESP32-P4`'s `128 KB` L2 cache** and running native closed-loop `walk` at **`50 Hz` on Core 1** with zero host or UDP dependency.
2. **MetaHuman OS publicly became [Ainekio OS (`Greg-Aster/ainekio-os`)](/posts/ainekio-os/)**: Consolidating 150+ route shims into a single 3-line Astro API adapter (`123` Core handlers), moving all **12 specialist model roles** into a unified right-drawer **`ServerStatus`** compute/voice plane (`Local` / `Hybrid` / `BB-Codex` / `BB-Gemini` presets + remote `llama.cpp`, Whisper, and Kokoro targets), adding a warm 2-session **`GeminiAcpServer`** daemon, and introducing durable 6-turn varied **Fast Body Event** reactions.

## 1. Compiling neural gait onto `ESP32-P4` Core 1 (`ae131f9` → `0.8.159-p4-microduck-v17`)

In the [November 6 milestone](/posts/2026-11-06-carpet-trials-rl-and-big-brother/), physical neural walking still relied on the Python host gateway streaming `50 Hz` `gait` frames over Wi-Fi UDP (`walk->host_nominal = true`). Any Wi-Fi jitter stretched the `20 ms` control loop.

In commit `ae131f9` (`2026-11-09`) and subsequent distillation runs through **`microduck-v17-p4`** (`2026-11-11`), we embedded neural policy inference directly inside `Slave/software/models/v2-12servo/microduck.c` and `Slave/firmware/esp32p4-wifi6/main/body.c` while deleting `-831` lines of legacy phase-residual learning (`gait_learning.c`):

- **Zero-dependency ONNX-to-C compiler (`compile_microduck.py`)**: At CMake build time, `compile_microduck.py` parses `policy.onnx`, extracts the `720`-element observation `mean` and `std` vectors, symmetrically quantizes each linear layer's weights per output row to `int8_t` (`scale[row] = max_abs / 127.0f`), packs `"AMDK"` `policy.bin`, and emits 16-byte-aligned `microduck_data.c`.
- **`50 Hz` integer MAC on Core 1 (`microduck.c` + `body.c`)**: Pinned to **Core 1** (`output_task`, `20,000 µs` period), the firmware normalizes the `720`-float causal ring buffer (`15 × 48` frames = `300 ms`), dynamically quantizes each layer's input vector to `int16_t`, and executes an **8-way loop-unrolled `int8 × int16 → int32` dot product** with `ELU` hidden activations and `tanh` outputs.
- **Exact 48-float per-frame proprioceptive vector (`ainekio-neural-gait-v7-proprio`)**:
  - `[0..11]`: 12 measured joint positions (`rad`, relative to calibrated neutral) from the 4th-wire `CD74HC4067` ADC multiplexer through the firmware One-Euro filter.
  - `[12..23]`: 12 measured joint velocities (`rad/s`) from the One-Euro derivative channel.
  - `[24..26]`: 3-axis body angular velocity (`rad/s`) from the `208 Hz` `LSM6DS3` gyroscope.
  - `[27..29]`: 3-axis linear acceleration (`g`) from the `LSM6DS3` accelerometer.
  - `[30..32]`: Negative projected gravity (`-g_body`) from the yaw-zeroed Madgwick `FusionAhrs` quaternion.
  - `[33..35]`: Commanded body twist `[vx, vy, wz]` (`-0.22..+0.20 m/s` on firmware, `±0.08 m/s` strafe, `±1.0 rad/s` turn).
  - `[36..47]`: Previous step's normalized `12D` action (`[-1, 1]`).
- **Closed-chain 4-bar safety fit**: Raw `[-1, 1]` outputs scale by `[45.0, 28.0, 30.0] mm` (`× stride_scale` / `z_scale`) into `[RL, RR, FL, FR]` foot offsets and pass through `ainekio_v2_nominal_foot_pose()`—solving analytical 4-bar IK with **8-pass Gauss-Newton Jacobian boundary projection** and **13 dyadic step-shortening fractions** before `PCA9685` PWM output. Camera UDP chunking in `controller.c` also yields immediately whenever the `20 ms` telemetry deadline arrives, and stand-range `[500, 2800] µs` false alerts are suppressed during wide neural strides.

## 2. GPU hardware twins & 2-round DAgger distillation into the `128 KB` L2 cache

To ensure policies trained at **4,096 parallel MuJoCo environments** transfer cleanly onto `$1.50` feedback-modified `MG90S` servos, we built exact GPU PyTorch twins of the C firmware (`Master/gateway/neural_gait/tensor_sensors.py` and `tensor_body.py`, cross-checked against C via `native.c` ctypes):

- **Simulated electrical & mechanical imperfections**: `tensor_sensors.py` models 12-bit ratiometric 4th-wire ADC quantization, **supply-rail sag (`0.96–1.02` + load droop)**, `1.5%` `60 ms` stale-sample holds, One-Euro filtering, `LSM6DS3` white noise/bias walk/clipping, and exact `FusionAhrs` integration. `tensor_body.py` models closed-chain `35/20/28/38/55 mm` 4-bar couplers, `PCA9685` `4.07 µs` tick quantization, weak-corner sag, and rear `2S` battery load compliance across **3 friction regimes (`0.14–1.45`: 45% slick wood, 35% wood/carpet split seam, 20% carpet)** plus **cable ledges (`21–40 mm` clearance)**.

The initial on-chip `512-256-128` actor (`547,040 B` in `ae131f9`) exceeded the `ESP32-P4`'s `128 KB` L2 cache by `4.2×`, forcing PSRAM traffic every `20 ms`. Adding **2-round DAgger distillation** (`_distill_from_teacher()` in `microduck.py`, `MAE = 0.0577`) compressed the actor to **`720 → 128 → 64 → 32 → 12`** (**`110,432 B` / `107.8 KB`**, a **`79.8%` reduction** that stays L2-cache-hot) while PPO continuation (`v14-p4` → `v17-p4`) actually **outperformed** the larger teacher:

In parallel, we added a **Stanford Pupper V3 MJX host actor** (`import_pupper_mjx.py`, `pupper_mjx_actor.py`) trained through `600M` and `800M` steps (`804.6M` env steps) and ran **13 physical floor bouts** on `ainekio-p4-e6ebe5` (`2026-11-09`) across slick hardwood (6 bouts: forward, reverse, and four turning arcs; peak roll `5.4°–15.4°`, zero falls) and **carpet↔hardwood transitions** (7 bouts crossing and straddling the seam; zero falls).

## 3. Body Control Cockpit HUD, optical-flow range & `489`-row Freestyle recovery

To instrument and drive both on-chip and host policies cleanly, `Master/gateway` added several operator upgrades (`ae131f9` & `1779b9c`):

- **Ainekio Cockpit HUD (`data-workspace="cockpit"`)**: Full-viewport flight-style drive view combining live `OV5647` video, WASD/gamepad twist controls, posture/emote triggers, speech controls, and a persistent top-bar **Ainekio OS / MetaHuman control** safety toggle (`#quick-behavior-enabled`) plus an **On-chip MicroDuck (`native`) vs. Host neural gait (`neural`)** walk-mode selector.
- **Optical-flow ground-contact range (`depth_motion.py`)**: Fuses pyramidal Lucas-Kanade optical flow (`cv2.calcOpticalFlowPyrLK`, up to 36 features) anchored at the lowest obstacle ground-contact point with relative inverse depth and forward displacement for live obstacle range readouts on the Cockpit HUD.
- **No-login local mode & hotspot fallback (`1779b9c`)**: Added `AINEKIO_DASHBOARD_NO_LOGIN` automatic `HttpOnly; SameSite=Strict` session minting for trusted local benches, plus automatic concrete-unit fallback in `hotspot.py` when `ainekio-hotspot.target` is absent.
- **`489`-row Freestyle `motion-targets-v1` dataset**: Expanded closed-loop jam/slip semantic teachers (`library_recovery.py`, `semantic_teachers.py`) across all `23 / 23` eligible base motions (`489` rows including `416` synthesized fault-recovery trajectories) and migrated recovery to native closed-loop `stand`.

## 5. MetaHuman OS becomes Ainekio OS (`Greg-Aster/ainekio-os`)

On the cognitive side (`2dc83693`, `96e74c01`, and November 11 runtime updates), the companion brain repository was publicly rebranded to **[Ainekio OS (`Greg-Aster/ainekio-os`)](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/ainekio-os)** and streamlined across its API, compute, and reflex layers:

- **Single Core API router & dead-path cleanup (`2dc83693`)**: Replaced 150+ Astro route shims with a 3-line catch-all `[...path].ts` adapter backed by `123` Core handler modules, added the live `/architecture` Svelte-Flow System Map, and deleted legacy RVC voice-training and obsolete skill wrappers.
- **Right-drawer `ServerStatus` & remote compute/voice targets (`96e74c01`)**: Moved all **12 specialist model roles** into `ServerStatus.svelte` with **`Local`**, **`Hybrid`**, **`BB-Codex`**, and **`BB-Gemini`** presets, remote `llama.cpp` discovery across LAN/Cloudflare nodes, and per-service **`local` ↔ `remote`** toggling on CUDA **Whisper (`:9883`)** and **Kokoro (`:9882`)** servers (with fast local Piper ONNX fallback).
- **Warm `GeminiAcpServer` daemon & direct cloud multimodal routing**: Added a persistent `agy acp` JSON-RPC daemon (`gemini-acp-server.ts`) maintaining **2 pre-warmed sessions** for sub-second multimodal subscription turns alongside `codex`, `claude-code`, and direct Gemini/Claude/OpenRouter REST providers.
- **Varied durable Fast Body Event reactions**: `fast-body-event-mode.json` now queries the last **6 non-cancelled `fast_body_event_response` speech turns** from `executions.sqlite` (`temperature = 0.8`, `repeatPenalty = 1.2`) so tip, shake, and servo-stall reflexes stay fresh and dryly observant.
