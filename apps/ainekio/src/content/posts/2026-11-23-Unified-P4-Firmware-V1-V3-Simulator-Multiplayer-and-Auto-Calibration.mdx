Over November 21–23, development converged across the entire stack—turning the standalone browser simulator at **[`simulator.ainek.io`](https://simulator.ainek.io/)** from a single-robot proof-of-concept into a **full three-generation multiplayer testbed**, unifying **V1, V2, and V3** into a **single `ESP32-P4` firmware codebase**, and overhauling **Body Control** and **Ainekio OS** with automated servo calibration, a dedicated Virtual Simulation Manager, and unified room chat:

1. **All Three Robots (`V1`, `V2`, `V3`) Live in [`simulator.ainek.io`](https://simulator.ainek.io/) + PeerJS Multiplayer & 19 m Robotics Lab:** Visitors can hot-switch live between **Ainekio V1** (8-servo clock-spring, `v1-microduck-p4`, `neural_pose_v1`), **Ainekio V2** (12-servo four-bar, `microduck-v19-snag-p4`, `neural-pose-v7-hysteresis`, compiled `ainekio-v2-firmware.wasm`), and **Ainekio V3** (12-servo `DS3225 PRO` Option A-1 + self-leveling crawler feet, panoramic Cyclops optic, `v3-microduck-p4`, `neural_pose_v3`), host or join **PeerJS WebRTC multiplayer rooms**, and drive across six worlds anchored by a **19 m multi-zone Robotics Lab obstacle course**.
2. **Unified Multi-Model `ESP32-P4` Firmware (`v1-8servo` · `v2-12servo` · `v3-12servo`) & Panoramic V3 Cyclops Optic:** One `ESP32-P4` firmware tree now compiles verified binaries for all three robots (`sdkconfig.v1-8servo`, default `v2-12servo`, `sdkconfig.v3-12servo`), introducing **direct 8-channel P4 ADC feedback (no analog mux)** on V1 and the **`face_cyclops.c` panoramic single-eye optic** (`2.8"` `ILI9341` SPI TFT + simulator WASM) on V3.
3. **V3 Mechanical Evolution (`DS3225 PRO` HV, Double-Parallelogram Self-Leveling Ankles & Trained RL):** Reconciled V3 (`v3-p4-ds3225pro-optA1-ankle-20261122`, `406.4 × 236.5 × 385.6 mm`, `2.97 kg` est.) around `35 kg·cm` `DS3225 PRO` HV coreless servos, PETG spiral knee springs (`0.167 N·m/rad`), `22 N` preloaded servo-saver rods, and **1:1 self-leveling double-parallelogram pivoting crawler feet** (`<= 0.27°` level error across `8/8` MuJoCo evaluation scenarios at `0.31 m/s` forward trot).
4. **Body Control Automated Servo Calibration, Virtual Simulation Manager & Zero-Pinning Ainekio OS Bridge:** Shipped closed-loop **Auto-Calibration (`auto_calibration.py`)** (`floor` and `legs_free` modes with `25°` tilt abort), replaced legacy daemon scripts with the **Virtual Simulation Manager (`simulation.py`, `:8792`, `ainekio-pair:`, 24/7 headless runner)**, removed `robotId` pinning in Ainekio OS so it auto-follows whichever physical or virtual robot Body Control selects, and bridged **Cockpit text input + `AIKSPK01` spoken text echo** into simulator room chat.
5. **Standalone `@ainekio/engine@v0.1.0` Extracted:** Extracted the shared ECS, Three.js, Rapier, WebRTC multiplayer, and robot-audio core into its own repository ([`Greg-Aster/ainekio-engine`](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/ainekio-engine)), cutting duplicated engine code out of `game.megameal`.

### PeerJS Multiplayer Rooms, Mobile Data Optimization & World Upgrades
- **WebRTC P2P Multiplayer (`src/multiplayer/*`, `remotePeerManager.ts`):** Click **Multiplayer** in the top bar to host or join a room, share a `?room=<name>` link, jump straight to another peer's world, and chat in real time. ## 2. Unified Multi-Model `ESP32-P4` Firmware & V3 Panoramic Cyclops Optic

Rather than maintaining separate firmware forks for V1, V2, and V3, `Slave/firmware/esp32p4-wifi6/` now builds **all three robot models from one unified `ESP32-P4` codebase** using `Kconfig.projbuild` (`AINEKIO_ROBOT_MODEL`, `AINEKIO_DISPLAY_BACKEND`, `AINEKIO_FEEDBACK_BACKEND`) and model overlays:

- **`sdkconfig.v1-8servo` (`body_v1.c`, `1,062` lines, `2.1 MB` compiled binary verified):** Drives 8× `MG90S` servos on `PCA9685` channels `0..7`, renders the `0.96"` `128×64` I2C OLED (`display_ssd1306.c`, `SCL=GPIO26`, `SDA=GPIO27`), and reads all 8 fourth-wire servo potentiometers on **8 direct `ESP32-P4` ADC pins with zero analog multiplexer** (`ADC1 CH4..7` on `GPIO20..23` for `J0..J3`, `ADC2 CH0..3` on `GPIO49..52` for `J4..J7`).
- **Default `v2-12servo` (`body.c`, `display.c`, `3.5 MB` verified):** Continues driving the active `650 g` 12-servo floor robot with `CD74HC4067` 12-channel mux feedback, `1.9"` `ST7789` LCD, and on-chip `50 Hz` `MicroDuck`.
- **`sdkconfig.v3-12servo` (`body_v3.c`, `1,012` lines, `2.1 MB` verified):** Drives 12× **`DS3225 PRO` HV coreless** servos (`35 kg·cm / 3.43 N·m`, `800 deg/s` on direct `2S` `7.4 V`), **`display_ili9341.c`** (`348` lines, `2.8"` `MSP2807` `320×240` 40 MHz SPI TFT centering the `320×170` canvas), and the brand-new **`face_cyclops.c`** (`964` lines) panoramic Cyclops optic—featuring a luminous outer iris ring with 24 mechanical aperture ticks, radial iris fibers, glossy dark pupil with dual specular glints, mechanical eyelid/brow shutters, twin flanking radar arcs, and a 16-second ambient saccadic glance/autofocus cycle compiled into both P4 firmware and simulator WASM.

### V3 Self-Leveling Crawler Feet & Trained RL Results
Reconciled under geometry `v3-p4-ds3225pro-optA1-ankle-20261122` (`406.4 × 236.5 × 385.6 mm`, `180.93 mm` shoulder height, `125.03 mm` belly clearance, `2.97 kg` estimated mass; physical print pending), each V3 leg pairs the Option A-1 four-bar (`54 / 22 / 46 / 56 / 135 mm`) with a **PETG flat spiral knee spring** (`~0.167 N·m/rad`), a **`~22 N` preloaded PETG servo-saver rod** (`±1.3 mm`), and a **2-stage double-parallelogram self-leveling pivoting crawler foot** (`1:1` flat sole, `±27.5°` range + outboard compliant damper).

In CPU MuJoCo qualification (`v3-microduck-p4` pass 6b, `66,940 B` int8 C policy; pass 7 training in progress), V3 survived **8/8 scenarios** with **`max_ankle_level_err_deg <= 0.27°`** across every run (`0.310 m/s` forward trot with `97.2 mm` stride and `40.3 mm` foot lift, `±1.33 rad/s` turns, `±0.15 m/s` crab). `neural_pose_v3` (`1.52 MB`, `0.0` L↔R reflection error) cleared **8/8 postures** at **`0.28°–1.11°` MAE**. V1's corresponding `v1-microduck-p4` (`53,696 B` int8 C, `17.2M` steps) and `neural_pose_v1` (`1.02 MB`, `0.0` reflection error) also cleared **8/8 gait and 8/8 posture suites**.

## 3. Body Control Auto-Calibration, Virtual Simulation Manager & Unified Comms

Three major host-side upgrades landed across `Ainekio-bot` (`Master/gateway/`) and `ainekio-os` (`ce743e7e` + Nov 23):

1. **Automated 4th-Wire + IMU Servo Calibration (`Master/gateway/auto_calibration.py`, `/api/calibration/auto`):**
   - Replaces tedious manual per-joint offset tweaking with a guided automated workflow inside Body Control (`#auto-calibration-card`).
   - Supports **`floor`** mode (low-friction surface: full 4th-wire range sweep + IMU/shaft-load 4-leg height & shoulder leveling) and **`legs_free`** mode (unloaded 4th-wire range sweep), executing exact `0.00°` closure-error closed-chain sweep poses behind a **`25.0°` tilt abort guard**, with one-click `Apply` and `Restore`.
2. **Virtual Simulation Manager (`Master/gateway/simulation.py`, `/api/simulation`):**
   - Retires the old `simulator_bridge.py` / `start-virtual-robot.sh` daemon scripts.
   - Serves `simulator.ainek.io` locally on dedicated port **`:8792`**, mints scoped virtual robot tokens (`ainekio-v1-sim`, `ainekio-v2-sim`, `ainekio-v3-sim`), generates zero-log `#hash` launch URLs and **`ainekio-pair:` Quick Pair codes** (supporting both local `ws://127.0.0.1:8790/robot` and the `wss://robot-gateway.ainek.io/robot` Cloudflare relay), and supervises a **24/7 headless Chromium runner** on the Brain host so autonomous virtual robots remain online even when operator browsers close.
3. **Zero-Pinning Ainekio OS Auto-Follow & Unified Room Chat / Spoken Echo:**
   - Commit `ce743e7e` in `ainekio-os` removed `robotId` pinning: Body Control now solely owns active robot selection, and Ainekio OS automatically follows whichever physical (`V1`/`V2`) or virtual (`ainekio-v*-sim`) body is attached on the same bridge stream without restarting (with `1,000 ms` base / `2,500 ms` local-unconnected reconnect backoff and dead `correction_failed` stall fields retired).
   - Added a direct text-input bar (`#cockpit-stream-form`) in Cockpit HUD, unified **Comms Feed** + top-bar **Tap to Talk** in `simulator.ainek.io`, and **`AIKSPK01` `speechText` (`<= 2,000` chars)** metadata on downward robot TTS packets so spoken replies echo cleanly into Cockpit and multiplayer room chat.
