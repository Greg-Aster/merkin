Ainekio's removable front face carrier ([`Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot`](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot)) pairs outward expression and forward perception in one compact nose assembly: on V2, a **1.9-inch `ST7789` color IPS LCD** (`320 × 170`) rendering **59 animated facial expressions**, paired with an **`OV5647` MIPI-CSI camera** tucked into the **lower-jaw / chin mount below the LCD** for action-correlated snapshots, Apache-2.0 **`RT-DETRv2 + OWLv2`** open-vocabulary detection, **`YuNet + SFace`** face recognition, **`MoGe-2`** corridor braking, camera odometry, and an 8-sector spatial curiosity map.

## Face displays across generations: V1 `SSD1306`, V2 `ST7789` & V3 Cyclops optic

Outward facial telemetry scales cleanly across all three hardware generations while sharing the same expression state model:

- **V1 (`0.96"` `SSD1306` OLED)**: High-contrast monochrome `I²C` eyes and status icons on the eight-servo `ESP32-S3` build.
- **V2 (`1.9"` `ST7789` color IPS LCD, `320 × 170`)**: Sits in a recessed red front bezel supported by four printed wireless spacers so Wi-Fi antenna clearance stays unobstructed. Onboard C firmware drives **59 procedural expression animations** at `15 fps`—covering idle blinking, directional eye glances, curiosity, sleep/doze states, live audio-synced voice bars during speech playback, Freestyle gesture accompaniments, `cosmic_horror`, and token-matched **body-contact reaction faces** when the robot is tipped, shaken, or stalled.
- **V3 (panoramic `2.8"` `ILI9341` Cyclops optic, `face_cyclops.c`)**: Renders a **`320 × 170` active widescreen canvas** centered inside the `320 × 240` SPI TFT, replacing twin cartoon eyes with a single sweeping mechanical Cyclops iris ringed by **24 aperture ticks** and flanked by live telemetry **radar arcs**.

## Chin-mounted `OV5647` MIPI-CSI camera

On V2 (`Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6`), vision moves from V1's parallel `OV3660` module on the `ESP32-S3` to a higher-throughput **`OV5647` MIPI-CSI sensor** mounted in the lower jaw directly beneath the `1.9"` LCD:

- **Lower-jaw routing & `180°` image rotation**: Seating the sensor in the chin mount below the LCD gives a clean floor-and-room sightline across full stance pitch and keeps the CSI flex ribbon clear of the shoulder servos; because the sensor PCB sits inverted in the chin pocket, the image pipeline rotates frames **`180°`** so all snapshots, Cockpit HUD views, and tracking boxes arrive upright.
- **`10,000 µs` (`10 ms`) motion-safe exposure ceiling**: Standard indoor auto-exposure algorithms stretch shutter times in dim rooms, smearing frames whenever the robot walks or turns. The V2 camera pipeline enforces a **`10,000 µs` maximum exposure cap**, keeping edges crisp during active `50 Hz` walking and visual odometry.
- **On-device microSD clip recording**: Alongside live JPEG streaming, the `ESP32-P4` can record segmented video clips directly to its onboard microSD slot for later review in Body Control.

## Action-correlated vision in Ainekio OS

Instead of flooding a multimodal model with continuous room video, the gateway binds still captures to specific events so **[Ainekio OS](/posts/ainekio-os/)** ([embodied bridge](/posts/metahuman-integration/)) always knows *when* an image was taken and *which physical step* it verifies:

| Capture Trigger | Correlation Key | Role in Cognition & Control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Explicit snapshot** | `action_id` | On-demand visual look requested by Ainekio OS or the human operator |
| **Post-motion verification frame** | `command_sequence_id` | Captured right before a movement's `done` receipt so Goal Review can confirm whether the physical move achieved its visual goal |
| **Spoken utterance end (VAD)** | `utterance_id` | Pairs what someone said in the room with what Ainekio was looking at during that turn |
| **Cockpit HUD, tracking & odometry** | `frame_id` + timestamp | Feeds Apache-2.0 `RT-DETRv2 + OWLv2` object tracking, `YuNet + SFace` face recognition, `MoGe-2` corridor braking, `SOLVEPNP_IPPE` / `PyrLK` odometry, and the 8-sector spatial curiosity map |

Visual locomotion goals require actual camera confirmation through Ainekio OS's vision specialist role rather than assuming a finished walk command automatically reached the target.

## Apache-2.0 detection, face recognition, `MoGe-2` depth, odometry & spatial curiosity

On the host gateway ([gateway guide](/posts/gateway-and-simulator/)), the chin camera drives an all-Apache-2.0 perception and spatial-measurement stack during live operation and walking:

1. **Open-vocabulary object detection & tracking (`RT-DETRv2 + OWLv2`)**: **`RT-DETRv2 + OWLv2`** (`detector_backend.py`) provides Apache-2.0 open-vocabulary detection and multi-frame tracking so Ainekio can search for, lock onto, and follow a specific person or object across frames—halting motion immediately if visual lock is lost during person-following.
2. **Face recognition (`YuNet + SFace`)**: **`YuNet + SFace`** extracts `128-D` embeddings against an enrolled face gallery of up to **`16` people**, supporting `single_person_resume` so Ainekio recognizes returning humans and resumes person-focused context automatically.
3. **Metric depth, 2-ray RANSAC & `< 0.30 m` corridor braking (`MoGe-2` + SIFT)**: **`MoGe-2` (`ViT-L` FP16)** paired with **`2,000`-keypoint SIFT** and a **100-hypothesis 2-ray RANSAC** solver supplies metric floor/obstacle geometry and **`< 0.30 m` forward corridor braking**. Heavy metric depth weights load on demand while an active locomotion goal or gait trial needs visual distance verification, unloading automatically afterward so workstation VRAM stays free for conversational models.
4. **Camera odometry (`SOLVEPNP_IPPE` + `PyrLK`)**: **Checkerboard `SOLVEPNP_IPPE`** calibration paired with **pyramidal Lucas-Kanade (`PyrLK`) optical flow** fuses with `208 Hz` `LSM6DS3` IMU attitude priors to estimate body-frame forward displacement and yaw progress.
5. **8-sector (`8 × 45°`) spatial curiosity map**: `spatial_curiosity.py` continuously projects detected objects, recognized `YuNet + SFace` identities, and free-floor clearance into eight `45°` compass sectors, feeding **[Ainekio OS](/posts/ainekio-os/)**'s System 1 Embodied Curiosity loop so Full Auto mode turns toward uninspected room sectors and escalates genuine discoveries to System 2 memory and speech.
