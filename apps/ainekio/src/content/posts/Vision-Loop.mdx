Ainekio's front face carrier combines visual expression and visual perception in one removable module: a **1.9-inch `ST7789` color IPS LCD** (`320 × 170`) displaying **59 animated facial expressions**, and an **`OV5647` MIPI-CSI camera** above the display that provides action-correlated snapshots, Cockpit HUD optical-flow obstacle range, YOLOE/ByteTrack target tracking, and monocular depth odometry.

## 1.9-inch `ST7789` LCD face and 59 expressions

The V2 face display uses a landscape 1.9-inch `ST7789` SPI module seated in a recessed red bezel with four wireless spacers (`2026-11-11`). The C firmware shader (`Slave/software/faces/face.c`) drives 59 procedural expression animations at `15 fps`—covering idle blinking, live audio-synced speaking bars, directional glances, curiosity, sleep/doze states, Freestyle gesture accompaniments, `cosmic horror`, and **`be_` token-matched Fast Body Event reactions** (`fast-body-event-mode.json`) when the robot is tipped, shaken, or stalled.

## `OV5647` MIPI-CSI camera and motion-safe capture

On V2 (`Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6`), vision is captured through an `OV5647` MIPI-CSI sensor (superseding V1's parallel `OV3660` sensor on the `ESP32-S3`):

- **180° inverted mounting**: To route the CSI ribbon cleanly inside the compact front face carrier above the LCD, the `OV5647` module is mounted upside-down and rotated **180°** in the image pipeline so all snapshots, previews, and tracking overlays arrive right-side up.
- **`10,000 µs` (`10 ms`) motion-safe exposure ceiling**: Indoor auto-exposure algorithms often lengthen shutter times at the expense of severe motion blur during walking or head turning. The V2 camera pipeline enforces a **`10,000 µs` maximum exposure ceiling**, keeping frames sharp during active locomotion and visual odometry.
- **On-device microSD video recording**: The `ESP32-P4` can simultaneously stream preview JPEGs and record segmented video clips to the onboard microSD card for retrieval through Body Control.

## How images connect to actions and conversation

Rather than flooding the language model with continuous raw room video, Ainekio binds still captures to specific events so cognition in **[Ainekio OS](/posts/ainekio-os/)** always knows **when** an image was taken and **which action** it reflects:

| Capture Trigger | Correlation Identifier | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Explicit snapshot (`captureImage`)** | `action_id` | On-demand visual inspection requested by Ainekio OS or the operator |
| **Post-motion snapshot** | `command_sequence_id` | Sent immediately before a movement's `done` receipt so Goal Review can verify the physical outcome |
| **Voice utterance end (VAD)** | `utterance_id` | Pairs what the user said with what Ainekio was looking at during that spoken turn |
| **Cockpit HUD, tracking & odometry** | `frame_id` + timestamp | Feeds Cockpit HUD optical-flow contact range, YOLOE/ByteTrack detection, and OpenGV / MoGe depth estimation |

In Ainekio OS, visual objectives require `visual_observation` evidence routed through the **`environmentVision`** specialist role (`Qwen3.5 9B` locally on `llama-cpp.service` or Hybrid warm `GeminiAcpServer` in `ServerStatus`) rather than assuming that a completed `Walk` command automatically satisfied a visual goal.

## Optical-flow obstacle range, YOLOE tracking, and depth odometry

Host perception in `Master/gateway` extends the camera from passive snapshots to active spatial measurement:

1. **Cockpit HUD Lucas-Kanade optical-flow + Depth-Anything obstacle contact range (`depth_motion.py`)**: During live walking in the **Cockpit HUD**, pyramidal Lucas-Kanade sparse optical flow is fused with Depth-Anything monocular ground-plane contact projection to compute real-time obstacle range and approach clearance directly in front of the `OV5647` lens.
2. **YOLOE + ByteTrack target tracking**: Body Control runs open-vocabulary YOLOE detection paired with ByteTrack multi-frame association, allowing Ainekio OS (`Active Task`) to search for, select, and track a specific object or person across frames with exact-image bounding-box overlays.
3. **OpenGV 2-point RANSAC depth odometry**: To measure real forward progress and turning during walking trials and locomotion goals, the host gateway combines monocular depth points with `LSM6DS3` IMU rotation priors inside an **OpenGV 2-point RANSAC** solver, transforming camera-frame motion into body-frame displacement via the calibrated face-carrier mounting transform.
4. **On-demand MoGe depth loading via `Active Task`**: Because monocular metric depth (`MoGe-2`) is compute-intensive, the depth model is loaded **on demand** only when `Active Task` or a gait-learning session actively requests visual depth progress, and unloaded when idle so GPU/host memory stays free for `llama-cpp.service`.
