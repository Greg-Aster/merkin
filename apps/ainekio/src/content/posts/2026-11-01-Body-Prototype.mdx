The body prototype is done. Most of my time is now going into the
[walking system](/posts/2026-11-01-neural-walking/).
The latest mechanical work is saved in `front-shell-fixes.blend`, with fresh
exports for the face, servo cover and underside power packaging.

## Latest Blender exports

The illustration above renders the exported meshes. The downloadable set keeps
the original filenames and groups the parts by their existing export folders.

| Area | October 27–29 exports | Role in the assembly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display | Black LCD carrier, recessed bezel, compact front-end panel | Mount and surround the face display |
| Exterior | Left/right chin, cheek fangs and side guards | Separate face and side-shell pieces |
| Servo | MG90S lower housing | Revised lower servo-cover geometry |
| Power | Rear charger retainer; combined battery floor and power-module cradle | Package the battery and charging/converter boards beneath the body |

These are the latest part files, not a new robot geometry or a change to the
twelve-servo linkage. The newest combined floor/cradle export is separate from
the earlier rear-carrier and retainer options; both are retained for comparison.

[Download the November 1 part snapshot](/posts/2026-11-01-progress/body-prototype-parts.zip).
The archive contains the twelve STL exports listed in its manifest.

## Hardware baseline

| Component | Current recorded arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Controller | Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6, with C6 wireless coprocessor |
| Actuation | Twelve Miuzei MG90S servos, PCA9685 output |
| Joint readings | Added potentiometer wires through a CD74HC4067 multiplexer |
| Power | 2S pack, Mini560 converter, HiLetgo 2S USB-C charger |
| Body design | Blender; compact 35 / 20 / 28 / 38 / 55 mm linkage |
| Last measured assembly mass | 650 g, recorded October 26 |

Completion of the prototype does not supply a new mass or battery-runtime
measurement. The earlier [power and support-stand entry](/posts/2026-10-26-body-power-and-training-stand/)
retains the component envelopes and stand downloads. The [V2 build reference](/posts/ainekio-v2/)
collects the assembly history and alternatives.

## Next physical work

The next useful record is a walking trial on this body, paired with shaft feedback,
IMU data and external video. The simulation is exposing where the learned gait
loses speed or turning authority; the physical trial needs to show how those
requests behave under the real weight and servo load.
