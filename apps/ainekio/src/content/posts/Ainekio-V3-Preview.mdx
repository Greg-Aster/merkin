> **Workbench status — Active CAD (`Scene 'v3'`, updated November 18, 2026):** The `650 g` twelve-servo **[Ainekio V2](/posts/ainekio-v2/)** remains the active physical robot walking untethered on the floor today, following the original eight-servo **[Ainekio V1](/posts/ainekio-v1/)**. **Ainekio V3** has progressed in Blender from an initial open-leg sketch into a complete, watertight **`608`-mesh industrial armored assembly** in `Scene 'v3'` (`436.3 × 236.5 × 381.4 mm` w/ whip antenna in the neutral exported 3D CAD, spanning `440.8–452.6 × 234.4–236.5 × 307.8–341.4 mm` across body/ROPS and fender refinements; paired with a `293`-mesh kinematic `V3 - Motion Library` rig) verified with zero static or dynamic `BVHTree` mesh collisions across all boards, fasteners, bearings, harnesses, and swing poses.

Getting the `650 g` micro-servo V2 robot walking untethered on its on-chip `50 Hz` `ESP32-P4` neural gait exposed two clear physical limits of a `~2 kg·cm` `MG90S` micro-chassis:

1. **Ground clearance, actuator torque, and outdoor exposure:** Micro-scale `55 mm` lower legs and unsealed hobby micro servos work well indoors on rugs and hardwood, but they restrict stride speed, rough-ground clearance, and damp or dusty outdoor operation.
2. **Offboard host dependency:** During the September [Radxa Dragon Q6A chassis study](/posts/q6a-chassis-and-active-loop/), carrying an onboard Linux single-board computer plus high-capacity cells pushed V2 past what twelve `MG90S` servos could lift cleanly—keeping **[Body Control](/posts/ainekio-cockpit/)** and **[Ainekio OS](/posts/ainekio-os/)** on an external computer over Wi-Fi.

**Ainekio V3** addresses both limits simultaneously by stepping up to **twelve waterproof `DSSERVO DS3235` (`~35 kg·cm` class) standard aluminum-case servos** (`~15×` V2's stall torque), scaling the four-bar leg linkages to **`87.65 / 40.00 / 56.00 / 93.55 / 158.40 mm`** (`131.3 mm` belly skid ground clearance), and packaging **both an `ESP32-P4-WIFI6` real-time motor controller and an onboard `Radxa Dragon Q6A` Linux SBC + `4× 21700` battery pack** inside a modular heavy-equipment shell with a rear clamshell `2.0 mm`-pin service piano hinge.

## 4. Shrouded `DS3235` crawler legs, `Part_003` clearance audit & why V2 motions cannot transfer 1:1

Rather than uniformly scaling fastener holes and bearing pockets (which would distort M3 threads and servo splines), V3 uses **piecewise-rigid linkage scaling**—stretching link lengths for stride and `131.3 mm` ground clearance while holding every **`25T` aluminum clamping horn**, **`F688ZZ`** (`8×16×5 mm`) shoulder counter-bearing, **`683ZZ`** (`3×7×3 mm`) linkage bearing, `0.2 mm` inner-race shim, and `M2.6` / `M3` fastener bore at exact 1:1 hardware tolerances:

1. **Heavy-duty four-bar truss geometry:** `87.65 mm` trussed carrier (`Part_023`), `40.00 mm` flanged crank (`Part_009`), `56.00 mm` lower-leg pickup rocker (`56 / 40 = 1.400`), `93.55 mm` curved trussed coupler rod (`Part_026`), and `158.40 mm` trussed lower leg (`Part_027`).
2. **20-piece sculpted leg armor (`5` parts × `4` legs):** Each corner wraps its structural truss in a **3-Ring Stepped Planetary Hip Drum** (`Ø56.4 / 49.6 / 37.0 mm`), **Cam-Contoured Thigh Shell**, **Twin Parallel Rear Thigh Actuator Struts** (guarding the `93.55 mm` coupler rod), **Contoured Shin Shell & Ø`31.6 mm` Knee Drum**, and a **`60°`-Sloped V-Split-Toe Crawler Foot** (`80.9 × 44.7 mm` overall crawler foot envelope)—verified collision-free across `±22°` hip roll and `±18°` carrier/crank swing envelopes.
3. **6-DOF `Part_003` hip-bracket swept-volume audit:** Prior to finalizing the V3 shoulder pods (`|OC| = 28.32 mm` carrier-to-crank shaft spacing with `6.50 mm` built-in axial plane stagger), a dedicated CAD clearance study caught and resolved three physical assembly blockers before printing: adding relief slots for the `18.3 × 6.8 × 4.5 mm` waterproof servo wire grommet during insertion, clearing a `0.185 mm` inner bolt-head interference on `25T` clamping horns, and upgrading the shoulder counter-pivot from `683ZZ` to a flanged **`F688ZZ`**.

While kinematic retargeting confirms zero armor-to-armor collisions across the workspace, **V2's 47 analytical gaits and joint trajectories cannot transfer directly to physical V3 hardware** for two fundamental engineering reasons:

1. **Non-isomorphic nonlinear 4-bar linkage ratios:** V3 is not a uniform geometric magnification of V2—to package `25T` aluminum clamping horns and `DS3235` cases cleanly inside the shoulder pods, V3 alters the internal four-bar transmission ratios (`carrier / crank = 2.19` on V3 [`87.65 / 40.0 mm`] vs. `1.75` on V2 [`35.0 / 20.0 mm`]; `rod / pickup = 1.67` [`93.55 / 56.0 mm`] vs. `1.36` [`38.0 / 28.0 mm`]). Replaying V2's `(h, alpha, theta)` joint-angle curves directly on V3 warps the nonlinear coupler Jacobian, skewing vertical foot lift, ground-plane flatness during stance sweep, and mechanical advantage near toggle boundaries.
2. **Square-cube mass/inertia scaling & crawler-foot scrubbing torque vs. actuator slew bandwidth:** Scaling linear dimensions by `~1.74×` increases structural volume and limb rotational inertia by roughly **`~5×`**, compounded by twelve heavy metal-case `DS3235` actuators, a `4× 21700` belly pack, and the `Radxa Dragon Q6A` + aluminum heatsink. At the ground interface, V3's wide **`80.9 × 44.7 mm` split-toe crawler feet** generate substantially higher yaw/roll scrubbing torque than V2's narrow curved rocking soles, while high-ratio `DS3235` geartrains trade peak unloaded slew speed for `~35 kg·cm` torque—requiring V3-specific cadence, swing clearance, and MuJoCo MJX policy training tailored to its own inertia tensor and actuator bandwidth.
