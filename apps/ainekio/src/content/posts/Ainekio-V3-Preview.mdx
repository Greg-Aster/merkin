> **Workbench status — Active CAD (`Scene 'v3'`, updated November 20, 2026):** The `650 g` twelve-servo **[Ainekio V2](/posts/ainekio-v2/)** and eight-servo **[Ainekio V1](/posts/ainekio-v1/)** remain the active physical quadrupeds walking on the floor today. **Ainekio V3** has advanced in Blender into a consolidated, printable **`537`-mesh industrial armored assembly** in `Scene 'v3'` (**`402.0 × 236.5 × 343.1 mm`** neutral CAD envelope with whip antenna; **`~260.6 mm`** to the ROPS/cargo roofline)—combining the **`Option A-1` (`54 / 22 / 46 / 56 / 135 mm`, `-35°` bellcrank) high-torque 5-link pantograph** (`+120%` vertical lift, `+116%` running thrust, `-48.6 mm` shorter rear overhang), a continuous **`3,860`-vert armored belly chassis tub**, a **`40°` sloped rear `F688ZZ` bearing undertray**, a **functional `Clip 26` leaf-spring clamshell latch**, and recessed **`AINEKIO` / `V-003 Field Companion Unit`** FDM armor badging ([Milestone #20](/posts/2026-11-20-clockspring-arms-phantom-jams-opt-a1-and-megameal-sim/)).

Getting the `650 g` micro-servo V2 robot walking untethered on its on-chip `50 Hz` `ESP32-P4` neural gait exposed two clear physical limits of a `~2 kg·cm` `MG90S` micro-chassis:

1. **Ground clearance, actuator torque, and outdoor exposure:** Micro-scale `55 mm` lower legs and unsealed hobby micro servos work well indoors on rugs and hardwood, but they restrict stride speed, rough-ground clearance, and damp or dusty outdoor operation.
2. **Offboard host dependency:** During the September [Radxa Dragon Q6A chassis study](/posts/q6a-chassis-and-active-loop/), carrying an onboard Linux single-board computer plus high-capacity cells pushed V2 past what twelve `MG90S` servos could lift cleanly—keeping **[Body Control](/posts/ainekio-cockpit/)** and **[Ainekio OS](/posts/ainekio-os/)** on an external computer over Wi-Fi.

**Ainekio V3** addresses both limits simultaneously by stepping up to **twelve waterproof `DSSERVO DS3235` (`~35 kg·cm` class) standard aluminum-case servos** (`~15×` V2's stall torque), pairing them with an optimized **`Option A-1` (`54 / 22 / 46 / 56 / 135 mm`, `-35°` angled bellcrank) compact 5-link pantograph** that shifts dynamic bounding from the `84%` stall region directly into the `46%` peak mechanical power band, and packaging **both an `ESP32-P4-WIFI6` real-time motor controller and an onboard `Radxa Dragon Q6A` Linux SBC + `4× 21700` battery pack** inside a unified armored chassis tub with a `Clip 26`-latched `Ø2.0 mm`-pin rear clamshell service hood.

## 2. Armored front fascia: recessed `2.8"` `MSP2807` vault display, ROPS bull-bar & dual brow pods

## 4. `Option A-1` compact high-torque 5-link pantograph (`54 / 22 / 46 / 56 / 135 mm`), `>5,000`-candidate optimization & why V2 motions cannot transfer 1:1

While V3's earlier `87.65 / 40.00 / 56.00 / 93.55 / 158.40 mm` baseline truss confirmed static housing clearances, a `270°` workspace and dynamic `DS3235` torque-power audit revealed three physical bottlenecks in the oversized linkage: rear coupler overhang (`-148 mm`), extended rear fender-lip interference at extreme swing, and long moment arms that pushed bounding foot loads into the **`84%` stall zone** where servo shaft speed drops and winding heat spikes.

Evaluating **`>5,000` candidate 5-link geometries** across full `270°` `DS3235` travel selected **`Option A-1` (`54 / 22 / 46 / 56 / 135 mm`, `-35°` angled bellcrank)** ([Milestone #20](/posts/2026-11-20-clockspring-arms-phantom-jams-opt-a1-and-megameal-sim/)), preserving exact 1:1 hardware bores on every **`25T` aluminum clamping horn**, **`F688ZZ`** (`8×16×5 mm`) shoulder counter-bearing, **`683ZZ`** (`3×7×3 mm`) linkage bearing, and `M2.6` / `M3` fastener:

1. **`Option A-1` compact 5-link pantograph geometry & load-sharing gains:** Pairs `Part_009` (**`22.0 mm` crank**), `Part_023` (**`54.0 mm` trussed carrier**), a **`46 mm` `-35°` angled bellcrank** pickup, `Part_026` (**`56.0 mm` trussed rod**), and `Part_027` (**`109.0 mm` angled lower leg**, `135 mm` effective foot reach). Relative to the oversized `87.7 / 40 / 93.6 / 158.4 mm` baseline, `Option A-1` delivers **`+120%` vertical lift**, **`+116%` running thrust**, **`0.93:1` dual-servo torque balance**, **`-48.6 mm` shorter rear overhang**, and **`8.4 mm` full-sweep fender clearance**.
2. **Refitted 20-piece sculpted leg armor & telescoped rear struts:** Each corner wraps its `Option A-1` truss in a **3-Ring Stepped Planetary Hip Drum** (`Ø56.4 / 49.6 / 37.0 mm`), **Refitted Contoured Thigh & Shin Armor**, telescoped **`29.2 / 27.7 mm` Twin Rear Thigh Struts**, and a **`60°`-Sloped V-Split-Toe Crawler Foot** (`80.9 × 44.7 mm` envelope)—verified collision-free across full 3-DOF travel.
3. **6-DOF `Part_003` hip-bracket swept-volume audit:** Prior to finalizing the V3 shoulder pods (`|OC| = 28.32 mm` carrier-to-crank shaft spacing with `6.50 mm` built-in axial plane stagger), a dedicated CAD clearance study resolved three physical assembly blockers before printing: adding relief slots for the `18.3 × 6.8 × 4.5 mm` waterproof servo wire grommet during insertion, clearing a `0.185 mm` inner bolt-head interference on `25T` clamping horns, and upgrading the shoulder counter-pivot from `683ZZ` to a flanged **`F688ZZ`**.

While kinematic retargeting validates multi-axis armor clearances, **V2's 47 analytical gaits and joint trajectories cannot transfer directly to physical V3 hardware** for two fundamental engineering reasons:

1. **Non-isomorphic nonlinear 5-link pantograph ratios & `-35°` bellcrank phase:** V3's `Option A-1` pantograph (`54 / 22 / 46 / 56 / 135 mm`, `-35°` angled bellcrank) is not a uniform geometric magnification of V2 (`35 / 20 / 28 / 38 / 55 mm`)—altering both internal transmission ratios (`carrier / crank = 2.45` on V3 `Option A-1` vs. `1.75` on V2; `rod / pickup = 1.22` vs. `1.36`) and bellcrank phase angle to achieve `0.93:1` dual-servo torque balance across `25T` horns. Replaying V2's `(h, alpha, theta)` joint-angle curves directly on V3 warps the nonlinear coupler Jacobian, skewing vertical foot lift and ground-plane flatness during stance sweep.
2. **Square-cube mass/inertia scaling & crawler-foot scrubbing torque vs. actuator slew bandwidth:** Scaling into a `~2 kg`-class heavy-equipment hull increases limb rotational inertia and total body mass substantially, compounded by twelve metal-case `DS3235` actuators, a `4× 21700` belly pack, and the `Radxa Dragon Q6A` + aluminum heatsink. At the ground interface, V3's wide **`80.9 × 44.7 mm` split-toe crawler feet** generate much higher yaw/roll scrubbing torque than V2's narrow curved rocking soles, requiring V3-specific cadence, swing clearance, and MuJoCo MJX policy training tailored to `Option A-1`'s `46%`-load power curve.
