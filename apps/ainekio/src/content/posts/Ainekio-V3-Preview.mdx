> **Workbench status — Active CAD (`v3-2.blend`, updated November 16, 2026):** The `650 g` twelve-servo **[Ainekio V2](/posts/ainekio-v2/)** remains the active physical robot walking untethered on the floor today, following the original eight-servo **[Ainekio V1](/posts/ainekio-v1/)**. **Ainekio V3** has progressed in Blender from an initial open-leg sketch into a complete, watertight **`293`-mesh industrial armored assembly** (`440.83 × 234.40 × 307.81 mm` body/ROPS envelope; `381.42 mm` with strobe beacon and whip antenna) verified with zero static or dynamic `BVHTree` mesh collisions across all boards, bearings, harnesses, and swing poses.

Getting the `650 g` micro-servo V2 robot walking untethered on its on-chip `50 Hz` `ESP32-P4` neural gait exposed two clear physical limits of a `~2 kg·cm` `MG90S` micro-chassis:

1. **Ground clearance, actuator torque, and outdoor exposure:** Micro-scale `55 mm` lower legs and unsealed hobby micro servos work well indoors on rugs and hardwood, but they restrict stride speed, rough-ground clearance, and damp or dusty outdoor operation.
2. **Offboard host dependency:** During the September [Radxa Dragon Q6A chassis study](/posts/q6a-chassis-and-active-loop/), carrying an onboard Linux single-board computer plus high-capacity cells pushed V2 past what twelve `MG90S` servos could lift cleanly—keeping **[Body Control](/posts/ainekio-cockpit/)** and **[Ainekio OS](/posts/ainekio-os/)** on an external computer over Wi-Fi.

**Ainekio V3** addresses both limits simultaneously by stepping up to **twelve waterproof `DSSERVO DS3235` (`~35 kg·cm` class) standard aluminum-case servos** (`~15×` V2's stall torque), scaling the four-bar leg linkages to **`87.65 / 40.00 / 93.55 / 158.40 mm`** (`131.3 mm` belly skid ground clearance), and packaging **both an `ESP32-P4-WIFI6` real-time motor controller and an onboard `Radxa Dragon Q6A` Linux SBC + `4× 21700` battery pack** inside a modular heavy-equipment shell.

## 2. Armored front fascia: recessed `2.8"` `MSP2807` vault display, ROPS bull-bar & dual brow pods

In the initial November 13 concept (preserved in Section 5 below), V3 wore an exposed `5.0"` glass tablet across its nose. Mechanical collision and field-durability checks quickly showed that an exposed `127 × 71 mm` glass face sits directly in the strike zone of stairs, brush, and nose-first tumbles.

Today's `v3-2.blend` front assembly splits the fascia into **14 bolted structural and armor panels** protected behind a tubular **Swept ROPS Bull-Bar**:

- **Recessed `2.8"` `MSP2807` (`ILI9341` SPI TFT) vault face:** Seats an `86.0 × 50.0 mm` `MSP2807` PCB (`57.6 × 43.2 mm` TFT active area, 4× Ø`3.2 mm` M3 mounts on `76.08 × 44.00 mm` pitch) behind a **4-bolt Cast Gunmetal Widescreen Smoked-Glass Bezel** (`77.2 × 36.6 mm` outer frame, `52.6 × 28.4 mm` widescreen aperture), wired directly to the `ESP32-P4` high-speed SPI bus (`SCK→GPIO20, SDI→GPIO21, CS→GPIO22, DC→GPIO23, RESET→GPIO26`) so procedural expressions render instantaneously at boot even while Linux is starting up or sleeping.
- **Onboard Linux PC & rear bulkhead docking preserved:** Moving the nose face to a recessed vault screen keeps the **`Radxa Dragon Q6A` Linux SBC** safely inside the upper dorsal bay—running the full **[Ainekio OS](/posts/ainekio-os/)** mind, Body Control gateway, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth peripherals, and local coding/terminal workflows onboard, with physical USB/Ethernet/HDMI/USB-C ports accessible through the rear bay for single-cable desktop monitor docking.
- **Vision, illumination & recovery hardware:** Above the bezel sit symmetric Left and Right **Brow Camera Pods** housing the **`Arducam B0066` (`OV5647` MIPI-CSI)** sensor and routed polyimide flex ribbon; flanking the vault are dual **Main Worklight Pods**, dual **Lower Foglight Pods**, sloped cheek/chin armor plates, and a **Lower Chin Skid with Curved D-Ring Tow Shackle**.

- **Upper dorsal Linux compute bay:** Houses the **`Radxa Dragon Q6A`** (`88.69 × 58.61 mm`) topped with the **`Radxa Armor 7056A`** CNC aluminum heatsink exhausting straight through the **7-slat Dorsal Radiator Core** and **9-slat Rear Tail Radiator**.

- **Low-CG `4× 21700` (`2S2P`) lower belly hull:** Four `21700` cells (`Ø21.4 × 70.15 mm` each: `Cell 1A/1B` `S1`, `Cell 2A/2B` `S2`) sit transversely along the lowest floor datum (`Z = 156.0 mm` world / right between the front and rear hip brackets) behind latched side service doors, paired with a dedicated **`Mini560` `5 V` logic buck** and rear-tunnel **`HiLetgo 2S` USB-C boost charger**.

## 4. Shrouded `1.44×` `DS3235` excavator legs & `Part_003` housing clearance audit

Rather than uniformly scaling fastness holes and bearing pockets (which would distort M3 threads and servo splines), V3 uses **piecewise-rigid `1.44×` linkage scaling**—stretching link lengths for stride and `131.3 mm` ground clearance while holding every **`25T` aluminum clamping horn**, **`F688ZZ`** (`8×16×5 mm`) shoulder counter-bearing, **`683ZZ`** (`3×7×3 mm`) linkage bearing, `0.2 mm` inner-race shim, and `M2.6` / `M3` fastener bore at exact 1:1 hardware tolerances:

1. **Preserved `1.400` crank-to-rocker leverage:** `87.65 mm` trussed carrier (`Part_023`), `40.00 mm` flanged crank (`Part_009`), `56.00 mm` lower-leg pickup rocker (`56 / 40 = 1.400`), `93.55 mm` curved trussed coupler rod (`Part_026`), and `158.40 mm` trussed lower leg (`Part_027`).
2. **20-piece sculpted leg armor (`5` parts × `4` legs):** Each corner wraps its structural truss in a **3-Ring Stepped Planetary Hip Drum** (`Ø56.4 / 49.6 / 37.0 mm`), **Cam-Contoured Thigh Shell**, **Twin Parallel Rear Thigh Actuator Struts** (guarding the `93.55 mm` coupler rod), **Contoured Shin Shell & Ø`31.6 mm` Knee Drum**, and a **`60°`-Sloped V-Split-Toe Crawler Foot** (`52 × 40 mm` grouser sole)—verified collision-free across `±22°` hip roll and `±18°` carrier/crank swing envelopes.
3. **6-DOF `Part_003` hip-bracket swept-volume audit:** Prior to finalizing the V3 shoulder pods (`|OC| = 28.32 mm` carrier-to-crank shaft spacing with `6.50 mm` built-in axial plane stagger), a dedicated CAD clearance study caught and resolved three physical assembly blockers before printing: adding relief slots for the `18.3 × 6.8 × 4.5 mm` waterproof servo wire grommet during insertion, clearing a `0.185 mm` inner bolt-head interference on `25T` clamping horns, and upgrading the shoulder counter-pivot from `683ZZ` to a flanged **`F688ZZ`**.

## Next steps on the V3 bench

3. **Full-CAD V3 Blender → MuJoCo MJCF export:** Export the `293`-mesh `v3-2.blend` assembly into MuJoCo MJX to train V3 `50 Hz` trotting, snag-clearance, and System 3 presence policies ahead of full 4-leg assembly.
