> **Workbench status — Reconciled CAD, Unified P4 Firmware & Live Simulator (`v3-p4-ds3225pro-optA1-ankle-20261122`, updated November 23, 2026):** The `650 g` twelve-servo **[Ainekio V2](/posts/ainekio-v2/)** and eight-servo **[Ainekio V1](/posts/ainekio-v1/)** remain the active physical quadrupeds walking on the floor today. **Ainekio V3** has reached a reconciled, printable industrial armored CAD & firmware milestone (**`406.4 × 236.5 × 385.6 mm`** neutral envelope, `180.93 mm` shoulder height, `125.03 mm` belly clearance, `219.45 × 190.72 mm` wheelbase × track, estimated mass **`2.97 kg`** — physical print and weigh-in pending): combining **12× DSServo `DS3225 PRO` HV coreless** servos (`270°` travel on direct `2S` `7.4 V`, `35 kg·cm`), **`Option A-1` (`54 / 22 / 46 / 56 / 135 mm`)** 4-bar legs with a **2-stage double-parallelogram self-leveling pivoting crawler foot** (`<= 0.27°` dynamic level error), compliant PETG knee spiral springs and preloaded servo-saver rods, unified **`sdkconfig.v3-12servo` (`body_v3.c`, `2.1 MB` compiled binary verified)** + panoramic **`face_cyclops.c`** (`964` lines) on **`display_ili9341.c`**, and trained **`v3-microduck-p4`** + **`neural_pose_v3`** RL policies already live in **[`simulator.ainek.io`](https://simulator.ainek.io/#model=v3)** ([Milestone #21](/posts/2026-11-23-unified-p4-firmware-v1-v3-simulator-multiplayer-and-auto-calibration/)).

Getting the `650 g` micro-servo V2 robot walking untethered on its on-chip `50 Hz` `ESP32-P4` neural gait exposed two clear physical limits of a `~2 kg·cm` `MG90S` micro-chassis:

1. **Ground clearance, actuator torque, and outdoor exposure:** Micro-scale `55 mm` lower legs and unsealed hobby micro servos work well indoors on rugs and hardwood, but they restrict stride speed, rough-ground clearance, and damp or dusty outdoor operation.
2. **Offboard host dependency:** During the September [Radxa Dragon Q6A chassis study](/posts/q6a-chassis-and-active-loop/), carrying an onboard Linux single-board computer plus high-capacity cells pushed V2 past what twelve `MG90S` servos could lift cleanly—keeping **[Body Control](/posts/ainekio-cockpit/)** and **[Ainekio OS](/posts/ainekio-os/)** on an external computer over Wi-Fi.

**Ainekio V3** addresses both limits simultaneously by stepping up to **twelve DSServo `DS3225 PRO` HV coreless standard servos** (`270°` travel on direct `2S` `7.4 V`, `500–2500 µs`, `35 kg·cm / 3.43 N·m` stall, `800 deg/s` no-load, `60 g` each), pairing them with an optimized **`Option A-1` (`54 / 22 / 46 / 56 / 135 mm`, `-35°` angled bellcrank)** leg + **2-stage double-parallelogram self-leveling pivoting crawler foot** (`1:1` flat sole, `±27.5°` range + outboard compliant ankle damper, `<= 0.27°` dynamic level error), PETG flat spiral knee spring (`~0.167 N·m/rad`), and `~22 N` preloaded PETG servo-saver rod (`±1.3 mm`), and packaging **both an `ESP32-P4-WIFI6` real-time motor controller and an onboard `Radxa Dragon Q6A` Linux SBC + `4× 21700` battery pack** inside a unified armored chassis tub with a `Clip 26`-latched `Ø2.0 mm`-pin rear clamshell service hood.

## 2. Armored front fascia: recessed `2.8"` `MSP2807` vault display, ROPS bull-bar & dual brow pods

In the initial November 13 concept (preserved in Section 5 below), V3 wore an exposed `5.0"` glass tablet across its nose. Mechanical collision and field-durability checks quickly showed that an exposed `127 × 71 mm` glass face sits directly in the strike zone of stairs, brush, and nose-first tumbles.

Today's `Scene 'v3'` front assembly splits the fascia into **14 bolted structural and armor panels** protected behind a tubular **Swept ROPS Bull-Bar**:

## 4. `Option A-1` legs (`54 / 22 / 46 / 56 / 135 mm`), self-leveling crawler feet, compliant linkages & trained V3 RL policies

While V3's earlier `87.65 / 40.00 / 56.00 / 93.55 / 158.40 mm` baseline truss confirmed static housing clearances, a `270°` workspace and dynamic torque-power audit revealed three physical bottlenecks in the oversized linkage: rear coupler overhang (`-148 mm`), extended rear fender-lip interference at extreme swing, and long moment arms that pushed bounding foot loads into the **`84%` stall zone** where servo shaft speed drops and winding heat spikes.

Evaluating **`>5,000` candidate 5-link geometries** across full `270°` servo travel selected **`Option A-1` (`54 / 22 / 46 / 56 / 135 mm`, `-35°` angled bellcrank)** ([Milestone #20](/posts/2026-11-20-clockspring-arms-phantom-jams-opt-a1-and-megameal-sim/)), subsequently upgraded in **`v3-p4-ds3225pro-optA1-ankle-20261122`** ([Milestone #21](/posts/2026-11-23-unified-p4-firmware-v1-v3-simulator-multiplayer-and-auto-calibration/)) with a **2-stage double-parallelogram self-leveling ankle** and PETG impact compliance while preserving exact 1:1 hardware bores on every **`25T` aluminum clamping horn**, **`F688ZZ`** (`8×16×5 mm`) shoulder counter-bearing, **`683ZZ`** (`3×7×3 mm`) linkage bearing, and `M2.6` / `M3` fastener:

Why V2's 47 analytical gaits and joint trajectories could never transfer directly to physical V3 hardware:

1. **Non-isomorphic nonlinear 5-link pantograph ratios & `-35°` bellcrank phase:** V3's `Option A-1` pantograph (`54 / 22 / 46 / 56 / 135 mm`, `-35°` angled bellcrank) is not a uniform geometric magnification of V2 (`35 / 20 / 28 / 38 / 55 mm`)—altering both internal transmission ratios (`carrier / crank = 2.45` on V3 `Option A-1` vs. `1.75` on V2; `rod / pickup = 1.22` vs. `1.36`) and bellcrank phase angle to achieve `0.93:1` dual-servo torque balance across `25T` horns. Replaying V2's `(h, alpha, theta)` joint-angle curves directly on V3 warps the nonlinear coupler Jacobian, skewing vertical foot lift and ground-plane flatness during stance sweep.
2. **Square-cube mass/inertia scaling & self-leveling crawler-foot kinematics:** Scaling into a **`~2.97 kg`** armored hull with twelve `60 g` `DS3225 PRO` HV coreless actuators, a `4× 21700` belly pack, and the `Radxa Dragon Q6A` + aluminum heatsink increases limb inertia dramatically—while the **2-stage double-parallelogram self-leveling crawler foot** and compliant PETG knee/servo-saver linkages have completely different contact dynamics than V2's narrow curved rocking soles.
