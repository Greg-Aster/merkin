The October 15 updates across MetaHuman OS and the V2 firmware tighten how Ainekio
decides what to do, who speaks, how locomotion progress is measured, and how the legs
move across Walk, Run, Crawl, Crab, and Wave.

## Ten model categories and a single owner for outward speech

Workflow nodes and the model settings panel now share a unified ten-category routing
table:

| Category | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| **Orchestrator** | Interpret requests and events; select routes, tools, and context (consolidating `Environment Intent`) |
| **Task Decisions** | Select executable body/sensing actions and task controls |
| **Planner** | Develop and revise multi-step plans |
| **Persona** | Express identity, personality, conversation, and self-directed intentions |
| **Psychotherapist** | Independently assess attributed experience and update long-term persona |
| **Vision** | Interpret camera images and support visually grounded planning |
| **Curator** | Review and prepare attributed memory and training records |
| **Coder** | Work on code and technical tasks |
| **Summarizer** | Condense history while preserving meaning and attribution |
| **Embedder** | Represent records for vector retrieval and similarity search |

### Removing speech steps from Task

Following the [October 14 speech/action confusion analysis](/posts/2026-10-14-startup-and-leveling/),
**Task speech steps have been removed completely.** Task now selects only physical or
sensing actions, Goal Review evaluates execution evidence, and **Persona** is the sole
owner of conversation persistence and spoken reply delivery. Dataset Version 16
(6,358 development, 1,518 evaluation, and 187 regression examples) updates the
specialist training corpus to this clean action-only contract.

### Desire review, authorship attribution, and Active Operator

- **Operational evidence in Desire review:** Agency's Desire review now receives
  selected-robot status, capabilities, original requests, selected programs, and
  actual body execution outcomes—allowing it to spot mismatches between what was
  requested and what the body actually did, adjust desire strength directly, or
  archive a zero-strength desire and cancel its active work.
- **Strict memory authorship:** Memories retain speaker, timestamp, cognitive mode,
  execution ID, and record type (keeping daydreams distinct from physical events),
  while the **Psychotherapist** agent is the sole autonomous editor of persona goals.
- **Active Operator & resume coalescing:** The autonomy scheduler is now registered as
  **Active Operator** (`Reactive`, `Semi`, `Full`) in the Agent Catalog. Pending
  workflow resume requests coalesce in the execution store (tested against a
  23-to-41 mixed-event burst with zero backlog growth), and running workflows pin
  their admitted graph template across live edits.

### 9B LoRA diagnostics for Autonomy and Persona

Two diagnostic remote LoRA runs on the pinned **Qwen3.5 9B** base tested whether tiny
seed sets (4/2 samples for Autonomy, 5/1 for Persona) could compress outputs or
replace static persona prompts. While Autonomy's held-out loss dropped from `0.416`
to `0.022`, serving checks through `llama.cpp` showed dropped lighting objectives in
Autonomy and source confusion when static persona context was reduced, confirming that
broader attributed datasets are required before promoting 9B adapters.

## Goal-bearing locomotion and signed lateral control (`0.8.51`)

Rather than dispatching open-ended Walk or Turn starts, MetaHuman's locomotion
contract now requires an explicit goal paired with sensor progress evidence:

| Locomotion goal | Progress & completion evidence |
| --- | --- |
| **Distance** | Measured linear displacement in an identified reference frame |
| **Relative turn** | Signed yaw change relative to the starting orientation |
| **Approach** | Reaching a specified relationship to a tracked visual target |
| **Follow** | Maintaining an ongoing target relationship until an ending condition |
| **Visual endpoint** | Camera observations evaluated against completion criteria |

If a required measurement is unavailable at start, the task reports incomplete
without moving; if the measurement drops mid-gait, it requests a clean `Finish` on
the active gait rather than falling back to blind timed dead-reckoning. Firmware
**`0.8.51-p4-lateral`** also adds signed lateral drift correction to the P4 gait
controller so the robot can correct sideways overshoot during visual approaches.

## Expressive gait refinements and faster seated Wave (`0.8.52`)

Installed over Wi-Fi through **`0.8.52-p4-wave`**, the V2 gait generator refines
foot clearance and stroke across all four locomotion modes on the bare-leg contact
profile (35 mm carrier, 20 mm crank, 28 mm pickup, 38 mm rod, 55 mm lower leg) and
staggers PCA9685 PWM channel start phases across each frame:

| Gait parameter | Previous | Revised (`0.8.52`) | Notes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| **Walk** planted sweep | 32 mm | **37 mm** | +18.6% planned travel (`44.44 → 52.71 mm/s` at Speed 100, `0.9 s` cycle) |
| **Walk** lift (low / full) & stance | 4 / 8 mm (80%) | **6 / 10 mm (78%)** | Broader swing arch clears floor earlier; lateral shift reduced `9 → 7 mm` |
| **Run** planted stroke & lift | 40 mm / 2–8 mm | **44 mm / 5–12 mm** | Front/rear paired bound |
| **Crawl** planted stroke & lift | 18 mm / 2–4 mm | **32 mm / 5–8 mm** | +59.5% planned cycle travel on bare-leg contact |
| **Crab** fore/aft & sideways stroke | 36 mm / 24 mm | **40 mm / 32 mm** | Stance width narrowed `190 → 160 mm`; lift `8–14 mm` (+25% sideways travel) |

### Snappier seated Wave (`16.0 s → 6.2 s`)

Seated **Wave** was retimed for much livelier interaction, cutting total clip
duration from 16.0 s down to **6.2 s** while more than doubling shoulder wave
amplitude:

| Wave phase (at 1× speed) | Previous | Revised (`0.8.52`) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Seated entry complete | 3.0 s | **1.2 s** |
| Front arm lift complete | 4.2 s | **1.6 s** |
| Shoulder wave amplitude (3× waves) | ±18° | **±42°** |
| Standing recovery & total duration | 15.2 s / 16.0 s | **6.0 s / 6.2 s** |

### Per-leg self-leveling telemetry and stationary gyro calibration

Body Control's self-leveling panel (`0.8.48+`) now displays live IMU roll/pitch even
when leveling is Off, target offset error, commanded body correction, and modeled
per-leg extension and joint-angle deltas. Operators can click **Use current pose as
level reference** to capture the current attitude or **Calibrate stationary gyro bias**
to average 256 fresh stationary samples into NVS without rebooting.
