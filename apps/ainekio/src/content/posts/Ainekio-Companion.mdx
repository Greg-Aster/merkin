Ainekio is a four-legged robot familiar built as a persistent conversational companion. Its twelve-servo physical body provides locomotion, expressive postures, a camera, a microphone, a speaker, and a 1.9-inch color face LCD; [MetaHuman OS](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/metahuman-os) provides identity, conversation, episodic memory, inner reflection, autonomous agency, and learning.

## Core companion functions

| Function | What it means for Ainekio |
| --- | --- |
| **Converse** | Hold natural text or voice conversations through a single **Standing Persona** (`persona/core.json`), grounded in live context and action results |
| **Perceive** | Combine `OV5647` camera snapshots, YOLOE/depth tracking, `WakeNet9s` speech capture, `LSM6DS3` IMU attitude, and 12-channel servo feedback |
| **Move & Express** | Pair spoken replies with 58 animated `ST7789` LCD facial expressions, closed-loop gaits, and 349+ Freestyle motion programs |
| **Remember** | Store conversations, observations, and task outcomes with explicit provenance so real observations stay distinct from imagined content |
| **Pursue Goals** | Track multi-step objectives across waits and reconnects, or delegate heavy computer tasks to **Big Brother** (`big-brother-tool-mode.json`) |
| **Reflect & Sleep** | Run background reflection, dreaming, memory curation, and **Psychotherapist** persona review during the software Sleep Workflow |
| **Learn** | Train specialist LoRA adapters (`Environment 0.8B/2B`, `Freestyle Qwen`) and motor policies (`walk_nominal_v1`, MuJoCo/MJX RL) from reviewed runs |

## Standing Persona and three layers of continuity

Ainekio's identity is anchored by a single **Standing Persona** stored in `persona/core.json`. To prevent drift or accidental self-modification during ordinary chat, only the **Psychotherapist** agent (during scheduled Sleep review) or the operator can edit `persona/core.json`. Short-term wants and candidate activities are managed separately by the **Desire Agent**, which adjusts desire strengths based on real outcomes.

Three mechanisms maintain continuity across time:

1. **Active working context**: Keeps the current conversation turn, live `Robot Status` (posture, IMU attitude, battery, feedback health), and recent internal reflections available to the unified `environment-mode.json` graph.
2. **Attributed persistent memory**: Indexes episodic memories using CPU-only embeddings (`Qwen3 Embedding 0.6B` and `Nomic Embed v1.5` via `llama-cpp.service`) while tagging each record's origin—user conversation, camera observation, task outcome, dream, or reflection—so imagined material is never confused with physical fact.
3. **Durable task executions**: Checkpoints multi-step goals in SQLite so an ongoing task survives movement waits, camera captures, or network reconnects.

## Inner activity, fast reflexes, and the Sleep Workflow

MetaHuman OS runs background cognitive workflows alongside live interaction:

- **Fast Body Events (`fast-body-event-mode.json`)**: When the robot is tipped, shaken, or experiences a servo stall, a low-latency Lightweight model call immediately triggers a token-matched `be_` LCD face expression and a brief in-character remark without cluttering long-term conversational memory.
- **Inner life**: Reflection, curiosity, and daydream workflows examine recent experiences and generate internal thoughts tagged with their provenance.
- **Sleep Workflow**: Consolidates episodic memory, refreshes embedding indexes, processes open desires, runs dreaming and **Psychotherapist** persona evaluation, and prepares curated datasets for specialist training. Software Sleep is a cognitive maintenance cycle, distinct from putting the physical `ESP32-P4` body into `Doze` or `Rest`.

## Autonomy modes: Reactive, Semi, and Full Auto

Ainekio's **Active Operator** supports three operating modes, detailed in [Robot Autonomy Modes](/posts/bounded-autonomy/):

| Mode | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Reactive** | Responds to user requests, wake-word speech, and physical body events (`fast-body-event-mode.json`) without self-initiating new activities |
| **Semi Mode** | Evaluates signed drive deltas every **5 minutes**, initiating bounded activities or reflections when internal drives cross their threshold |
| **Full Auto Mode** | Continuously selects high-reasoning activities (`Qwen3.5 9B`)—never idling—by chaining perception, movement, memory review, Desire plans, and Big Brother delegations |

## Learning through models and the physical body

Improvement happens across two complementary tracks, both served through the unified `llama-cpp.service` router (`:8080`, on-demand loading with 5-minute idle unload):

1. **Cognitive & Motion Specialists**:
   - **Orchestrator & Task LoRAs (`Environment 0.8B / 2B`)**: Per-request LoRA adapters trained on curated situation-dependent datasets (including ongoing task continuity, cancellation, and tool delegation) select actions and route context cleanly.
   - **Freestyle Motion Model (`Freestyle Qwen`)**: Trained against a 349-example SQLite motion library in Body Control to translate natural-language gesture requests and jam/fall recovery prompts into checked 12-servo target sequences.
2. **Embodied Locomotion Learning**:
   - **Closed-loop gait & neural policies**: Body Control logs 12-channel potentiometer feedback, `208 Hz` IMU telemetry, and visual odometry to evaluate `50 Hz` neural policies (`walk_nominal_v1`) on physical carpet and train quadruped reinforcement-learning policies (Walk in the Park DroQ, Microduck RL, Pupper V3, and full-CAD Ainekio MJCF in JAX/Brax/MJX). See the [Gait Development Guide](/posts/gait-development/).

## Steering and inspecting the companion

Operators can interact with and inspect Ainekio through two connected web surfaces:

- **MetaHuman OS UI ([`https://mh.ainek.io`](https://mh.ainek.io))**: Exposes the conversation view, `TerminalChat.svelte` (**Big Brother** terminal), cognitive node editor, memory inspector, Standing Persona & Agency dashboards, and live `Robot Status`.
- **Body Control (`Master/gateway`)**: Provides direct manual posture and gait controls, 12-channel servo feedback calibration curves, IMU self-leveling tuning, the 349-example Freestyle editor, camera/audio controls, and over-the-air `ESP32-P4` firmware management.
