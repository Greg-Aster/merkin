Most small hobby quadrupeds are built around gait demos: you press a button on a gamepad, the robot trots in a circle, and when you flip the power switch off it has no idea who you are or what happened five minutes ago.

Ainekio (`650 g` V2 body in [`Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot`](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot), paired with **[Ainekio OS](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/ainekio-os)**; [architecture guide](/posts/ainekio-os/)) is built around day-to-day room presence—combining a compact twelve-servo body that can sit safely next to a monitor or walk across a rug with a persistent local-first mind that remembers past conversations, reacts when picked up, and consolidates what it learned overnight.

## Voice conversation without robotic filler

In a room, talking to Ainekio starts with onboard **`WakeNet9s`** wake-word detection on the `ESP32-P4` body. When it hears its wake phrase, the robot perks its front shoulders slightly (`+6°`) so you can tell physically that it is listening, captures your spoken turn until voice activity settles, and pairs the audio with a camera frame if visual context is helpful.

Rather than narrating every internal tool call (*"Step 1: standing up. Step 2: taking photo"*), Ainekio OS separates action selection from outward speech:

- **Silent task execution**: Specialist models dispatch movements, camera checks, or posture shifts quietly in the background.
- **One outward voice**: A single **Standing Persona** synthesizes the spoken reply once Ainekio has actually moved or looked, played back through the onboard speaker (`ES8311` codec + `NS4150B` amplifier) using local or LAN-hosted **Kokoro** text-to-speech (with a fast local **Piper** fallback). While speaking, the body suppresses wake-word and microphone re-triggering so it never answers its own voice.

## A 59-expression face and expressive body language

Because a headless four-legged frame is hard to read across a desk, Ainekio's V2 front carrier pairs a **1.9-inch `ST7789` color IPS LCD** (`320 × 170` landscape at `15 fps`) with a chin-mounted **`OV5647` camera** tucked into the lower jaw below the screen.

- **59 procedural expressions**: The onboard display renders **59 animated expressions**—spanning idle blinks, directional glances, curiosity, drowsiness, live audio-synced voice bars while talking, gesture accompaniments, startled/grumpy contact faces, and `cosmic_horror`.
- **Freestyle postures and fault recovery**: Beyond analytical gaits (`Walk`, `Run`, `Crawl`, `Crab`), Core 1's `50 Hz` neural walk, and 23 compiled gestures (`Wave`, `Bow`, `Stretch`, `Sit`, `Play Dead`), Ainekio draws on a **349-motion Freestyle library**. Its generative posture specialist was trained on a **489-row dataset** combining **73** curated library motions with **416** synthesized fault-recovery trajectories that safely untangle snagged legs and return to a stable `Stand`.

## Physical reactions when picked up, tipped, or stalled

A desk or floor companion inevitably gets bumped, picked up, or caught on the edge of a rug. Every joint on V2 has a 4th wire soldered to its internal potentiometer wiper and routed through channels `C4–C15` of a `CD74HC4067` multiplexer into `GPIO49` (`ADC2_CH0`), paired with a `208 Hz` `LSM6DS3` IMU.

When Ainekio is tipped over, shaken, or hits a servo stall:

1. **Immediate hardware awareness**: Onboard firmware detects the attitude excursion or shaft-tracking error in milliseconds and emits a structured body event without cutting servo holding power unnecessarily on minor snags.
2. **Non-repetitive vocal and facial reflexes**: Instead of playing a fixed sound effect, Ainekio OS routes sudden jostles through a fast reflex path that checks the **last 6 body-event remarks** (with elevated sampling diversity) so it pairs a matching LCD expression with a fresh, dryly varied comment rather than repeating the same canned line every time you pick it up.
3. **Preserved task context**: Answering a bump or pickup does not erase a paused multi-step goal that Ainekio was working on beforehand.

## Continuity across days (and at `02:00 AM`)

What makes Ainekio feel like an ongoing familiar rather than a fresh session each morning is how memory and identity are separated from transient chat context:

- **Provenance-tagged episodic memory**: Conversations, visual observations, task results, internal curiosity chains, and dreams are indexed locally with explicit source tags. Ainekio knows whether it *saw* your coffee mug through its chin camera, *heard* you mention it, or *imagined* it during a background reflection.
- **Protected Standing Persona**: Core character traits, values, and voice live in a dedicated persona record that ordinary chat turns cannot overwrite, preventing prompt drift over long conversations.
- **Overnight `02:00` consolidation**: During its nightly software sleep cycle, Ainekio organizes the day's memories, curates clean adapter training examples, samples 15 weighted memories into symbolic dreams, and runs an internal psychotherapist review that updates long-term persona notes and autonomous desire strengths. (See [Ainekio OS Architecture](/posts/ainekio-os/) for the full 8-stage pipeline.)

## What works standalone vs. with a host PC

Because the physical body weighs `650 g` and runs on a microcontroller rather than carrying a heavy laptop GPU on its back, capabilities divide cleanly between standalone onboard operation and host-connected companion mode:

| Operating Setup | What runs onboard the `ESP32-P4` V2 robot | What the host PC or `Radxa Dragon Q6A` adds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Standalone Body (`ESP32-P4` only)** | Closed-loop `Stand` (`2 mm` settle), analytical `Walk`/`Run`/`Crawl`/`Crab`, 23 built-in gestures, **on-chip `50 Hz` `107.8 KB` MicroDuck neural walking**, `208 Hz` IMU self-leveling, `CD74HC4067` (`C4–C15` → `GPIO49`) stall detection, **59 LCD expressions**, `WakeNet9s` wake detection, microSD clip recording, and local failsafe / inactivity `Rest` | *Not required for self-contained standing, leveling, or onboard controller/gait testing* |
| **Connected Companion (Body + Host Gateway & Ainekio OS)** | All real-time motor control, workspace projection, shaft-arrival verification, audio capture/playback, and camera/IMU streaming | Whisper speech-to-text, Kokoro/Piper speech synthesis, Standing Persona conversation, multimodal vision & optical-flow obstacle range, Freestyle posture synthesis, attributed episodic memory, Reactive/Semi/Full Auto agency ([autonomy guide](/posts/bounded-autonomy/)), and overnight `02:00` sleep consolidation |

You can inspect and steer both halves from the local web workbench (for chat, memory inspection, cognitive workflows, and model routing) and the **Body Control / Cockpit HUD** interface ([gateway guide](/posts/gateway-and-simulator/)) for live camera views, servo calibration, and manual teleoperation.
