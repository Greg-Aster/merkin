## A familiar with a physical body

Ainekio is a four-legged robot familiar being developed as a persistent
conversational companion. The aim is a recognizable personality that carries
experience from one interaction into the next: talking, noticing its surroundings,
expressing itself, choosing activities and learning from what happens.

Its body provides movement, a camera, a microphone, a speaker and a face display.
**MetaHuman OS** provides the software for identity, conversation, memory,
perception, decisions and learning. Body Control connects the two and gives the
owner a manual interface. The original eight-servo V1 and the twelve-servo V2
have different physical capabilities; the connected body's capabilities determine
which actions are available.

This guide describes the companion's intended functions and the systems being
built to support them. The complete companion experience is still in development.
[Current status](/posts/current-status/) and the dated build reports record what
has been tested and what remains unresolved.

## What Ainekio is being built to do

| Function | What it means for the companion |
| --- | --- |
| Converse | Respond through text or speech, follow the exchange and connect an answer to the relevant situation |
| Perceive | Use camera observations, recognition and body sensing to inform answers and activities |
| Move and express | Combine supported postures, gestures, locomotion, speech and face expressions |
| Remember | Retain useful experiences and retrieve them for later conversation and decisions |
| Pursue goals | Keep track of an objective, choose steps, examine results and decide what to do next |
| Reflect and imagine | Produce attributed internal reflections, dreams, daydreams and questions that can contribute to later activity |
| Learn | Use reviewed interactions and corrections to improve specialist skills and, ultimately, conversational personality |

These functions belong to one companion. For example, an intended request to
help find an object would connect conversation, visual evidence, selected
actions and review of the result. The model chooses the steps from the situation;
there is no fixed search routine for every object. Reliable autonomous search
and navigation still require their own demonstrations.

## How the software functions connect

An agent coordinates a function; its node workflow connects context, model calls
and results. A model category assigns a responsibility to each call, while the
status widget selects the concrete model. Active Operator coordinates the
selected autonomy mode; Body Event Trigger interprets physical events.

Orchestrator routes the request, Task Decisions selects supported actions,
Vision handles visual evidence, and Planner handles complex planning. Persona
owns the outward reply using selected results and applicable assessment.
Task no longer inserts speech steps between motions. This separates action
selection from expression while allowing independent work to overlap.

[The October 15 systems report](/posts/2026-10-15-companion-systems/) explains
all ten categories, the actual activation records and remaining failures.

## Personality and continuity

Ainekio's **persona** describes its identity, background, personality, values and
goals. Relevant parts can inform how it responds and what it considers doing.
The aim is consistent character with reactions appropriate to the moment.
Variation should come from context, experience and model choices.

Personality and memory play different roles. A persona can describe an interest;
a memory records a particular experience. A generated story or imagined event
must remain distinguishable from something the robot actually observed.

Three forms of continuity work together:

- **Recent context** keeps the current conversation and relevant internal activity available across successive turns.
- **Persistent memory** stores experiences beyond that recent context and retrieves useful records later.
- **Saved task progress** retains an objective, corrections, actions and results while work continues.

Model training is a fourth, slower process: it changes learned behavior. Saving
a conversation does not immediately train the model, and a trained model does
not automatically contain the current conversation.

## Inner activity and sleep

MetaHuman includes workflows for reflection, dreams, daydreams, curiosity,
associative thought and mood review. Reflection examines supplied experience;
dreams and daydreams produce imaginative material. Some internal activity may
inform a later conversation or choice without being spoken as it is generated.
Here, “inner activity” names software-generated content, not a claim of subjective
experience.

The software's **Sleep Workflow** brings together memory organization, curation,
eligible personalization training, desire processing, dreaming, persona review
and memory-index refresh. Which stages run depends on their settings and results.
This cognitive maintenance is separate from putting the physical body into
Doze or a low-power state. A body waking up does not mean a software sleep
cycle has completed.

The intended connection is that useful material remains available and can
influence later interaction. Recorded tests have found dreams that were saved
but did not reach the answering model. Generation, storage, retrieval and
use therefore remain separate parts of the work to verify.

## Choosing when to act

Ainekio has three intended robot autonomy modes:

| Mode | Intended behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Reactive | Respond to requests and physical events without independently choosing new activities |
| Semi-autonomous | Let configured agents and triggers initiate selected functions and activities |
| Fully autonomous | Choose high-level activities, pursue goals, review desires and use outcomes to decide what comes next |

A **persona goal** is a longer-term developmental or aspirational direction.
A **desire** is a specific desired outcome managed in Agency. An execution
objective identifies what a particular task should achieve. An action is one
step toward that outcome.
Completing a camera capture, for instance, does not prove that a missing object
has been found.

Full autonomy is intended to support purposeful, varied activity with continuity.
Repeated commands without progress do not demonstrate that capability. Ordinary
conversation also has value on its own; every exchange need not become a
physical task or a new goal. The [autonomy guide](/posts/bounded-autonomy/)
explains the operating modes and their development history.

## Carrying a movement through to its outcome

The new [locomotion goal interface](/posts/2026-10-15-locomotion-goals/) describes
a measured distance, relative turn, target relationship or visual endpoint
alongside gait controls. The task receives progress evidence and the outcome of
finishing that movement. A motor setting or completed gait cycle does not prove
arrival. Installed support is recorded; calibration, model selection and physical
accuracy still need qualification.

## Reviewing intentions and identity

Desire review can consider standing goals, attributed memories, selected-robot
status, capabilities, original requests, chosen actions and actual results.
It can revise an intention's strength or archive it when it is no longer
warranted. Agency's All Open view includes desires that are not yet active.

The Psychotherapist agent reviews experience for persona changes during manual
review or Sleep. It is the sole autonomous persona editor; ordinary Desire
reinforcement no longer creates persona goals. Using the Psychotherapist model
category for a Desire call does not give that workflow authority to edit persona.
Manual persona editing remains available.

These implemented boundaries improve what the models can assess. They do not
establish that every assessment is correct or that a complete learning cycle
has been qualified.

## Learning skills and learning a personality

The project has two related learning directions. **Specialist training** improves
functions such as interpreting intent and selecting task actions. **Conversational
personalization** aims to teach the model Ainekio's identity and expression from
reviewed companion replies and experience.

The intended local model arrangement uses small 0.8B specialists for Intent and
Task, with a larger 9B model for conversation and higher-level functions. Roles
are configurable, and an investigation may use another provider. A particular
running assignment is documented separately from this design direction.

Today, a persona description supplies identity through context. The longer-term
aim is a trained conversational persona that expresses that identity without
needing the full description repeated in every request. Selected memories,
current observations and instructions would still provide the information needed
for the situation. This is a development direction, not a claim that the learned
persona has already been deployed.

Learning requires review and correction, training, comparison on separate test
cases, and a decision about deployment. Fluent replies and successful training
runs are not enough to establish improvement. The
[Task training report](/posts/2026-10-14-speech-and-task-training/) shows why
better aggregate scores can still leave a candidate undeployed.

## Latest learning evidence

Two small independent 9B diagnostics for autonomy and conversational personality
completed on October 15. Serving tests still found lost objectives and invented
experience; neither adapter qualified for production. The
[diagnostic report](/posts/2026-10-15-learning-diagnostics/) explains the limited
coverage and why lower training loss did not establish a better companion.

## Understanding and steering the system

MetaHuman's node editor exposes workflows, prompts, model roles and results.
Conversation, memory, persona and agency interfaces let the owner inspect
different aspects of the companion. Robot Status shows body evidence and task
state. Body Control supplies manual motion, sensing, audio and maintenance
controls for the selected robot.

Processing can be distributed between the body, a nearby computer and more
powerful compute. The body retains motor timing and peripheral control while
MetaHuman handles high-level cognition. Seamless transfer of an ongoing goal
between installations remains a separate development problem.

The test of this project is the connected experience: useful conversation,
appropriate action, remembered context and meaningful improvement. The current
work still includes reliable recall, sustained autonomy, voice delivery, correct
motion selection and physical validation. Individual working components are
progress toward that experience.
