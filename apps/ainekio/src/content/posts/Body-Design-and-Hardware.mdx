> **V1 hardware reference:** For an end-to-end overview of how all of V1's mechanics, firmware, vision, voice, 52 OLED expressions, and `protocol-v1` gateway work together, start with the **[Ainekio V1 Systems Guide](/posts/ainekio-v1/)**. For the active `650 g` twelve-servo `ESP32-P4` robot and the upcoming ~1.7× `DS3235` + Linux PC V3 build, see **[Ainekio V2](/posts/ainekio-v2/)**, **[V1 vs. V2 Comparison](/posts/v1-v2-comparison/)**, and **[Ainekio V3 Preview](/posts/ainekio-v3-preview/)**.

Ainekio's original **V1** hardware baseline is an eight-degree-of-freedom (`4 legs × 2 DOF`) quadruped built around a **Freenove `ESP32-S3-WROOM CAM`** (`N16R8`) microcontroller and eight **`MG90S`** micro servos. Each corner leg uses two planar joints—an inboard hip yaw servo (`R1` front-right, `R2` rear-right, `L1` front-left, `L2` rear-left) sweeping an upper `R2V10` trussed bracket horizontally, and an outboard knee pitch servo (`R4` front-right, `R3` rear-right, `L3` rear-left, `L4` front-left) lifting a curved, serrated lower leg vertically—while **[Ainekio OS](/posts/ainekio-os/)** (formerly MetaHuman OS) handles conversation, memory, and high-level planning over Wi-Fi.

## Mechanical structure, tracked STLs & dedicated Blender CAD scene

Modeled in Blender as a custom spider-stance quadruped, V1's printed enclosure evolved across tracked revisions in [`Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot`](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot) (`FrameV1` → `Frame6` → `Frame7` → `Frame8`, alongside `Bottom-Housing1`, `oled-box1`, `R2V10` leg brackets, serrated lower legs, and internal board mounts `Board-hold1..3`).

- **Open-loop 2-DOF gait & timed turns:** V1 plays 38 stored `.amot` keyframe clips (`walk_forward`, `walk_backward`, continuous turns, and 22 gestures/dances) plus bounded `motion_plan_v1` sequences within calibrated per-joint limits. Because V1 carries neither an IMU nor servo potentiometer feedback, its named `45°`, `90°`, and `180°` scrub turns are **open-loop timed estimates** whose exact floor rotation depends on carpet friction, horn alignment, and battery voltage.
- **Original direct-`MCPWM` prototype vs. refined `'v1 -'` Blender CAD scene:** On the August 2026 physical build, all eight `MG90S` signal wires connected directly to `ESP32-S3` `MCPWM` pins with a single `2S` `1000 mAh` pack tucked inside the central cavity. In the dedicated **`Scene 'v1 -'`** Blender CAD assembly ([shown in full in the V1 Systems Guide](/posts/ainekio-v1/)), `Frame7` (`122 × 92 × 21.3 mm`) and the `Frame8` top cap (`110 × 78 × 48 mm`) are paired with symmetric left/right `2S` `14500` battery cradles, snap-in board clips, and a central `PCA9685` header deck for clean wire management.

## Complete V1 bill of materials

Compiled from `docs/v1-8servo/SPECIFICATIONS.md` and the September 2, 2026 [ESP32-S3 Build & Firmware Reference](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1JZMMG3J96v6cnw2YupPp9OF9fiw0H4j0tS8t8fsplJk/edit):

| Subsystem | Documented V1 Part & Specification |
| --- | --- |
| **Body controller** | **Freenove `ESP32-S3-WROOM CAM`** (`FNK0085`, `N16R8`): dual-core `160 MHz` Xtensa LX7, `16 MiB` flash, `8 MiB` octal PSRAM (`80 MHz`) |
| **Joint actuators (8 DOF)** | 8× **`MG90S`** metal-gear micro servos (`4 legs × 2 DOF`: `R1, R2, L1, L2` hip sweep + `R4, R3, L3, L4` knee lift; `50 Hz`, `732–2500 µs`) |
| **Face display** | **`0.96"` `SSD1306` I2C monochrome OLED** (`128 × 64`, `0x3C`, **52 animated expressions** + idle blink & speech mouth overlay) |
| **Camera** | Delivered **`OV3660`** DVP parallel camera (`XGA` `1024×768` JPEG `q=10`, manual `72`-line `~1/60 s` indoor exposure; superseded early `OV2640` docs; `Arducam B0066` modeled on the CAD face mount) |
| **Microphone & speaker** | External **`INMP441`** I2S MEMS microphone + **`MAX98357A`** I2S Class-D amplifier driving an **`8 Ω 2 W`** cavity speaker (`16 kHz` full-duplex I2S) |
| **Battery & USB-C charging** | **Sea Jump `2S` (`7.4 V` nominal / `8.4 V` full), `1000 mAh` `10C` Li-ion pack** (`2× 14500`, `SM-3P`) + **`HiLetgo 2S` USB-C boost charger** + **`KCD1`** master power switch |
| **Dual `5 V` power rails** | **2× `MINI560 PRO` `5 V / 5 A` buck converters**: **Buck #1** + **`1000 µF`** bulk capacitor dedicated to the 8 servos; **Buck #2** dedicated to `ESP32-S3` `5V` logic and `MAX98357A` audio |
| **Battery telemetry & storage** | **`100 kΩ / 47 kΩ`** divider into `GPIO3` (`ADC1`) + `9.375 MiB` `LittleFS` (`/assets`), `512 KiB` `SPIFFS` (`vadnet1_medium`), and 1-bit `SD_MMC` rolling CRC logs |

Notably, the **`1000 mAh` `2S` `14500`** pack replaced an earlier `2600 mAh` `18650` proposal from the July shopping list that proved too bulky for the V1 chassis cavity.

## V1 wiring & power isolation

The authoritative V1 pin assignments live in [`Slave/firmware/esp32s3/components/ainekio_platform/include/ainekio/platform/pin_map.h`](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot/blob/main/Slave/firmware/esp32s3/components/ainekio_platform/include/ainekio/platform/pin_map.h). Because `GPIO33–37` are occupied internally by octal PSRAM on the `N16R8` module, earlier provisional `GPIO33/34` servo maps were retired in favor of `GPIO47/48`; see the [V1 ESP32-S3 Firmware Reference](/posts/controller-firmware/) for the complete GPIO table.

Splitting `2S` battery power across **two separate `MINI560 PRO` `5 V / 5 A` buck regulators** (sharing a common ground reference, with a `1000 µF` reservoir capacitor across the servo bus) prevented simultaneous 8-servo stall transients from resetting the `ESP32-S3` during Wi-Fi bursts or camera capture. Battery voltage sampled through the `100 kΩ / 47 kΩ` divider on `GPIO3` triggered telemetry warnings at `7.2 V`, refused new motion commands below `7.0 V`, and flagged critical alerts at `6.8 V`—relying on the pack's inline BMS and `KCD1` switch for physical cutoff rather than software deep sleep.

## How V1 led to Ainekio V2 (and V3)

Running V1 on real floors proved out the end-to-end voice, vision, and `protocol-v1` loop while exposing the exact physical limits of two-joint open-loop legs: no lateral hip roll for side-stepping or dynamic balance, no way to detect when a foot stalled on carpet, and tight GPIO contention on the `ESP32-S3`. Those lessons drove two clean-sheet successors:

- **[Ainekio V2 (Active 650 g 12-Servo Quadruped)](/posts/ainekio-v2/)**: Added lateral hip roll (`12` servos), compact `35/20/28/38/55 mm` four-bar linkages, **4th-wire potentiometer feedback** (`CD74HC4067` `C4–C15` → `GPIO49`) on a **`Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6`**, a `208 Hz` `LSM6DS3` IMU, a **59-expression** `1.9"` `ST7789` color LCD, `WakeNet9s` wake-word audio, and **on-chip `50 Hz` `107.8 KB` L2-cached neural walking**.
- **[Ainekio V3 (In Development · ~1.7× Scale `DS3235` & Mobile Linux PC)](/posts/ainekio-v3-preview/)**: Scales up ~1.7× with waterproof **`DS3235`** standard servos, bearing-supported trussed four-bar legs, `2S2P` `21700` power, a **`5.0"` `1280×720` capacitive touch face**, and an **onboard Wi-Fi/Bluetooth Linux SBC + `ESP32-P4`** inside the shell so the robot runs autonomously without an external PC and doubles as a desktop Linux workstation.
