Ainekio's autonomy architecture governs when and how the companion initiates physical actions, speech, internal reflections, or computer tasks without waiting for a human prompt. Managed by **Active Operator** in **[Ainekio OS (`Greg-Aster/ainekio-os`)](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/ainekio-os)** (formerly MetaHuman OS; [architecture guide](/posts/ainekio-os/)), every autonomous decision flows through the same unified `environment-mode.json` cognitive graph, Work Coordinator lease checks, five progressive trust levels (`observe` → `adaptive_auto`), and `ESP32-P4` hardware safety boundaries as a direct user command.

## The three robot autonomy modes

In addition to Ainekio OS's four cognitive modes (`Dual Consciousness`, `Agent`, `Emulation`, and `Environment`), embodied operation offers three robot autonomy levels—plus an instant top-bar **Ainekio OS control** enable/pause toggle in Body Control and Cockpit HUD:

| Mode | Scheduling & Decision Mechanism | Typical Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reactive** | Dormant between external triggers. Responds immediately to user chat/voice and physical disturbances (`fast-body-event-mode.json`). | Bench testing, quiet companionship, and direct operator control |
| **Semi Mode** | Runs a **5-minute periodic evaluation** that applies **signed delta updates** to internal drives and initiates bounded activities when a drive crosses threshold. | Ambient room presence with periodic self-initiated check-ins, stretches, or observations |
| **Full Auto Mode** | **Continuous high-reasoning activity selection** (`Qwen3.5 9B` locally or Hybrid `ServerStatus` routing). Never idles: as soon as an execution settles, the controller selects the next purposeful activity. | Autonomous exploration, goal pursuit, environment inspection, and multi-step task chaining |

## Semi Mode: 5-minute signed drive deltas

In **Semi Mode**, Ainekio does not loop continuously. Instead, Active Operator ticks on a configurable **5-minute (`300 s`) interval**:

1. **Signed delta drive updates**: Each tick evaluates recent interaction history, time elapsed since the last conversation or movement, sensor state, and pending Agency desires, applying signed deltas (`+/-`) to internal drive states (such as curiosity, social engagement, or restlessness).
2. **Situation-dependent trigger**: If drives remain below threshold or a user turn is already active, the tick settles quietly without firing a conversational turn. When a drive crosses its activation threshold, Active Operator launches an `environment-mode.json` execution tailored to that situation—such as taking a fresh camera look (`boredom-observer`), performing a Freestyle stretch (`boredom-movement`), or running internal curiosity/reflection (`inner-curiosity.json`, `train-of-thought.json`).

## Full Auto Mode: continuous high-reasoning agency

In **Full Auto Mode**, Ainekio stays continuously active—never idling between completed tasks:

1. **Continuous activity selection**: Whenever no execution is currently running or waiting on a hardware receipt, Active Operator invokes the high-reasoning `fullAutoController` role (`Qwen3.5 9B` or Hybrid specialist in `ServerStatus`) with the **Standing Persona** (`persona/core.json`), attributed episodic memories, live `Robot Status`, recent action outcomes, and open Agency desires.
2. **Continuity across turns**: Rather than issuing isolated one-off commands, Full Auto can resume an unfinished objective, inspect the visual or telemetry result of its previous step, adjust its plan, or transition into a new activity when the prior goal is satisfied.
3. **Multi-tool reach**: Full Auto can choose physical robot locomotion (on-chip `50 Hz` MicroDuck neural walking, closed-loop `Walk`/`Stand`, or `Freestyle`), camera inspection, spoken remarks, internal reflection, or asynchronous computer delegation via **Big Brother** (`codex`, `claude-code`, and warm 2-session `GeminiAcpServer` via `big-brother-tool-mode.json` and `system-code-review`).

## Varied Fast Body Events (`fast-body-event-mode.json`)

Regardless of whether Ainekio is in Reactive, Semi, or Full Auto mode, physical disturbances from the `ESP32-P4` body are handled immediately:

- **Trigger sources**: The `208 Hz` `LSM6DS3` IMU and 12-channel `CD74HC4067` servo feedback multiplexer detect `tipped`, `shaken`, and motor `jam/stall` events.
- **Lightweight reflex path with 6-turn variety**: Tip, shake, and stall events route to `fast-body-event-mode.json`, which queries the last **6 `fast_body_event_response` speech entries** (`temperature = 0.8`, `repeatPenalty = 1.2`) and uses a single fast model call to emit a **`be_` token-matched facial expression** (rendered across 59 expressions on the 1.9" `ST7789` LCD) and a fresh, dryly varied vocal reaction.
- **Non-disruptive**: Fast body events do not clobber an in-progress durable objective, while high-rate feedback health updates are absorbed into `Robot Status` state without spamming the cognitive queue.

## Desire lifecycle vs. Standing Persona

Ainekio OS separates long-term identity from actionable autonomous intentions:

- **Standing Persona (`persona/core.json`)**: Defines Ainekio's enduring character and long-term values. Autonomous edits to `persona/core.json` are restricted exclusively to the **Psychotherapist** agent during the `02:00` Sleep Cycle.
- **Desire Agent**: Evaluates candidate desires in Agency against attributed memories, robot capabilities, and actual task outcomes. It updates **desire strengths** via signed adjustments and archives completed or obsolete desires.
- **Finite execution contracts**: Before a Desire can drive physical robot commands, it must be formulated into a finite plan (`1–10` ordered steps with explicit completion criteria). Each step executes through the Work Coordinator and returns its verified receipt (`done`, `cancelled`, `nak`) to Agency before the next step is admitted.

## Preemption, Sleep, and emergency stop

- **Native inference cancellation**: If a user speaks a correction or issues a stop command while an autonomous turn is generating on `llama-cpp.service`, the router cancels the in-flight inference natively and admits the user's input.
- **Direct emergency stop**: `stop` bypasses language-model planning altogether, cancelling queued coordinator actions for the session and instructing the gateway and `ESP32-P4` firmware to halt motion immediately.
- **Software Sleep vs. hardware rest**: Entering the 8-stage `02:00` cognitive **Sleep Cycle** pauses Active Operator so memory curation, LoRA adapter fitting, symbolic dreaming (`dreamer-mode.json`), and Psychotherapist review can run cleanly. Separately, the `ESP32-P4` firmware can relax the body into a low-power `Rest` posture after configurable physical inactivity.
