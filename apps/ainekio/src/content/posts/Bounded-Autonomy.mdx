Letting a language model initiate physical motion on a walking robot only works if agency and actuator safety have a hard wall between them. In **[Ainekio OS](/posts/ainekio-os/)** ([`Greg-Aster/ainekio-os`](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/ainekio-os)), every self-initiated action passes through the same [embodied bridge](/posts/metahuman-integration/), trust-tier checks, and onboard `ESP32-P4` geometric/electrical safety gates ([`Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot`](https://github.com/Greg-Aster/Ainekio-bot)) as a direct human command.

## The three robot operating modes

When running in embodied mode, Ainekio offers three distinct autonomy levels—alongside a one-click **Ainekio OS pause switch** in the top bar of the Body Control workbench and Cockpit HUD:

| Operating Mode | Scheduling & Initiative | When to Use It |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reactive** | Quiet between external triggers. Responds only when spoken to, messaged, or physically jostled/tipped. | Bench calibration, focused desk work, and direct manual control |
| **Semi Mode** | Evaluates internal drives every **5 minutes**, applying signed (`+/-`) delta updates and initiating a bounded look, stretch, or reflection only when a drive crosses threshold. | Everyday room companionship with occasional unprompted check-ins or posture shifts |
| **Full Auto** | Continuous high-reasoning activity selection. As soon as one task settles, Ainekio evaluates its surroundings, memories, and open desires to pick the next purposeful step. | Supervised floor exploration, multi-step errands, and active room inspection |

### Semi Mode: 5-minute drive ticks

Rather than polling an LLM in a tight loop while nothing is happening, **Semi Mode** runs on a calm **5-minute (`300 s`) cadence**:

1. **Signed drive updates**: Each tick weighs time since the last interaction or movement, current posture and battery health, and open desires—applying small positive or negative deltas to internal curiosity, restlessness, and social engagement drives.
2. **Thresholded initiative**: If all drives sit below threshold (or a human conversation is already underway), nothing fires. Once a drive crosses threshold, Ainekio launches a single bounded activity—snapping a chin-camera frame to see what changed in the room, running a brief Freestyle leg stretch, or recording an associative inner reflection.

### Full Auto: continuous purposeful chaining

In **Full Auto**, Ainekio never sits idle between settled executions. Whenever no movement or tool wait is active, a high-reasoning controller reviews the Standing Persona, attributed episodic memories, live V2 telemetry, optical-flow obstacle clearance, and open desires to continue an unfinished objective or start a new one—chaining on-chip `50 Hz` MicroDuck walking, camera checks, spoken remarks, or background computer tasks.

## Why the LLM never drives raw servo PWM

Regardless of operating mode, **no language model can write raw pulse widths or unvalidated angles to a servo pin**. Autonomy is bounded by four nested safety layers:

1. **Capability-filtered intent vocabulary**: Ainekio OS can only choose high-level semantic postures, bounded velocity twists, locomotion goals, or structured 12-DOF Freestyle target programs that match the connected robot's live readiness state (V2 `ESP32-P4` vs. V1 `ESP32-S3`).
2. **Onboard Cartesian workspace projection**: Each V2 leg uses a coupled `35/20/28/38/55 mm` four-bar linkage. Before converting any foot target or neural offset into servo angles, `ESP32-P4` firmware projects out-of-reach coordinates back onto the valid reachable linkage boundary using analytical inverse kinematics and 8-pass Gauss-Newton refinement—preventing linkage lockups and mechanical bind.
3. **4th-wire shaft tracking & current staging**: All twelve `MG90S` servos feed potentiometer wiper voltages through `CD74HC4067` channels `C4–C15` into `GPIO49` (`ADC2_CH0`) while `PCA9685` PWM pulses are phase-staggered across the `50 Hz` frame to prevent `5 V` rail brownouts. Target sequences wait for measured physical shaft arrival (`2 mm` settle in `Stand`) rather than blindly trusting a timer.
4. **Command leases, preemption & instant `stop`**: Spoken human interruptions cancel in-flight local model inference immediately, while a `stop` command bypasses language models altogether—dropping queued actions and halting the servos on the microcontroller. If Wi-Fi drops mid-stride, lease watchdogs halt locomotion locally without replaying stale motion on reconnect ([protocol guide](/posts/protocol-and-safety/)).

## Fast reflexes when tipped, shaken, or stalled

Physical surprises cannot wait for multi-step deliberation:

- **Millisecond hardware detection**: Comparing `208 Hz` `LSM6DS3` IMU attitude and `CD74HC4067` potentiometer velocities against commanded trajectories lets the `ESP32-P4` catch falls, pickups, shakes, and rug snags immediately.
- **Non-terminal stall recovery**: On a minor foot snag, the body raises a `servo_stall` event while keeping holding torque alive so a backoff-and-`Stand` recovery trajectory (trained across the **489-row** Freestyle dataset: **73** library motions + **416** fault-recovery trajectories) can clear the leg cleanly.
- **Varied 6-turn vocal & facial reactions**: Tip, shake, and stall events trigger one of the **59 animated expressions** on the `1.9"` `ST7789` LCD alongside a quick vocal remark that checks the last 6 reactions to avoid repeating canned lines—all without wiping out a paused multi-step goal.

## How longer goals persist safely

Longer autonomous intentions are kept bounded so an old idea never turns into an infinite loop:

- **Finite `1–10` step Desire plans**: Candidate desires are scored against real outcomes and must be compiled into a finite `1–10` step plan with explicit completion criteria before executing any physical robot commands.
- **Verified step checkpoints**: Each step suspends in SQLite until the gateway returns a correlated hardware receipt (`done`, `cancelled`, `nak`, or `outcome_unknown`) and, for visual goals, a fresh post-motion camera frame confirming the result.
- **Software sleep vs. hardware rest**: Entering the nightly `02:00` software sleep cycle pauses autonomous initiative while memory curation, dreaming, and persona review run; independently, the `ESP32-P4` can relax the legs into a low-power `Rest` posture during extended room quiet.
