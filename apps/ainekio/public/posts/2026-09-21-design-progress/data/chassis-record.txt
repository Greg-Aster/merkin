## Validation and limits

Boolean numerical fragments below 0.001 mm3 were removed; they are listed explicitly in the report. Side-interface comparisons use numerical tolerances, while all protected rigid parts retain identical mesh-coordinate hashes.

Imported near-planar plate faces were restored to their nominal planes within 0.0002 mm before filling the obsolete center holes. The front mesh was simplified in Blender's local float32 coordinate representation with a 0.0001 mm tolerance. Sub-0.00005 mm degenerate edges were cleaned during import, with native closed-mesh and face-area assertions before accepting each part.

Static interference checks include the retained translated leg/support solids and the LCD/camera. Existing near-coplanar plate contacts are approximately 0.012-0.016 mm3 in the original scene; the new layout does not increase those contacts. There is no substantive new collision in that set at frame 1. The user-supplied carapace remains a visual sculpt in progress and is not treated as a qualified enclosure solid.

`saved-verification.json` is written only after reopening the saved file and checking mesh topology, exact action hashes, protected geometry, side translations, and unchanged screen/sculpt transforms. Archived objects' world matrices are reconstructed from unchanged local transforms because disabled collections have unevaluated transform caches after reopening.

This is a chassis packaging prototype. Full motion clearance, new interior electronics mounting, final carapace attachments, print tolerances, strength and physical fit remain unqualified.
